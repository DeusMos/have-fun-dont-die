# About the author

My mom died at 60 and nobody could tell me why. There was no named disease and no paper to point at. She was just gone. Sixty isn't a finished run, and I want a lot more clock than that for the people I love.

I've never bought "natural causes" as an answer. It's a filing category for machinery that broke in specific ways nobody bothered to name. My view is we'd be a lot further toward agelessness if that were actually anyone's assignment. It isn't. Institutional science would rather do nothing than be wrong in public. I think they'll get there eventually, but my people are on a timer.

This started way before my mom. I went to university for molecular biology because I wanted to eliminate tooth decay. Cavities were the tutorial: one tissue, one ecosystem, a rot you can actually corner. I meant to keep going after that. I stayed a fifth year on purpose, chasing a double bachelor's in molecular biology and chemistry with a minor in botany, because I was trying to collect enough mechanism to make my loved ones immortal. I never finished it.

When the school figured out that was the plan, they weren't charmed. Meetings were had and concern was expressed, and I was nudged toward wanting something smaller and more employable. Then the laws changed and made what I was working on very, very illegal. So I dropped out and went into software. Honestly it's the same problem with a different compiler.

Then a beautiful woman walked in. I fell in love before I had a protocol for it, and the ordinary campaign started going suspiciously well. 😉 👶👶👶

After my mom died, the papers came back off the shelf.

I run agent swarms for a living now, so of course I built one for this. The agents do the reading and the sourcing. The thinking stays with me, and with you. Laziness is just the relentless pursuit of efficiency.

This repo is what I'm leaving on the table: sourced notes, evidence marks, and agents you can point at the literature the journals won't get around to until 2048. It's for anyone who wants that knowledge and doesn't already know how to build the machine to get it.
