# Bischoff-Ferrari, Gängler, Wieczorek, et al., 2025. Individual and additive effects of vitamin D, omega-3 and exercise on DNA methylation clocks of biological aging in older adults from the DO-HEALTH trial. Nature Aging.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00793-y PMC11922767
Used for: Post-hoc DO-HEALTH clock analysis: vitamin D 2,000 IU/day and home exercise were null on PhenoAge, GrimAge, GrimAge2, and DunedinPACE; omega-3 1 g/day moved three of four next-generation clocks by d=0.16–0.32 (2.9–3.8 months over 3 years); GrimAge v1 and all first-generation clocks did not move.
