# Bell et al., 2019. DNA methylation aging clocks: challenges and recommendations. Genome Biology.
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1824-y PMID 31767039
Used for: DNA methylation clocks are highly accurate chronological-age correlates, but how far clock residuals capture functional deterioration versus prediction error is unsettled; the Hannum clock is partly driven by age-related blood-cell composition shifts; Horvath/Hannum CpGs are not clearly enriched for function over promoter background; clocks trained on small samples are prone to cell-composition confounding.
