# Fahy, Brooke, Watson, et al., 2019. Reversal of epigenetic aging and immunosenescent trends in humans. Aging Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028 PMC6826138
Used for: TRIIM reported a mean ~2.5-year drop in epigenetic-minus-chronological age after 12 months of rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men aged 51–65 with no placebo arm (10 enrolled, one removed); clocks were four estimators including GrimAge; side effects included arthralgias (2), carpal tunnel (1), fluid retention (1), mild gynecomastia (1).
