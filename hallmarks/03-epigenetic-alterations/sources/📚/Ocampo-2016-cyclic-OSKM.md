# Ocampo et al., 2016. In Vivo Amelioration of Age-Associated Hallmarks by Partial Reprogramming. Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.052 PMID 27984723 PMC5679279
Used for: 📚 Cyclic 2-on/5-off OSKM extended LAKI 4F progeria median/maximal lifespan and restored heterochromatin marks; improved injury repair in 12-month WT mice. Continuous OSKM was lethal.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: LAKI 4F survival −Dox n=18 / +Dox n=15, start 8 weeks, log-rank p<0.0001; LAKI no-4F doxycycline controls n=20/−13 did not benefit. Median lifespan ~18→24 weeks (~30%) from the survival plot. Heterochromatin H3K9me3/H4K20me3 restored in kidney/spleen after 6 weeks. WT 12-month 4F: 3-week cyclic OSKM then STZ pancreas or CTX muscle (muscle n=4/group). Homozygous 4F cyclic OSKM produced teratomas. Not wild-type lifespan extension.
