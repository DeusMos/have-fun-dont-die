Used for: In RCTs of GH as an anti-aging agent in community-dwelling adults, GH significantly increased soft-tissue edema, arthralgias, carpal tunnel syndrome, and gynecomastia versus no GH, with a non-significant excess of new diabetes/impaired fasting glucose.
