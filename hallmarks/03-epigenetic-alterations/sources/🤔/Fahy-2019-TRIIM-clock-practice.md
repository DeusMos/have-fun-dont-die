# Fahy et al., 2019. Reversal of epigenetic aging and immunosenescent trends in humans. Aging Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028 PMID 31496122 PMC6826138
Used for: 🤔 TRIIM pilot (rhGH + DHEA + metformin) in 9 completing men reported a ~2.5-year clock decrement at 12 months; no placebo. Practice / small uncontrolled human clock move, not healthspan efficacy.

N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 10 men 51–65 enrolled, 9 completed; 12 months on (rhGH ~0.015 mg/kg 3–4×/week + DHEA 50 mg + metformin 500 mg, then individualized), 6 months off.
