## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that age-associated epigenetic alterations — DNA-methylation drift, histone and chromatin change, heterochromatin loss, 3D-genome shift, repeat derepression, transcriptional noise — accumulate with ordinary human aging, causally drive it, can be scored as “biological age,” and can be reversed so people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity clinics, supplement brands, and cash-pay labs sell that claim as a product: TruAge / Index / TallyAge / NOVOS Age as treat-to-target dashboards, GlycanAge as another “biological age,” TRIIM (rhGH + DHEA + metformin) as thymus-plus-clock reversal, NAD capsules bundled with the same company’s swab, and Life Biosciences ER-100 first-patient press as human epigenetic restoration (TruDiagnostic shop; Elysium Index and Basis; Tally Health; NOVOS Age; GlycanAge; Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019; Life Biosciences 9 Jun 2026; AgelessRx TruAge + rapamycin storefront).

📚 López-Otín et al. listed epigenetic alterations among nine tentative hallmarks in 2013 and kept the category among twelve in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that a commercial clock is the master machinery of aging, or as a license to sell a reset product, is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Horvath-class age estimation, GrimAge/DunedinPACE mortality HRs, CALERIE/DO-HEALTH clock deltas, Ocampo/Lu/Browder mouse OSK(M), and iPSC methylation age near zero — into “buy a biological age and reverse it” (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013; Lu et al., Aging, 2019; Belsky et al., eLife, 2022; Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023; Bischoff-Ferrari et al., Nature Aging, 2025; Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016; Lu et al., Nature, 2020; TruDiagnostic; Elysium; Tally; NOVOS).

## What the words mean

💯 Cytosine-5 methylation at CpG dinucleotides is a covalent DNA modification; it is the analyte most aging clocks actually read (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2013-14-10-r115, PMID 24138928).

📚 In the 2013/2023 hallmark papers, epigenetic alterations are a bag: DNA methylation, histone marks and variants, chromatin accessibility, heterochromatin, nuclear architecture / 3D contacts, regulatory RNAs, and identity-network drift — not one master clock (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Pal and Tyler, Science Advances, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.1600584, PMID 27482540).

📚 “Epigenetic age” in a paper is an algorithm’s output from selected CpGs trained on chronological age, a clinical/mortality proxy, or longitudinal physiology; Horvath (353 CpGs, 51 tissues) and Hannum (71 blood CpGs) are chronological-age estimators (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013; Hannum et al., Molecular Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.molcel.2012.10.016, PMID 23177740).

📚 DNAm PhenoAge is 513 CpGs trained on a nine-biomarker-plus-age clinical score; GrimAge is a two-stage mortality estimator (DNAm pack-years + seven protein surrogates) that the authors call “biological age” only metaphorically; GrimAge2 adds DNAm logCRP and logA1C; DunedinPACE is 173 CpGs distilled from a 20-year 19-organ Pace of Aging in one birth cohort (Levine et al., Aging, 2018, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.101414, PMID 29676998; Lu et al., Aging, 2019, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.101684, PMID 30669119; Lu et al., Aging, 2022, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.204434, PMID 36516495; Belsky et al., eLife, 2022, https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.73420, PMID 35390065).

🤔 Clinic English uses “biological age” / “epigenetic age” / “how old you really are” for TruAge, Index, TallyAge, NOVOS Age, or GlycanAge, and treats a retest decrement as reversal (TruDiagnostic; Elysium Index; Tally Health; NOVOS; GlycanAge).

⛔ GlycanAge is an IgG N-glycan score, not DNA methylation; filing it as a DNAm clock is a vocabulary collision (GlycanAge, https://glycanage.com/).

📚 Full OSKM reprogramming to iPSC erases identity and drives methylation age toward zero in culture; partial/transient OSK(M) is a time- or factor-limited attempt to restore youthful expression without pluripotency; those are not the same operation (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013; Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016; Lu et al., Nature, 2020; Yücel and Gladyshev, Nature Communications, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46020-5).

## Mechanism

📚 Ordinary human tissues accumulate age-associated DNA methylation change that a 353-CpG elastic-net (Horvath) reads as chronological age across 51 healthy tissue/cell types (test r=0.96, median absolute error 3.6 years; n=7,844 non-cancer samples / 82 datasets) (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013).

📚 Whole-blood methylation at 71 CpGs predicts chronological age at r=0.96 / error 3.9 years, and 70,387 of 485,577 array markers (15%) associate with age at FDR<0.05 (Hannum et al., Molecular Cell, 2013; N=656, ages 19–101).

📚 In same-sex aging twins, leukocyte methylation at 358,836 shared probes changes across a decade: mean broad heritability falls from 23.8% to 18.0%, and person-specific environment drives the emergent change (Reynolds et al., Aging Cell, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13197, PMID 32705757; N=53 SATSA + 43 LSADT pairs, ~10 years).

📚 A 2016 Science Advances review compiles that organismal aging and replicative senescence carry reduced core-histone levels, altered histone PTMs and DNA methylation, histone-variant replacement, and ncRNA change, ending in aberrant expression, transposon reactivation, and genomic instability (Pal and Tyler, Science Advances, 2016).

📚 WRN-null human ESC-derived MSCs lose H3K9me3 and heterochromatin architecture; catalytically inactive SUV39H1 knock-in in wild-type MSCs recapitulates accelerated senescence; MSCs from older donors show lower WRN and heterochromatin marks (Zhang et al., Science, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aaa1356, PMID 25931448).

📚 In late replicative senescence, LINE-1 RNA rises 4–5-fold, cytoplasmic L1 cDNA drives a type-I interferon response that helps maintain SASP, and lamivudine downregulates IFN-I and tissue inflammaging in aged mice (De Cecco et al., Nature, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0784-9, PMID 30728521).

📚 SIRT6 packages L1 into heterochromatin by mono-ADP-ribosylating KAP1 and recruiting HP1α; SIRT6 leaves L1 loci with age and DNA damage, and SIRT6-null cells show L1 5′-UTR hypomethylation and H3K9me3 loss (Van Meter et al., Nature Communications, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms6011, PMID 25297529).

📚 Existing epigenetic clocks and age-related differential methylation are not enriched for CpG sites identified as putatively causal for aging-related traits by epigenome-wide Mendelian randomization of 420,509 CpGs against eight aging traits (Ying et al., Nature Aging, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00557-0, PMID 38243142).

📚 A 10-CpG mortality score’s top hits are smoking-EWAS loci (AHRR cg05575921 first); 48/58 validated mortality CpGs were smoking-associated; a score of 5+ vs 0 had HR 7.36 (95% CI 3.69–14.68) for all-cause death (Zhang et al., Nature Communications, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms14617).

📚 Immune-cell composition ranked second after chronological age in explaining Horvath, Hannum, and PhenoAge variation, and was the top variable for DunedinPACE and EpiTOC2 TNSC (Zhang, Reynolds, Stolrow, Chen, Christensen, Salas, Aging Cell, 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38146185/, PMID 38146185, PMC10928575).

🥼 Yang et al. interpret ICE-mouse I-PpoI “faithful DSB repair” as loss of epigenetic information that advances aging and is OSK-reversible (information theory of aging); Timmons and Brenner argue I-PpoI is cytotoxic, that prior Sinclair-lab I-PpoI papers already showed p53-driven cell elimination within a month, that the first-month window was not analyzed, and that the Cell paper did not demonstrate functional restoration of old tissue — ITOA as tested is a live paper fight, not a settled software-reinstall fact (Yang et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.12.027, PMID 36638792; Timmons and Brenner, Cell, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.01.013; Brenner-lab PDF, https://www.brennerlab.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Timmons24.pdf).

## Animal data

📚 Cyclic 2-days-on / 5-days-off OSKM in LAKI 4F progeria mice, started at 8 weeks, increased median and maximal lifespan vs untreated LAKI 4F (log-rank p<0.0001) and restored H3K9me3 / H4K20me3 in kidney and spleen; continuous OSKM killed 4F mice in days; the same cyclic protocol improved STZ-pancreas and CTX-muscle repair in 12-month wild-type 4F mice (Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.052, PMID 27984723; survival −Dox n=18 / +Dox n=15).

📚 Eight weeks of the same cyclic protocol in mice carrying two copies of the OSKM and rtTA cassettes produced teratomas in liver, kidney, and pancreas (Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016).

📚 Transient in-vivo OSKM produced teratomas from multiple organs and circulating iPS cells in reprogrammable mice (Abad et al., Nature, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12586, PMID 24025773).

📚 Premature termination of in-vivo OSKM produced kidney and pancreas tumors with altered epigenetic regulation, not just teratomas after full reprogramming (Ohnishi et al., Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2014.01.005, PMID 24486105).

📚 AAV2 OSK (no MYC) in mouse RGCs restored youthful methylation and transcriptome, promoted post-crush axon regeneration, and reversed vision loss in a microbead glaucoma model and in 12-month-old mice; Tet1 or Tet2 knockdown/knockout blocked survival and regeneration (Lu et al., Nature, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2975-4, PMID 33268865; glaucoma optomotor/PERG n=10/7/12/12; Sinclair is a Life Biosciences founder).

📚 Long-term cyclic OSKM in physiologically aging wild-type 4F mice (7 months from 15 mo; 10 months from 12 mo) lowered kidney and skin methylation-clock age, shifted inflammation/senescence/stress transcripts, and produced no obvious teratomas; a 1-month pulse at 25 months did not reverse those clocks (Browder et al., Nature Aging, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00183-2, PMID 37118377).

📚 Yücel and Gladyshev state that even one fully reprogrammed cell is already too many for teratoma risk, that continuous Yamanaka-factor expression produces teratomas, that iPSC reprogramming elevates coding point-mutation rate, and that >2 days continuous OSKM can be lethal in mice (Yücel and Gladyshev, Nature Communications, 2024).

## Human data

📚 DNAm PhenoAge associates with all-cause mortality at HR=1.045 per year after chronological-age adjustment (Levine et al., Aging, 2018; validation WHI/FHS/NAS/JHS; meta p=9.9×10⁻⁴⁷).

📚 AgeAccelGrim HR=1.10 per year for all-cause death (meta P=2.0×10⁻⁷⁵) and remains associated after blood-cell adjustment; CHD HR=1.07 (Lu et al., Aging, 2019; FHS train 1,731 / test 625; validation 7,375 arrays from 6,935 people, mean follow-up 13.7 y; Horvath/Lu later Altos affiliation on v2).

📚 GrimAge2 outperforms v1 for mortality across 13,399 blood samples in nine cohorts (AgeAccelGrim2 meta P=3.6×10⁻¹⁶⁷ vs AgeAccelGrim P=2.6×10⁻¹⁴⁴) (Lu et al., Aging, 2022).

📚 DunedinPACE predicts later morbidity and death and adds information beyond GrimAge in Framingham: NAS n=771 men mean 77 y, mortality HR=1.26 [1.14–1.40] per SD; FHS Offspring n=2,471 mean 66 y, mortality HR=1.65 [1.51–1.79], CVD HR=1.39 [1.26–1.54]; in-sample r=0.78 with 20-year Pace of Aging; ICC=0.96 on 36 technical replicates (Belsky et al., eLife, 2022; Belsky is an inventor of DunedinPACE, licensed to TruDiagnostic).

📚 In Generation Scotland, 14 clocks vs 174 incident diseases over 10 years: GrimAge v2 all-cause mortality HR=1.54 per SD [1.46–1.62], P=7.1×10⁻⁶²; 176 Bonferroni-significant clock–disease pairs after lifestyle/SES adjustment (Mavrommatis et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-66106-y, PMID 40791724; N=18,859; n_cases mortality=842; Belsky inventor / TruDiagnostic license disclosed).

📚 No aging biomarker of any FDA-BEST category has been approved by U.S. regulators for clinical applications, and a reduction in epigenetic age is only meaningful if it is shown to predict a specific clinical benefit — a bar no clock has cleared (Moqri, Herzog, Poganik, Biomarkers of Aging Consortium, Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2023.08.003, PMID 37657418).

📚 DNA methylation clocks are highly accurate chronological-age correlates, but Bell et al. state that how far clock residuals capture functional deterioration versus prediction error is unsettled, that the Hannum clock is partly driven by age-related blood-cell composition shifts, and that Horvath/Hannum CpGs are not clearly enriched for function over promoter background (Bell et al., Genome Biology, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1824-y, PMID 31767039).

📚 As chronological-age prediction is improved by training-set size, the association of age-acceleration residual with death attenuates to null: best Elastic-Net predictor AAR HR 1.08 (95% CI 0.91–1.27) in LBC1921 and HR 1.00 (0.79–1.28) in LBC1936 (Zhang et al., Genome Medicine, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-019-0667-1).

📚 In CALERIE (n=220 randomized to prescribed 25% CR vs ad libitum for 2 years; analyzed CR n=128, AL n=69; achieved CR mean 11.9%), PC PhenoAge and PC GrimAge did not differ by arm (PhenoAge 24-month d=0.05, 95% CI −0.11 to 0.20, P>0.50; GrimAge 24-month d=0.05, −0.07 to 0.17, P>0.40), while DunedinPACE fell 2–3% (12-month d=−0.29; 24-month d=−0.25); the analysis was post hoc; follow-up stopped at the intervention (Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00357-y, PMID 37118425).

📚 In DO-HEALTH’s post-hoc clock analysis (n=777 of the parent trial, 3 years), vitamin D 2,000 IU/day and the home exercise program were null on PhenoAge, GrimAge, GrimAge2, and DunedinPACE; omega-3 1 g/day moved three of four next-generation clocks by standardized d=0.16–0.32 (≈2.9–3.8 months over 3 years); GrimAge v1 and all first-generation clocks did not move (Bischoff-Ferrari et al., Nature Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00793-y, PMID 39900648).

📚 TranslAGE tested responsiveness of 16 clocks across 51 human intervention studies (3,128 samples), not surrogacy: the authors write it is still unclear whether short-term biomarker changes correspond to long-term disease, healthspan, or lifespan improvement (Sehgal et al., Nature Medicine, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-026-04562-9).

📚 TRIIM reported a mean ~2.5-year drop in epigenetic-minus-chronological age after 12 months of rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men aged 51–65 with no placebo arm (10 enrolled, one removed); clocks were four estimators including GrimAge; side effects included arthralgias (2), carpal tunnel (1), fluid retention (1), mild gynecomastia (1); corresponding author Fahy is at Intervene Immune, which markets the protocol (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31496122/, PMID 31496122, PMC6826138).

📚 TRIIM-X (NCT04375657) is an ongoing Intervene Immune Phase 2 (estimated n=85), last checked without posted results, with a required results-reporting date of 2026-12-31 (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04375657, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT04375657).

☠︎︎ In RCTs of GH as an anti-aging agent in community-dwelling adults (mean age 69; 220 GH-exposed completers, 107 person-years, mean 27 weeks), GH significantly increased soft-tissue edema, arthralgias, carpal tunnel syndrome, and gynecomastia versus no GH (Liu et al., Annals of Internal Medicine, 2007, https://doi.org/10.7326/0003-4819-146-2-200701160-00005, PMID 17227934; 18 unique RCT populations).

⛔ NCT07290244 is a Phase 1 open-label, non-randomized, single-eye intravitreal AAV-OSK study (estimated n=18 OAG/NAION, primary completion May 2027, no results posted) whose primary endpoint is safety/tolerability, not systemic epigenetic age or healthspan; first-patient-dosed press (Life Biosciences, 9 Jun 2026) is not a reset (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07290244, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07290244; Life Biosciences, https://www.lifebiosciences.com/life-biosciences-announces-first-patient-dosed-in-phase-1-trial-of-er-100-for-optic-neuropathies/).

📚 Azacitidine and decitabine, the DNMT inhibitors sometimes laundered as “epigenetic rejuvenation,” cause myelosuppression (neutropenia, thrombocytopenia) as their dominant toxicity in MDS/AML — oncology cytopenia, not a preventive chromatin-reset regimen (Derissen, Beijnen, Schellens, The Oncologist, 2013, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3662854/).

⛔ Oncology responses to HDAC/DNMT inhibitors are disease-specific cytotoxicity/hypomethylation in myeloid neoplasia, not evidence that those drugs reverse ordinary human aging (Derissen et al., The Oncologist, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

☠︎︎ FDA and CDC documented serious adverse events in Nebraska patients treated with unapproved products marketed as containing exosomes; there are no FDA-approved exosome products (FDA CBER, Public Safety Notification on Exosome Products, 2019-12-06, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/public-safety-notification-exosome-products).

📚 FDA CBER (19 Feb 2019) states there is no proven clinical benefit of young-donor plasma infusions for aging or the listed neurologic/cardiac indications, and lists infectious, allergic, TRALI, and circulatory-overload risks (FDA CBER, 2019, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/important-information-about-young-donor-plasma-infusions-profit).

🐉 No compiled randomized trial in intact humans moved death, frailty, or hard disease as a primary endpoint after an intervention chosen to reverse epigenetic age; that absence is not a published disproof of a future effect (flight search result; inference from PubMed / ClinicalTrials.gov sweep this round).

## Measurement

📚 Illumina 27K → 450K → EPIC (~850k selected CpGs) is what most published clocks and consumer kits actually run; WGBS is base-resolution and expensive; bisulfite does not distinguish 5mC from 5hmC (Horvath, Genome Biology, 2013; Hannum et al., Molecular Cell, 2013; Bell et al., Genome Biology, 2019; NOVOS Age shop advertises EPIC).

📚 Bell et al. recommend naming the specific clock, separating intrinsic vs cell-composition (extrinsic) signal, and not treating a clock as a mechanistic map or individual-level clinical decision tool (Bell et al., Genome Biology, 2019).

📚 Technical noise produces deviations up to 9 years between technical replicates for six prominent clocks (Horvath1 median 1.8 y / max 4.8 y; PhenoAge median 2.4 y / max 8.6 y; other-clock maxima 4.5–8.6 y) in 36 blood sample-pairs aged 37.3–74.6; principal-component retraining brought most replicates within 1.5 years (Higgins-Chen et al., Nature Aging, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00248-2, PMID 36277076).

📚 The Horvath clock systematically underestimates age after ~60, most strongly in cerebellum, so “age acceleration” is itself age-dependent (El Khoury et al., Genome Biology, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-019-1810-4).

📚 In a 112-year-old woman sampled across 30 tissues, cerebellum showed the lowest (negative) age-acceleration versus bone, marrow, and blood, which ran older — a blood or saliva number is not brain or liver age (Horvath et al., Aging, 2015, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.100742, PMC4468311).

📚 Systems Age from a single blood draw splits into 11 physiological-system scores with distinct disease associations, so one commercial “biological age” is a collapse of heterogeneous subsystems (Sehgal et al., Nature Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-025-00958-3).

⛔ A commercial clock number is not “your real age”: it is an algorithm output from selected CpGs trained on calendar age, mortality proxies, or longitudinal physiology, contaminated by smoking, immune mix, and technical noise, and it is not a qualified treatment surrogate (Bell 2019; Lu 2019; Zhang 2017; Zhang 2019; Zhang 2024; Higgins-Chen 2022; Moqri 2023; Sehgal 2026).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient, tissue-restricted OSK (or a smaller downstream effector set) that restores youthful expression without a fully reprogrammed cell — the Lu 2020 retina window, not Abad/Ohnishi whole-body OSKM — is a mechanistic hope, not a human healthspan result (inference from Lu 2020 + Ocampo 2016 + Yücel 2024; no compiled human efficacy).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body epigenetic-integrity score — cell-type-resolved methylation, chromatin, and repeat silencing sampled from more than blood, plus a qualified surrogate link to events — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation and trial-design project, not a TruAge subscription (inference from Moqri 2023 + Bell 2019 + Higgins-Chen 2022 + Horvath 2015 tissue mismatch).

🐉 ITOA’s “backup copy / passive observers” story, if the ICE confounders were removed, would still need a demonstration that restoring those observers in intact aged humans moves function, not just a clock (inference from Yang 2023 vs Timmons/Brenner 2023; no source for human software-reinstall).

🐉 Combining senescent-cell clearance with a later partial-reprogramming pulse is a stacking fantasy that inherits both identity/cancer risk and the surrogate gap; it is not a protocol with a human n (inference from Ocampo 2016 + Yücel 2024 + Moqri 2023).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Ordinary human tissues accumulate age-associated DNA methylation and chromatin change that can be scored; later clocks (PhenoAge, GrimAge/v2, DunedinPACE) predict death and incident disease in large cohorts; cyclic OSKM / OSK can restore selected function and extend progeria-mouse lifespan, and TET enzymes are required for the retina result (Horvath 2013; Hannum 2013; Levine 2018; Lu 2019/2022; Belsky 2022; Mavrommatis 2025; Ocampo 2016; Lu 2020; Browder 2022).

🥼 Whether loss of epigenetic information is the causal driver of ordinary mammalian aging is a live paper fight (Yang ICE / ITOA vs Timmons/Brenner cytotoxicity and missing functional OSK data). Whether clock CpGs are causal is independently weak: EWMR finds no enrichment of standard clocks for putatively causal sites (Ying 2024).

⛔ A commercial clock decrement is not age reversal, not a qualified surrogate, and not organismal youth. CALERIE and DO-HEALTH moved some clocks and nullified others in the same papers. TRIIM is n=9, no placebo. ER-100 is one-eye Phase 1 safety with no results. Full OSKM teratomas and failed-reprogramming cancers are mouse four-factor results, not a license that scheduled partial OSK is solved (Moqri 2023; Waziry 2023; Bischoff-Ferrari 2025; Fahy 2019; NCT07290244; Abad 2013; Ohnishi 2014; Ocampo 2016).

🤔 People are buying TruAge, Index, TallyAge, NOVOS Age, GlycanAge, TRIIM-shaped GH stacks, and clock-bundled rapamycin/metformin under that slogan anyway (TruDiagnostic; Elysium; Tally; NOVOS; GlycanAge; Intervene Immune; AgelessRx; Rapamycin News).
