# The Hallmarks of Aging: Stem-Cell Exhaustion

**Hallmark:** Stem-cell exhaustion  
**Evidence cutoff:** September 2, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 2, 2026

This repository is not medical advice. It is a sourced information dump for people who already experiment on themselves.

The slogan in the wild is a refill-the-tank fad: you are running out of stem cells; an IV of cord / Wharton’s jelly / adipose SVF / “exosomes,” a STEMREGEN-class botanical mobilizer, or a young-plasma drip will replenish the pool and reverse aging. Nearby true facts — HSC function and clonality change with age, satellite-cell content falls in the oldest-old, HSCT and omidubicel work after conditioning, mouse parabiosis moves regenerative readouts, MSCs secrete factors in culture — get laundered into that product.

## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that adult stem-cell pools lose useful regenerative capacity with ordinary aging, that this decline causally drives human aging, that it can be scored, and that it can be restored so people live longer or healthier. Numerical depletion is allowed but not required.

🤔 Longevity clinics, supplement brands, and cash-pay labs sell that claim as a product: R3 ReBella WJ/XO/CB IVs, Blue Horizon cord and WJ-MSC exosomes, Dynamic Stem Cell “Anti-Aging Stem Cell Therapy,” StemGenex-class SVF, Celltex Cancun autologous MSC, Panama Stem Cell Institute “frailty of aging,” STEMREGEN Release–Mobilize–Signal, Qualia Stem Cell 4-day pulse, Ambrosia/Ivy young plasma, and Altos/NewLimit partial-reprogramming pages (FDA CBER warning letters 11 Feb / 26 May / 14 Aug 2026; FDA StemGenex 2018; Celltex site; cellmedicine.com; STEMREGEN; Qualia; Popular Mechanics 2024 Ambrosia history; Altos science page).

📚 López-Otín et al. listed stem-cell exhaustion among nine tentative hallmarks in 2013 and kept it among twelve in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that ordinary aging is an empty stem-cell tank, or as a license to sell a hallmark-named IV or capsule, is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Qualia explainer, https://www.qualialife.com/what-is-stem-cell-exhaustion; Qualia sells the adjacent SKU).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Pang-class HSC myeloid bias, Mitchell late-life oligoclonality, Jaiswal CHIP HRs, Karlsen type-II satellite-cell loss, Conboy parabiosis, Horwitz omidubicel neutrophil recovery, ISCT MSC culture criteria, Jensen AFA CD34 bumps — into “buy a refill” (Pang 2011; Mitchell 2022; Jaiswal 2014; Karlsen 2019; Conboy 2005; Horwitz 2021; Dominici 2006; Jensen 2007).

## What the words mean

💯 Tissue-resident adult stem cells are defined by long-term self-renewal plus production of the right differentiated progeny — HSC, muscle satellite cell, intestinal Lgr5, hair-follicle stem cell, regional neural stem/progenitor — not by whatever is printed on a vial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Dominici et al., Cytotherapy, 2006).

📚 In the papers, “exhaustion” is loss of useful output: failed activation, failed return to quiescence, lineage bias, clonal restriction, niche failure, or numerical depletion. Those are different operations (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Pang et al., PNAS, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1116110108, PMID 22123971; Mitchell et al., Nature, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04786-y, PMID 35650442).

⛔ Clinic English uses “stem cell” for umbilical-cord blood, Wharton’s jelly, adipose SVF, and “exosomes” as a universal tissue-building refill (FDA R3 letter 14 Aug 2026, https://www.fda.gov/inspections-compliance-enforcement-and-criminal-investigations/warning-letters/r3-medical-companies-726330-08142026; FDA Blue Horizon 26 May 2026; FDA Dynamic 11 Feb 2026).

📚 ISCT’s 2006 MSC identity criteria are plastic adherence, ≥95% CD105/CD73/CD90, ≤2% hematopoietic/endothelial markers, and in-vitro trilineage differentiation — research identity, not a release spec and not an HSC or satellite-cell replacement (Dominici et al., Cytotherapy, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1080/14653240600855905, PMID 16923606).

📚 ISCT’s 2019 statement keeps the acronym MSC, requires a tissue-source prefix, and reserves “mesenchymal stem cell” for in-vitro *and* in-vivo stemness; otherwise the population is a mesenchymal *stromal* cell with secretory/immunomodulatory properties (Viswanathan et al., Cytotherapy, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcyt.2019.08.002, PMID 31554591).

📚 Heterochronic parabiosis surgically joins two animals so they share circulation *and* organs, immunity, and behavior (Conboy et al., Nature, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03260, PMID 15716955).

📚 Heterochronic blood exchange transfers a defined blood volume through a circuit without joining organs (Rebo et al., Nature Communications, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13363, PMID 27874859).

📚 Neutral blood exchange / plasma dilution replaces ~50% of mouse platelet-rich plasma with saline + 5% albumin and returns the animal’s own cells — an operation that *removes* old plasma factors rather than adding young plasma (Mehdipour et al., Aging, 2020, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.103418, PMID 32474458, PMC7288919).

⛔ Collapsing parabiosis, blood exchange, dilution, Alkahest fractions, and Ambrosia’s $8k–$12k young-donor drip into “young blood reverses aging” is a vocabulary collision, not a shared endpoint (Conboy 2005; Rebo 2016; Mehdipour 2020; FDA CBER 19 Feb 2019, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/important-information-about-young-donor-plasma-infusions-profit).

📚 Omidubicel / HSCT is graft engineering after myeloablative or reduced-intensity conditioning, scored as neutrophil recovery and infection, not organismal rejuvenation (Horwitz et al., Blood, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2021011719, PMID 34157093, PMC8442498).

📚 Circulating CD34+ is a blood-count of a marker-defined subset; it is not serial-transplant HSC function, not a PAX7 muscle pool, and not a healthspan trial (Jensen et al., Cardiovasc Revasc Med, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carrev.2007.03.004, PMID 17765649).

📚 CHIP as used in hematology is a leukemia-driver mutation at VAF ≥2% without a WHO myeloid neoplasm — clone size in blood, not a whole-body stem-cell inventory (Steensma et al., Blood, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2015-03-631747, PMID 25931582; already filed under hallmarks/01).

## Mechanism

📚 Aged human immunophenotypic HSCs (Lin−CD34+CD38−CD90+CD45RA−) are more frequent and less quiescent than young HSCs, myeloid-biased in stroma coculture, and give roughly half the NSG chimerism with a ~14-fold higher myeloid:lymphoid ratio — functional exhaustion with phenotypic expansion (Pang et al., PNAS, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1116110108, PMID 22123971, PMC3250189; N=13 young 20–35 y vs 11 elderly 65–85 y marrow; culture n=8/6; xeno 10/9 donors; HSC % of CD34+ up P<10−7; G0 down P<0.013; 16-week NSG).

⛔ The clinic slogan that ordinary aging is “running out of stem cells” is already contradicted by that human HSC count: aged marrow has *more* marker-positive HSCs, not fewer (Pang et al., PNAS, 2011).

📚 Late-life human hematopoiesis stays massively polyclonal until about age 65 (estimated 20,000–200,000 contributing HSC/MPPs) then collapses so that 12–18 clones account for 30–60% of blood; HSC/MPPs accrue ~17 mutations/year and lose ~30 bp telomere/year; only 22% of expanding clones carried known drivers (Mitchell et al., Nature, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04786-y, PMID 35650442; N=3,579 colony genomes, 10 hematologically normal people aged 0–81).

📚 Simulations in that paper reproduce oligoclonal takeover with a *constant* HSC/MPP population size plus lifelong driver mutations of modest fitness — restoring “count” is the wrong operation for that failure mode (Mitchell et al., Nature, 2022).

📚 Human clonal hematopoiesis is competitive ecology with mutation-specific growth: 92.4% of 697 clones in 385 people ≥55 grew at a stable exponential rate over median 13 years, from ~5%/yr (DNMT3A, TP53) to >50%/yr (SRSF2 P95H) (Fabre et al., Nature, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04785-z, PMID 35650444).

📚 In 17,182 unselected people, detectable hematologic-cancer-gene mutations in blood were rare before 40 and rose to 9.5% at 70–79, 11.7% at 80–89, and 18.4% at 90–108, with HR 11.1 for hematologic cancer, 1.4 for death, 2.0 for CHD, and 2.6 for ischemic stroke — HSC clonal restriction tracks organismal disease, not a missing-cell count (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408617, PMID 25426837; already filed under hallmarks/01).

📚 Across 165 human vastus biopsies from birth to 86 y, type-II fibre satellite-cell content falls with adult age (r=−0.57) in lockstep with type-II atrophy (r=−0.56) (Verdijk et al., Age, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-013-9583-2; children n=13, 18–49 n=50, 50–69 n=53, 70–86 n=49; fibre-type PAX7 IHC).

📚 Aged mouse ISCs are not simply fewer; they regenerate worse after irradiation and form poorer organoids because canonical Wnt signaling falls in the stem cell and the niche; Wnt3a restored aged murine and human ISC organoid function (Nalapareddy et al., Cell Reports, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2017.02.056, PMID 28297666, PMC5987258; young 2–3 mo vs aged 20–22 mo mice plus donated human organoids).

📚 In >20-month-old mice, hair-follicle stem cells escape the bulge into dermis; ~14.5% of HF-SC compartments are miniaturized, and Foxc1/Nfatc1 deletion phenocopies escape and produces baldness by 12–16 months (Zhang et al., Nature Aging, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-021-00103-w; live-imaged Krt14-H2BGFP mice; compartment size P=4.86×10−20).

📚 Raising Cdc42-GTP ages young mouse HSCs; a ~16 h 5 μM CASIN pulse drops aged Cdc42-GTP to young levels, restores polarity and H4K16ac, and rejuvenates serial-transplant function (Florian et al., Cell Stem Cell, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2012.04.007, PMID 22560076, PMC3348626).

📚 After IV infusion the majority of MSCs are trapped in the lung first pass, with a “therapeutically questionable” number reaching arterial blood (Fischer et al., Stem Cells Dev, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1089/scd.2008.0253, PMID 19099374, PMC2777695; rat MSC vs MAPC/NSC/BMMC).

📚 Viable MSCs do not pass the mouse lung and are gone by 24 h; none were reculturable from blood, liver, spleen, kidney, or marrow at any time, including after liver ischemia-reperfusion (Eggenhofer et al., Frontiers in Immunology, 2012, https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2012.00297, PMID 23056000; 5×10⁵ DsRed/⁵¹Cr MSCs IV).

## Animal data

📚 Aged mouse HSCs are five times as frequent in marrow but only one-quarter as efficient at homing and engrafting irradiated recipients, and they cycle more often — the same more-cells/worse-function pattern as Pang’s human marrow (Morrison et al., Nature Medicine, 1996, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm0996-1011).

📚 Heterochronic parabiosis for 5 weeks restored aged satellite-cell Delta/Notch activation and muscle regeneration (circulating GFP+ myotubes <0.1%) and raised aged hepatocyte proliferation (Conboy et al., Nature, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature03260, PMID 15716955; N≥3 pairs/condition; regeneration index up P<0.005; young 2–3 mo / old 19–26 mo).

📚 A single old↔young blood exchange without joining organs rapidly inhibited multiple tissues via old blood; young blood helped old muscle regeneration in that circuit; the authors noted dilution of old plasma as an unseparated alternative (Rebo et al., Nature Communications, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms13363, PMID 27874859).

📚 A single neutral blood exchange (50% plasma replaced with saline + 5% albumin, no young donor) met or exceeded the muscle-repair, liver, and hippocampal-neurogenesis readouts attributed to heterochronic blood sharing in old mice — young plasma is not the necessary operation even in the animal system clinics cite (Mehdipour, Conboy et al., Aging, 2020, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.103418, PMID 32474458, PMC7288919).

📚 Four daily systemic CASIN doses restore aged MuSC injury repair and HSC serial reconstitution; transplanting those HSCs into aged NBSGW mice extended median lifespan +24.8% and maximum +34.0% versus aged-HSC recipients (Montserrat-Vazquez et al., npj Regenerative Medicine, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41536-022-00275-y; fitness 20/17/15 young/aged/CASIN; lifespan aged n=10, CASIN n=16, untransplanted n=19; ≥80-week C57BL/6 donors).

📚 CASIN-treated old HSCs keep young-like function in young recipients but an aged or osteopontin-low niche restrains that restore — cell-intrinsic HSC function is reversible in a transplant assay and the aged microenvironment can take it back (Guidi et al., Stem Cells, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1002/stem.3372, PMID 33847429; 200 old HSCs ±16 h 5 μM CASIN into young 8–10 wk vs aged 19–24 mo vs young OPN-KO; follow-up to ~23 weeks).

📚 Oral ABT-263 / navitoclax cleared senescent HSCs and muscle stem cells in 21–22-month-old mice and improved HSC clonogenicity/multilineage engraftment and MuSC myogenic colony formation (Chang et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4010, PMID 26657143; already written in hallmarks/08).

📚 Cyclic OSKM in 12-month wild-type 4F mice improved cardiotoxin muscle injury repair (n=4/group) after a 3-week pulse (Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.11.052, PMID 27984723; already filed under hallmarks/03).

📚 Pulsed OSKM in old mice restored SVZ neuroblast proportions and, when targeted to the niche, also aNSC–NPC share, with a cell-proportion clock shift of −10.9 months and more new neurons in vitro and in old brains (Xu et al., Nature Aging, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00594-3; iOSKM young 3–4 mo vs old 20–26 mo).

📚 In-vivo OSKM produced multi-organ teratomas and circulating iPS cells (Abad et al., Nature, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12586, PMID 24025773; already filed under hallmarks/03).

📚 Premature termination of in-vivo OSKM produced kidney and pancreas tumors with altered epigenetic regulation, not just teratomas after full reprogramming (Ohnishi et al., Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2014.01.005, PMID 24486105; already filed under hallmarks/03).

📚 Continuous OSKM killed 4F mice in 4 days; the same cyclic protocol that is sold as “partial reprogramming” produced liver/kidney/pancreas teratomas in two-copy mice at 8 weeks (Ocampo et al., Cell, 2016; already filed under hallmarks/03).

📚 Partial-reprogramming translation remains identity- and cancer-limited: one fully reprogrammed cell is already too many for teratoma risk (Yücel and Gladyshev, Nature Communications, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46020-5; already filed under hallmarks/03).

## Human data

📚 In 83–94-year-old men, type-II satellite-cell content was 52% lower and type-II fibres 38% smaller than in 19–27-year-old men; 12 weeks of heavy resistance training did not expand the satellite-cell pool, add myonuclei, or hypertrophy type II fibres (Karlsen et al., Acta Physiologica, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/apha.13271, PMID 30828982; N=29 old randomized ±12 weeks RT, 9 young).

📚 After 2 weeks of immobilization, men aged 61–74 failed to raise PAX7+ satellite cells or recover myofibre area during 4 weeks of retraining, while men aged 21–30 did both (Suetta et al., Journal of Physiology, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2013.257121, PMID 23732643, PMC3768552; N=9 old / 11 young).

🥼 Adult human hippocampal neurogenesis is a live paper fight, so “brain stem cells are running out and can be scored/refilled” does not have a settled assay: Sorrells et al. found proliferating progenitors and young neurons drop in the first year of life and were undetectable in adult dentate gyrus (18–77 y; n=17 postmortem + 12 epilepsy; Nature, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature25975, PMID 29513649), while Boldrini et al. reported thousands of DCX+ immature neurons stable from 14–79 y (n=28 healthy autopsy; Cell Stem Cell, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2018.03.015, PMID 29625071) and Moreno-Jiménez et al. reported thousands of DCX+ DG neurons in 13 neurologically healthy subjects 43–87 y and blamed over-fixation for Sorrells-negative IHC (Nature Medicine, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0375-9, PMID 30911133).

📚 FDA CBER stated there is no proven clinical benefit of young-donor plasma infusions for aging or listed neurologic/cardiac indications; plasma carries infectious, allergic, TRALI, and circulatory-overload risks (FDA CBER, 2019, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/important-information-about-young-donor-plasma-infusions-profit; already filed under hallmarks/01 and 03).

📚 The first human young-plasma AD trial (PLASMA) was a safety study and did not move the cognitive endpoint: ADAS-Cog 13 change −1.04 (95% CI −3.49 to 1.41; P=.27) (Sha et al., JAMA Neurology, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2018.3288, PMID 30383097, PMC6436930; N=18 mild–moderate AD, mean age 74.2; 4 weekly ~250 mL young FFP from males 18–30 vs saline; Alkahest collaborated).

📚 Alkahest/Grifols GRF6019 in severe AD was not powered for efficacy and showed no cognitive/functional difference versus placebo (Hannestad et al., Journal of Alzheimer’s Disease, 2021, https://doi.org/10.3233/jad-210011, PMID 33612547; N=18 GRF6019 vs 8 placebo; 250 mL IV daily × 5 days; Alkahest/Grifols sell the fraction).

📚 The companion GRF6019 mild-to-moderate AD Phase 2 had no placebo arm, so “no decline over 24 weeks” is an uncontrolled observation (Hannestad et al., Alzheimer’s & Dementia (N Y), 2020, https://doi.org/10.1002/trc2.12115, PMID 33354618, PMC7744029; N=47, 100 vs 250 mL × 5 days at weeks 1 and 13).

📚 AFA extract (StemEnhance, 1 g) produced an 18% rise in circulating CD34+ at 1 hour in 12 healthy adults (25% after dropping 3 “noncompliant”); counts were back near baseline by 2–4 h — a same-morning blood-count, not tissue restoration (Jensen et al., Cardiovasc Revasc Med, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carrev.2007.03.004, PMID 17765649; double-blind crossover vs green-dyed potato-flake placebo).

⛔ STEMREGEN (Biomics LLC; Christian Drapeau CSO) sells “Release 10 million more of your own stem cells” / “80%…10 million more with 2 capsules” as restored repair capacity; the human datum under that slogan is a transient circulating CD34+ bump (STEMREGEN, https://www.stemregen.co/; Jensen et al., 2007; STEMREGEN sells the capsule they cite).

📚 Omidubicel shortened neutrophil recovery after myeloablative conditioning and cord-blood transplant (median 12 vs 22 days) in hematologic malignancy — graft engineering after ablation, not restoration of ordinary aged stem-cell systems in intact people (Horwitz et al., Blood, 2021, PMID 34157093; N=125, age 13–65, NCT02730299; FDA approved Omisirge 17 Apr 2023 for that indication; Gamida/Ayrmid SAA expansion 5 Dec 2025, https://www.gamida-cell.com/press_release/fda-approves-omisirge-as-first-approved-cell-therapy-to-treat-severe-aplastic-anemia/).

📚 Plerixafor is labeled only to mobilize HSCs into blood for collection and subsequent autologous transplant in NHL/MM, in combination with filgrastim — apheresis logistics, not rejuvenation of an aged pool (Mozobil USPI, DailyMed, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=0ed08d2b-5051-46b2-aa37-1d6275bf9003).

📚 Cochrane’s living review of intra-articular stem-cell injections for knee OA found only low-certainty, possibly unnoticed pain/function shifts versus placebo and remained uncertain on structural regeneration, quality of life, treatment success, and safety — not a multi-tissue stem restore (Whittle et al., Cochrane Database Syst Rev CD013342, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD013342.pub2, PMID 40169165; 25 RCTs / 1,341 participants; placebo pain −1.2 on 0–10 from 4.5, I²=80%, 7 studies, 445 people).

📚 The closest human “anti-aging stem cell” RCT (Longeveron CRATUS) measured 6-minute walk and TNF, not HSC/MuSC/NSC function; the 200-million cell dose was null on 6MWT (p=.263) and SPPB (p=.812) while 100 million moved 6MWT within-group (345.9→410.7 m, p=.011) — dose-incoherent, n=30, industry signal, primary was 1-month TE-SAE safety (Tompkins, Hare et al., Journals of Gerontology A, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glx137, PMID 28977399, PMC5861900, NCT02065245; Longeveron sells the cells).

📚 Authors do not recommend CHIP-mutation screening of unselected individuals; there is no evidence base for managing the associated complications (Libby, Sidlow et al., JACC, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2019.06.007, PMID 31345432; already filed under hallmarks/01).

📜 Six months of metformin titrated to 1,500 mg/day did not significantly change CH VAF versus placebo for any gene, including DNMT3A R882 (Blood 2025 abstract, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2025-4954; N=332 non-diabetic female breast-cancer survivors; already filed under hallmarks/01).

☠︎︎ Three patients paid ~$5,000 at US Stem Cell Clinic (Sunrise, FL; NCT02024269 listing) for bilateral intravitreal autologous adipose “stem cells” and went from 20/30–20/200 to 20/200–no light perception at 1 year (hemorrhagic retinopathy, traction/rhegmatogenous detachments, zonular failure) (Kuriyan et al., NEJM, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1609583, PMID 28296617; N=3 consecutive clinic cases).

☠︎︎ FDA and CDC documented serious adverse events in Nebraska patients treated with unapproved products marketed as containing exosomes; there are no FDA-approved exosome products (FDA CBER, 2019-12-06, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/public-safety-notification-exosome-products; already filed under hallmarks/03).

☠︎︎ Intrathecal “MSC / embryonic / fetal neural stem cell” infusions at commercial clinics in China, Argentina, and Mexico produced a donor-derived glioproliferative thoracic-cord mass (STR fingerprinting: predominantly non-host cells) with progressive paraplegia and incontinence in a 66-year-old stroke patient not on immunosuppressants (Berkowitz et al., NEJM, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMc1600188, PMID 27331440; N=1 established tumor from exogenous cells).

📚 FDA’s patient page states the only FDA-approved stem-cell products in the US are cord-blood hematopoietic progenitor cells for blood-production disorders and records reports of blindness, tumor formation, and infections from marketed regenerative products (FDA CBER, “Important Patient and Consumer Information About Regenerative Medicine Therapies,” https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/consumers-biologics/important-patient-and-consumer-information-about-regenerative-medicine-therapies).

🐉 No completed human RCT has restored multi-tissue adult stem-cell function (HSC serial-transplant quality + MuSC pool + a settled NSC assay) with a healthspan or mortality endpoint; Longeveron frailty, Alkahest plasma fractions, AFA CD34 bumps, and post-ablation grafts are different operationalizations (flight search: NCT02065245, NCT03169231, NCT03520998, NCT03765762, NCT02730299; Cochrane CD013342; absence is not a published disproof of the hallmark).

## Measurement

📚 Pang-class flow (Lin−CD34+CD38−CD90+CD45RA−) is immunophenotype, not a serial-transplant HSC and not a consumer “stem-cell age” (Pang et al., PNAS, 2011).

📚 Colony WGS phylogeny scores contributing clone number and mutation/telomere rates; it is retrospective, tissue-limited, and not a treat-to-target kit (Mitchell et al., Nature, 2022).

📚 CHIP VAF is mutant-clone ecology in leukocytes (Steensma et al., Blood, 2015; Fabre et al., Nature, 2022; Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014).

📚 Muscle satellite-cell content is PAX7+ / DAPI+ on laminin-outlined fibres in a vastus biopsy — a local count, not whole-body reserve (Karlsen et al., Acta Physiologica, 2019; Verdijk et al., Age, 2014).

📚 Adult hippocampal neurogenesis, when claimed, is DCX/PSA-NCAM IHC on postmortem dentate gyrus; fixation, postmortem interval, and marker choice are the fight, not a clinic score (Sorrells 2018; Boldrini 2018; Moreno-Jiménez 2019).

📚 Circulating CD34+ after AFA / STEMREGEN-class botanicals or G-CSF/plerixafor is a hours-to-days blood-count (Jensen et al., 2007; Mozobil USPI).

🐉 No compiled source validates a whole-body consumer stem-cell-reserve score with treat-to-target outcome data (flight search; inference from Pang/Mitchell/Karlsen/ISCT limits).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient, tissue-restricted partial reprogramming that restores HSC/MuSC/NSC function without a fully reprogrammed cell is a mechanistic hope; the human objects on the table are company pages, not a stem-function n (inference from Ocampo 2016 + Xu 2024 + Yücel 2024 + Altos/NewLimit pages; already filed 🐉 under hallmarks/03 for the identity-reset version).

🐉 A human CASIN polarity-restoration exposure that restores aged HSC output in an intact person, rather than in a transplant assay, is a leap from Florian/Montserrat-Vazquez/Guidi mice; Mogling is preclinical (inference from Florian 2012 + Guidi 2021 + Mogling page).

🐉 Neutral plasma dilution, not young-factor addition, as the operative mouse knob would make Ambrosia-class drips the wrong product even if the animal regenerative readouts hold (inference from Mehdipour 2020 + Rebo 2016 + FDA CBER 2019).

🐉 Conditioning-free or antibody-conditioned replacement of oligoclonal hematopoiesis with a verified polyclonal autologous graft is an engineering hope, not a completed human aging result (inference from Mitchell 2022 + Horwitz 2021; no compiled intact-person restore).

🐉 Combining senescent-niche clearance with a later partial-reprogramming pulse inherits identity/cancer risk and the surrogate gap; it is not a protocol with a human n (already filed 🐉 under hallmarks/03 and 08).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body stem-cell-reserve score — serial-transplant-quality HSC plus PAX7 plus a settled NSC assay plus pathogenic-clone map — does not exist as a validated product (inference from Pang/Mitchell/Karlsen/Sorrells-Boldrini limits).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Ordinary human HSCs become more frequent by marker count, myeloid-biased, and, after ~75 y, oligoclonal; type-II satellite-cell content falls with age and can fail to expand in the oldest-old; aged mouse ISCs and HFSCs lose useful output by Wnt drop and niche escape (Pang 2011; Mitchell 2022; Fabre 2022; Verdijk 2014; Karlsen 2019; Nalapareddy 2017; Zhang 2021).

🥼 Whether adult human hippocampal neurogenesis persists, and therefore whether “brain stem cells” are a refillable tank, is a live paper fight (Sorrells 2018 vs Boldrini 2018 / Moreno-Jiménez 2019).

📚 Mouse stem/progenitor function can move: parabiosis, plasma dilution, CASIN, Wnt3a organoids, ABT-263, pulsed OSKM (Conboy 2005; Mehdipour 2020; Montserrat-Vazquez 2022; Nalapareddy 2017; Chang 2016; Xu 2024). An aged niche can restrain a CASIN-treated HSC (Guidi 2021). Full or failed OSKM makes tumors (Abad 2013; Ohnishi 2014; Ocampo 2016).

🐉 No compiled human trial restored multi-tissue stem function and then extended healthspan (flight search result).

🤔 People are buying WJ/exosome/cord IVs, SVF shots, Cancun/Panama tourism, STEMREGEN and Qualia mobilizer stacks, young plasma, and CHIP panels under a refill slogan anyway (FDA 2026 letters; STEMREGEN; Qualia; Ambrosia history; Mito Health).
