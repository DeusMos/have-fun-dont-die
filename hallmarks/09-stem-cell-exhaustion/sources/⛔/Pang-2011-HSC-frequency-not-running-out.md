# Pang et al., 2011. Human bone marrow hematopoietic stem cells are increased in frequency and myeloid-biased with age. PNAS.
https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1116110108 PMID 22123971
Used for: The clinic slogan that ordinary aging is “running out of stem cells” is contradicted by the human HSC count: aged marrow has more immunophenotypic HSCs, not fewer.
