# Fabre et al., 2022. The longitudinal dynamics and natural history of clonal haematopoiesis. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04785-z
Used for: Human clonal hematopoiesis is competitive ecology with mutation-specific growth rates, not a tank running dry.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 697 clones from 385 people ≥55y, median 13 years follow-up; plus 1,731 colony WGSs from 7 older people. 92.4% of clones grew at a stable exponential rate. Growth ~5%/yr (DNMT3A, TP53) to >50%/yr (SRSF2 P95H). Same-mutation growth differed ±5%/yr.
Conflict if any: Academic (same Cambridge/Sanger cluster as Mitchell 2022).
