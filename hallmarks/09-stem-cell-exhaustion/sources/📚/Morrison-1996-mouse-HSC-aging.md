# Morrison et al., 1996. The aging of hematopoietic stem cells. Nature Medicine.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nm0996-1011 PMID 8782459
Used for: 📚 Aged mouse HSCs are five times as frequent in marrow but only one-quarter as efficient at homing/engraftment — functional self-renewal decline with phenotypic expansion.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Purified HSCs from young, middle-aged, and old mice; limit-dilution single reconstituting HSCs. Frequency ×5 in old BM; engraftment/homing efficiency ×0.25 vs young. Old HSCs frequently in S/G2/M; young/middle-aged rarely cycling. Single reconstituting HSCs from old vs young had indistinguishable progenitor output once engrafted. Endpoint=limit-dilution reconstitution and cell-cycle. Ordinary aging, not serial-transplant exhaustion.
