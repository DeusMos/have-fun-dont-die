# Mehdipour, Conboy et al., 2020. Rejuvenation of three germ layers tissues by exchanging old blood plasma with saline-albumin. Aging.
https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.103418 PMID 32474458
Used for: A single neutral blood exchange (50% plasma → saline + 5% albumin, no young donor) met or exceeded heterochronic muscle/liver/hippocampal readouts in old mice — young plasma is not the necessary operation.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Old (and young) mice; one NBE replacing ~50% platelet-rich plasma over ~30 min. Endpoints: muscle repair, liver adiposity/fibrosis, hippocampal neurogenesis. Human TPE serum compared proteomically; single TPE abrogated old-serum inhibition of progenitor proliferation in vitro.
Conflict if any: Academic (Conboy lab). Kiprov collaborates on clinical TPE.
