# Florian et al., 2012. Cdc42 activity regulates hematopoietic stem cell aging and rejuvenation. Cell Stem Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.stem.2012.04.007 PMID 22560076 PMC3348626
Used for: 📚 Elevated Cdc42-GTP is causal for mouse HSC aging (polarity loss, myeloid bias, poor serial reconstitition); 5 μM CASIN ex vivo for ~16 h restores polarity, H4K16ac, and transplant function of aged LT-HSCs.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Aged vs young C57BL/6 LT-HSCs (LSK CD34low/− Flk2−). Cdc42GAP KO young HSCs phenocopy aging. CASIN 5 μM reduces aged Cdc42-GTP to young levels without changing cell cycle or apoptosis. Polarized fraction of aged LT-HSCs rises dose-dependently toward young. Serial competitive transplantation: CASIN-treated aged LT-HSCs regain lymphoid output and self-renewal vs vehicle-aged. Endpoint=polarity IF, AcH4K16, serial PB chimerism. Ex vivo pulse, then in vivo transplant.
Conflict if any: Academic (Geiger / Zheng). CASIN later used by the same labs; Mogling-adjacent translation interest is subsequent.
