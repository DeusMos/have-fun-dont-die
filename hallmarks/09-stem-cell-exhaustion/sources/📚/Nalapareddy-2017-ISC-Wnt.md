# Nalapareddy et al., 2017. Canonical Wnt Signaling Ameliorates Aging of Intestinal Stem Cells. Cell Reports.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cellrep.2017.02.056 PMID 28297679 PMC5987258
Used for: 📚 Aged mouse (and human organoid) ISCs keep cell number but lose regenerative output because Wnt signaling falls; adding Wnt3a restores organoid formation — functional exhaustion that can be scored and reversed in vitro.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Young mice 2–3 mo vs aged 20–22 mo; typical histology/organoid arms n=3–4 mice/group. Absolute ISC number not reduced; regenerative response after ionizing radiation impaired; organoid growth and lobes/organoid after three passages lower in aged crypts. Recombinant Wnt3a restored aged murine organoid morphology/function and enhanced aged human ISC organoids from donated tissue. Endpoint=crypt architecture, organoid formation/complexity, Wnt gene expression in ISCs/Paneth/mesenchyme.
