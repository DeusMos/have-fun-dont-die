# Fischer et al., 2009. Pulmonary passage is a major obstacle for intravenous stem cell delivery: the pulmonary first-pass effect. Stem Cells and Development.

Used for: After IV infusion the majority of MSCs are trapped in the lung first pass, with a therapeutically questionable number reaching arterial blood.
