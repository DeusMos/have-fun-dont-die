# Zhang et al., 2021. Escape of hair follicle stem cells causes stem cell exhaustion during aging. Nature Aging.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-021-00103-w
Used for: 📚 Aged mouse HFSCs leave the bulge into dermis; ~14.5% of HF-SC compartments are miniaturized by ~20 mo, and Foxc1/Nfatc1 deletion recapitulates escape, miniaturization, and hair loss — numerical plus functional niche-exit exhaustion in a non-blood compartment.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Live two-photon imaging of Krt14-H2BGFP HFs; young ~6–8 mo vs old >20 mo. HF-SC compartment size reduced (P=4.86×10−20). ~14.5% of old HF-SC compartments miniaturized vs smallest young; ~5.8% of aged HFs had escaped epithelial cells (P=0.002); HFs with escape were smaller. Apoptotic caspase-3+ HF-SCs similar young vs old (~4–6%). Foxc1/Nfatc1 epithelial dKO: hair thinning from ~4 mo, complete baldness by 12–16 mo; live imaging captured bulge escape. Endpoint=compartment size, escape events, hair coat. Mouse ordinary aging plus genetic phenocopy.
