# Mitchell et al., 2022. Clonal dynamics of haematopoiesis across the human lifespan. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04786-y PMID 35650442
Used for: 📚 Human HSC/MPP clonal diversity collapses after ~75 y: a few expanded clones take 30–60% of blood production while census size stays large — measurable functional restriction, not running out of cells.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 3579 single-cell-derived HSC/MPP colony genomes from 10 haematologically normal humans aged 0–81 y. Mutation rate 17/y after birth; telomere loss 30 bp/y. Adults <65 y: polyclonal, estimated 20,000–200,000 contributing HSC/MPPs. Age >75: 12–18 independent clones each contribute 1–34% and together 30–60% of haematopoiesis. Most expansions started before age 40; only 22% carried known myeloid drivers. Simulations with constant HSC population size plus modest-fitness drivers explain the late collapse. Endpoint=WGS phylogenies / clonal diversity. Cross-sectional.
Conflict if any: Academic (Wellcome Sanger / Cambridge). Lee-Six and Fabre are coauthors on this paper.
