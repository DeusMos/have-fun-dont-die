# Xu et al., 2024. Restoration of neuronal progenitors by partial reprogramming in the aged neurogenic niche. Nature Aging.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-024-00594-3 PMID 38553564
Used for: 📚 Pulsed OSKM in old mice restores SVZ neuroblast (and, when niche-targeted, NSC/NPC) proportions and new-neuron production — partial reprogramming scored on neural stem/progenitor function, not a human refill.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: iOSKM mice; young 3–4 mo vs old 20–26 mo; whole-body pulsed OSKM plus SVZ-targeted Cre/AAV OSKM. Old age drops DCX+ neuroblast proportion/density; whole-body OSKM raises it back toward young (scRNA-seq two cohorts + IHC DCX and PSA-NCAM). Cell-proportion clock: whole-body −2.7 months predicted age (NS at mouse level); SVZ-targeted −10.9 months. Niche-targeted OSKM also raises aNSC–NPC share. Old NSC cultures: OSKM restores neuroblast fraction during differentiation and new-neuron output in vitro and in old brains. Buckley is a Retro Biosciences cofounder; other authors declare no COI.
