# Suetta et al., 2013. Ageing is associated with diminished muscle re-growth and myogenic precursor cell expansion early after immobility-induced atrophy in human skeletal muscle. Journal of Physiology.
https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2013.257121 PMID 23732643
Used for: 📚 After immobilization, older men fail to expand Pax7+ satellite cells or recover myofibre area on 4 weeks of retraining, while young men do both — age-dependent loss of useful MuSC output.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Older men n=9, mean 67.3 y (61–74); young men n=11, mean 24.4 y (21–30). 2 weeks unilateral cast immobilization, then 4 weeks supervised resistive retraining. Endpoints=vastus lateralis myofibre area and Pax7+ SC per type I/II fibre at Imm, +3 d, +4 weeks. Young: MFA up (P<0.05) and Pax7+ cells up, more Pax7+ per type II fibre than old at +3 d and +4 weeks (P<0.05). Old: no detectable MFA gain and no Pax7+ increase after 4 weeks. Myostatin downregulation weaker in old at +3 d (P<0.05). Local IGF-1/MyoD/HGF mRNA not age-different.
