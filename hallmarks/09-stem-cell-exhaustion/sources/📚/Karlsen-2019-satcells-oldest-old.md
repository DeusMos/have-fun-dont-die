# Karlsen et al., 2019. Lack of muscle fibre hypertrophy, myonuclear addition, and satellite cell pool expansion with resistance training in 83–94-year-old men and women. Acta Physiologica.
https://doi.org/10.1111/apha.13271 PMID 30828981
Used for: 📚 Very-old human type II satellite-cell content is about half of young, and 12 weeks of heavy resistance training did not expand the pool or grow fibres — useful regenerative capacity is lost at the far end of ordinary aging.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Old 83–94 y, n=29 randomized to 12 weeks heavy RT vs untrained; young men 19–27 y, n=9 comparison (no RT). Pre-training old men vs young: type II fibre size −38% (P<0.001), satellite-cell content −52% (P<0.001), myonuclear domain −30% (P<0.001), myonuclei/fibre −13% (P=0.09). RT: no effect on fibre size, myonuclear addition, or SC pool in these oldest-old men and women. Endpoint=IHC Pax7 / fibre morphometry, vastus lateralis. Duration=12 weeks.
Conflict if any: Academic (Bispebjerg / Copenhagen). Not a product trial.
