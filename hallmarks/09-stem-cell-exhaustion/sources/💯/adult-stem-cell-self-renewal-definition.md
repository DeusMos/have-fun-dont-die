# Textbook definition. Adult tissue-resident stem cells self-renew and produce differentiated progeny.
López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013 DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023 DOI 10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001; Dominici et al., Cytotherapy, 2006 DOI 10.1080/14653240600855905
Used for: 💯 Tissue-resident adult stem cells are defined by long-term self-renewal plus production of the right differentiated progeny — HSC, muscle satellite cell, intestinal Lgr5, hair-follicle stem cell, regional neural stem/progenitor — not by whatever is printed on a vial.
