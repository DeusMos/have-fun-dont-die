# Krzeski et al., 2007. Development of musculoskeletal toxicity without clear benefit after administration of PG-116800, a matrix metalloproteinase inhibitor, to patients with knee osteoarthritis. Arthritis Research & Therapy.
https://doi.org/10.1186/ar2315 PMC2212568 PMID 17933748
Used for: ☠︎︎ Broad MMP inhibition produced established musculoskeletal toxicity in humans: PG-116800 in 401 knee-OA patients (25–200 mg BID, 12 months) gave no joint-space or WOMAC benefit, forced drop of the 200 mg arm, arthralgia in 35%, ≥30% shoulder ROM loss in 23% of evaluable patients (worse at high dose), and hand oedema / palmar fibrosis / Dupuytren contracture / tendon nodules in 13%.
