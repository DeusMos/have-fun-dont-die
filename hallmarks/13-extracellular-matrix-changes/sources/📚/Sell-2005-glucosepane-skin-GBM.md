# Sell et al., 2005. Glucosepane is a major protein cross-link of the senescent human extracellular matrix. Relationship with diabetes. JBC.
https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M500733200 PMID 15677467
Used for: 📚 Glucosepane is the dominant quantified lysine–arginine AGE cross-link in senescent human skin collagen, reaching ~2000 pmol/mg at age 90 in nondiabetics versus ~30 pmol/mg MODIC and ~15 pmol/mg GODIC, and rising to ~5000 pmol/mg in diabetes.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Insoluble skin collagen n=110 (65 Caucasian, 45 African-American; 56 M / 54 F) and GBM n=28; enzymatic digest + LC-MS/MS isotope dilution. Skin glucosepane vs age r=0.76, p<0.0001 in nondiabetics without renal failure; ~2000 / 30 / 15 pmol/mg at age 90 for glucosepane / MODIC / GODIC; diabetes to ~5000 pmol/mg (p<0.0001). GBM glucosepane up to 500 pmol/mg, raised by diabetes not old age. Cross-sectional; does not show a breaker.
Conflict if any: Academic (Case Western / Hohenheim); NIA and NIDDK funding.
