# Liang et al., 2024. Efficacy and safety of collagen derivatives for osteoarthritis: A trial sequential meta-analysis. Osteoarthritis and Cartilage.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joca.2023.12.010
Used for: 📚 Liang 2024 (meta itself unfunded) pooled 25 RCTs / 2856 OA patients and found small-to-moderate pain (SMD −0.35, 95% CI −0.48 to −0.22) and function (SMD −0.31, 95% CI −0.41 to −0.22) effects for collagen derivatives — symptom noise, not radiographic matrix restore or healthspan.
