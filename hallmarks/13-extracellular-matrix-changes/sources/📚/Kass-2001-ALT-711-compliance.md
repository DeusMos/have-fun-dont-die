# Kass et al., 2001. Improved Arterial Compliance by a Novel Advanced Glycation End-Product Crosslink Breaker. Circulation.
https://doi.org/10.1161/hc3801.097806 PMID 11571237
Used for: 📚 Oral ALT-711 210 mg daily for 56 days raised total arterial compliance 15% versus no change on placebo and dropped pulse pressure −5.3 versus −0.6 mm Hg (P=0.034 treatment); cfPWV fell 8% (P<0.05 at day 56, P=0.08 for treatment).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 93 adults ≥50 y, PP >60 mm Hg, SBP >140 mm Hg, 9 US centers; ALT-711 210 mg daily n=62 vs placebo n=31 for 56 days; 90% stayed on preexisting antihypertensives. TAC +15% vs 0 (P=0.015); PP −5.3 vs −0.6 mm Hg (P=0.034); mean pressure −4 mm Hg both arms (P=0.34 treatment); PWV −8% (P<0.05 day 56, P=0.08 treatment). Not a glucosepane assay; not a hard-outcome trial.
