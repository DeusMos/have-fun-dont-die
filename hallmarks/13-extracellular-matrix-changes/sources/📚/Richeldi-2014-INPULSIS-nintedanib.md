# Richeldi et al., 2014. Efficacy and Safety of Nintedanib in Idiopathic Pulmonary Fibrosis. NEJM.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1402584 PMID 24836310
Used for: 📚 In IPF, nintedanib 150 mg BID slowed annual FVC decline versus placebo: −114.7 vs −239.9 ml (difference 125.3 ml, 95% CI 77.7–172.8) in INPULSIS-1 and −113.6 vs −207.3 ml (difference 93.7 ml, 95% CI 44.8–142.7) in INPULSIS-2.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 1066 IPF patients randomized 3:2, 52 weeks, two replicate phase 3 trials. Annual FVC decline differences as above (P<0.001 both). Exacerbation HR discordant across the two trials. Disease antifibrotic, not healthy-aging restore.
Conflict if any: Funded by Boehringer Ingelheim (sells nintedanib).
