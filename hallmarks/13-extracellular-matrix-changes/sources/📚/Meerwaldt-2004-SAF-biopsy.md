# Meerwaldt et al., 2004. Simple non-invasive assessment of advanced glycation endproduct accumulation. Diabetologia.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s00125-004-1451-2 PMID 15243705
Used for: 📚 Meerwaldt et al. validated the Diagnoptics-class Autofluorescence Reader against arm-skin biopsies in 46 people with diabetes and 46 controls (biopsies in 43); SAF correlated with CLF r=0.62, pentosidine r=0.55, CML r=0.55, CEL r=0.47; validation stated for non-pigmented skin.
