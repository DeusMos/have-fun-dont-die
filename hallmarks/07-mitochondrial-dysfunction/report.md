## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that mitochondrial dysfunction — lower capacity, mtDNA damage, failed mitophagy and quality control, redox/NAD imbalance, inflammatory leakage — accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives it, and can be scored and restored.

🤔 Longevity brands, NAD clinics, peptide telehealth, methylation-kit vendors, and Right-to-Try transplant shops sell that claim as a product: Timeline Mitopure 500–1000 mg; MitoQ 10–20 mg mitoquinol; Elysium Basis NR; Peach IV NAD 100–1000 mg; DoNotAge NMN 500–1000 mg; AgelessRx NAD nasal; MOTS-c 5–10 mg SubQ 1–3×/week; Kaneka-style ubiquinol ~200 mg; Troscriptions Just Blue 16 mg; TruDiagnostic TruHealth; Mosaic OAT; mescreen; Mitrix/MTI clinics (Timeline Mitopure; MitoQ; Elysium Basis; Peach IV; DoNotAge; AgelessRx; Telehealth Ally MOTS-c; Kaneka; Troscriptions; TruDiagnostic; MosaicDX; mescreen; Mitrix). Those companies sell the capsules, infusions, peptides, kits, or procedures they are citing.

📚 López-Otín et al. listed mitochondrial dysfunction among the 2013 hallmarks and kept it in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark,” or Forzinity’s Barth accelerated approval, as proof that ordinary mitochondrial aging can be scored and restored in intact people is a taxonomy paper or a rare-disease label doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; FDA, 2025, https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Short’s age-associated muscle ATP decline, Kraytsberg SN deletions, Ryu worm lifespan, Andreux/Singh/Liu/Denk UA signals, Martens blood NAD, Rossman FMD, Reynolds exercise MOTS-c — into “buy the stack / the FDA mito drug / the ATP kit” (Short et al., PNAS, 2005; Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Ryu et al., Nature Medicine, 2016; Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019; Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022; Liu et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022; Denk et al., Nature Aging, 2025; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018; Rossman et al., Hypertension, 2018; Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021).

## What the words mean

💯 Mitochondria run oxidative phosphorylation: complexes I–IV build the inner-membrane proton gradient; complex V makes ATP (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 Human mtDNA encodes 13 OXPHOS proteins plus rRNAs and tRNAs; the rest of the proteome is nuclear-encoded and imported (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 Electron transport produces superoxide/H2O2 as both a damage source and a physiological signal; “less ROS” is not a universal objective (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 Mitophagy is selective mitochondrial cargo (PINK1/Parkin, BNIP3/NIX, and other receptors), not bulk macroautophagy and not an “autophagy hour” (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

📚 In the hallmark papers, mitochondrial dysfunction is the bag of capacity, coupling, mtDNA integrity, dynamics, quality control, metabolite/NAD/redox signaling, and DAMP leakage — not a single battery meter (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 Clinic English uses “mito renewal,” “cellular energy,” and “hallmark 07” for a Mitopure softgel, a MitoQ bottle, an NAD drip, or a MOTS-c vial (Timeline; MitoQ; Peach IV; Telehealth Ally).

⛔ Collapsing selective mitophagy, blood NAD, methylation “ATP Synthesis Activity,” Barth cardiolipin disease, and Polg-mutator point mutations into one “mitochondrial age” is a vocabulary collision (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018; TruDiagnostic TruHealth; FDA, 2025; Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007).

## Mechanism

📚 Age-associated muscle mitochondrial decline in Short 2005 is operationalized as lower mtDNA abundance, lower mitochondrial transcripts, lower ATP production, lower selected mitochondrial proteins, and higher 8-oxo-dG — a cross-sectional human biopsy series, not a restore trial (Short et al., PNAS, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0501559102; N=146 healthy men and women, 18–89 y).

📚 Cytosolic mtDNA, cardiolipin, and formyl peptides can trigger cGAS–STING / NLRP3 innate sensing; that is the inflammatory-leakage branch of the hallmark, not a consumer CRP-down from UA (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 NAD is a substrate for sirtuins, PARPs, and CD38; a blood NAD metabolome is not compartmental sirtuin activity and not OXPHOS capacity (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018).

🥼 Whether ordinary human aging is a ROS-driven mtDNA catastrophe is not settled: Short 2005 reads 8-oxo-dG up as support for oxidative damage; Kujoth 2005’s mutator mice showed apoptosis without increased oxidative-stress markers; Kauppila 2017 treats most mammalian mtDNA mutations as POLG replication errors, not oxidative hits; Pérez 2009 found CuZnSOD/catalase overexpression did not extend mouse life; Ristow 2009 found C+E blocked training adaptations (Short et al., PNAS, 2005; Kujoth et al., Science, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1112125; Kauppila, Kauppila, and Larsson, Cell Metabolism, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2016.09.017, PMID 28076704; Pérez et al., Aging Cell, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2008.00449.x, PMID 19113837; Ristow et al., PNAS, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0903485106, PMID 19433800).

## Animal data

📚 Homozygous Polg proofreading-deficient mice accumulate mtDNA mutations and show premature-aging phenotypes (Trifunovic et al., Nature, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02517, PMID 15164064; Kujoth et al., Science, 2005).

📚 Kujoth 2005 correlated mutator aging features with apoptotic markers in high-turnover tissues, not with increased oxidative-stress markers (Kujoth et al., Science, 2005).

📚 Heterozygous Polg mice carried ~30-fold higher mtDNA point-mutation burden than the oldest wild-type without rapidly accelerated aging; Polg+/mut vs wild-type survival did not differ (log-rank P=0.875; median 758 vs 864 days) (Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17334366/; RMC assays, young WT n=4, old WT n=6, young Polg+/mut n=3).

🥼 Ordinary mtDNA point-mutation burden as the aging clock is a live paper fight: mutator homozygotes are extreme; heterozygotes were not cut; Tamashiro 2025 reports respiratory dysfunction tracking the nuclear Polg genotype more than mtDNA mutation load (Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007; Tamashiro, Ishikawa, Nakada et al., Aging Cell, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.70085, PMID 40318128).

📚 DCT-1/PINK-1/PDR-1 impairment blocked stress- and daf-2-induced mitophagy and shortened the long lifespans of daf-2, isp-1, clk-1, and eat-2 mutants without cutting wild-type lifespan (Palikaras et al., Nature, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature14300, PMID 25896323).

📚 Urolithin A 50 μM extended C. elegans mean lifespan 45.4% (pink-1/dct-1 dependent) and improved grip/running in aged mice (Ryu et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4132, PMID 27400265; Amazentis-associated authors).

📚 Adult-onset Parkin overexpression raised mean and maximum Drosophila lifespan (Rana et al., PNAS, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1216197110, PMID 23650379; n>200 ubiquitous, n>180 neuronal females).

📚 Mitochondria-targeted catalase (MCAT) increased mouse median and maximum lifespan ~5–5.5 months (~20%); peroxisomal or nuclear catalase did much less (Schriner et al., Science, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1106653, PMID 15879174).

📚 Raising NAD in C. elegans via PARP loss/inhibitors or NR/NAM extended mean lifespan 15–29% in a sir-2.1- and UPRmt-dependent manner (Mouchiroud et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.06.016, PMID 23870130). That is worm NAD→mito-stress signaling, not human blood-NAD restoration.

📚 Late-life intermittent MOTS-c (3×/week from 23.5 months) increased physical capacity in mice; daily 5–15 mg/kg IP improved treadmill performance in young mice on chow or high-fat diet (Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20790-0).

## Human data

📚 In 146 healthy adults 18–89 y who exercised ≤2 d/week, vastus-lateralis mitochondrial ATP production rate declined ~8%/decade (~5%/decade after mitochondrial-protein normalization); mtDNA and COX mRNA fell; 8-oxo-dG was ~25% higher at 65–80 vs 20–35 y; MAPR correlated with VO2max (r=0.54) (Short et al., PNAS, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0501559102, PMID 15800038).

📚 Vastus-lateralis 31P-MRS oxidative capacity was ~50% lower in elderly vs adult muscle (0.61 ± 0.04 vs 1.16 ± 0.15 mM ATP s−1) with lower mitochondrial volume density (Conley et al., Journal of Physiology, 2000, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1469-7793.2000.t01-1-00203.x, PMID 10878112; N=9 adult mean 38.8 y vs 40 elderly mean 68.8 y).

📚 Lean sedentary elderly vs activity-matched young adults had ~40% lower in-vivo muscle TCA flux and ATP synthesis by 13C/31P NMR (Petersen et al., Science, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1082889, PMID 12750520; N=15 elderly 70 ± 2 y vs 13 young 27 ± 2 y).

📚 Permeabilized vastus-lateralis myofibers from older adults showed lower maximal coupled respiration and lower respiration linked to ATP production than young adults (Porter et al., American Journal of Physiology — Endocrinology and Metabolism, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1152/ajpendo.00125.2015, PMID 26037249; N=24 young 28 ± 7 y / 31 older 62 ± 8 y).

🥼 Muscle respiratory capacity does not fall with calendar age once activity is measured: in 139 men 20–93 y, permeabilized-fiber respiration was unaltered across age in active men (Cefis, Picard, Gouspillou et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2025.101968, PMID 39933528; N=51 inactive / 88 active); older active adults matched young active adults on 31P-MRS ATP max, P/O, and OXPHOS while older sedentary adults were lower on all three (Distefano et al., Journal of Cachexia, Sarcopenia and Muscle, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.12272, PMID 29368427; N=10 YA / 10 OA / 19 OS). Short himself noted prior MAPR studies disagreed and could not exclude undetected activity differences (Short et al., PNAS, 2005).

📚 Individual pigmented substantia nigra neurons from ordinary aged humans carry clonally expanded mtDNA deletions that increase with age (P<0.0001) and often exceed the ~60% heteroplasmy threshold; COX-deficient neurons had higher deletion fractions than COX-positive neighbors (Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1778, PMID 16604072; N=80 single neurons, donors 33–102 y).

📚 A companion single-neuron qPCR series found mean deleted mtDNA of 43.3% ± 9.3% in aged-control SN neurons, with COX-deficient cells at 67% vs 48% in COX-positive cells (Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1769, PMID 16604074).

📚 Vastus-lateralis fibers with ETS abnormalities rose from an estimated 6% at age 49 to 31% at age 92; 48 COX−/SDH++ fibers carried clonal mtDNA deletions that reached >90% of total mtDNA inside the abnormal segment (Bua et al., American Journal of Human Genetics, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1086/507071, PMID 16909385).

🥼 Human SN-neuron mtDNA deletions and mouse Polg point-mutation burden are not one “mtDNA aging clock” (Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007).

📚 Four weeks of oral urolithin A 500–1000 mg/day in sedentary elderly changed skeletal-muscle mitochondrial gene expression and lowered plasma acylcarnitines (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-019-0073-4, PMID 32694802; Part B N=36, 9/arm, 28 days; Amazentis/Timeline sells Mitopure).

📚 Four months of Mitopure urolithin A 500 or 1000 mg/day in 88 overweight middle-aged adults enriched Parkin-pathway / mitophagy-linked muscle proteins and raised some strength/endurance secondaries (~12% hamstring at 500 mg, p=0.027) but missed the primary peak-power endpoint (Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633; N=88, 79 completers, 40–64 y).

📚 In 66 older adults (mean 71.7 y, 75.8% women, all White), UA 1000 mg/day × 4 months improved FDI and TA contractions-to-fatigue at 2 months vs placebo (FDI +95.3 vs +11.6; TA +41.4 vs +5.7) and lowered several acylcarnitines, ceramides, and CRP; the 6-minute-walk primary (+60.8 vs +42.5 m) and MRS ATP max were not significant vs placebo (Liu et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279, NCT03283462; Amazentis authors/funding).

📚 Denk 2025 (MitoImmune): UA 1000 mg/day × 28 days in 50 healthy adults 45–70 expanded naive-like, less terminally exhausted CD8+ cells (treatment difference 0.50 percentage points; 95% CI 0.16–0.83; P=0.0437) and CD8+ fatty-acid-oxidation capacity (+14.72 pp; 95% CI 6.46–22.99; P=0.0061); no multiple-comparison adjustment on the cytometry panel (Denk et al., Nature Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-025-00996-x, NCT05735886).

📚 Oral NR 500 mg BID × 6 weeks raised blood NAD+ ~60% in 24 lean adults 55–79 (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03421-7, PMID 29599478; NIAGEN supplied by ChromaDex).

📚 NR 2000 mg/day × 12 weeks was null on clamp insulin sensitivity and body composition in 40 sedentary men with BMI >30 (Dollerup et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/nqy132, PMID 29992272).

📚 NR 1 g/day × 21 days in 12 men (median 75 y) raised muscle NAAD and nicotinamide clearance products but did not raise muscle NAD+ and did not change complex I/II OXPHOS, max respiratory capacity, citrate synthase, mtDNA copy number, or grip (Elhassan et al., Cell Reports, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.07.043, PMID 31412242; ChromaDex supplied Niagen).

📚 Freeberg 2023 synthesizes that oral NAD precursors are generally safe and raise NAD-related metabolites, while “the clinical evidence that raising NAD+ concentrations can improve physiological function is unclear” (Freeberg et al., Journals of Gerontology Series A, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glad106, PMC10692436).

📚 Rossman 2018: 20 healthy older adults (60–79 y, FMD <6%) took MitoQ 20 mg/day or placebo for 6 weeks in crossover; brachial FMD was 42% higher after MitoQ; oxLDL fell; aortic stiffness fell only in the high-baseline subset (n=11); no clinical events (Rossman et al., Hypertension, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1161/HYPERTENSIONAHA.117.10787).

📚 MitoQ 40 or 80 mg/day × 12 months did not slow Parkinson progression vs placebo on UPDRS in 128 newly diagnosed untreated patients (Snow et al., Movement Disorders, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.23148, PMID 20568096).

📚 Phase 3 QE3 of CoQ10 1200 or 2400 mg/day (plus 1200 IU vitamin E in all arms) was stopped for futility in 600 early unmedicated PD patients (Parkinson Study Group QE3 Investigators, JAMA Neurology, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2014.131, PMID 24664227).

📚 Roshanravan 2021: 39 older adults (60–85 y) selected for low FDI ATP max (<0.7 mM/s) and P/O <1.9; a single 2-hour elamipretide infusion raised ATP max (~27% vs ~12% placebo; %Δ P=0.045) immediately; the difference was gone at day 7; infusion-day fatigue resistance was not significant (Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253849).

📚 MMPOWER-3: elamipretide 40 mg SC daily × 24 weeks in genetically confirmed primary mitochondrial myopathy did not meet coprimary 6MWT (LS mean Δ −3.2 m, 95% CI −18.7 to 12.3, P=0.69) or PMMSA total fatigue (P=0.37); Class I evidence of no 6MWT/fatigue benefit vs placebo (Karaa et al., Neurology, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000207402, PMID 37268435, NCT03323749; Stealth-funded).

📚 FDA granted accelerated approval to Forzinity (elamipretide) injection on 19 September 2025 for Barth syndrome in patients weighing at least 30 kg, based on improved knee-extensor strength with a required confirmatory RCT; daily SubQ; common AEs were mild-to-moderate injection-site reactions (FDA, 2025, https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome).

🥼 CALERIE-class CR does not speak with one mito voice: six months of CR raised muscle mtDNA +35±5% without changing TCA/β-oxidation/COX enzyme activities (Civitarese et al., PLOS Medicine, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.0040076; N=36 overweight young); twelve months in a CALERIE site (n=51, 25–50 y) left in-vivo muscle ATP max and P/O unaffected and left biogenesis/oxidative-stress transcripts unmoved (Sparks et al., JCEM, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2016-3211, PMID 27778643, PMC5413108).

📚 Metformin attenuated VO2max and insulin-sensitivity gains and blocked the mitochondrial-respiration increase from 12 weeks aerobic training in older adults (n=27 metformin / 26 placebo; mean 62 y) (Konopka et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12880, PMID 30548390).

📚 In 10 sedentary healthy young men, bicycle exercise raised skeletal-muscle MOTS-c ~11.9-fold and circulating MOTS-c ~1.5–1.6-fold, returning toward baseline after 4 h rest (Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021; n=10; Wilcoxon).

## Measurement

💯 There is no single assay of “mitochondrial age.” Biopsy respirometry and 31P-MRS ATP max / PCr recovery score tissue bioenergetics; bulk mtDNA copy number is not heteroplasmy; blood NAD is not compartmental NAD; a methylation EBP is not ATP (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Short et al., PNAS, 2005; Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021).

📚 31P-MRS ATP max in Roshanravan is FDI PCr recovery after a short contraction, operationalized as ATP max = PCr_rest / τ_PCr with PCr_rest = 25.5 mM — in-vivo capacity, not a mail-order score (Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021).

🤔 TruDiagnostic TruHealth lists “Mitochondrial Fission Activity,” “Mitochondrial ATP Synthesis Activity,” and NAD+ catabolism / nicotinamide / NR precursor status as finger-prick epigenetic biomarker proxies (TruDiagnostic, https://shop.trudiagnostic.com/products/truage-truhealth). TruDiagnostic sells the kit.

📜 Metabolite EBPs in the Chen 2024 medRxiv training set had mean Spearman 0.29; Energy-pathway metabolites were the weakest block (mean ρ=0.23) (Chen, Higgins-Chen et al., medRxiv, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1101/2024.12.06.24318612). TruDiagnostic-affiliated authors; TruDiagnostic sells TruHealth EBPs.

🤔 MosaicDX Organic Acids Test markets a urine “Mitochondrial Health” block (Krebs / FAO intermediates) from one sample; Mosaic pairs OAT with MycoTOX for mitochondrial-function copy (MosaicDX, https://mosaicdx.com/test/organic-acids-test/). Mosaic sells the test.

🤔 mescreen (Verséa Discovery) sells an at-home capillary-blood “mitochondrial efficiency” kit (~$549–599, 3–4 week turnaround) with NASA Twin Study / cellular-energy copy (mescreen, https://mescreen.com/). Verséa sells the kit.

⛔ A TruHealth “ATP Synthesis Activity” number, a Mosaic OAT Krebs block, or a mescreen efficiency score is not Short-style biopsy ATP production or Roshanravan ATP max (TruDiagnostic TruHealth; MosaicDX; mescreen; Short et al., PNAS, 2005; Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021; Chen et al., medRxiv, 2024).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient mitophagy plus biogenesis — UA-style Parkin-axis pulse without stripping mitochondria faster than replacement — might raise tissue reserve in selected muscle or immune compartments; that is a scheduling hope, not a healthspan result (inference from Ryu 2016 + Andreux/Singh/Liu/Denk; no source for organismal restoration).

🐉 Allele-specific mtDNA base editing (DdCBE/TALED) or mito-nucleases shifting heteroplasmy in post-mitotic organs is not systemic aging therapy; delivery, rebound, and off-target/bystander edits are the engineering problems (inference from hallmark 01 briefing neighbor + Vermulst’s point-mutation-threshold cut; no compiled primary this round).

🐉 A treat-to-target mitochondrial score — biopsy or 31P-MRS in the organ you care about, plus single-cell heteroplasmy, not TruHealth ATP EBPs or blood NAD — does not exist as a validated product (inference from Short 2005 + Roshanravan 2021 + TruDiagnostic menu).

🐉 Late-life MOTS-c or a next analogue might copy Reynolds mouse capacity without gray-market peptide quality; CohBar’s CB4211 analogue program was abandoned after formulation/Phase 1b and CohBar dissolved — that is a pipeline corpse, not a result (Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021; Alzheimer’s Drug Discovery Foundation MOTS-c brief, https://www.alzdiscovery.org/uploads/cognitive_vitality_media/MOTS-c.pdf).

🐉 Somatic mitochondrial transfer beyond pediatric cardiac ischemia might seed functional organelles in other tissues; McCully-class work is still ischemia, Mitrix clinics are Right-to-Try practice, and Bertero 2018 is the evidence-gap paper — not an aging RCT (Harvard Magazine, 2025; Mitrix, 2026; Bertero and Maack, JCI, 2018).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Ordinary human muscle shows age-associated drops in mtDNA, transcripts, and ATP production in sedentary series; ordinary aged SN neurons carry clonal mtDNA deletions above a COX threshold; ETS-abnormal VL fibers rise from ~6% at 49 y to ~31% at 92 y (Short et al., PNAS, 2005; Conley et al., Journal of Physiology, 2000; Petersen et al., Science, 2003; Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Bua et al., AJHG, 2006).

🥼 Whether the limiter is ordinary point-mutation burden, clonal deletions, failed mitophagy, inactivity, or nuclear Polg genotype is a live paper fight: Cefis 2025 / Distefano 2018 read activity as the confounder; Tamashiro 2025 reads nuclear Polg genotype over mutation load; homozygous mutator progeria is not settled as a low-dose version of ordinary aging (Vermulst 2007; Tamashiro 2025; Cefis 2025; Distefano 2018; Kujoth 2005; López-Otín 2023).

📚 Urolithin A moves muscle mito transcripts / Parkin-axis proteins and some endurance/strength secondaries and a 28-day immune-phenotype panel; it missed Liu 6MWT and Singh peak power; Amazentis funds and authors the UA stack (Andreux 2019; Singh 2022; Liu 2022; Denk 2025).

📚 NAD precursors raise blood NAD and are generally tolerated; functional benefits are unclear; Dollerup’s clamp was null; Elhassan did not raise muscle NAD+ or change OXPHOS (Martens 2018; Freeberg 2023; Dollerup 2018; Elhassan 2019).

📚 MitoQ 20 mg × 6 weeks moved FMD in a 20-person crossover and 40–80 mg × 12 months did not slow PD; QE3 CoQ10 was futile in PD; elamipretide moved one-dose ATP max and failed MMPOWER-3 6MWT in PMM; Forzinity is Barth, not aging (Rossman 2018; Snow 2010; QE3 2014; Roshanravan 2021; Karaa 2023; FDA 2025).

⛔ A Mitopure softgel, a MitoQ bottle, an NAD drip or nasal, a MOTS-c vial, a methylene-blue troche, a TruHealth / Mosaic / mescreen proxy, a Mitrix Right-to-Try transplant, or “it is a hallmark” is not organismal mitochondrial restoration in healthy people (Timeline; MitoQ; Peach IV; AgelessRx; Telehealth Ally; Troscriptions; TruDiagnostic; MosaicDX; mescreen; Mitrix; López-Otín 2023).

🤔 People are buying 500–1000 mg Mitopure, 10–20 mg MitoQ, NAD IV/NR/NMN/nasal, ubiquinol ~200 mg, 5–10 mg MOTS-c, 4–16 mg methylene blue, and Mitrix waitlist slots under that slogan anyway (Timeline; MitoQ; Peach IV; Elysium; DoNotAge; AgelessRx; Kaneka; Telehealth Ally; Troscriptions; Mitrix).

☠︎︎ The antioxidant-as-protection stack has a hard-endpoint record: ATBC high-dose beta-carotene raised lung cancer and death in smokers; SELECT vitamin E 400 IU/day raised prostate cancer (HR 1.17) in 35,533 men (ATBC, NEJM, 1994; Klein et al., JAMA, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2011.1437, PMID 21990298). That is not a documented body count for 20 mg MitoQ or 500 mg UA.
