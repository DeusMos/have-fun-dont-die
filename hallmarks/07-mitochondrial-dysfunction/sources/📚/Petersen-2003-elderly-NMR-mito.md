# Petersen et al., 2003. Mitochondrial Dysfunction in the Elderly: Possible Role in Insulin Resistance. Science.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1082889 PMID 12750520 PMC3004429
Used for: 📚 Lean sedentary elderly vs activity-matched young adults had ~40% lower in vivo muscle mitochondrial TCA flux (62 ± 5 vs 96 ± 10 nmol/g/min, P<0.006) and ATP synthesis (4.06 ± 0.65 vs 7.50 ± 0.77 μmol/g/min, P<0.004) by 13C/31P NMR, with ~40% lower clamp glucose disposal.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=15 elderly 70 ± 2 y vs 13 young 27 ± 2 y, BMI-matched, lean sedentary nonsmokers; ~40% OXPHOS/TCA and clamp uptake; healthy humans; in vivo 13C/31P NMR + hyperinsulinemic-euglycemic clamp; cross-sectional.
