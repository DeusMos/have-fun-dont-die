# Parkinson Study Group QE3 Investigators, 2014. A randomized clinical trial of high-dosage coenzyme Q10 in early Parkinson disease: no evidence of benefit. JAMA Neurology.
https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaneurol.2014.131 PMID 24664227
Used for: Phase 3 QE3 of CoQ10 1200 or 2400 mg/d was stopped for futility; UPDRS worsening was not better than placebo (slight adverse trend).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=600 early unmedicated PD, mean 62.5 y; 16 months or until dopaminergic need. UPDRS +6.9 placebo vs +7.5 (1200 mg, P=0.49) vs +8.0 (2400 mg, P=0.21). All arms got 1200 IU/d vitamin E.
Conflict if any: Cooperative-group trial. CoQ10/ubiquinol remains the default consumer mito pill.
