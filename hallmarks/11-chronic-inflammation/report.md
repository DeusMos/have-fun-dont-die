## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that sterile, low-grade, age-associated inflammatory tone (inflammaging) accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally contributes to aging and age-related disease, can be scored in blood, and that interrupting selected pathways (IL-1β / NLRP3 / IL-6 / resolution failure / CHIP-driven IL-1) makes people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity clinics and protocol blogs sell that claim as a product: HealthRX’s 5-step hsCRP/IL-6/NLR/clock ladder then metformin/senolytic/NAD/HRT; Advanced Vitality Group’s cytokine table plus optional colchicine 0.5 mg; WePrevent “near-zero” markers; RegenStep curcumin 500–1000 mg + omega-3 2–4 g against hsCRP <0.5; Function Health / InsideTracker / SiPhox hsCRP dashboards; Life Extension SPM + Curcumin Elite; AgeRejuvenation EBOO ozone (HealthRX, https://healthrx.com/longevity-rx/inflammaging; Advanced Vitality Group, https://advancedvitalitygroup.com/longevity/inflammation-control; WePrevent, https://weprevent.org/resources/inflammaging-mitigation-protocol/; RegenStep, https://regenstep.com/silent-inflammation-markers-blood-tests/; Function Health, https://www.functionhealth.com/pricing; InsideTracker InnerAge 2.0; SiPhox; Life Extension item 02223; AgeRejuvenation, https://agerejuvenation.com/wellness-center/cellular-plasma-recharge/). Those companies sell the consult, the membership, the capsule, or the drip.

📚 López-Otín et al. parked inflammaging inside altered intercellular communication in 2013 and promoted chronic inflammation to a standalone hallmark among twelve in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349; already filed under hallmarks/10).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark,” a CANTOS MACE HR, or an AHA hsCRP band as proof that ordinary sterile tone can be scored and restored so healthy people live longer is a taxonomy paper or a residual-risk cardiology trial doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Ridker et al., NEJM, 2017).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Franceschi’s coinage, CANTOS IL-1β, COLCOT/LoDoCo2 colchicine, IL6R Mendelian randomization, CHIP–IL-1, Coppé SASP, weekly-rapamycin SASP-suppression in culture — into “buy the panel and the stack” (Franceschi 2000; Ridker 2017; Tardif 2019; Nidorf 2020; IL6R Consortium 2012; Jaiswal 2017; Fuster 2017; Coppé 2008; Laberge 2015).

## What the words mean

💯 Acute inflammation is a coordinated, time-limited innate and adaptive response to infection or injury: detection, recruitment, clearance, then resolution (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Serhan, Nature, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13479, PMID 24899309).

📚 Franceschi et al. coined “inflamm-aging” for a progressive, usually macrophage-centered increase in proinflammatory status with age, driven by lifelong antigenic load and stress (Franceschi et al., Ann N Y Acad Sci, 2000, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1749-6632.2000.tb06651.x, PMID 10911963).

📚 The same 2000 paper already used centenarians to illustrate the paradox: high plasma inflammatory cytokines, acute-phase proteins, and coagulation factors in people who reached extreme age (Franceschi et al., Ann N Y Acad Sci, 2000).

📚 Franceschi et al. 2007 framed healthy aging as an imbalance between inflammatory and anti-inflammatory networks and proposed that “anti-inflammaging” — efficient counter-regulatory networks, not the absence of markers — sits next to inflammaging as a longevity determinant (Franceschi et al., Mech Ageing Dev, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.mad.2006.11.016, PMID 17116321).

📚 This hallmark is sterile / systemic / low-grade age-associated tone, not a billed RA flare, an abscess, or a day of post-exercise soreness (Franceschi et al., 2000; Ferrucci and Fabbri, Nat Rev Cardiol, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41569-018-0064-2, PMID 30065258).

📚 Metaflammation is nutrient-excess / adipose-driven inflammation; it overlaps inflammaging and is not a synonym (Franceschi et al., 2000; Ferrucci and Fabbri, 2018).

📚 Immunosenescence is adaptive/innate remodeling — thymic involution, myeloid bias, CMV-restricted TEMRA — paired with inflammaging, not identical to it (Franceschi et al., 2000; Ferrucci and Fabbri, 2018).

💯 C-reactive protein is a hepatic pentraxin produced largely in response to IL-6; the high-sensitivity assay reads the low milligram-per-liter range used for residual atherosclerotic risk (Pearson et al., Circulation, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1161/01.cir.0000052939.59093.45, PMID 12551878).

📚 CDC/AHA 2003 set hsCRP cardiovascular-risk tertiles at <1.0 mg/L (low), 1.0–3.0 (average), and >3.0 (high), and said values ≥10 mg/L should be repeated after looking for infection or other acute inflammation — residual-risk bands, not a biological-age score (Pearson et al., Circulation, 2003).

📚 IL-6 is context-dependent: a myokine after exercise, a tonic catabolic/prothrombotic signal when chronically high, and a ligand whose receptor genetics are not the same operation as ligand-antibody blockade (IL6R Genetics Consortium / Swerdlow et al., Lancet, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(12)60110-X, PMID 22421340; Novo Nordisk 6-K, 31 Jul 2026).

📚 “Anti-inflammatory” is pathway-specific. CIRT showed a real rheumatologic drug that did not move the IL-1β–IL-6–CRP axis or events (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809798, PMID 30415610).

📚 Specialized pro-resolving mediators — lipoxins, resolvins, protectins, maresins — are an active resolution program of acute inflammation, not a completed human aging outcome (Serhan, Nature, 2014).

📚 CHIP as used in hematology is a leukemia-driver mutation at VAF ≥2% without a WHO myeloid neoplasm — clone size in blood, not an inflammaging score (Steensma et al., Blood, 2015; already filed under hallmarks/01).

⛔ Collapsing 2013-communication-bucket inflammaging, 2023 standalone chronic inflammation, hallmark 08 SASP-as-cell-state, hallmark 10 milieu products, and a Function/InsideTracker hsCRP tile into one “inflammation age” is a vocabulary collision (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Ogrodnik / Saul / Lee / Park synthesis already filed under hallmarks/08 as `SASP-clock-is-not-a-census.md`).

🤔 Clinic English uses “inflammaging” for any hsCRP above 1.0, any IL-6 printout, and any stack that cites CANTOS (HealthRX; Advanced Vitality Group; RegenStep).

## Mechanism

📚 Ferrucci and Fabbri review inflammageing as elevated blood inflammatory markers in most older people, carrying high susceptibility to chronic morbidity, disability, frailty, and premature death, and state that the CVD association has randomized-trial support while non-CVD clinical-course benefit remains controversial (Ferrucci and Fabbri, Nat Rev Cardiol, 2018, PMID 30065258).

📚 Furman et al. frame systemic chronic inflammation as a life-span disease etiology: intermittent inflammation is required for injury/infection, while persistent SCI is linked to CVD, cancer, diabetes, CKD, NAFLD, autoimmunity, and neurodegeneration, and they attribute >50% of worldwide deaths to inflammation-related diseases from cited GBD-style sources (Furman et al., Nat Med, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0675-0, PMID 31806905). Perspective, not a new cohort.

📚 In the InCHIANTI population sample, older age associated with higher circulating IL-6, IL-1ra, IL-18, CRP, and fibrinogen in both sexes (sIL-6R rose with age only in men); the age coefficients shrank after adjustment for cardiovascular risk factors and morbidity, and a low-risk no-CVD subgroup of 51 men and 45 women still showed the age pattern (Ferrucci et al., Blood, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2004-07-2599, PMID 15572589; N=595 men + 748 women, age 20–102; cross-sectional).

📚 Genotoxic-stress-induced senescent human fibroblasts and epithelial cells secrete a delayed SASP of inflammatory and matrix-remodeling factors; IL-6 and IL-8 are required for paracrine EMT and invasiveness (Coppé et al., PLoS Biol, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0060301, PMID 19053174; already filed under hallmarks/08). SASP-as-source here; senolysis stays in 08.

📚 Late-senescent human fibroblasts upregulate LINE-1 RNA 4–5-fold; cytoplasmic L1 cDNA drives IFN-I and SASP, and lamivudine downregulates IFN-I/tissue inflammaging in aged mice (De Cecco et al., Nature, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0784-9, PMID 30728521; already filed under hallmarks/03). 03 owns chromatin; 11 takes the DAMP/IFN broadcast.

📚 Rapamycin blunts SASP via MTOR-dependent IL1A translation without reversing arrest — senomorphic, not senolytic (Laberge et al., Nat Cell Biol, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb3195, PMID 26147232; already filed under hallmarks/08). 06 owns the drug and the weekly-dose tables.

📚 Partial (10%) bone-marrow reconstitution with Tet2-deficient cells in Ldlr−/− mice was sufficient for clonal expansion and a marked increase in aortic-root atherosclerotic plaque after 9 weeks of high-fat/high-cholesterol diet; Tet2-deficient macrophages hyper-secreted NLRP3-dependent IL-1β; the NLRP3 inhibitor MCC950 showed greater atheroprotection in TET2-deficient chimeras than in nonchimeric mice (Fuster et al., Science, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aag1381, PMID 28104796; n typically 3–15/assay; 9–10 weeks HFHC).

📚 An IL6R SNP (rs7529229 marking p.Asp358Ala) that mimics tocilizumab-like IL-6-receptor blockade raised circulating IL-6 +9.45% per allele (95% CI 8.34–10.57), lowered CRP −8.35% (7.31–9.38) and fibrinogen −0.85% (0.60–1.10), and associated with lower CHD odds (per-allele OR 0.95, 95% CI 0.93–0.97, p=1.53×10⁻⁵) (IL6R Genetics Consortium / Swerdlow et al., Lancet, 2012, PMID 22421340; N=up to 133,449 in 40 biomarker studies; 25,458 CHD cases / 100,740 controls). This is IL6R signaling, not IL-6 ligand titer and not CRP-as-cause.

📚 Mendelian randomization does not support CRP itself as causal for coronary disease: the CRP CHD Genetics Collaboration, individual-participant data from 194,418 people including 46,557 CHD cases across 47 studies, found a risk ratio of 1.00 (95% CI 0.90–1.13) per 1 SD higher genetically raised ln(CRP), against an observational RR of 1.33 (1.23–1.43) for the same circulating increment (P=0.001 for discordance) (CRP CHD Genetics Collaboration / Wensley, Gao, Burgess et al., BMJ, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.d548, PMID 21325005).

⛔ Titrate-to-target hsCRP as a causal aging clock, or as a treat-to-target longevity score for curcumin, is a hepatic pentraxin being asked to do a job genetics already declined for CHD (CRP CHD Genetics Collaboration, BMJ, 2011).

## Animal data

📚 Ablation of the canonical Nlrp3 inflammasome protected chow-fed mice from age-related sterile innate activation in periphery and brain, improved glycemic control, attenuated bone loss and thymic demise, reduced astrogliosis, and improved Stone T-maze errors and rotarod; IL-1 receptor deletion mediated the cognitive/motor limb but not the thymic/T-cell remodeling limb; caspase-11 was not required (Youm et al., Cell Metab, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2013.09.010, PMID 24093676; typically n=6–9/genotype/age for GTT and behavior, n=5–8 for 24-mo bone µCT, n=12 for 20-mo Il1r−/− maze/rotarod; 2 mo vs 18–24 mo; no lifespan table).

📚 Chronic progressive low-grade inflammation from nfkb1 knockout (constitutive NF-κB) reduced mean and maximum mouse lifespan, made 36-week knockouts resemble ≥104-week wild-type on aging-biomarker tables, cut liver and gut regeneration, and accumulated telomere-dysfunctional senescent cells; ibuprofen 50 mg/kg/day (4–8 weeks) or dietary BHA 0.7% w/w (4 weeks) rescued telomere-associated foci and regenerative potential; the authors did not attempt lifespan rescue because of chronic-NSAID toxicity (Jurk et al., Nat Commun, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5172, PMID 24960204; exact days are figure-only).

📚 Fuster 2017 Tet2-chimera atherosclerosis via NLRP3/IL-1β is the CHIP-as-inflammatory-source animal result, not a human clone-erasure trial (Fuster et al., Science, 2017).

## Human data

📚 A combined z-score inflammation domain predicted all-cause mortality in Tokyo very-old and (semi-)supercentenarian cohorts (HR 1.89, 95% CI 1.21–2.95 in the very old; HR 1.36, 95% CI 1.05–1.78 in (semi-)supercentenarians), explained 10.8% of capability and 8.6% of cognition variance in (semi-)supercentenarians better than chronological age or sex, and was lower in centenarian offspring than age-matched spouse controls (Δ −0.795, 95% CI −1.436 to −0.154); telomere length did not predict successful aging in those centenarians (Arai et al., EBioMedicine, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2015.07.029, PMID 26629551; N=1554 including 684 centenarians/(semi-)supercentenarians).

📚 A two-marker inflammation index (IL-6 + soluble TNF-α receptor-1) was the best of 15 NF-κB-pathway markers for 10-year all-cause mortality in Cardiovascular Health Study after age/sex/education/race/smoking/BMI adjustment (HR 1.62, 95% CI 1.54–1.70) and validated in InCHIANTI (HR 1.33, 95% CI 1.17–1.52) (Varadhan et al., J Gerontol A, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glt023, PMID 23689826; discovery 1,155 InCHIANTI then 5,600 CHS).

📚 In InCHIANTI participants aged ≥65, higher IL-6 and CRP associated with worse walking speed, chair-stand, and standing-balance performance after adjustment for demographics, chronic conditions, and medications (Cesari et al., J Gerontol A, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/59.3.m242, PMID 15031310; N=1,020; cross-sectional).

📚 In four human case–control sets totaling 4,726 coronary-heart-disease cases and 3,529 controls, CHIP carriers had 1.9-fold CHD risk in two prospective nested analyses (95% CI 1.4–2.7) and 4.0-fold early-onset MI in two retrospective sets (95% CI 2.4–6.7); DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, and JAK2 each associated with CHD (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1701719, PMID 28636844). The 2014 prevalence paper is a different job and is already filed under hallmarks/01.

📚 In CANTOS, canakinumab 150 mg s.c. every 3 months in 10,061 post-MI patients with hsCRP ≥2 mg/L cut the primary MACE composite vs placebo (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.74–0.98, P=0.021; 3.86 vs 4.50 events/100 person-years) at median 3.7 years without lowering LDL; 150 mg also cut MACE plus urgent-revascularization unstable angina (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.73–0.95, P=0.005); hsCRP median reductions vs placebo at 48 months were 26 / 37 / 41 percentage points at 50 / 150 / 300 mg; all-cause mortality was neutral (pooled HR 0.94, 95% CI 0.83–1.06, P=0.31) (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1707914, PMID 28845751, NCT01327846; mean age 61, 25.7% women, median hsCRP 4.20 mg/L, median LDL 82.4 mg/dL). Novartis funded CANTOS and sells Ilaris.

☠︎︎ CANTOS recorded more fatal infection or sepsis on canakinumab than placebo (0.31 vs 0.18 / 100 person-years, P=0.02); the dead tended to be older and more often diabetic (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2017).

📚 The CANTOS 150 mg MACE HR is a selected secondary-prevention disease-event result, not a healthy-person healthspan result, not a longevity result, and not a warrant that any CRP-lowering capsule works (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2017).

📚 In an exploratory CANTOS genomic substudy, targeted sequencing of 3,923 baseline samples found CHIP in 338 (8.6%), with TET2 more common than DNMT3A (103 vs 85); TET2-CHIP carriers on pooled canakinumab had MACE HR 0.38 (95% CI 0.15–0.96) vs TET2-CHIP on placebo, with a non-significant interaction vs others (P=0.14); placebo CHIP vs no-CHIP MACE HR 1.32 (95% CI 0.86–2.04, P=0.21) (Svensson et al., JAMA Cardiol, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamacardio.2022.0386, PMID 35385050). Hypothesis-generating; not a CHIP-stratified RCT. Authors include Novartis-affiliated and Ridker/Libby.

📚 CIRT killed the “any anti-inflammatory” reading of CANTOS: low-dose methotrexate 15–20 mg weekly in 4,786 patients with prior MI or multivessel CAD plus T2D or metabolic syndrome did not lower IL-1β, IL-6, or hsCRP versus placebo and did not cut events (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2019, PMID 30415610, NCT01594333).

📚 CIRT’s final primary (MACE or unstable angina leading to urgent revascularization) was 201 vs 207 events after median 2.3 years (4.13 vs 4.31 / 100 person-years; HR 0.96, 95% CI 0.79–1.16, P=0.67); the original MACE primary was HR 1.01 (0.82–1.25); all-cause death 96 vs 83 (HR 1.16, 0.87–1.56); the trial was stopped for futility (Ridker et al., NEJM, 2019). Median hsCRP at randomization was 1.6 mg/L, not the CANTOS residual-risk gate of ≥2 mg/L.

📚 In RESCUE, monthly ziltivekimab (IL-6 ligand mAb) 7.5 / 15 / 30 mg vs placebo in CKD plus hsCRP ≥2 mg/L collapsed median hsCRP at 12 weeks by 77% / 88% / 92% vs 4% placebo (pairwise differences −66.2% / −77.7% / −87.8%, all P<0.0001), stable through 24 weeks, with parallel drops in fibrinogen, SAA, haptoglobin, sPLA2, and Lp(a); no MACE endpoint (Ridker et al., Lancet, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)00520-1, PMID 34015342, NCT03926117; N=264, 66/arm). Novo-developed antibody. Biomarker, not events.

📜 ZEUS is the surrogate-CRP killer, not an eraser of CANTOS or of IL6R genetics: once-monthly ziltivekimab 15 mg in >6,300 people (registry actual N=6,385) with ASCVD + CKD + hsCRP ≥2 lowered free IL-6 and hsCRP as designed and missed 3-point MACE (HR 0.99, 95% CI 0.88–1.11) (Novo Nordisk SEC Form 6-K, 31 Jul 2026, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/353278/000117184326005109/f6k_073126.htm; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05021835). Company topline, not a peer-reviewed paper; full manuscript pending. Novo sells the antibody franchise and booked a Q3 2026 impairment.

📜 ZEUS reported similar overall AE/SAE rates versus placebo, a higher proportion of serious infections on ziltivekimab, and no all-cause mortality difference; HERMES and ARTEMIS remain open (Novo Nordisk 6-K, 31 Jul 2026). Infection counts were not in the 6-K; not upgraded to ☠︎︎.

⛔ “Lower the blood marker, treat aging” fails the ZEUS operationalization: target engagement and hsCRP collapse without a MACE move is exactly the clinic dashboard claim, run in the residual-risk population the dashboard borrowed (Novo Nordisk 6-K, 31 Jul 2026; NCT05021835).

📚 HERMES (NCT05636176) is Novo’s Phase 3 ziltivekimab vs placebo in HFmrEF/HFpEF plus hsCRP ≥2 mg/L, status ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING, actual enrollment 4,899, primary completion 2027-07-02; primary endpoint is time to CV death, HF hospitalization, or urgent HF visit — not healthy-person aging (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05636176).

📚 ARTEMIS (NCT06118281) is Novo’s Phase 3 ziltivekimab vs placebo after type-1 AMI, status RECRUITING, estimated N=10,000, primary completion 2026-12-14; primary endpoint 3-point MACE (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06118281).

📚 In COLCOT, colchicine 0.5 mg daily started within 30 days after MI cut the composite of CV death, resuscitated arrest, MI, stroke, or urgent angina hospitalization leading to revascularization (5.5% vs 7.1%; HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.61–0.96, P=0.02) over median 22.6 months; pneumonia SAE 0.9% vs 0.4% (P=0.03) (Tardif et al., NEJM, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1912388, PMID 31733140, NCT02551094; N=4,745). Disease events, not aging.

📚 In LoDoCo2, colchicine 0.5 mg daily in chronic coronary disease cut CV death, spontaneous MI, ischemic stroke, or ischemia-driven revascularization (6.8% vs 9.6%; 2.5 vs 3.6 /100 person-years; HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.57–0.83, P<0.001) over median 28.6 months (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2021372, PMID 32865380; N=5,522, mean age 66, 15.3% women).

📚 LoDoCo2 noncardiovascular death was 53/2,762 vs 35/2,760 (0.7 vs 0.5 / 100 person-years; HR 1.51, 95% CI 0.99–2.31); all-cause death 73 vs 60 (HR 1.21, 0.86–1.71) (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020).

🥼 The LoDoCo2 non-CV death HR 1.51 CI includes 1.00; a later cause-of-death paper (133 deaths; cancer 26 vs 22, infection 4 vs 4) did not pin a mechanism and treated the split as chance plus comorbidity (Opstal et al., Int J Cardiol, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijcard.2022.12.026, PMID 36535561). Live safety ambiguity in the same trial quoted as colchicine-for-inflammaging.

📚 CLEAR SYNERGY is the colchicine population-specificity null: 7,062 patients with acute MI at 104 centers, colchicine vs placebo (2×2 with spironolactone), median 3 years; primary (CV death, recurrent MI, stroke, or unplanned ischemia-driven revascularization) 322/3,528 (9.1%) vs 327/3,534 (9.3%); HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.85–1.16, P=0.93) (Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2405922, PMID 39555823, NCT03048825).

📚 CLEAR SYNERGY still moved CRP (least-squares mean difference −1.28 mg/L at 3 months, 95% CI −1.81 to −0.75) and still missed the outcome; diarrhea 10.2% vs 6.6% (P<0.001); serious infections did not differ (Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025).

🥼 Low-dose colchicine is not a settled CAD drug across populations and is not a healthy-person inflammaging pill: LoDoCo2 HR 0.69 in chronic CAD versus CLEAR SYNERGY HR 0.99 in post-MI PCI (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020; Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025).

📚 DailyMed LODOCO (colchicine 0.5 mg) is labeled to reduce MI, stroke, coronary revascularization, and CV death in adults with established atherosclerotic disease or multiple CV risk factors, dose 0.5 mg once daily; contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 or P-gp inhibitors and in renal failure / severe hepatic impairment (AGEPHA / FDA label, revised 8/2024, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=ff06d68f-d65f-d097-e053-6294a90a7e5f).

📚 DailyMed ILARIS (canakinumab) is an IL-1β blocker labeled for CAPS (FCAS/MWS), TRAPS, HIDS/MKD, FMF, active Still’s disease (AOSD/SJIA ≥2 y), and adult gout flares when NSAIDs and colchicine fail; there is no aging or ASCVD indication on the US label (Novartis DailyMed, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=7d271f3b-e4f9-4d80-8dcf-28d49123f80e).

📚 The exploratory CANTOS lung-cancer signal did not survive dedicated NSCLC trials. CANOPY-1: 643 first-line advanced NSCLC, canakinumab 200 mg q3w + pembrolizumab + platinum doublet vs placebo combo; median PFS 6.8 vs 6.8 months (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.67–1.09, P=0.102); median OS 20.8 vs 20.2 months (HR 0.87, 0.70–1.10, P=0.123) (Paz-Ares et al., J Clin Oncol, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00980, NCT03631199).

📚 CANOPY-2: 237 previously treated stage IIIB–IV NSCLC, canakinumab + docetaxel vs placebo + docetaxel; median OS 10.5 vs 11.3 months (HR 1.06, 95% CI 0.76–1.48); median PFS 4.17 vs 4.21 months (HR 1.12, 0.85–1.50) (Paz-Ares et al., Ann Oncol, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.annonc.2021.08.1799, NCT03626545).

☠︎︎ CANOPY-2 reported fatal infections 6.7% vs 1.8% on canakinumab + docetaxel vs placebo + docetaxel (Paz-Ares et al., Ann Oncol, 2021).

📚 CANOPY-A: 1,382 randomized completely resected stage II–III NSCLC, adjuvant canakinumab 200 mg q3w vs placebo; median DFS 35.0 vs 29.7 months; HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.78–1.14), one-sided P=0.258; CRP and IL-6 fell on canakinumab and had no correlation with differential clinical outcomes (Garon et al., J Clin Oncol, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00910, PMID 37788412, NCT03447769).

⛔ Filing the CANTOS exploratory lung-cancer signal as a cancer-prevention aging win, after CANOPY-1/2/A missed PFS, OS, or DFS while still dropping CRP/IL-6, is a definition game (Garon et al., 2023; Paz-Ares et al., 2021/2024).

📚 ASPREE is the closest large RCT to “give a common anti-inflammatory to healthy older people and see if they live healthier”: 19,114 community-dwelling adults, median age 74, no CVD/dementia/disability, 100 mg enteric aspirin vs placebo, median 4.7 years; composite death, dementia, or persistent disability 21.5 vs 21.2 / 1000 person-years (HR 1.01, 95% CI 0.92–1.11, P=0.79); stopped for futility (McNeil et al., NEJM, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1800722, PMID 30221596, NCT01038583). Bayer supplied drug and had no other role.

📚 ASPREE all-cause mortality was higher on aspirin: 12.7 vs 11.1 / 1000 person-years (HR 1.14, 95% CI 1.01–1.29); cancer accounted for 1.6 excess deaths / 1000 person-years (cancer-death HR 1.31, 1.10–1.56) (McNeil et al., NEJM, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1803955, PMID 30221595).

☠︎︎ ASPREE major hemorrhage was higher on aspirin (3.8% vs 2.8%; HR 1.38, 95% CI 1.18–1.62, P<0.001) (McNeil et al., NEJM, 2018, PMID 30221596).

☠︎︎ APPROVe is the COX-2 “chronic anti-inflammatory for a long-horizon indication” harm case: 2,586 patients with prior colorectal adenomas, rofecoxib 25 mg daily vs placebo, confirmed thrombotic events 1.50 vs 0.78 / 100 patient-years (RR 1.92, 95% CI 1.19–3.11, P=0.008), apparent after 18 months; investigator-reported CHF/pulmonary edema/cardiac failure HR 4.61 (1.50–18.83); Merck’s Vioxx was withdrawn (Bresalier et al., NEJM, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa050493, PMID 15713943).

☠︎︎ ORAL Surveillance is established JAK-inhibitor harm in humans: 4,362 RA patients ≥50 y with ≥1 CV risk factor, tofacitinib 5 or 10 mg BID vs a TNF inhibitor, median 4.0 years; combined tofacitinib MACE 98/2,911 (3.4%) vs 37/1,451 (2.5%), HR 1.33 (0.91–1.94), failed noninferiority (upper 95% CI >1.8); cancers excluding NMSC 122 (4.2%) vs 42 (2.9%), HR 1.48 (1.04–2.09) (Ytterberg et al., NEJM, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2109927, PMID 35081280, NCT02092467). Pfizer funded the trial and sells Xeljanz.

☠︎︎ Tofacitinib 10 mg BID in ORAL Surveillance raised adjudicated all-cause death vs TNF inhibitor (39/1,456 vs 17/1,451; HR 2.37, 95% CI 1.34–4.18) and pulmonary embolism (24 vs 3); the 10 mg arm was dose-reduced in February 2019 after the DSMB saw the PE and mortality excess (Ytterberg et al., NEJM, 2022).

📚 FDA’s 1 Sep 2021 Drug Safety Communication, updated Dec 2021, required Boxed Warnings for tofacitinib, baricitinib, and upadacitinib for serious heart-related events, cancer, blood clots, and death, and restricted approved use to TNF-inadequate responders, after reviewing the completed ORAL Surveillance RCT (FDA DSC, 2021, https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/fda-requires-warnings-about-increased-risk-serious-heart-related-events-cancer-blood-clots-and-death).

📚 XELJANZ (tofacitinib) is labeled for moderately to severely active RA after TNF-blocker failure at 5 mg BID or XR 11 mg daily, with that boxed warning (Pfizer DailyMed, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/lookup.cfm?setid=cf74ba2f-afc5-4baa-8594-979c889a5831).

📚 Off-label weekly rapamycin as an “inflammaging” drug already failed the endpoints that would make that slogan true, in writeups already filed under hallmark 06: PEARL (NCT04488601) 48-week RCT, 114 completers 50–85 y, compounded rapamycin 5 or 10 mg/week, primary visceral adiposity null (P=0.942); RAPA-EX-01 ITT 30-s chair-stand −2.13 reps vs placebo (95% CI −4.61 to 0.34, P=0.089) with more AEs (99 vs 63) and one possibly drug-related SAE pneumonia; Kaeberlein survey mouth-ulceration 14.7% vs 4.7% (FDR P=0.0081) (Moel et al., Aging, 2025; Stanfield et al., J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, 2026; Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023; already in hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md). AgelessRx sells the PEARL drug.

📚 Six weeks of everolimus (RAD001) 0.5 mg/day or 5 mg/week in 218 adults ≥65 y raised influenza-vaccine response about 20% and reduced PD-1+ T cells (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3009892, PMID 25540326; already in hallmark 06). Novartis-sponsored. Immune surrogate, not lifespan.

📚 RTB101 10 mg daily for 16 winter weeks in 1024 adults ≥65 y did not cut clinically symptomatic respiratory illness versus placebo (26% vs 25%; OR 1.07, 90% CI 0.80–1.42, P=0.65); the indication was dropped (Mannick et al., Lancet Healthy Longev, 2021; already in hallmark 06).

📚 White 2019 is the curcumin systematic null on the markers the stack claims to move: 19 RCTs, turmeric n=356 and curcuminoids n=988, 4–16 weeks; vs control, CRP MD −2.71 mg/L (95% CI −5.73 to 0.31, P=0.08), hsCRP MD −1.44 mg/L (−2.94 to 0.06, P=0.06), IL-1β MD −4.25 pg/mL (−13.32 to 4.82, P=0.36), IL-6 MD −0.71 pg/mL (−1.68 to 0.25, P=0.15), TNF-α MD −1.23 pg/mL (−3.01 to 0.55, P=0.18) (White, Pasupuleti, Roman, Li, Hernández, Pharmacol Res, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.phrs.2019.104280, PMID 31108175).

🥼 Later curcumin meta-analyses report CRP/IL-6/TNF drops (Ferguson 2021 Nutrition Reviews WMD CRP −1.55 mg/L; Dehzad 2023 I²=98.9% for CRP) while White 2019 and disease-restricted pools do not; none of those papers is a healthspan or mortality RCT, and Life Extension / RegenStep sell the capsule they are citing (White et al., 2019; Ferguson et al., Nutr Rev, 2021, PMID 34378053).

📚 Kuszewski 2020 is a clinic-stack-shaped RCT that missed its primary: 16-week 2×2, 152 sedentary overweight/obese adults 50–80 y, fish oil (2 g DHA + 400 mg EPA/day) ± curcumin 160 mg/day; cerebrovascular responsiveness to hypercapnia unchanged; curcumin alone or added to fish oil did not move the vascular or cardiometabolic endpoints the authors highlighted (Kuszewski et al., Nutr Metab Cardiovasc Dis, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.numecd.2019.12.010, PMID 32127336).

📚 STRENGTH is the high-dose marine-oil outcomes null: 13,078 statin-treated high-CV-risk patients, omega-3 carboxylic acid 4 g/day vs corn oil, median ~42 months, stopped for futility; primary MACE 785/6,539 (12.0%) vs 795/6,539 (12.2%), HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.90–1.09, P=0.84); GI AEs 24.7% vs 14.7%; baseline median hsCRP 2.1 mg/L (Nicholls et al., JAMA, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2020.22258, PMID 33190147). AstraZeneca’s Epanova. OTC fish-oil / SPM “resolution” stacks are not this trial and are not an aging trial.

⛔ Qualia-funded open-label proteomics found no significant change in pre-specified primary SASP proteins (TNF, IL-1β, CXCL8, VEGFA; P>0.38; 53 paired of 71 enrolled) while Qualia sells Qualia Senolytic (1,400 mg fisetin + stack) as if Mayo-style pulses were the human result (already filed under hallmarks/08; Blomquist et al., IJMS, 2026; NCT06953518).

📚 Authors do not recommend CHIP-mutation screening of unselected individuals; there is no evidence base for managing the associated complications (Libby et al., JACC, 2019; already filed under hallmarks/01).

🐉 No compiled source is a completed human RCT whose primary endpoint is interrupting sterile age-associated inflammatory tone in people without residual-risk CAD or a billed inflammatory disease and then extending disability-free survival or all-cause survival (flight search result; absence is not a published disproof of the mechanism).

## Measurement

📚 Pearson 2003 AHA/CDC hsCRP bands are residual atherosclerotic-risk tertiles, not an inflammaging clock (Pearson et al., Circulation, 2003).

📚 Otvos et al. defined GlycA as an NMR signal from N-acetyl methyl groups on circulating acute-phase glycoproteins; in MESA (N=5,537 baseline spectra) GlycA correlated with hsCRP r=0.56, fibrinogen r=0.46, IL-6 r=0.35; intra-individual CV 4.3% over 5 weekly draws in 23 healthy volunteers vs 29.2% for hsCRP (Otvos et al., Clin Chem, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1373/clinchem.2014.232918, PMID 25779987). Lower noise is not a treat-to-target license.

📚 Rasmussen, Petersen, and Eugen-Olsen argue suPAR is a candidate SCI marker because it is upregulated by immune activation, correlates with CRP/IL-6/TNF, and is less swingy than acute-phase proteins; the 2021 paper is a hypothesis/review, not an assay that restores aging (Rasmussen et al., Front Immunol, 2021, https://doi.org/10.3389/fimmu.2021.780641, PMID 34925360).

📚 Pounis et al. built INFLA-score in Moli-sani (N=5,948 women + 5,965 men, age ≥35) as a composite of CRP, WBC, platelet count, and granulocyte-to-lymphocyte ratio, scored by deciles to a range of −16 to +16 (Pounis et al., Thromb Haemost, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1160/TH15-06-0487, PMID 26353847).

📚 Liu / Li et al. applied a weighted INFLA-score (CRP + WBC + platelets + NLR) in UK Biobank (N=448,574; median follow-up 12.65 y; 23,784 premature deaths) and reported highest vs lowest INFLA associated with age-related hospitalization HR 1.19 (1.17–1.21) and premature death HR 1.58 (1.52–1.64), plus 2.96–4.14 fewer years of life expectancy at age 60 (Liu et al., BMC Public Health, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-024-18888-x). Composite-marker association, not a treat-to-target protocol.

⛔ A blood SASP multiplex, CRP, or IL-6 is not a senescent-cell census and is not an inflammaging clock (already filed: hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/sources/⛔/SASP-clock-is-not-a-census.md).

📚 Blood mutation or CHIP VAF assays are tissue-specific clone ecology; they do not automatically represent brain, plaque, synovium, or marrow inflammatory tone (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014; Steensma et al., Blood, 2015; already filed under hallmarks/01).

🐉 No compiled source validates a whole-body consumer inflammaging score with treat-to-target outcome data (flight search result; inference from Pearson bands + CRP MR + ZEUS topline + Qualia SASP null).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient, source-selective interruption of NLRP3 / IL-1β in TET2-CHIP carriers — CANTOS post hoc HR 0.38, interaction P=0.14 — might prevent first atherosclerotic events without continuous cytokine blockade in everyone; that is a hope sitting on a non-significant interaction, not a CHIP-stratified RCT (inference from Svensson 2022 + Fuster 2017 + CANTOS infection cost).

🐉 ZEUS’s null is population- or node-specific (ASCVD+CKD, IL-6 ligand) rather than a general failure of inflammatory causality; HERMES/ARTEMIS could still move HF or post-AMI events. Also a hope. The inverse — that any CRP collapse equals aging treated — is already falsified at topline (inference from Novo 6-K 2026 + IL6R MR 2012 + CANTOS 2017).

🐉 Human SPM deficiency plus a resolution-pathway drug that improves function is mechanistically coherent from Serhan’s lipid-mediator program and is not a quantified aging lesion with a completed restore result (inference from Serhan 2014 + Life Extension precursor SKU + STRENGTH null).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body inflammaging score — tissue plus blood, CHIP plus SASP plus resolution tone, sampled from more than one hsCRP — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation project, not a curcumin stack (inference from Pearson 2003 + CRP MR 2011 + ZEUS 2026 + SASP-clock ⛔).

🐉 Intermittent senomorphic rapamycin or oral NLRP3 inhibition might unload SASP/CHIP-IL-1 without locking in the ORAL Surveillance / CANTOS infection-and-clot bill; PEARL, RAPA-EX-01, and CANTOS infection deaths are why that remains a leap (inference from Laberge 2015 + hallmark 06 human nulls + Ridker 2017).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Blood inflammatory markers rise, on average, with age and predict death, CVD, frailty, and physical performance; extreme-old inflammation scores beat telomere length for those outcomes in Arai’s Japanese cohorts (Ferrucci 2005; Cesari 2004; Varadhan 2014; Arai 2015; Furman 2019).

📚 Human genetics and CANTOS establish that the IL-1β–IL-6-receptor axis can be causal for atherosclerotic events in residual-risk populations; CRP itself is not causal in the 2011 IPD Mendelian randomization (Ridker 2017; IL6R Consortium 2012; CRP CHD Genetics Collaboration 2011).

🥼 Whether interrupting “inflammation” as one knob treats aging is a live paper fight: CANTOS yes on MACE / no on death, CIRT null, ZEUS IL-6-ligand topline null, COLCOT/LoDoCo2 vs CLEAR SYNERGY, ASPREE null-plus-bleed, CANOPY CRP drop without NSCLC benefit (Ridker 2017; Ridker 2019; Novo 2026; Tardif 2019; Nidorf 2020; Jolly 2025; McNeil 2018; Garon 2023).

🐉 No compiled human trial lowered sterile age-associated inflammatory tone in people without residual-risk CAD or a billed inflammatory disease and then extended healthspan (flight search result).

🤔 People are buying hsCRP dashboards, curcumin/SPM stacks, off-label colchicine next to weekly sirolimus, and ozone “immune reset” drips under an inflammaging slogan anyway (HealthRX; Function; InsideTracker; SiPhox; RegenStep; Life Extension; Advanced Vitality; AgeRejuvenation; Rapamycin News).

☠︎︎ The chronic-anti-inflammatory-as-longevity stack has a hard-endpoint record of fatal infection (CANTOS, CANOPY-2), thrombosis and heart-failure (APPROVe), major hemorrhage and excess death (ASPREE), and JAK-class MACE/malignancy/PE/death versus TNF blockers (ORAL Surveillance) (Ridker 2017; Paz-Ares 2021; Bresalier 2005; McNeil 2018; Ytterberg 2022).
