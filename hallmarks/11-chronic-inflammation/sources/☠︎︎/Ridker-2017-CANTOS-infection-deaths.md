https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1707914
PMID: 28845751
NCT01327846
Used for: ☠︎︎ CANTOS recorded more fatal infection or sepsis on canakinumab than placebo (0.31 vs 0.18 / 100 person-years, P=0.02).
Mark: ☠︎︎
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Same 10,061-person, 3.7-year trial. Fatal infection/sepsis incidence 0.31 vs 0.18 / 100 py, P=0.02, pooled canakinumab vs placebo. Deaths from infection tended to be older and more often diabetic. Six confirmed TB cases, rates similar (0.06% each arm); five in India, one in Taiwan. Neutropenia and thrombocytopenia more common on drug.
