# Svensson et al., 2022. TET2-Driven Clonal Hematopoiesis and Response to Canakinumab: An Exploratory Analysis of the CANTOS Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA Cardiol.
https://doi.org/10.1001/jamacardio.2022.0386 PMID 35385050
Used for: 📚 In an exploratory CANTOS genomic substudy, targeted sequencing of 3,923 baseline samples found CHIP in 338 (8.6%), with TET2 more common than DNMT3A (103 vs 85 patients); TET2-CHIP carriers on pooled canakinumab had MACE HR 0.38 (95% CI 0.15–0.96) vs TET2-CHIP on placebo, with a non-significant interaction vs others (P=0.14).
