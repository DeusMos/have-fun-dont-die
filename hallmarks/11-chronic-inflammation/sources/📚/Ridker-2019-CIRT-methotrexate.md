# Ridker et al., 2019. Low-Dose Methotrexate for the Prevention of Atherosclerotic Events. N Engl J Med.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1809798
PMID: 30415610
NCT01594333
Used for: 📚 CIRT: methotrexate 15–20 mg weekly in 4,786 stable-atherosclerosis patients did not lower IL-1β, IL-6, or hsCRP and did not cut events (final primary HR 0.96, 0.79–1.16); stopped for futility at median 2.3 years.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=4786 randomized (2391 MTX, 2395 placebo) after open-label run-in; prior MI or multivessel CAD plus T2D or MetS; median age 66; median hsCRP at randomization 1.6 mg/L; median 2.3 years (max 5). Final primary 201 vs 207 (4.13 vs 4.31 / 100 py; HR 0.96, 95% CI 0.79–1.16, P=0.67). Original MACE HR 1.01 (0.82–1.25). All-cause death 96 vs 83 (HR 1.16, 0.87–1.56). NHLBI-funded.
