# Ferrucci et al., 2005. The origins of age-related proinflammatory state. Blood.
https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2004-07-2599 PMID 15572589
Used for: 📚 In the InCHIANTI population sample, older age associated with higher circulating IL-6, IL-1ra, IL-18, CRP, and fibrinogen in both sexes (sIL-6R rose with age only in men); the age coefficients shrank after adjustment for cardiovascular risk factors and morbidity, and a low-risk no-CVD subgroup of 51 men and 45 women still showed the age pattern.
