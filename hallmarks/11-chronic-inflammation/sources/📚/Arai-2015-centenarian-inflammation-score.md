# Arai et al., 2015. Inflammation, But Not Telomere Length, Predicts Successful Ageing at Extreme Old Age: A Longitudinal Study of Semi-supercentenarians. EBioMedicine.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2015.07.029 PMID 26629551 PMC4634197
