# Jolly et al., 2025. Colchicine in Acute Myocardial Infarction. N Engl J Med.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2405922
PMID: 39555823
NCT03048825
Used for: 📚 CLEAR SYNERGY: colchicine after MI did not cut the composite primary (HR 0.99, 0.85–1.16) over median 3 years despite a 3-month CRP drop of −1.28 mg/L.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=7062 at 104 centers in 14 countries; 2×2 factorial with spironolactone; colchicine n=3528, placebo n=3534. Primary (CV death, recurrent MI, stroke, unplanned ischemia-driven revascularization) 322 (9.1%) vs 327 (9.3%); HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.85–1.16, P=0.93). CRP LS-mean difference −1.28 mg/L (95% CI −1.81 to −0.75) at 3 months. Diarrhea 10.2% vs 6.6% (P<0.001). Serious infections similar. CIHR and others funded.
Conflict if any: Investigator-initiated. Not a longevity-clinic product paper.
