# Furman et al., 2019. Chronic inflammation in the etiology of disease across the life span. Nat Med.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0675-0 PMID 31806905 PMC7147972
Used for: 📚 Furman et al. frame systemic chronic inflammation (SCI) as a life-span disease etiology: intermittent inflammation is required for injury/infection, while persistent SCI is linked to CVD, cancer, diabetes, CKD, NAFLD, autoimmunity, and neurodegeneration, and they attribute >50% of worldwide deaths to inflammation-related diseases.
