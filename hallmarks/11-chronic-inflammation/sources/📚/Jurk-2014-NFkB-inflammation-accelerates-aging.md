# Jurk et al., 2014. Chronic inflammation induces telomere dysfunction and accelerates ageing in mice. Nat Commun.
https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5172 PMID 24960204
Used for: 📚 Chronic progressive low-grade inflammation from nfkb1 knockout (constitutive NF-κB) reduced mean and maximum mouse lifespan (Fig. 2a; exact days not stated in main text), made 36-week knockouts resemble ≥104-week wild-type on aging-biomarker tables, cut liver and gut regeneration, and accumulated telomere-dysfunctional senescent cells; ibuprofen 50 mg/kg/day (4–8 weeks) or dietary BHA 0.7% w/w (4 weeks) rescued telomere-associated foci and regenerative potential.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: nfkb1−/− vs WT C57BL/6. Observational 12–132 weeks; treatments 4–8 weeks. Arrays typically n=4/group. Lifespan days are figure-only. Authors did not attempt lifespan rescue (chronic-NSAID toxicity).
