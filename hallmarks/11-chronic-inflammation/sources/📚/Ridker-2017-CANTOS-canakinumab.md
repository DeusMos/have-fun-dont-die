# Ridker et al., 2017. Antiinflammatory Therapy with Canakinumab for Atherosclerotic Disease. NEJM.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1707914 PMID 28845751 NCT01327846
Used for: 📚 In CANTOS, canakinumab 150 mg s.c. every 3 months in 10,061 post-MI patients with hsCRP ≥2 mg/L cut the primary MACE composite vs placebo (HR 0.85, 95% CI 0.74–0.98, P=0.021; 3.86 vs 4.50 events/100 person-years) at median 3.7 years without lowering LDL; 150 mg also cut MACE plus urgent-revascularization unstable angina (HR 0.83, 95% CI 0.73–0.95, P=0.005); all-cause mortality was neutral (pooled HR 0.94); fatal infection/sepsis rose (0.31 vs 0.18 /100 person-years, P=0.02).
