# Varadhan et al., 2014. Simple Biologically Informed Inflammatory Index of Two Serum Cytokines Predicts 10 Year All-Cause Mortality in Older Adults. J Gerontol A Biol Sci Med Sci.
https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glt023 PMID 23689826
Used for: 📚 A two-marker inflammation index (IL-6 + soluble TNF-α receptor-1) was the best of 15 NF-κB-pathway markers for 10-year all-cause mortality in Cardiovascular Health Study after age/sex/education/race/smoking/BMI adjustment (HR 1.62, 95% CI 1.54–1.70) and validated in InCHIANTI (HR 1.33, 95% CI 1.17–1.52).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Discovery 1,155 InCHIANTI (15 markers) → 5,600 CHS → validation InCHIANTI. Endpoint=10-year all-cause death. Index is IL-6 + sTNFR1, not a treat-to-target longevity clock.
