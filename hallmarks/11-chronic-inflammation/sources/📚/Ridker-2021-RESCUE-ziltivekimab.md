# Ridker et al., 2021. IL-6 inhibition with ziltivekimab in patients at high atherosclerotic risk (RESCUE): a double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled, phase 2 trial. Lancet.
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)00520-1 PMID 34015342 NCT03926117
Used for: 📚 In RESCUE, monthly ziltivekimab (IL-6 ligand mAb) 7.5 / 15 / 30 mg vs placebo in CKD plus hsCRP ≥2 mg/L collapsed median hsCRP at 12 weeks by 77% / 88% / 92% vs 4% placebo (pairwise differences −66.2% / −77.7% / −87.8%, all P<0.0001), stable through 24 weeks, with parallel drops in fibrinogen, SAA, haptoglobin, sPLA2, and Lp(a); no MACE endpoint.
