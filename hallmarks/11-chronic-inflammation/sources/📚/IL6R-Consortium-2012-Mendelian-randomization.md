# IL6R Genetics Consortium / Swerdlow et al., 2012. The interleukin-6 receptor as a target for prevention of coronary heart disease: a mendelian randomisation analysis. Lancet.
https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(12)60110-X PMID 22421340 PMC3316968
Used for: 📚 An IL6R SNP (rs7529229 marking p.Asp358Ala) that mimics tocilizumab-like IL-6-receptor blockade raised circulating IL-6 +9.45% per allele (95% CI 8.34–10.57), lowered CRP −8.35% (7.31–9.38) and fibrinogen −0.85% (0.60–1.10), and associated with lower CHD odds (per-allele OR 0.95, 95% CI 0.93–0.97, p=1.53×10−5).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Up to 133,449 in 40 biomarker studies; 25,458 CHD cases / 100,740 controls. Lifelong genotype. IL6R signaling ≠ IL-6 ligand titer ≠ CRP-as-cause.
Conflict if any: Consortium academic. Tocilizumab comparison is to Roche’s RA drug, not a trial of that drug for CHD.
