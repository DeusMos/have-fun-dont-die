# Tardif et al., 2019. Efficacy and Safety of Low-Dose Colchicine after Myocardial Infarction. NEJM.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1912388 PMID 31733140 NCT02551094
Used for: 📚 In COLCOT, colchicine 0.5 mg daily started within 30 days after MI cut the composite of CV death, resuscitated arrest, MI, stroke, or urgent angina hospitalization leading to revascularization (5.5% vs 7.1%; HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.61–0.96, P=0.02) over median 22.6 months; pneumonia SAE 0.9% vs 0.4% (P=0.03).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=4,745 (2,366 vs 2,379). Recent-MI secondary prevention. Median 22.6 mo. Disease events, not aging or healthy-person healthspan.
Conflict if any: Government of Quebec and others. Colchicine 0.5 mg later gained an FDA residual-risk label (Lodoco); not counted here as a longevity license.
