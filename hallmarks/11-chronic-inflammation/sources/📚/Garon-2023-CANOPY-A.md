# Garon et al., 2023. Canakinumab as Adjuvant Therapy in Patients With Completely Resected Non–Small-Cell Lung Cancer: Results From the CANOPY-A Double-Blind, Randomized Clinical Trial. J Clin Oncol.
https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.23.00910
PMID: 37788412
NCT03447769
Used for: 📚 CANOPY-A missed DFS (HR 0.94, 0.78–1.14, one-sided P=0.258) after surgery + adjuvant cisplatin; CRP/IL-6 fell and did not track outcomes. CANTOS exploratory lung-cancer signal failed this operationalization.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Phase 3; 1,382 randomized of ~1,500 planned; stage II–IIIA or IIIB (T>5 cm N2) completely resected NSCLC after adjuvant cisplatin. Canakinumab 200 mg SC q3w vs placebo, up to 18 cycles. Median DFS 35.0 vs 29.7 months; HR 0.94 (95% CI 0.78–1.14); one-sided P=0.258. OS not formally tested. Terminated after primary analysis; no new safety signals per investigators. Novartis.
