# Opstal et al., 2023. Drivers of mortality in patients with chronic coronary disease in the low-dose colchicine 2 trial. Int J Cardiol.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijcard.2022.12.026
PMID: 36535561
Used for: 🥼 LoDoCo2’s non-CV death HR 1.51 was not pinned to cancer, infection, or a single mechanism; authors treated the split as chance plus comorbidity. Live safety ambiguity, not a closed harm call.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Same 5522-person LoDoCo2 cohort; 133 deaths (2.4%) at median 28.6 months. CV deaths 20 vs 25 (HR 0.80, 0.44–1.44). Non-CV 53 vs 35 (HR 1.51, 0.99–2.31). Cancer 26 vs 22; end-stage pulmonary 9 vs 4; infection 4 vs 4; dementia 4 vs 1; MOF 3 vs 2. Age >65 was the only independent baseline predictor of non-CV death (HR 3.65, 2.06–6.47).
