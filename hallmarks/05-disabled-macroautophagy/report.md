## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that macroautophagy becomes disabled with ordinary human aging, that disablement causally drives aging, and that restoring flux — scored and sold as a fasting hour, a ketone, a blood LC3/p62 or SNP panel, or an “autophagy stack” — makes people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity brands, fasting apps, and clinics sell that claim as a product: spermidineLIFE CelVio 1–2 mg/day, Primeadine 1 mg, DoNotAge Pure Spermidine 8 mg, FormBlends spermidine 3–5 mg on 18:6/20:4 “autophagy days” plus monthly 24–36 h fasts, Siim Land 18–20 h minimum / 36–48 h “deeper cleanup,” Timeline Mitopure 500–1000 mg as cellular recycling, ProLon 5-day FMD “autophagy on day 3,” Buchinger Wilhelmi ≥10-day therapeutic fasts, and 16:8 app copy that autophagy “reliably crosses” a threshold at 16 hours (spermidineLIFE product page; Oxford Healthspan Primeadine; DoNotAge Pure Spermidine; FormBlends Protocol 2026; Longevity Kitchens Siim Land writeup; Timeline Mitopure; ProLon; Buchinger Wilhelmi; Fasted app). Longevity Labs, Oxford Healthspan, DoNotAge, FormBlends, Timeline/Amazentis, L-Nutra, Buchinger, and Fasted sell the products or stays they are citing.

📚 López-Otín et al. parked autophagy under loss of proteostasis in 2013 and split disabled macroautophagy into its own hallmark in 2023 because organelles, lipids, pathogens, and lysosomal capacity are not just folding; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that ordinary human flux is the master clock, or as a license to sell a cleanup capsule or a 16-hour switch, is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Nobel-era mechanism, mouse Atg5 restore and Beclin-1 longevity alleles, a working PBMC chloroquine-flux assay, SmartAge’s existence, urolithin A muscle/mito RCTs, mTORC1 restraint of ULK1 — into “buy the stack / fast until the clock says on” (Cassidy et al., Nature Communications, 2020; Fernández et al., Nature, 2018; Bensalem et al., Autophagy, 2021; Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022; Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019).

## What the words mean

💯 Macroautophagy packages cytoplasmic cargo inside a double-membrane autophagosome and delivers it to a lysosome for degradation (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2020.1797280, PMID 33634751, PMC7996087; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 Microautophagy and chaperone-mediated autophagy are other machines; mitophagy, aggrephagy, lipophagy, and xenophagy are cargo programs, not the whole pathway (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 Flux is cargo entering, traversing, and exiting the pathway over time; static LC3-II, LC3 puncta, or p62 can rise because formation increased or because the lysosome is jammed, and the 4th-edition guidelines require a lysosomal perturbation or complementary kinetic evidence to call flux (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021).

📚 In the 2023 hallmark paper, disabled macroautophagy is the logistics/lysosome bag split off from proteostasis (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 Clinic English uses “autophagy,” “cellular cleanup,” or “hallmark 05” for a spermidine capsule, a Mitopure softgel, a 16:8 timer, a 5-day FMD kit, or a Buchinger stay (spermidineLIFE; Timeline; Fasted; ProLon; Buchinger Wilhelmi).

⛔ Collapsing selective mitophagy, mTOR pharmacology, ketone readings, and SNP panels into one “autophagy score” is a vocabulary collision: urolithin A’s published human work is a Parkin-axis / mitochondrial program (hallmark 07), rapamycin is a pleiotropic mTORC1 inhibitor (hallmark 06), and ketones are not ΔLC3B-II (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019; Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022; Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021).

📚 Hydroxychloroquine and chloroquine block lysosomal degradation of autophagosome cargo; they are the cancer-trial autophagy *inhibitors* and the flux-assay reagent, not activator-stack drugs (Chude and Amaravadi, International Journal of Molecular Sciences, 2017, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms18061279, PMID 28621712, PMC5486101; Mohsen et al., Current Oncology, 2022, https://doi.org/10.3390/curroncol29030141, PMID 35323350, PMC8946954; Bensalem et al., Autophagy, 2021).

⛔ A universal human on-switch at hour 16 is not a supported operationalization: no flux-controlled human biopsy shows a whole-body kick-on at 16 h, and 12–24 h vastus-lateralis marker data are null or down (Chaudhary et al., Nutrition, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nut.2022.111662, PMID 35660501; Dethlefsen et al., Journal of Applied Physiology, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.01146.2017, PMID 30161009; Satislent “12 to 16 hours… muscle biopsy studies confirm” copy, https://www.satislent.com/en/blog/longevity/autophagy-what-it-is-how-to-activate-it-and-how-many-hours-of-fasting-you-need).

## Mechanism

📚 Initiation is ULK1-dependent and tonically restrained by mTORC1 when nutrients are up; Beclin-1/VPS34 nucleates the phagophore; ATG conjugation lipidates LC3/GABARAP; receptors such as p62/SQSTM1 select cargo; autophagosomes fuse with lysosomes; TFEB/TFE3 transcribe lysosome-and-autophagy capacity (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 ATG proteins also run secretion, LC3-associated phagocytosis, immune signaling, and apoptosis jobs, so an Atg5 or Beclin-1 phenotype is not automatically a flux phenotype (Subramani and Malhotra, EMBO Reports, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/embor.2012.220; Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021).

📚 Human prefrontal-cortex autophagy-gene transcription is down-regulated in normal aging relative to young adult brain (Lipinski et al., PNAS, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1009485107, PMID 20660724, PMC2922576; cross-sectional transcriptome).

🥼 Whether ordinary human aging disables macroautophagy flux is not a single-direction result: brain ATG transcripts fall (Lipinski 2010) while the largest physiologic human flux series goes *up* with age in PBMCs (Bensalem 2023, Spearman r=0.2943); those are different tissues and different quantities (Lipinski et al., PNAS, 2010; Bensalem et al., GeroScience, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00884-5, PMID 37498479).

📚 Rubicon protein/transcript rose with age in *C. elegans*, female *Drosophila*, and 20- vs 2-month mouse kidney/liver, and reducing it extended worm and fly lifespan in an autophagy-dependent manner (Nakamura et al., Nature Communications, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-08729-6, PMID 30783089).

🥼 Rubicon is not a universal age-up brake: in mouse adipocytes Rubicon *falls* with age and autophagy rises, and adipocyte Rubicon knockout caused fat atrophy, glucose intolerance, dyslipidaemia, and hepatic fat via autophagic degradation of PPARγ coactivators (Yamamuro et al., Nature Communications, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-17879-x, PMID 32811819). Nakamura and Yamamuro share the Yoshimori orbit; this is paper vs paper, not a forum split.

📚 Autophagy can be tumor-suppressive early and tumor-promoting in established cancers; flux is hard to score in human tumors (Amaravadi, Kimmelman, and White, Genes & Development, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1101/gad.287524.116, PMID 27664235, PMC5066235).

📚 Excess autophagy can kill cells by autosis — Na+/K+-ATPase-dependent death induced by Tat-Beclin 1 in culture and seen in neonatal-rat hippocampal neurons after hypoxia–ischemia — a ceiling on “more flux is always better,” not a documented harm of 1–8 mg spermidine or 16:8 (Liu et al., PNAS, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1319661110, PMID 24277826, PMC3870705).

## Animal data

📚 Adult-onset Atg5 knockdown from 8 weeks produced kyphosis, greying (16/28), myeloid skew, muscle degeneration, senescence-marker rise, and median survival of ~6 months on dox (males 185 days, females 207 days); restoring Atg5 after 4 months dropped frailty over the next 4 months and extended median survival from 185 to 493 days from treatment start, still short of restored controls (p<0.01), with higher and earlier spontaneous tumors (p<0.001) (Cassidy et al., Nature Communications, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-14187-x, PMID 31949142; mixed C57BL/6×129 Atg5i; bone-marrow n=5–6/group).

📚 Ubiquitous Atg5 overexpression extended median mouse lifespan 17.2% (line 25 +119 days; max 781±22 vs 900±34 days, p<0.01; males +120 d, females +117 d; further lines +113, +100, +130 days) with leanness and better insulin sensitivity (Pyo et al., Nature Communications, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms3300, PMID 23939249; both sexes; survival headcount not stated in the open text; lifelong overexpression).

📚 Becn1 F121A knock-in disrupted beclin 1–BCL2 binding, raised basal flux, and extended median lifespan from 26 to 29 months (females 27→30, males 25→28) with less 20-month renal/cardiac pathology and less spontaneous tumorigenesis (Fernández et al., Nature, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0162-7, PMID 29849149, PMC5992097; kidney n=20 WT/26 KI, heart n=19/26 at 20 months; flux blots n=3/genotype).

📚 In 20-month-old Becn1 F121A mice, flux stayed higher than wild-type in heart, kidney, and skeletal muscle but not liver; muscle-fiber atrophy was reduced and hepatic aging phenotypes were not (Sebti et al., Autophagy, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2022.2111852, PMID 35993407, PMC9530577).

📚 Rubicon-knockout mice had less age-associated kidney fibrosis and less brain α-synuclein; the paper did not establish a mouse lifespan curve (Nakamura et al., Nature Communications, 2019).

📚 Spermidine induced autophagy and extended lifespan in yeast, flies, and worms; loss of ATG7 (yeast/fly) or Beclin-1 (worm) abolished the longevity gain (Eisenberg et al., Nature Cell Biology, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb1975, PMID 19801973).

📚 Oral spermidine (0.3 mM lifelong from 4 months, or 3 mM from 18 months) extended C57BL/6 median lifespan by ~10% when started late, improved diastolic function, and failed to protect Atg5-null cardiomyocytes (Eisenberg et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4222, PMID 27841876; Madeo and Eisenberg later disclosed equity in The Longevity Labs, which sells spermidineLIFE).

⛔ Reading Cassidy/Pyo/Fernández/Nakamura as “ordinary human aging has been slowed by restoring flux,” or as a product result, fails the designs: developmental or inducible mouse genetics, tissue-restricted rescue, and ATG-independent jobs (Cassidy 2020; Pyo 2013; Fernández 2018; Subramani 2013).

## Human data

📚 Whole-blood chloroquine (150 µM, 60 min, 37 °C) plus PBMC LC3B-II ELISA is a working human macroautophagy flux assay; ex vivo leucine+insulin changed the signal (Bensalem et al., Autophagy, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2020.1846302, PMID 33155519; Bensalem and Sargeant are inventors on related flux-assay patents).

📚 In 114 adults at elevated T2D risk (mean age 58.4, BMI 34.6), basal PBMC ΔLC3B-II correlated *positively* with age (Spearman r=0.2943, P=0.0015; multivariate β=2.44 ng LC3B-II/mg protein/h per year after sex/BMI/AUSDRISK/SBP, P=0.0189) after a 12 h overnight fast (Bensalem et al., GeroScience, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00884-5, PMID 37498479; NCT03689608 baseline).

📚 Six months of iTRE (30% energy by noon, then 20 h fast, 3 non-consecutive days/week) produced a post-hoc higher change-from-baseline in PBMC LC3B-II flux versus standard care (adjusted difference 59.83 [4.59, 115.06], P=0.04); the within-iTRE rise was not significant, CR vs SC was not (P=0.38), 2-month between-group was null, and the authors note the contrast may be driven partly by an SC decline (Bensalem et al., Journal of Physiology, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1113/JP287938, PMID 40345145; N=121 randomized, 6-month flux n SC=20 / CR=35 / iTRE=35; parent Teong 2023 endpoints were glucose/lipids, not aging).

📚 Twelve-month spermidine-rich wheat-germ extract at 0.9 mg/day did not beat placebo on the prespecified primary memory endpoint in older adults with subjective cognitive decline (adjusted MST difference −0.03, 95% CI −0.11 to 0.05, P=0.47); ITT secondaries were also null (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.13875, PMID 35616942, PMC9136623; N=100 ITT, 89 completers, mean age 69; capsules from The Longevity Labs).

📚 Madeo, Eisenberg, Sigrist, and Stekovic disclosed equity, advisory, CEO, and/or patent ties to The Longevity Labs on SmartAge and related spermidine papers; TLL sells the extract they cite (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022; Madeo et al., Autophagy, 2018, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6287690/, PMID 30306826; Hofer et al., Nature Aging, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00322-9).

📚 Human vastus lateralis after 12–24 h intermittent fasting did not activate autophagy markers; IF70 after a 12 h overnight fast lowered BECN1, SQSTM1, and LAMP2 mRNA, and a 24 h fast raised SQSTM1 (Chaudhary et al., Nutrition, 2022, PMID 35660501; 50 women, 51±2 y, BMI 31.8±4.3; 24 h fast 3 d/wk × 8 wk at 70% or 100% energy).

📚 Thirty-six-hour fasting only modestly moved static autophagy mediators in human muscle, and trained subjects did not show the LC3/p62 drop seen in untrained subjects (Dethlefsen et al., Journal of Applied Physiology, 2018, PMID 30161009; N=7 untrained + 6 trained men, age ~27–28; biopsies at 2/12/24/36 h).

📚 A 72-hour fast raised muscle LC3B-II ~30% and p62 ~10% in the same biopsies, so the authors could not tell induction from jammed degradation (Vendelbo et al., PLoS ONE, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0102031, PMID 25020061; N=8 healthy men, crossover; no lysosomal-inhibitor flux assay).

📚 Four-day early time-restricted feeding (eat 08:00–14:00) in 11 overweight adults raised morning whole-blood LC3A mRNA +22±5% (p=0.001); that is transcript inventory, not flux (Jamshed et al., Nutrients, 2019, https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11061234, PMID 31151279, PMC6616534).

📚 Four weeks of oral urolithin A 500–1000 mg/day in sedentary elderly changed skeletal-muscle mitochondrial transcripts and lowered plasma acylcarnitines (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-019-0073-4, PMID 32694802; Part B N=36, 9/arm, 28 days; Amazentis/Timeline sells Mitopure).

📚 Four months of Mitopure urolithin A 500 or 1000 mg/day in 88 overweight middle-aged adults enriched Parkin-pathway / mitophagy-linked muscle proteins and raised some strength/endurance secondaries (~12% hamstring at 500 mg, p=0.027) but missed the primary peak-power endpoint (Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633; N=88, 79 completers, 40–64 y).

📚 PBMCs from older adults failed to raise LC3-II after rapamycin plus bafilomycin the way young PBMCs did (McCormick et al., Physiology International, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1556/2060.105.2018.3.20, PMID 30269563, PMC12159423; young 21.8±1.9 vs old 64.0±3.7 y; ex vivo culture, not in-vivo weekly dosing).

📚 Adding hydroxychloroquine 600 mg BID to gemcitabine/nab-paclitaxel did not improve 12-month overall survival in metastatic pancreatic cancer (41% vs 49%; median OS 11.1 vs 12.1 mo) despite a higher response rate (38.2% vs 21.1%, P=0.047) (Karasic et al., JAMA Oncology, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2019.0684, PMID 31120501, PMC6547080; N=112; open-label phase 2). That is oncology blocking flux, not aging restoring it.

🐉 No compiled human trial isolated restored macroautophagic flux as the cause of longer healthspan or lifespan (Phase 1 search log; absence is not a published disproof of the hallmark).

## Measurement

📚 Klionsky 2021 is the field’s flux-vs-inventory rulebook: no single static LC3/p62 blot is sufficient, and core ATG proteins are not a flux meter by themselves (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021, PMID 33634751).

📚 Bensalem 2021 is a research whole-blood CQ → PBMC LC3B-II method, not a marketed clinical score and not a brain/liver/muscle reading (Bensalem et al., Autophagy, 2021).

🤔 No consumer LC3/p62/Beclin-1 chloroquine-flux “autophagy age” panel was found for sale on 2026-09-02; FormBlends states direct autophagy measurement is not available to consumers and substitutes ketones >0.5 mmol/L, glucose, insulin, and hs-CRP (FormBlends Protocol 2026, https://formblends.com/articles/biohacking-hub/autophagy-protocols-protocol-2026; Phase 1 domain hunt).

⛔ Dante Labs sells a €29 genome add-on over autophagy-related SNPs framed as whether “endogenous cleansing mechanisms are intact”; that cannot score or titrate current macroautophagic throughput (Dante Labs Autophagy Report, https://dantelabs.com/products/autophagy-panel; saliva WGS add-on, >50 genes).

⛔ Ketones, CGM, hunger, or “hours fasted” are not flux (Klionsky et al., Autophagy, 2021; FormBlends proxy list; Fasted app 16-hour copy).

## Speculative

🐉 Late-life, tissue-restricted restoration of complete flux — not more autophagosomes dumped onto a jammed lysosome — might unload Cassidy-style frailty without the restore-then-tumor pattern; that leap has no completed healthy-person aging RCT (inference from Cassidy 2020 + Sebti 2023 organ-restricted flux + Amaravadi 2016 duality; no source for the human restoration step).

🐉 If aged solid organs are fusion- or digestion-limited rather than ULK1-limited, TFEB/lysosomal-capacity or RUBICON-maturation knobs could outperform rapamycin-as-autophagy; Yamamuro 2020 is why “kill Rubicon everywhere” is the wrong cartoon (inference from Nakamura 2019 vs Yamamuro 2020 + Klionsky jam rule).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body flux score — chloroquine-perturbed cargo turnover in the tissue you care about, not PBMC ΔLC3B-II, not ketones, not SNPs — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an assay-and-trial project, not a Dante Labs add-on (inference from Bensalem 2021 method + Klionsky 2021 + no consumer SKU).

🐉 Allele- or cargo-selective degraders (ATTEC/AUTOTAC/Tat-Beclin-class) that clear a named organelle or conformer without bulk catabolism are a mechanistic hope; delivery, tumor context, and autosis are the live engineering problems, and there is no compiled human aging n (inference from Shoji-Kawata 2013 + Liu 2013 + Békés/AUTOTAC pipeline already filed under hallmark 04).

🐉 Periodic, not chronic, activation might restore dynamic range without locking cells in autosis or feeding an occult clone; that is a schedule hypothesis, not a result (inference from Liu 2013 + Cassidy tumor duality + no human pulse-vs-chronic flux trial).

## Adjacent hallmarks

📚 Loss of proteostasis is the 2023-split sibling (hallmark 04): folding/UPS stay there; spermidine, fasting clocks, LC3 flux, and FormBlends/Siim Land autophagy protocols live here first (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; hallmarks/04-loss-of-proteostasis/report.md).

📚 mTOR, translation, rapamycin, and time-restricted eating as nutrient-sensing interventions sit in hallmark 06; autophagy-initiation release is one downstream branch, not the whole drug (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023).

📚 Urolithin A / Mitopure mitophagy is the hallmark 07 cargo overlap; a Parkin-axis muscle signature is not general macroautophagy restoration (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019; Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022).

📚 Retro RTR242 is already named in hallmark 03 as an oral autophagy/lysosome Phase 1, not an OSK protocol (ANZCTR 390484; hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md).

📚 Senescence-marker rise after adult Atg5 loss is the hallmark 08 overlap (Cassidy et al., Nature Communications, 2020).

📚 Inflammasome restraint and cytosolic-cargo innate signaling are the hallmark 11 adjacency (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Amaravadi et al., Genes & Development, 2016).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Adult Atg5 inhibition wrecks mice; Atg5 overexpression, Becn1 F121A, invert Rubicon knockdown, and autophagy-dependent spermidine extend life in model organisms; a human PBMC flux assay exists and can move (Cassidy 2020; Pyo 2013; Fernández 2018; Nakamura 2019; Eisenberg 2009/2016; Bensalem 2021/2025).

🥼 Ordinary human aging does not have a single flux direction: brain ATG transcripts down, PBMC flux up with age in a diabetes-risk cohort; Rubicon is up in some aged tissues and down in aged adipose (Lipinski 2010; Bensalem 2023; Nakamura 2019; Yamamuro 2020).

⛔ A 16-hour clock, a ketone, a Dante Labs SNP report, a spermidine capsule, Mitopure, a Buchinger stay, or “it is a hallmark” is not organismal macroautophagy restoration in healthy people. SmartAge missed MST. iTRE flux was post-hoc and blood-only. Urolithin A missed peak power and is mitophagy. Muscle 12–24 h markers did not switch on (Schwarz 2022; Bensalem 2025; Singh 2022; Chaudhary 2022; Satislent; Dante Labs; López-Otín 2023).

🤔 People are buying 1–8 mg spermidine, 500–1000 mg Mitopure, FMD kits, clinic fasts, trehalose, and weekly sirolimus under that slogan anyway (spermidineLIFE; Primeadine; DoNotAge; Timeline; ProLon; Buchinger; FormBlends; Kaeberlein 2023).

☠︎︎ Daily transplant-dose sirolimus has an established infection and pneumonitis body count; weekly geroscience exposure was not equated (FDA Rapamune 2013).
