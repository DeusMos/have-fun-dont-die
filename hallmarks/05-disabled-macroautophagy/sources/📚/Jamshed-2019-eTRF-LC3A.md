# Jamshed et al., 2019. Early Time-Restricted Feeding Improves 24-Hour Glucose Levels and Affects Markers of the Circadian Clock, Aging, and Autophagy in Humans. Nutrients.
https://doi.org/10.3390/nu11061234 PMID 31151279 PMC6627766
Used for: 4-day crossover eTRF (eat 08:00–14:00) vs 08:00–20:00 control. N=11 overweight adults. Morning whole-blood LC3A mRNA +22±5% (p=0.001); SIRT1 +10±3% (p=0.004). Inventory/transcript, not flux. Mean 24-h glucose −4±1 mg/dl.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=11, 4 days per arm, washout 3.5–5 weeks. Whole-blood gene expression, not tissue flux.
Conflict if any: Academic. Often cited as “16-hour autophagy on.” Eating window was 6 h (18 h overnight fast), not classic 16:8.
