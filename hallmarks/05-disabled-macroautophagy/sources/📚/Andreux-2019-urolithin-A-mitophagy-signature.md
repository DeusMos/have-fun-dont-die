# Andreux et al., 2019. The mitophagy activator urolithin A is safe and induces a molecular signature of improved mitochondrial and cellular health in humans. Nature Metabolism.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-019-0073-4 NCT02655393
Used for: 📚 Four weeks of oral urolithin A 500–1000 mg/day in sedentary elderly changed skeletal-muscle mitochondrial gene expression and lowered plasma acylcarnitines; the trial is framed as mitophagy / mitochondrial quality control, not general macroautophagy or healthspan.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Part A single dose n=24 (250–2000 mg or placebo). Part B 28-day MAD n=36 (9/arm: 250, 500, 1000 mg or placebo), healthy sedentary elderly. Primary: safety. Secondary: plasma acylcarnitines down; muscle mitochondrial transcript signature up, dose-dependent, FDR<0.25 at 28 days. No flux assay of bulk macroautophagy. No lifespan/healthspan endpoint.
