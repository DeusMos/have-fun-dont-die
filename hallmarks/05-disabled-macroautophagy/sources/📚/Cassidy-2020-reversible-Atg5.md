# Cassidy et al., 2020. Temporal inhibition of autophagy reveals segmental reversal of ageing with increased cancer risk. Nature Communications.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-14187-x PMID 31949142 PMC6965206
Used for: 📚 Adult-onset Atg5 knockdown in mice produced premature aging phenotypes and cut median survival to ~6 months on dox; restoring Atg5 after 4 months of inhibition reversed frailty and extended median survival from 185 to 493 days from treatment start, still short of controls, with more and earlier spontaneous tumors.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Mixed C57BL/6 × 129 Atg5i mice, dox from 8 weeks. LT-Atg5i median survival ~6 months on dox (males 185 d, females 207 d). Restoration cohort: dox 4 months then off; frailty score fell over next 4 months; median survival 493 vs 185 days since treatment began vs LT-Atg5i; still shorter than R-Control (p<0.01). Tumor frequency higher in R-Atg5i (p<0.001). Kyphosis largely irreversible (3/26 recovered). Greying 16/28 LT-Atg5i. Bone-marrow transplant n=5–6/group.
Conflict if any: Academic. Not a human trial.
