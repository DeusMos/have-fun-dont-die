# Klionsky et al., 2021. Guidelines for the use and interpretation of assays for monitoring autophagy (4th edition). Autophagy.
https://doi.org/10.1080/15548627.2020.1797280
Used for: Static LC3-II or p62 inventory is not flux; high LC3-II can mean more formation or a blocked lysosome, and the field guidelines require a perturbation or complementary evidence.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Consensus methods paper, Autophagy 17(1):1–382. Not a trial. States that no single assay is sufficient; LC3-II accumulation without a lysosomal block cannot distinguish induction from stalled degradation; multiple ATGs should be targeted genetically because ATGs have non-autophagic roles. This is the definition-fail paper for “more LC3 = autophagy on” clinic/blog copy and for ketone/hours-fasted meters.
Conflict if any: None. Multi-author guidelines.
