# Eisenberg et al., 2016. Cardioprotection and lifespan extension by the natural polyamine spermidine. Nature Medicine.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4222 PMID 27841876 PMC5806691
Used for: 📚 Oral spermidine in drinking water extended C57BL/6 mouse median lifespan (~10% when started late in life) and improved diastolic function; the cardiac benefit was lost in cardiomyocyte-specific Atg5 knockout mice.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Life-long (from 4 months, 0.3 mM for the lifespan estimate) and late-life (from 18 months, 3 mM) in drinking water. Late-life median lifespan +~10% (Breslow on Kaplan–Meier; exact cohort N in Supplementary Tables 1–2, not in the open HTML). Cardiomyocyte Atg5−/− vs Atg5+/+ treated from 1 month, function read at 16 weeks: spermidine reduced LV hypertrophy and raised Ees/VVC only in Atg5+/+. Dahl salt-sensitive rats: delayed hypertensive heart-failure progression. Human arm is food-frequency epidemiology (Bruneck), not a capsule RCT.
Conflict if any: Madeo/Eisenberg later associated with Longevity Labs / spermidineLIFE, which sells the wheat-germ extract used in Charité-adjacent human trials.
