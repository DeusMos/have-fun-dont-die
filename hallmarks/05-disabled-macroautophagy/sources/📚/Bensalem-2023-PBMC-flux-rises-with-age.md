# Bensalem et al., 2023. Basal autophagic flux measured in blood correlates positively with age in adults at increased risk of type 2 diabetes. GeroScience.
https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00884-5 PMID 37498479 PMC10643809
Used for: Basal autophagic flux in PBMCs, measured by chloroquine-blocked LC3B-II accumulation in whole blood, correlated positively with chronological age in adults at elevated diabetes risk, the opposite of “disabled = uniformly lower flux.”
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=114/119 (5 excluded for ELISA CV). Age 35–74, mean 58.4±10.2 y, 54% female, BMI 34.6±4.3, AUSDRISK ≥12, no diabetes. Overnight 12 h fast. ΔLC3B-II = LC3B-II(+CQ 150 µM, 1 h) − LC3B-II(−CQ). Spearman age r=0.2943, P=0.0015. Quartile trend P=0.0116 (66–74 y drove). Multivariate β=2.443 ng/mg protein/h per year (95% CI 0.41–4.48) after sex, BMI, AUSDRISK, SBP. Mean ΔLC3B-II 299.3±95.4. Cross-sectional baseline of NCT03689608.
