# Dethlefsen et al., 2018. Training state and skeletal muscle autophagy in response to 36 h of fasting. Journal of Applied Physiology.
https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.01146.2017
Used for: Thirty-six-hour fasting only modestly moved static autophagy mediators in human muscle, and trained subjects did not show the LC3/p62 drop seen in untrained subjects.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Healthy men, 7 untrained (VO2max <45) + 6 trained (VO2max >55), age 28±3 vs 27±4 y. Standardized meal then biopsies at 2, 12, 24, 36 h. Vastus lateralis Westerns: LC3I, LC3II, p62 reduced P<0.05 in untrained only. AKT/AMPK/ULK1/Beclin phosphorylation differed by training state. Authors: “skeletal muscle autophagy was only modestly affected in humans by 36 h of fasting.” Static inventory, no lysosomal-inhibitor flux. 12 h and 24 h timepoints do not show a human 16 h on-switch.
Conflict if any: None.
