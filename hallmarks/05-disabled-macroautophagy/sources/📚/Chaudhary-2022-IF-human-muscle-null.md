# Chaudhary et al., 2022. Intermittent fasting activates markers of autophagy in mouse liver, but not muscle from mouse or humans. Nutrition.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nut.2022.111662 PMID 35660501 Europe PMC 35660501
Used for: Human vastus lateralis after 12–24 h intermittent fasting did not activate autophagy markers; several transcripts fell after weight-loss IF, and a 24 h fast raised SQSTM1, the opposite of a 16-hour on-switch.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Mice: C57BL/6J males, chow or HFD 8 wk then AL vs IF (24 h fast, 3 d/wk) 8 wk, n=8–16/group; liver LC3-I/Map1lc3b up with fasting, muscle markers not activated. Humans: 50 women, 51±2 y, BMI 31.8±4.3; randomized IF70 or IF100 (24 h fast 3 d/wk, 70% or 100% energy) 8 wk. Vastus lateralis at 08:00 after 12 h and 24 h fasts. qPCR Map1lc3b/Becn1/Sqstm1/Lamp2; immunoblot LC3/BECLIN1/LAMP1. 24 h fast increased SQSTM1. IF70 after 12 h overnight fast: BECLIN1, SQSTM1, LAMP2 mRNA decreased. Authors: human muscle autophagy markers reduced, likely with weight loss. Static markers, not CQ-flux.
