# Bensalem et al., 2025. Intermittent time-restricted eating may increase autophagic flux in humans: an exploratory analysis. Journal of Physiology.
https://doi.org/10.1113/JP287938 PMID (Wiley; DOI above)
Used for: DIRECT-Fast4flux exploratory RCT substudy. iTRE (30% energy before noon + 20 h fast, 3 non-consecutive days/week) vs CR (70% energy daily) vs standard care, 6 months. iTRE vs SC change-from-baseline at 6 mo P=0.04 post hoc (adjusted difference 59.83 [4.59, 115.06]); no significant within-iTRE rise; CR vs SC ns (P=0.38). N=121 enrolled; flux n at 6 mo SC=20, CR=35, iTRE=35.
