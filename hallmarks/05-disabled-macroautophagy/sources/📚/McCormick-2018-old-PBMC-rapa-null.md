# McCormick et al., 2018. The effect of aging on the autophagic and heat shock response in human peripheral blood mononuclear cells. Physiology International.
https://doi.org/10.1556/2060.105.2018.3.20
Used for: PBMCs from older adults failed to raise LC3-II after rapamycin plus bafilomycin the way young PBMCs did, so “weekly sirolimus restores autophagy in old humans” is not supported by this assay.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Young 21.8±1.9 y vs old 64.0±3.7 y. Ex vivo PBMCs treated with rapamycin ± BAF. LC3-II protein rose after Rapa in young, not old. MAP1LC3B mRNA response also blunted in old. HSP70 protein unchanged; HSPA1A mRNA up only in young. Cultured cells in artificial media, not in-vivo weekly geroscience dosing. Complements Bensalem 2023 (basal flux up with age): the age lesion, if any, is stimulated not basal, and rapamycin does not obviously fix it in old PBMCs.
Conflict if any: None.
