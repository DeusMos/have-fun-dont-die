# Singh et al., 2022. Urolithin A improves muscle strength, exercise performance, and biomarkers of mitochondrial health in a randomized trial in middle-aged adults. Cell Reports Medicine.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633 PMID 35584623 PMC9133463 NCT03464500
Used for: 📚 Four months of Mitopure urolithin A (500 or 1000 mg/d) raised Parkin-pathway / mitophagy-linked proteins in skeletal muscle and improved some strength and endurance secondaries; peak power (primary) missed; this is selective mitophagy cargo, not general macroautophagy restoration.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=88 overweight sedentary adults 40–64 y, BMI 25–34.9, low VO2max; 79 completers. 500 mg or 1000 mg UA vs placebo, 4 months. Primary peak power: not significant. Hamstring strength ~12% at 500 mg (p=0.027). 1000 mg: aerobic endurance up to ~15% (p=0.03), gait speed +7% (p=0.004). Muscle proteomics: Parkin-mediated ubiquitin/proteasome pathway enriched at 500 mg; phospho-Parkin and mitophagy-related proteins up. BNIP3 down at 1000 mg. No LC3 flux / general autophagy assay.
Conflict if any: Amazentis (Timeline) manufactures Mitopure and funded/authored the trial.
