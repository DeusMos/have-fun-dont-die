**Evidence cutoff:** September 2, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This repository is not medical advice. It is a sourced information dump for anyone who wants the details.

## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that loss of proteostasis — failed folding, trafficking, stress-response coordination, ubiquitin–proteasome clearance, and lysosomal disposal — accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives it, can be scored, and can be restored so people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity brands and protocol blogs sell that claim as a product: spermidineLIFE CelVio 1–2 mg/day, Primeadine 1 mg, DoNotAge Pure Spermidine 8 mg, Eternal Elixir TUDCA 250–500 mg with meals in an NMN/resveratrol/spermidine stack, Finnish sauna 80–100°C for 15–30 min 3–7×/week as HSP induction, and SomaScan organ-age as a retestable cleanup score (spermidineLIFE product pages; Oxford Healthspan Primeadine; DoNotAge Pure Spermidine; Eternal Elixir stack page; Bellyproof hallmark guide; Julia Douwes HSP protocol; SomaLogic organ-age blog). DoNotAge, Longevity Labs, Oxford Healthspan, Eternal Elixir, and SomaLogic sell the products they are citing.

📚 López-Otín et al. listed loss of proteostasis among nine tentative hallmarks in 2013 and kept it among twelve in 2023, splitting disabled macroautophagy into its own hallmark; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that ordinary aggregate burden is the master clock, or as a license to sell a cleanup capsule, is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — ATTR-ACT / ATTRibute-CM stabilizer outcomes, lecanemab/donanemab plaque clearance, worm HSF1 and rpn-6 lifespan genetics, SmartAge’s existence, KIHD sauna mortality, Oh 2023 organ-age prediction — into “buy cellular cleanup and reverse protein aging” (Maurer et al., NEJM, 2018; Gillmore et al., NEJM, 2024; van Dyck et al., NEJM, 2023; Sims et al., JAMA, 2023; Hsu et al., Science, 2003; Vilchez et al., Nature, 2012; Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022; Laukkanen et al., JAMA Intern Med, 2015; Oh et al., Nature, 2023).

## What the words mean

📚 Labbadia and Morimoto define the proteostasis network as the machines with an immediate role in synthesis, folding, disaggregation, or degradation — translation, chaperones and co-chaperones, the ubiquitin–proteasome system, and autophagy — organized across compartments and tissues (Labbadia and Morimoto, Annual Review of Biochemistry, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biochem-060614-033955, PMID 25784053).

📚 Vilchez, Saez, and Dillin treat UPS and autophagy–lysosome as the two clearance arms and review age-associated decline of both (Vilchez, Saez, Dillin, Nature Communications, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms6659).

📚 In the 2023 hallmark paper, loss of proteostasis stays as the folding/quality-control bag; disabled macroautophagy is a separate hallmark because lysosomal cargo handling can fail on its own axis (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 Clinic English uses “cellular cleanup,” “protein quality control,” or “hallmark 04” for a spermidine capsule, a TUDCA stack, a sauna package, or an organ-age retest (DoNotAge; Eternal Elixir; Bellyproof; SomaLogic).

⛔ Collapsing autophagy, plasma-protein abundance, and HSP70-after-sauna into one “proteostasis score” is a vocabulary collision: LC3 flux is hallmark 05, Oh/SomaScan is an abundance clock, and heat-shock induction is not network restoration (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Oh et al., Nature, 2023; Laukkanen et al., JAMA Intern Med, 2015).

🥼 Whether TUDCA is a classical chemical chaperone is a live paper fight: folding-disease literature still uses that label, while Chadwick et al. report yeast rescue of tunicamycin only at a micelle-forming ~5 mM concentration and conclude TUDCA should no longer be classified as a chemical chaperone (Chadwick et al., Molecular Biology of the Cell, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E24-04-0147, PMID 39661468; N=yeast genetics/imaging, not humans).

## Mechanism

📚 During ordinary human brain aging, 32% of a 332-gene chaperome — the ATP-dependent machines — is repressed and 19.5% — ATP-independent chaperones and co-chaperones — is induced; both clusters shift further in Alzheimer, Huntington, and Parkinson brains (Brehme et al., Cell Reports, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2014.09.042, PMID 25437566; N=human brain expression datasets plus 219 *C. elegans* ortholog knockdowns).

📚 Replicative senescence in primary human WI-38 fibroblasts (passage 24 vs 36) impairs heat-shock transcriptional induction of a 161-gene chaperone cluster, drops nuclear phospho-HSF1 more than three-fold after 2 h at 44 °C, and leaves proteasome activity unrecovered after return to 37 °C (Sabath et al., PNAS, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2018138117, PMID 32759216).

📚 Proteasome-mediated degradation is lower in T cells from elderly adults (65–85 y) than from young adults (21–30 y), in both untreated and TNF-treated cells (Ponnappan, Zhong, and Trebilcock, Cellular Immunology, 1999, https://doi.org/10.1006/cimm.1998.1418, PMID 10087185).

📚 Aging killifish brain increased ribosome stalling at basic-amino-acid stretches, depleted basic DNA/RNA-binding proteins independently of mRNA, and coupled transcript–protein decoupling to detergent insolubility (Pearson R = 0.28, P < 2.2×10⁻¹⁶) (Di Fraia et al., Science, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.adk3079; N=adult 12 wph vs old 39 wph *Nothobranchius furzeri* brain).

📚 Aging exacerbated ribosome pausing at polybasic stretches in *C. elegans* and *S. cerevisiae*, increased collisions that feed ribosome-associated quality control, and enriched age-paused nascent chains among age-dependent aggregates; long-lived yeast mutants reduced age-dependent pausing (Stein et al., Nature, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-04295-4, PMID 35046576).

📚 Labbadia and Morimoto review proteostasis-network collapse as tissue-specific and, in *C. elegans*, as an early-adulthood programmed change in network composition rather than a late-life whole-body folding-quality score available in humans (Labbadia and Morimoto, Annual Review of Biochemistry, 2015).

🥼 Whether visible aggregates are the toxic driver or a coping sink is a live literature fight: inclusion-body formation reduced diffuse mutant huntingtin and predicted longer neuronal survival, while many neurons died without inclusions (Arrasate et al., Nature, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature02998; N=automated time-lapse of transfected rat striatal neurons).

🥼 Prefibrillar oligomers, not mature amyloid deposits, are argued as the more toxic species, so plaque clearance is a different operationalization than “restoring proteostasis” (Treusch, Cyr, and Lindquist, Cell Cycle, 2009, https://doi.org/10.4161/cc.8.11.8503; van Dyck et al., NEJM, 2023, mechanism language on soluble protofibrils).

## Animal data

📚 Several hundred *C. elegans* proteins became more detergent-insoluble with age (mean fold-change ~3.5 ± 0.8 in *gon-2* animals); 691–710 of 711 shared insolubles rose ≥1.5-fold, and homologs of 31–54% of proteins previously found in human disease aggregates sat in that set (David et al., PLoS Biology, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.1000450, PMID 20711477; N=two iTRAQ experiments, 1,125 and 856 insolubles, 711 overlap).

📚 In aging *C. elegans*, ~90% of quantified proteins (975/1,083) accumulated in the insoluble fraction by day 12 versus day 1, and abundant proteins dominate aggregate mass via supersaturation after proteome remodeling — a different species mix than TTR, polyQ, or Aβ (Walther et al., Cell, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2015.03.032, PMC4643853).

📚 In *C. elegans*, aging rewired the ubiquitin proteome toward global deubiquitination (day 5 vs 15: 350 Ub-peptides up, 1,813 down of 3,373 sites / 1,485 proteins); lowering age-dysregulated proteasome targets after development extended lifespan, ubiquitin-less IFB-2/EPS-8 mutants shortened it, and a broad DUB inhibitor (PR-619) in old worms restored Ub-protein levels and extended lifespan (Koyuncu et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03781-z, PMID 34321666).

📚 Reducing *C. elegans* *hsf-1* accelerated tissue aging and shortened lifespan; *hsf-1* overexpression extended lifespan and was required for *daf-2* insulin/IGF-1 mutants to live long, in part through small heat-shock proteins that also delayed polyQ aggregation (Hsu, Murphy, and Kenyon, Science, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1083701, PMID 12750521).

📚 Ectopic expression of the 19S proteasome subunit *rpn-6* was sufficient to raise somatic proteasome activity, improve proteotoxic-stress resistance, and extend *C. elegans* lifespan (Vilchez et al., Nature, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11315, PMID 22922647).

📚 In male C57BL/6J mice, low-solubility protein fractions were preferentially age-affected: LSF analysis doubled the number of age-DEPs, post-transcriptional dysregulation became evident after 24 months, and at 30 vs 6 months LSF DEPs were 6–8% in heart/kidney/muscle and 14–15% in lung/skin (FDR < 0.05, fold-change > 1.5) (Takasugi et al., Nature Communications, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-52845-x, PMID 39353907; N=4 biological replicates per age × 6/15/24/30 months, 9,283 proteins).

📚 Lee, Vilchez and colleagues reported that 15 °C induces PA28γ/PSME-3–dependent trypsin-like proteasome activity required for cold-longevity in *C. elegans*, and that 36 °C does the same in cultured human cells, reducing disease-protein aggregation in those systems (Lee et al., Nature Aging, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00383-4).

📚 Katsiki et al. reported oleuropein stimulated proteasome activities in vitro more than other chemical activators and extended human embryonic fibroblast replicative lifespan by about 15% with continuous treatment (Katsiki et al., Rejuvenation Research, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1089/rej.2006.0513, PMID 17518699).

⛔ Reading worm percent-lifespan, cold-PA28γ, or oleuropein fibroblast PDL as a human cleanup stack fails the design of those papers: model genetics and culture endpoints, not organismal human restoration (Hsu et al., Science, 2003; Vilchez et al., Nature, 2012; Lee et al., Nature Aging, 2023; Katsiki et al., Rejuvenation Research, 2007).

## Human data

📚 HTT CAG-repeat length predicted Huntington disease age of onset (r = 0.70, p = 10⁻⁷) and accounted for ~50% of onset variance in 360 affected people from 259 families (Andrew et al., Nature Genetics, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng0893-398, PMID 8401589).

📚 A parametric survival model on 2,913 HD individuals from 40 centers predicted, for example, a 91% chance (95% CI 90–93%) that a 40-year-old with 42 CAG repeats has motor onset by age 65 (Langbehn et al., Clinical Genetics, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1399-0004.2004.00241.x, PMID 15025718).

📚 Autopsy series summarized in the 2019 JACC ATTR-CM review found wild-type TTR myocardial deposits in as many as 20–25% of octogenarians and 37% of people >95 years; among 109 HFpEF autopsies, 17% had wtATTR deposits and that rate was 40% in the >80 subset (n=35) (Ruberg et al., JACC, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2019.04.003, PMID 31171094).

📚 In a 20-year Boston ATTRwt cohort, 121 biopsy-proven patients (median enrollment age 75.6 y, range 62.6–87.8) had median survival 46.7 months from diagnosis and 35.7% five-year survival (95% CI 25–46); 78% of deaths were cardiac (Connors et al., Circulation, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.115.018852, PMID 26660282).

📚 In ATTR-ACT, tafamidis cut all-cause death from 42.9% (76/177) to 29.5% (78/264) over 30 months (HR 0.70, 95% CI 0.51–0.96) and cut CV hospitalization rate (0.48 vs 0.70 per year; RR 0.68, 95% CI 0.56–0.81) in diagnosed ATTR-CM (Maurer et al., NEJM, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1805689, PMID 30145929; N=441). Pfizer funded.

📚 In ATTRibute-CM, acoramidis HCl 800 mg BID for 30 months beat placebo on a four-step hierarchical endpoint (death, CV hospitalization, NT-proBNP, 6-minute walk) with win ratio 1.8 (95% CI 1.4–2.2); 30-month survival was 80.7% vs 74.3%; death alone was not significant (Gillmore et al., NEJM, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2305434; N=632 randomized / 611 efficacy eGFR≥30). BridgeBio funded.

⛔ ATTR stabilizer mortality is a one-protein, diagnosed-cardiomyopathy result; it is not a test of organismal proteostasis restoration in healthy aging (Maurer et al., NEJM, 2018; Gillmore et al., NEJM, 2024).

📚 In CLARITY-AD, lecanemab 10 mg/kg IV every 2 weeks slowed CDR-SB change at 18 months (1.21 vs 1.66; difference −0.45, 95% CI −0.67 to −0.23) and dropped amyloid PET by −59.1 centiloids vs placebo in a 698-person substudy (van Dyck et al., NEJM, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2212948, PMID 36449413; N=1,795 early AD). Eisai/Biogen funded.

📚 In TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2, donanemab every 4 weeks for up to 72 weeks slowed iADRS decline at 76 weeks by 3.25 points in the low/medium-tau group (−6.02 vs −9.27) and 2.92 in the combined population (Sims et al., JAMA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.13239; N=1,736). Lilly funded.

☠︎︎ Anti-amyloid mAbs carry a boxed warning that ARIA can be fatal; CLARITY-AD ARIA-E was 12.6% on lecanemab vs 1.7% placebo (2.8% symptomatic) and ARIA-H 17.3% vs 9.0%; TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 ARIA-E was 24.0% on donanemab (6.1% symptomatic) vs 2.1% placebo, with three ARIA-associated deaths on donanemab (van Dyck et al., NEJM, 2023; Sims et al., JAMA, 2023; DailyMed Leqembi, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=9d1ff786-e577-410a-a273-c4d7d0e4e975; DailyMed Kisunla, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=190352d4-ef62-4679-b4fa-e846e2766afa).

⛔ Plaque clearance in early symptomatic AD is not organismal proteostasis restoration and is not a healthy-person aging therapy (van Dyck et al., NEJM, 2023; Sims et al., JAMA, 2023).

📚 Oral arimoclomol citrate 1200 mg/day (HSP70 co-inducer that prolongs HSF1 activation) did not change Combined Assessment of Function and Survival over 76 weeks versus placebo in early ALS (mean CAFS 0.51 vs 0.49, p=0.62, Cliff’s δ 0.039, 95% CI −0.116 to 0.194) (Benatar et al., Lancet Neurology, 2024, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38782015/, PMID 38782015, https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(24)00134-0; N=239 mITT of 245 randomized). Orphazyme funded.

📚 Twelve-month spermidine-rich wheat-germ extract at 0.9 mg spermidine/day did not beat placebo on the prespecified primary memory endpoint in older adults with subjective cognitive decline (adjusted MST difference −0.03, 95% CI −0.11 to 0.05, P=0.47); secondary neuropsychological and physiological endpoints were also null on ITT (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.13875, PMID 35583873; N=100 ITT, 89 completers). Capsules from The Longevity Labs.

📚 The global phase 3 PHOENIX trial of sodium phenylbutyrate plus taurursodiol (AMX0035 / Relyvrio) missed ALSFRS-R change at 48 weeks (p=0.667) and secondary endpoints; Amylyx discontinued US/Canadian marketing (Amylyx AAN 2024 slides, https://www.amylyx.com/document/final-aan-oral-presentation.pdf; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT05021536, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05021536; N=664). Peer-reviewed journal paper was not located this round.

📚 European TUDCA monotherapy phase 3 (TUDCA-ALS) failed its month-18 ALSFRS-R responder primary and found no significant difference on survival or neurofilament light (TUDCA-ALS consortium, 2024, https://www.tudca.eu/top-line-results-announcement/; N=336 of planned 440). Peer-reviewed journal paper was not located this round.

☠︎︎ Bortezomib, a 20S proteasome inhibitor, causes established predominantly sensory peripheral neuropathy in labeled rates of 38% overall, Grade 3 in 11%, and discontinuation in 8% (FDA Velcade PI / DailyMed, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=1521d321-e724-4ffc-adad-34bf4f44fac7).

☠︎︎ Daily beta-carotene 20 mg increased lung-cancer incidence 18% and total mortality 8% in male smokers — the antioxidant-as-protein-protection slogan does not survive the trial (ATBC Study Group, NEJM, 1994, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJM199404143301501, PMID 8127329; N=29,133 male smokers 50–69, Finland).

📚 A Cochrane review of antioxidant-vitamin RCTs in cognitively healthy mid/late-life adults found mixed, low-certainty cognitive signals and low-certainty evidence of no effect on dementia incidence for antioxidant combinations or vitamin E ± selenium (Rutjes et al., Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD011906.pub2; N=8 antioxidant trials, ~47,840 participants).

📚 KIHD associated 4–7 Finnish sauna sessions/week vs 1/week with lower all-cause death (62/201 = 30.8% vs 295/601 = 49.1%; adjusted ~40% lower risk) over median 20.7 years in 2,315 eastern-Finnish men aged 42–60; duration >19 min associated with lower SCD but not all-cause death (Laukkanen et al., JAMA Internal Medicine, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2014.8187). Observational.

⛔ KIHD is an observational mortality association in Finnish men, not a randomized proteostasis-restoration trial and not HSP70 as a qualified surrogate (Laukkanen et al., JAMA Intern Med, 2015).

📜 A 2026 preprint reported detergent-fractionation proteomics of disease-free female hippocampus (n=52, ages 20–88) remodeled the insoluble proteome starting in midlife: 82% of SDS-insoluble proteins decreased with age (n=1,934) while ~10% of the sarkosyl-insoluble proteome (n=303 proteins) increased after the geriatric transition; proteasome and cytosolic chaperone abundance predicted individual aggregate burden as strongly as age (Anderton et al., bioRxiv, 2026, https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.27.714902).

## Measurement

📚 Gillmore et al. showed 99mTc-DPD/PYP/HMDP bone scintigraphy grade 2 or 3 plus absence of a monoclonal protein (serum/urine IFE + sFLC) had 100% PPV (CI 98.0–100) for cardiac ATTR among 1,217 suspected-amyloid referrals; scan alone was >99% sensitive and 86% specific, with AL explaining most false positives (Gillmore et al., Circulation, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1161/CIRCULATIONAHA.116.021612, PMID 27143678).

📚 Palmqvist et al. showed plasma p-tau217 discriminated neuropathologic AD vs non-AD (AUC 0.89) and clinical AD dementia vs other neurodegenerative disease (AUC 0.96) across 1,402 people; it tracks AD tau/amyloid pathology, not organismal folding quality (Palmqvist et al., JAMA, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2020.12134, PMID 32722745).

📚 CSF α-synuclein RT-QuIC/SAA detects seeding activity: Fairfoul et al. reported 95% sensitivity in PD (19/20) and 92% in DLB at 100% specificity versus AD and controls; Siderowf et al. reported 88% sensitivity / 96% specificity for PD vs healthy controls in PPMI (Fairfoul et al., Annals of Clinical and Translational Neurology, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1002/acn3.338, PMID 27752516; Siderowf et al., Lancet Neurology, 2023, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37059509/, PMID 37059509; N=1,123 CSF in PPMI).

📚 Oh et al. trained SomaScan plasma-protein models of 11-organ age in 5,676 adults; ~20% had extreme aging in one organ and 1.7% in multiple; accelerated organ age conferred 20–50% higher mortality and 250% higher heart-failure risk for accelerated heart age — an abundance clock, not a solubility/UPS-flux assay (Oh et al., Nature, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-023-06802-1; N=5,676 / five cohorts).

⛔ Filing Oh/SomaScan organ-age as a proteostasis or folding-quality score fails the paper’s method: bagged LASSO on circulating abundance, organ-enriched by GTEx RNA, not oligomer load or UPS flux (Oh et al., Nature, 2023).

🤔 SomaLogic markets the Oh/SomaScan work as noninvasive organ-age from circulating proteins and does not state that the assay measures misfolding, oligomers, or UPS flux (SomaLogic, https://somalogic.com/blog/blood-proteins-reveal-biological-age-of-human-organs-to-help-track-health-and-disease/). SomaLogic sells SomaScan.

📚 Low-solubility / detergent-fractionation proteomics is a working research score of age-associated folding failure in mice, worms, and (as a 2026 preprint) human brain, not a consumer product (Takasugi et al., Nature Communications, 2024; David et al., PLoS Biology, 2010; Anderton et al., bioRxiv, 2026).

🐉 No compiled source this round is a validated whole-body human proteostasis score — folding quality plus clearance capacity across tissues — used as a qualified clinical endpoint; disease PET, ATTR scintigraphy, CSF/plasma Aβ–p-tau, seed-amplification, protein-carbonyl/AGE/ubiquitin panels, and PBMC HSP/proteasome assays are different operationalizations (inference from Oh 2023 method plus the flight search; no FDA-qualified organismal folding/flux endpoint found).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient restoration of youthful chaperone/UPS/translation coordination — not one capsule, not HSP70 after a sauna — might unload age-insoluble fractions the way Koyuncu/Hsu/Vilchez genetics do in worms; that leap has no completed healthy-person aging RCT (inference from Brehme 2014, Sabath 2020, Koyuncu 2021, Hsu 2003, Vilchez 2012; no source for the human restoration step).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body proteostasis score — solubility classes plus oligomer/seed load plus UPS/lysosomal flux, sampled from more than blood, plus a qualified surrogate link to events — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation and trial-design project, not a SomaScan subscription (inference from Oh 2023 method + Takasugi/David fractionation + no FDA-qualified folding/flux endpoint).

🐉 Allele-selective intracellular degraders (ATTEC/AUTOTAC-class) that remove a toxic conformer while sparing the physiological protein might turn cleanup from a global intervention into a selective one; delivery, brain access, and off-target loss are the live engineering problems, and there is no compiled human aging n (inference from Li 2019, Ji 2022, Békés 2022).

🐉 Late-life, tissue-restricted HSF1 or proteasome activation that avoids stabilizing oncoproteins is a mechanistic hope; arimoclomol’s ALS null and bortezomib’s labeled neuropathy are why “turn the dial up” is the wrong cartoon (inference from Benatar 2024 + Velcade label).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Ordinary human tissues show age-associated chaperome repression, T-cell proteasome decline, senescent-fibroblast HSR failure, and highly prevalent late-life wtTTR deposits; worms, killifish, and mice accumulate detergent-insoluble proteomes and can live longer when HSF1, UPS, or ubiquitin-target load is genetically moved (Brehme 2014; Ponnappan 1999; Sabath 2020; Ruberg 2019; David 2010; Walther 2015; Koyuncu 2021; Hsu 2003; Vilchez 2012; Takasugi 2024; Di Fraia 2025).

🥼 Whether ordinary late-life insolubility is the same causal species as Mendelian proteinopathies, and whether visible deposits are toxic or protective, is a live paper fight; TUDCA’s “chemical chaperone” label is independently in a fight (Arrasate 2004; Treusch 2009; Chadwick 2025; David 2010 vs ATTR/polyQ/Aβ).

⛔ A commercial cleanup stack, a sauna package, an organ-age decrement, an ATTR stabilizer, or an anti-amyloid infusion is not organismal proteostasis restoration in healthy people. Arimoclomol missed CAFS. SmartAge missed MST. PHOENIX and TUDCA-ALS missed ALSFRS-R. KIHD is observational. Oh 2023 is abundance (Benatar 2024; Schwarz 2022; Amylyx 2024; TUDCA-ALS 2024; Laukkanen 2015; Oh 2023; Maurer 2018; van Dyck 2023).

🤔 People are buying 1–8 mg spermidine, 250–500 mg TUDCA, Finnish and infrared sauna packages, and organ-age retests under that slogan anyway (spermidineLIFE; Primeadine; DoNotAge; Eternal Elixir; Bellyproof; Douwes; SomaLogic).

☠︎︎ The anti-amyloid class has established fatal ARIA; proteasome inhibition has established neuropathy; high-dose beta-carotene killed smokers in ATBC (van Dyck 2023; Sims 2023; Velcade label; ATBC 1994).
