# TUDCA-ALS consortium (Albanese et al.), 2024. Announcement of top-line results. TUDCA-ALS consortium.

Used for: European TUDCA monotherapy phase 3 (TUDCA-ALS) failed its month-18 ALSFRS-R responder primary and found no significant difference on survival or neurofilament light.

N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 336 of planned 440 participants, 25 sites, 7 countries, 18 months. Primary (ALSFRS-R responders at month 18) not met. No significant difference on survival or NfL. COVID reduced recruitment; ~half reached 18 months per arm. Horizon 2020 grant 755094. Journal paper not located this round.
Conflict if any: Consortium included Bruschettini s.r.l. (Italy). EU-funded academic trial.
