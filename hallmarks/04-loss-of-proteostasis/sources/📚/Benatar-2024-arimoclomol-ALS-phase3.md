# Benatar et al., 2024. Safety and efficacy of arimoclomol in patients with early amyotrophic lateral sclerosis (ORARIALS-01): a randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled, multicentre, phase 3 trial. Lancet Neurology.
https://doi.org/10.1016/S1474-4422(24)00134-0
Used for: Oral arimoclomol citrate 1200 mg/day (HSP70 co-inducer that prolongs HSF1 activation) did not change Combined Assessment of Function and Survival over 76 weeks versus placebo in early ALS.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=239 mITT (160 arimoclomol / 79 placebo) of 245 randomized at 29 centres in 12 countries. Mean CAFS 0.51 (SD 0.29) vs 0.49 (0.28); p=0.62; Cliff’s δ 0.039 (95% CI −0.116 to 0.194). Deaths 29/160 (18%) vs 18/79 (23%). Treatment-related AEs 65% vs 52%; discontinuation 16% vs 5%. Early ALS, ALSFRS-R ≥35, SVC ≥70% predicted. Primary: CAFS over 76 weeks. NCT03491462.
Conflict if any: Funded by Orphazyme. Several authors affiliated with Orphazyme. Orphazyme developed arimoclomol.
