# Sims et al., 2023. Donanemab in Early Symptomatic Alzheimer Disease: The TRAILBLAZER-ALZ 2 Randomized Clinical Trial. JAMA.
https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.13239
Used for: Donanemab produced ARIA-E in 24.0% (52 symptomatic) versus 2.1% on placebo, and three donanemab deaths versus one placebo death were judged treatment-related.
