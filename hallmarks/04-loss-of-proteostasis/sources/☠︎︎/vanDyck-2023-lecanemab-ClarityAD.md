# van Dyck et al., 2023. Lecanemab in Early Alzheimer’s Disease. New England Journal of Medicine.
https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2212948
Used for: Lecanemab produced ARIA-E in 12.6% versus 1.7% on placebo and ARIA-H in 17.3% versus 9.0% in Clarity AD; paper also states soluble protofibrils are more toxic than monomers or insoluble fibrils.
Mark: ☠︎︎
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=1795 randomized (898 lecanemab / 897 placebo), age 50–90, early symptomatic AD, amyloid-positive. 18 months. ARIA-E 12.6% vs 1.7%; ARIA-H 17.3% vs 9.0%; infusion reactions 26.4% vs 7.4%. CDR-SB difference −0.45 (95% CI −0.67 to −0.23). NCT03887455.
