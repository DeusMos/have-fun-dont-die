# FDA / DailyMed, 2025. VELCADE (bortezomib) prescribing information. DailyMed.

Used for: Bortezomib, a 20S proteasome inhibitor, causes established predominantly sensory peripheral neuropathy in labeled rates of 38% overall, Grade 3 in 11%, and discontinuation in 8%.
Mark: ☠︎︎
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Labeling pooled/phase-3 populations. Peripheral neuropathies 38%; Grade 3 11%; ≥Grade 4 <1%; 8% discontinued for PN. Incidence higher in mantle-cell lymphoma (54%) than multiple myeloma (36%). Dose-limiting, predominantly sensory; severe sensory and motor cases reported.
Conflict if any: Takeda markets Velcade.
