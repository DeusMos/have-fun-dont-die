# Chadwick et al., 2025. TUDCA modulates drug bioavailability to regulate resistance to acute ER stress in Saccharomyces cerevisiae. Molecular Biology of the Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.E24-04-0147 PMID 39661468
