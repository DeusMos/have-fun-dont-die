# Anderton et al., 2026. Shifts in protein aggregate stability define proteostasis decline in the aging human brain. bioRxiv.
https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.03.27.714902
Used for: 📜 Human brain aging remodeled the insoluble proteome starting in midlife: maximum-stability aggregates fell in old age while intermediate-stability aggregates accumulated and accelerated after age 80; proteasome and cytosolic chaperone abundance predicted individual aggregate burden as strongly as age.
Mark: 📜
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Preprint. Disease-free female hippocampus n=52, ages 20–88 (NIH Neurobiobank). 82% of SDS-insoluble proteins decreased with age (n=1,934); ~10% of sarkosyl-insoluble proteome (n=303) increased after geriatric transition. Proteasome/chaperone abundance predicted burden as strongly as age. Endpoint: SDS vs sarkosyl insoluble fractions. Duration: cross-sectional lifespan.
