**Hallmark:** Psychosocial isolation  
**Evidence cutoff:** September 3, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This repository is not medical advice. It is a sourced information dump for anyone who wants the details.

The slogan in the wild is a public-health-to-product launder: loneliness is as deadly as smoking 15 cigarettes a day; it is the missing hallmark; score it with UCLA-3 like hsCRP, restore it with connection, hearing aids, CBT, volunteering, compounded oxytocin (“Oxipops”), MDMA/psychedelic “connection” sessions, or a Replika-class companion. ## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that chronic psychosocial isolation — objective scarcity of contact/roles, subjective loneliness, bereavement, and the HPA/SAM/CTRA physiology that can follow — accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives it, can be scored, and can be restored so people live longer or younger.

🤔 Longevity newsletters, telehealth peptide shops, compounding pharmacies, AI-companion apps, pet-industry institutes, Blue Zones licensing, NHS link-worker programs, and cash-pay psychedelic clinics sell pieces of that claim: Zenith Within treats UCLA-3 6–9 as “moderate” the way a slightly elevated CRP would be; Superpower sells a consult plus compounded IN oxytocin and sublingual “Oxipops”; Mochi Health pitches IN OT for “social bonding, stress reduction, or emotional wellness” at research-cited 18–48 IU (24 IU modal) in 10–30 mL / 50–500 IU bottles; Bayview compounds IN 25/40/100/120 units/mL and ~200-unit troches from ~$118 / 10 mL; Replika markets an “AI friend” (42 million users claimed); Different Clinic (Toronto) sells ketamine-available psychedelic-assisted therapy with “profound connection” copy; HABRI/Mars Petcare sell pets-add-years; Blue Zones Power 9 sells Belong / Right Tribe / moai as years-added (Zenith Within, https://www.zenithwithin.com/p/social-health-longevity; Superpower, https://superpower.com/guides/peptides/oxytocin; Mochi Health, https://joinmochi.com/blog/oxytocin-nasal-spray-benefits-side-effects-how-it-works; Bayview Pharmacy, https://www.bayviewrx.com/formulations/oxytocin; Replika, https://replika.com/; Different Clinic, https://different.clinic/blog/psychedelic-assisted-therapy-for-depression/; HABRI, https://habri.org/blog/the-relationship-between-pet-ownership-and-longevity/; Blue Zones, https://www.bluezones.com/2016/11/power-9/). Those companies sell the consult, the vial, the subscription, the book, or the session.

📚 López-Otín et al. listed nine tentative hallmarks in 2013 and twelve in 2023 (genomic instability through dysbiosis). Psychosocial isolation is not on either list; the 2023 listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349; already filed under hallmarks/10).

⛔ Treating “it is this repo’s 14th slot,” a Surgeon General “15 cigarettes” callout, or a UCLA drop as proof that ordinary isolation can be scored and restored so healthy people live longer is a taxonomy paper or a slogan doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Holt-Lunstad et al., PLoS Medicine, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1000316).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Holt-Lunstad 2010/2015 and Wang 2023 mortality metas, Cole CTRA, Elovainio residual isolation–death, Moon widowhood, Livingston isolation-as-dementia-risk, Berger in-session OT — into “buy the score and the spray” (Holt-Lunstad 2010; Holt-Lunstad 2015; Wang 2023; Cole 2007; Office of the Surgeon General 2023, https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf).

## What the words mean

💯 Social isolation is objective scarcity of contacts and roles. Loneliness is the aversive gap between desired and actual connection. Living alone is household composition. They are not synonyms (Holt-Lunstad, Smith, and Layton, PLoS Medicine, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1000316; N=148 studies / 308,849; living-alone survival OR 1.19, 95% CI 0.99–1.44 vs complex integration OR 1.91, 1.63–2.23).

💯 Oxytocin is a nine-amino-acid hypothalamic nonapeptide released from the posterior pituitary. The FDA-approved product is injectable obstetric oxytocin (Pitocin class), not a nasal spray or troche (DailyMed Pitocin label; Superpower 2026; Bayview 2026).

📚 UCLA Loneliness Scale Version 3 is a 20-item subjective discrepancy instrument (alpha 0.89–0.94; 1-year test–retest r=0.73), not a headcount (Russell, Journal of Personality Assessment, 1996, https://doi.org/10.1207/s15327752jpa6601_2).

📚 The Hughes 3-item UCLA (companionship / left out / isolated) was built for telephone epidemiology and correlates only modestly with objective isolation (Hughes, Waite, Hawkley, Cacioppo, Research on Aging, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1177/0164027504268574).

📚 de Jong Gierveld’s 11-item (and 6-item short) scale splits emotional loneliness (missing an intimate tie) from social loneliness (missing a broader network); those subscales are not interchangeable with UCLA’s global factor (NASEM, Social Isolation and Loneliness in Older Adults, 2020, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK557967/).

📚 Berkman–Syme Social Network Index scores marital status, close friends/relatives, church, and group membership into integration levels — a structural count (Berkman and Syme, American Journal of Epidemiology, 1979, PMID 425958; NASEM 2020).

📚 Lubben Social Network Scale was adapted from Berkman–Syme for older adults and weights family and friend contact; marital status and religious participation were dropped because they vary less in that age band (NASEM 2020 Ch. 6).

📚 Cohen’s Social Network Index scores diversity of role types and embeddedness, not felt loneliness; ACHIEVE used it as the isolation exploratory (Cohen et al., JAMA, 1997, PMID 9200634; Reed, Chen, Huang et al., JAMA Internal Medicine, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2025.1140).

📚 UK Biobank “social isolation” is a 0–3 sum: lives alone, friend/family visits less than monthly, no weekly group activity; isolated = score ≥2. Loneliness is a separate two-item (often feel lonely + cannot confide) (Elovainio et al., Lancet Public Health, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/s2468-2667(17)30075-0; N=466,901).

📚 CTRA is a leukocyte transcriptional composite — proinflammatory genes up (IL1B, IL6, CXCL8, PTGS2, TNF), type I interferon genes down (IFI/MX/OAS) — seen across loneliness, poverty, bereavement, caregiving, PTSD, and chronic threat, not isolation-only. It can be scored as GO tagging, TELiS TF inference, myeloid cell-origin analysis, or a 53-gene a-priori contrast; the 53-gene score is one convenient metric, not the definition (Cole, Current Opinion in Behavioral Sciences, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cobeha.2019.01.008, PMC6779418; Cole, PLoS Genetics, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1004601).

⛔ Collapsing isolation, loneliness, living alone, chosen solitude, social strain, bereavement, CTRA, a clock, and a compounded spray into one “social age you restore” is a vocabulary collision (Holt-Lunstad 2010; Cole 2014; Superpower 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Chronically lonely adults showed a leukocyte transcriptome with NF-κB/pro-inflammatory bias and GRE/antiviral under-expression, not explained by circulating cortisol (Cole et al., Genome Biology, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1186/gb-2007-8-9-r189; N=14 CHASRS, 6 high- vs 8 low-UCLA; 209 transcripts, 78 up / 131 down; NF-κB p=0.011, GRE p=0.032).

📚 Loneliness-associated transcripts mapped to plasmacytoid dendritic cells and monocytes more than to other leukocyte subsets, and tracked subjective isolation more than network size (Cole et al., PNAS, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1014218108; N=14, same 6 vs 8 split).

📚 Chronically high perceived isolation predicted a 6.5% CTRA elevation (12.2% after stress/depression/support covariates) across 412 transcriptome surveys in 141 older adults; CTRA also predicted later isolation (Cole et al., PNAS, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1514249112; 26% chronically high PSI; 5 yearly surveys).

📚 CTRA is mediated by β-adrenergic signaling that biases hematopoietic output toward myeloid cells and is a recurrent pattern across several chronic-threat contexts, not a loneliness-only switch (Cole, Current Opinion in Behavioral Sciences, 2019, PMC6779418).

📚 Chronic social isolation increases HPA activation in the review synthesis; those effects depend more on disruption of a significant social bond than on objective isolation count (Cacioppo, Cacioppo, Capitanio, and Cole, Annual Review of Psychology, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-psych-010814-015240).

📚 Trait loneliness predicted a larger cortisol awakening response in middle-aged workers after waking-value and covariate adjustment (Steptoe, Owen, Kunz-Ebrecht, and Brydon, Psychoneuroendocrinology, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0306-4530(03)00086-6; N=240, ages 47–59; CAR p=0.046).

📚 Prior-day loneliness, sadness, threat, and lack of control predicted a higher next-morning CAR; morning CAR did not predict those states later the same day (Adam, Hawkley, Kudielka, and Cacioppo, PNAS, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0605053103; N=156 older adults, CHASRS; saliva × 3 days).

📚 Loneliness had no association with CRP (least-adjusted r=0.047, 95% CI −0.003 to 0.098; most-adjusted r=0.023) or fibrinogen; loneliness–IL-6 was NS least-adjusted (r=0.082) and small most-adjusted (r=0.070); isolation–CRP vanished after confounders (r=0.186 → 0.021) (Smith, Gavey, Riddell, Kontari, and Victor, Neuroscience & Biobehavioral Reviews, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.neubiorev.2020.02.002; 14 loneliness / 16 isolation papers).

⛔ Reading a small, construct-specific CRP/IL-6 r as license to run the hallmark-11 inflammaging stack (hsCRP treat-to-target, CANTOS, colchicine) as isolation care fails both the Smith meta and the 11/14 split (Smith et al., 2020; hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 In a macaque perceived-isolation model, CTRA activated with CD14++/CD16− classical-monocyte bias, functional glucocorticoid desensitization, type I/II interferon down, and impaired SIV control (Cole et al., PNAS, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1514249112).

📚 Four weeks of sibling isolation in female prairie voles raised resting heart rate, cut HR variability and vagal tone, reduced sucrose intake, and increased FST immobility; 14 days of systemic oxytocin (20 µg SC) prevented the autonomic and behavioral changes (Grippo, Trahanas, Zimmerman, Porges, and Carter, Psychoneuroendocrinology, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.psyneuen.2009.05.017; N=26 autonomic + 31 behavioral; time × group × peptide F(1,22)=8.30 for HR).

📚 Elabd et al. showed mouse plasma OT / muscle OTR fall with age and that systemic OT restores satellite-cell regeneration — muscle/communication, already filed under hallmark 10, not isolation restoration (Elabd et al., Nature Communications, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms5082, PMID 24915299; already filed under hallmarks/10).

⛔ Counting Elabd 2014 or the Espinoza 2021 IN OT lean-mass pilot (24 IU QID × 8 weeks, n=21 sarcopenic obesity) as isolation therapy is the muscle-launder (Elabd 2014; Espinoza 2021, PMC8567747; already filed under hallmarks/10).

## Human data

📚 Adults with stronger social relationships had 50% higher odds of survival than those with weaker relationships (Holt-Lunstad, Smith, and Layton, PLoS Medicine, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pmed.1000316, PMID 20668659; N=308,849 / 148 prospective studies; random-effects OR 1.50, 95% CI 1.42–1.59; mean follow-up 7.5 y).

📚 Social isolation, loneliness, and living alone each predicted early death after covariate control at similar magnitude (Holt-Lunstad, Smith, Baker, Harris, and Stephenson, Perspectives on Psychological Science, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1177/1745691614568352; N=3,407,134 / 70 prospective studies; isolation OR 1.29, loneliness OR 1.26, living alone OR 1.32; mean follow-up ~7 y).

📚 In 90 prospective cohorts, social isolation associated with all-cause death ES 1.32 (95% CI 1.26–1.39) and loneliness ES 1.14 (1.08–1.20); isolation also associated with CVD death ES 1.34 and cancer death ES 1.24 (Wang et al., Nature Human Behaviour, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-023-01617-6; N=2,205,199).

⛔ Holt-Lunstad 2010’s OR 1.50 is an observational odds calibration against other risk-factor metas, not a molecular identity with smoking 15 cigarettes/day; living-alone (inversed) was the weakest structural subtype and its CI includes 1 (Holt-Lunstad et al., 2010; Office of the Surgeon General, 2023).

🥼 In UK Biobank, isolation retained residual all-cause mortality after socioeconomic, behavioral, depressive, biological, cognitive, and self-rated-health adjustment (minimally adjusted HR 1.73 → fully adjusted HR 1.26, 95% CI 1.20–1.33), while loneliness’s minimally adjusted HR 1.38 fell to 0.99 (0.93–1.06) (Elovainio et al., Lancet Public Health, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/s2468-2667(17)30075-0; N=466,901; mean age 56.5; mean 6.5 y). Isolation residual and loneliness residual are not the same claim.

📚 Isolation predicted first AMI HR 1.43 and first stroke HR 1.39 after demographic adjustment; ~84% of that excess attenuated with full risk-factor control (fully adjusted isolation AMI HR 1.07, 0.99–1.16; stroke HR 1.06, 0.96–1.19), but isolation still predicted post-AMI death HR 1.25 (1.03–1.51) and post-stroke death HR 1.32 (Hakulinen et al., Heart, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1136/heartjnl-2017-312663; N=479,054; 7.1 y; 5,731 AMI, 3,471 stroke).

📚 Widowhood associated with all-cause death, largest in the first six months and in men (Moon, Kondo, Glymour, and Subramanian, PLoS ONE, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0023465; N=2,263,888 / 15 cohorts; RR 1.41 first 6 months, 1.14 thereafter; men RR 1.23, women RR 1.04).

📚 Social isolation associated with later all-cause dementia after full adjustment including loneliness and depression (HR 1.26, 1.15–1.37); loneliness’s dementia HR was 1.04 (0.94–1.16) and 75% of its crude link was depressive symptoms; isolated participants had lower gray-matter volume in temporal, frontal, and hippocampal regions (Shen et al., Neurology, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000200583; N=462,619 / 4,998 dementia cases, mean 11.7 y; MRI subset n=32,263).

📚 Lancet Commission listed later-life social isolation as a modifiable dementia risk with RR 1.6 (1.3–1.9) and weighted PAF 3.5%, assuming causation (Livingston et al., Lancet, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736(20)30367-6, PMID 32738937).

📚 Poor social relationships associated with incident CHD RR 1.29 (1.04–1.59) and stroke RR 1.32 (1.04–1.68) (Valtorta, Kanaan, Gilbody, Ronzi, and Hanratty, Heart, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1136/heartjnl-2015-308790; 16 longitudinal datasets; 4,628 CHD + 3,002 stroke events).

📚 Social isolation associated with 17 of 59 UKB health conditions (all-cause aHR 1.28, 1.25–1.32; 11 diseases aHR 1.08–1.17) and was uncorrelated with the majority; two-sample MR using 13 SNPs for a latent isolation factor offered little evidence of causality for 9 Bonferroni-surviving diseases; proteomic signatures (PERM 36–49%), health behaviours (32–59%), and socioeconomic factors (22–42%) explained much of the observational link (Ai et al., BMC Medicine, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12916-026-04784-1, PMID 41872870; N=489,741; median 12.5 y).

📚 SNP-heritability of loneliness was 4.2% (SE 0.02) with genetic correlation 0.84 to depressive symptoms and 0.69 to neuroticism (Day, Ong, and Perry, Nature Communications, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-04930-1; N≤452,302).

🥼 Bidirectional two-sample MR found loneliness → major depression and depression → loneliness across PGC-MD (N=142,646) and MVP-MD (N=250,215), so the loneliness signal is genetically entangled with depression rather than a one-way aging driver (Sbarra et al., Molecular Psychiatry, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41380-023-02259-w).

📚 Triangulated observational, sibling-control, and MR analyses found effects of loneliness/isolation on mental health and of loneliness on some general-health indices, but not consistent effects on specific physical-health outcomes (CAD, stroke, T2DM) (Hilliard et al., Nature Communications, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-74758-7; UKB N=8,004–414,432 + GWAS 17,526–2,083,151; Nesta-funded).

📚 Loneliness–epigenetic-age associations in MIDUS were r≈0.08–0.11 after demographics and r≈0.06–0.08 after health behaviors; EAA did not significantly mediate loneliness → multimorbidity (Freilich, Mann, Krueger, and Graham, Psychology and Aging, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1037/pag0000822; N=1,310 / 445 follow-up).

📚 Among RCTs, interventions that targeted maladaptive social cognition reduced loneliness more than support, skills, or access in the overall randomized set (RCT overall −0.20, −0.32 to −0.08); the social-cognition strategy mean from three RCT cells was −1.58 with CI touching the null (−3.18 to 0.02, p=0.053) (Masi, Chen, Hawkley, and Cacioppo, Personality and Social Psychology Review, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1177/1088868310377394; 20 randomized comparisons).

📚 Psychological interventions, mostly CBT, reduced loneliness versus control at Hedges g=0.43 (Hickin et al., Clinical Psychology Review, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cpr.2021.102066; 28 RCTs, N=3,039).

📚 An umbrella review graded the only statistically significant loneliness interventions as very-low (meditation/mindfulness MD −6.03; social-cognitive training SMD −0.49) or low (social support SMD −0.13) certainty; four of seven intervention types were NS (Veronese et al., Health & Social Care in the Community, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1111/hsc.13248; 7 meta-analyses).

📚 Lonely older-adult volunteers who delivered 6 months of telephone psychosocial intervention dropped UCLA and de Jong Gierveld loneliness versus an active psychoeducation control (Lai et al., Lancet Healthy Longevity, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.lanhl.2024.100664; N=375, ages 50–70, UCLA-3 ≥6; UCLA dppc2=−0.41, DJG dppc2=−0.70 at 6 months; most effects not sustained at 12 months except among those still volunteering >2 h/week). Score RCT, no mortality/clock endpoint.

📚 ACHIEVE hearing-aid intervention versus health-education control did not change 3-year global cognition in the total cohort (difference 0.002 SD, −0.077 to 0.081; p=0.96) (Lin et al., Lancet, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/s0140-6736(23)01406-x, PMID 37478886; N=977, ages 70–84).

📚 The same ACHIEVE trial’s exploratory social outcomes: hearing intervention slowed 3-year loss of Cohen SNI network size by +1.05 people (0.01–2.09) and reduced UCLA loneliness by −0.94 (−1.78 to −0.11); the paper states the loneliness change is not a clinically meaningful UCLA shift (Reed, Chen, Huang et al., JAMA Internal Medicine, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2025.1140).

📚 Eight-week MBSR versus wait-list reduced loneliness and down-regulated NF-κB-associated leukocyte gene expression in older adults; CRP/IL-6 did not move (Creswell et al., Brain, Behavior, and Immunity, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bbi.2012.07.006; N=40, ages 55–85; condition × time F(1,35)=7.86, p=0.008 for loneliness).

📚 Five weekly group-therapy sessions reduced trait UCLA loneliness through 3-month follow-up (b=−1.90, d=−0.33; 3-month d=−0.41 vs T1); 24 IU IN oxytocin before sessions did not augment trait loneliness (PLC −3.63±6.57 vs OT −0.43±7.49), quality of life, or stress, but did cut in-session state loneliness (d=−0.34) and raise positive group bonding (Berger et al., Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1159/000538752; N=78 high-lonely adults, UCLA ≥55; NCT04137432).

📚 SOARS-B 48 IU/day IN oxytocin for 24 weeks did not improve social functioning versus placebo in ASD (ABC-mSW LS-mean difference −0.2, −1.5 to 1.0; p=0.61) (Sikich et al., NEJM, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2103583, PMID 34614324; N=277 modified ITT, ages 3–17). ASD social-functioning trial, not an isolation-aging trial; it still kills “OT restores social biology” as a general claim.

📚 Befriending showed no loneliness benefit (SMD −0.03; five comparisons) and no significant depression, QoL, self-esteem, social-support, or well-being effects (Siette, Cassidy, and Priebe, BMJ Open, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-014304; 14 trials / 2,411).

📚 Fifteen UK social-prescribing evaluations were all high risk of bias (no comparative controls, short follow-up, missing data, unmeasured confounding) and still published positive conclusions (Bickerdike, Booth, Wilson, Farley, and Wright, BMJ Open, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1136/bmjopen-2016-013384).

📚 Informal caregivers vs non-caregivers showed medium differences in depression (g=0.58) and stress (g=0.55) and a small physical-health decrement (g=0.18), larger in dementia caregiving — piling on contact via overloaded caregiving is not a restore protocol (Pinquart and Sörensen, Psychology and Aging, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1037/0882-7974.18.2.250; 84 articles).

📚 Australian lockdown-day intensity did not causally increase loneliness after individual/region/year fixed effects and contact/WFH channels (Kong and Lam, Journal of Economic Behavior & Organization, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jebo.2024.06.034; N=17,124 HILDA). COVID contact reduction is not a clean isolation experiment.

📚 Increased social-chatbot use predicted higher single-item emotional isolation four months later (standardized β≈0.07–0.08) in an exploratory RI-CLPM; chatbot use did not predict the broader 20-item social-connection score (Folk and Dunn, Psychological Science, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1177/09567976261427747; N=2,149 adults, 4 countries, 12 months / 4 waves).

📚 FDA issued a Complete Response Letter (9 Aug 2024) refusing midomafetamine (MDMA) capsules for PTSD and requesting another Phase 3; there is no aging or loneliness indication (Lykos Therapeutics, 2024, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lykos-therapeutics-announces-complete-response-letter-for-midomafetamine-capsules-for-ptsd-302219182.html).

☠︎︎ Solitary confinement is associated with worse psychological symptomatology (SMD 0.45; n=4,517 from 5 studies) and higher self-harm/suicide-related mortality signals in 13 studies totaling 382,440 inmates (Luigi, Dellazizzo, Giguère, Goulet, and Dumais, Frontiers in Psychiatry, 2020, https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyt.2020.00840). Extreme imposed isolation, not weekend solitude or a UCLA-3 of 8.

☠︎︎ Any restrictive housing during incarceration predicted post-release year-1 all-cause death HR 1.24 (1.12–1.38), suicide HR 1.78, homicide HR 1.54, and opioid-overdose HR 2.27 in the first two weeks (Brinkley-Rubinstein et al., JAMA Network Open, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2019.12516; N=229,274 releases / 398,158 incarcerations, North Carolina 2000–2015).

🐉 No fetched RCT shows that lowering a loneliness or isolation score extends human lifespan, healthspan, or epigenetic age (flight search result; inference from Elovainio 2017 + Hickin 2021 + Lai 2024 + Huang 2025 + Lin 2023).

## Measurement

📚 UCLA-20 / UCLA-3 measure subjective discrepancy. de Jong Gierveld splits emotional vs social loneliness. Berkman–Syme, Lubben, and Cohen SNI measure structural integration. UKB three-item measures a coarse isolation sum. None of these is a biological-age test (Russell 1996; Hughes 2004; NASEM 2020; Elovainio 2017; Cohen 1997).

📚 CTRA is a research RNA profile. The UCSF Stress Measurement Network lists per-sample cost $150–$500; the USC-UCLA Social Genomics Core consults investigators — it does not sell a consumer kit (Cole, UCSF SMN, 2018, https://www.stressmeasurement.org/gene-expression; Cole 2019).

🐉 No direct-to-consumer CTRA product was found on TruDiagnostic / InsideTracker / Function-class pages or Cole-lab materials this pass; treating CTRA as a sold “social age” blood test is an inference from the research composite, not a SKU (search result; Cole 2019; UCSF SMN).

🤔 No consumer “social age” laboratory SKU was found this pass. The circulating treat-to-target object is UCLA-3 (Zenith Within 2026) or a homemade close-contact count, not a clock vendor product.

⛔ A blood CRP, a GrimAge tile, a telomere T/S, or a CTRA 53-gene contrast collapsed into one “social age you titrate a spray against” is a vocabulary collision (Smith 2020; Freilich 2024; Cole 2014; hallmarks/03 and /02 own the clock and telomere kits).

Practice is not efficacy. Doses below are observed, not instructions.

Superpower sells the consult.

Mochi sells the telehealth prescription and ships the vial.

## Speculative

🐉 Transient restoration of wanted, reciprocal roles — plus a sensory or depression lever that was the actual barrier — might unload CTRA / CAR / post-event death risk without a peptide; that is a hope sitting on residual isolation–mortality plus score-moving trials, not a completed restore (inference from Elovainio 2017 + Hakulinen 2018 + Creswell 2012 + Lai 2024).

🐉 An IN OT adjunct that turns Berger’s in-session state-loneliness / bonding effect into a durable trait and then a clinical endpoint would be the first pharmacologic isolation-aging signal; Berger’s trait null and Sikich’s social-functioning null are why that remains a leap (inference from Berger 2024 + Sikich 2021).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body “social age” score — isolation plus loneliness plus strain plus CTRA plus a clock, sampled from more than a UCLA-3 — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation project, not an Oxipop (inference from Cole 2014 + Freilich 2024 + no DTC CTRA SKU).

🐉 Late-life reconnection might prevent further drift more easily than it erases accumulated risk; reverse causation is strongest near dementia and death (inference from Shen 2022 short-follow-up problem + Ai 2026 MR null).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Isolation and loneliness are distinct, moderately correlated, and both associated with earlier death in very large observational literatures; living alone is a weak proxy; isolation retains a residual mortality and dementia association in UK Biobank after hard adjustment; loneliness often attenuates once depression and behaviors are included (Holt-Lunstad 2010, 2015; Wang 2023; Elovainio 2017; Shen 2022).

📚 CTRA, HPA/CAR shifts, and prairie-vole isolation autonomic biology are real mechanisms. Circulating CRP/IL-6/fibrinogen links are small and construct-specific. Clocks associate weakly and do not mediate multimorbidity in MIDUS (Cole 2007–2019; Steptoe 2004; Adam 2006; Grippo 2009; Smith 2020; Freilich 2024).

🥼 Whether isolation is a causal aging driver after SES, behavior, depression, and genetics is a live paper fight: residual isolation–death vs loneliness NS (Elovainio 2017); ~85% CVD attenuation (Hakulinen 2018); Ai 2026 and Hilliard 2026 MR/triangulation give little genetic causality for specific physical disease; Sbarra 2023 is bidirectional with depression (Elovainio 2017; Hakulinen 2018; Ai 2026; Hilliard 2026; Sbarra 2023; Day 2018).

📚 Score-moving trials exist (Hickin g=0.43; Lai volunteering; ACHIEVE network +1 person / UCLA −0.94; Creswell NF-κB; Berger group therapy). Hard-outcome and even some score tests fail or are GRADE-weak (Lin 2023 cognition NULL; Siette befriending −0.03; Veronese GRADE low/very low; Berger OT trait null; Sikich social-functioning null; Bickerdike social-prescribing bias).

🤔 People are buying compounded IN/sublingual oxytocin, UCLA-as-CRP coaching, AI companions, pets-as-years, Blue Zone social protocols, and “connection” psychedelic sessions under the isolation-aging slogan anyway (Superpower; Mochi; Bayview; Zenith Within; Replika; HABRI; Blue Zones; Different Clinic; Rapamycin News).

☠︎︎ Imposed extreme isolation (solitary / restrictive housing) has a hard-endpoint record of psychological harm and post-release death. That is not a model of ordinary UCLA-range loneliness and not a license to force contact onto unsafe or overloaded ties (Luigi 2020; Brinkley-Rubinstein 2019; Pinquart 2003).
