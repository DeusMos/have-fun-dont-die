## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that genomic instability — DNA lesions, failed or misregulated repair, chromosomal and mtDNA change, and selected mutant clones — accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives it, and can be scored and restored.

🤔 Longevity clinics, supplement brands, and cash-pay labs sell that claim as a product: NAD IV and oral NR/NMN as “DNA repair,” *Fucus vesiculosus* fucoidan as a SIRT6 “genomic guardian,” urine 8-OHdG as a genome-age score you titrate against, HBOT and young plasma as rejuvenation, and 15-gene CHIP NGS as a longevity screen (Peach IV NAD protocol page; DoNotAge SIRT6Activator®; Doctor’s Data DNA Oxidative Damage Assay; Mito Health / Lucence CardioHemeRISK; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07500649 collaborator list naming DoNotAge.org; FDA CBER 2019 young-plasma communication).

📚 López-Otín et al. listed genomic instability as the first of nine tentative hallmarks in 2013 and kept it as a primary hallmark among twelve in 2023; the 2023 paper’s listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that ordinary point-mutation burden is the master clock, or that a hallmark-named capsule restores the aged genome, is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — NAD as a PARP/sirtuin substrate, SIRT6’s BER/DSB-adjacent and LINE1 functions, ROS oxidizing bases, Cagan scaling, Mendelian repair disease, CHIP–disease associations, clock and telomere movement — into “buy a DNA-repair stack / genome-age test” (Peach IV; DoNotAge; Doctor’s Data; Mito Health; NCT07500649 collaborator list).

## What the words mean

💯 DNA in living cells is chemically unstable and is continuously hit by endogenous hydrolysis, replication errors, and reactive oxygen species as well as exogenous radiation and chemicals (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 Organisms run a network of lesion-specific pathways — BER, NER, MMR, HR, NHEJ, ICL, TLS — not a single “DNA repair” switch you flip with an infusion (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 A lesion (oxidized base, adduct, crosslink, strand break) is chemistry on DNA; a mutation is a heritable sequence change after misrepair or miscopy; a clone is a selected expansion; γH2AX/53BP1 foci report DDR signaling, not unrepaired DSBs one-for-one (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 In the 2013/2023 hallmark papers, genomic instability is the bag of nuclear and mitochondrial sequence, structure, and architecture failures: point mutations, indels, rearrangements, aneuploidy, SSB/DSB, nuclear-architecture defects, transposon/virus insertion, cytosolic DNA, and mosaicism (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 Clinic English uses “DNA repair” for NAD, fucoidan, antioxidants, HBOT, or plasma (Peach IV; DoNotAge; Doctor’s Data; FDA CBER 2019).

🤔 “Genome age” in the wild is almost always a methylation clock or a leukocyte telomere, not duplex-sequenced burden (NCT07500649 GrimAge primary; Doctor’s Data urine 8-OHdG menu).

📚 CHIP VAF is clone size in blood, not organismal mutation load (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014; Steensma et al., Blood, 2015).

## Mechanism

📚 Nuclear genomic instability is treated as a causal nidus of aging in the 2018 Annual Review: lesion burden rises with age in mammals, inherited repair defects produce segmental progeria, and persistent genotoxic stress feeds senescence and metabolic decline — the review itself separates that from proof that ordinary late-life SNV counts limit intact humans (Niedernhofer et al., Annual Review of Biochemistry, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-biochem-062917-012239, PMID 29925262).

📚 A 2021 Nature review synthesizes that persistent DNA lesions, not only fixed mutations, are a central mechanistic input to ordinary aging via transcription stress, senescence, and tissue dysfunction (Schumacher, Pothof, Vijg, Hoeijmakers, Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03307-7).

📚 In wild-type 2-year-old mouse liver, ~40% of elongating RNAPII is stalled, nascent RNA synthesis falls ~1.5-fold, and the stalling is gene-length-dependent (~0.35% loss/kb), matching endogenous transcription-blocking lesions; the authors estimate >18,000 stalled complexes per aged hepatocyte (Gyenis et al., Nature Genetics, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41588-022-01279-6, PMID 36658433; N=3 WT mice/group for liver EU-seq/ChIP-seq; duration=15 weeks vs 2 years).

📚 The same gradual loss of productive transcription appears in aged mouse kidney and in reanalyzed human tendon RNA-seq (old n=4, 69.5 ± 7.3 y vs young n=4, 19 ± 5.8 y) (Gyenis et al., Nature Genetics, 2023).

📚 Age-related transcriptional failure in that paper is operationalized as RNAPII stalling on endogenous lesions, not as SNV count (Gyenis et al., Nature Genetics, 2023).

📚 UV-challenged human dermal fibroblasts from older donors remove thymine dimers and (6-4) photoproducts more slowly than those from younger donors (P<0.001), with lower ERCC3, PCNA, RPA, XPA, and p53 protein (Goukassian et al., FASEB J, 2000, https://doi.org/10.1096/fasebj.14.10.1325, PMID 10877825; endpoint=photoproduct removal and NER-protein levels).

📚 Endogenous γH2AX foci in peripheral-blood lymphocytes increase with donor age across 26 people aged 21–72, and DSB-repair proteins take longer to reach IR-induced foci as donor age rises (newborn ~10 min vs 61-year-old ~4 h for maximal Mre11 colocalization) (Sedelnikova et al., Aging Cell, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2007.00354.x, PMID 18005250; N=26 lymphocyte donors plus fibroblast strains).

📚 Pretreating adult human fibroblasts with pTT or a p9mer oligonucleotide for 48 h raised NER proteins 200–650% and left fewer thymine dimers at 24 h post-UV (40±5% remaining on diluent vs 20±9% pTT vs 15±11% p9mer), matching newborn repair rates in that dish (Goukassian et al., FASEB J, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.01-0829fje, PMID 11923222).

📚 A flagship SIRT6-as-DSB-resection paper (Kaidi et al., 2010) was retracted after a University of Cambridge investigation found falsified data by the first author (Kaidi, Weinert, Choudhary, Jackson, Science retraction, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aax4558).

🥼 Ordinary point-mutation fixation as the proximal clock of human aging is not settled: López-Otín 2023 states there is no clear evidence that the normal rate of mutation fixation is responsible for aging; Schumacher 2021 argues persistent DNA damage and DDR (not only fixed SNVs) is the unifying driver; Vijg 2021 argues somatic mutations themselves are a universal primary cause and says that claim remains untested (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001; Schumacher et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03307-7; Vijg, Ageing Research Reviews, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.arr.2021.101316).

## Animal data

📚 Annual somatic single-base-substitution rates in intestinal crypts scaled inversely with species lifespan across 16 mammals (208 crypts, 56 individuals); rates ranged from 47 SBS/year (human) to 796 SBS/year (mouse); end-of-lifespan burden varied ~3-fold despite ~30-fold lifespan and ~40,000-fold body-mass variation; inverse-of-lifespan explained 82% of inter-species substitution-rate variance (P=2.9×10⁻⁹); mean end-of-life burden 3,206 substitutions/genome/crypt (95% CI 2,684–3,729) (Cagan et al., Nature, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04618-z, PMID 35418684).

⛔ Reading Cagan et al. 2022 as proof that ordinary point-mutation burden is the master clock of human aging, or as an intervention that lowering adult-human SNV burden extends life, fails the paper’s own design: observational LME on intestinal epithelium only; the authors write that rates “may be a contributing factor in ageing” (Cagan et al., Nature, 2022).

🥼 Ordinary SNV burden is not a prerequisite for shortened lifespan or premature-aging features in DNA-repair-deficient mice: Xpd^m/m mice showed prominent premature-aging features and ~20% lifespan reduction without elevated lacZ mutant frequencies, and Csb−/− mice had mild premature-aging features with no lifespan cut and no elevated mutations, while Xpa−/− and Ercc1−/m did accumulate extra lacZ mutants (Dollé et al., Mutation Research, 2006, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16472827/; N=four NER genotypes crossed to lacZ reporters; liver/kidney through 104 weeks; Ercc1−/m to 6 months).

📚 Heterozygous Polg proofreading-deficient mice carried a ~30-fold higher mtDNA point-mutation burden than the oldest wild-type mice, and young Polg^mut/mut mice ~2,500-fold higher than young wild-type, without rapidly accelerated aging in the heterozygotes; Polg+/mut vs wild-type survival did not differ (log-rank P=0.875; median 758 vs 864 days) (Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17334366/; RMC assays, young WT n=4, old WT n=6, young Polg+/mut n=3).

🥼 Whether Mendelian or engineered repair-defect progerias are ordinary aging at lower dose is a live literature fight, not a settled dose-response: Burtner and Kennedy treat the resemblance as unresolved and as acceleration of a subset of pathologies, not identity with the late-life human clock (Burtner and Kennedy, Nature Reviews Molecular Cell Biology, 2010, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/20651707/).

📜 A 2025 preprint reports *F. vesiculosus* fucoidan activates SIRT6 mono-ADP-ribosylation, represses LINE1, and extended median lifespan 13% in aged male C57BL/6 mice (Gehan-Breslow p=0.009) with a non-significant 3% female change (p=0.12) (Biashad / Gorbunova et al., bioRxiv, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.03.24.645072; chow/water fucoidan starting ~19–25 months).

⛔ Selling that preprint plus a brand assay as human SIRT6-in-a-bottle genome restoration does not survive the missing human tissue target-engagement and mutation-burden data (Biashad / Gorbunova et al., bioRxiv, 2025; DoNotAge SIRT6Activator® product page, https://donotage.org/sirt6-activator; DoNotAge sells the capsule).

## Human data

### Sequence and clones

📚 Human adult stem cells of small intestine, colon, and liver accumulate somatic mutations linearly across life at ~40 novel mutations per year from ages 3–87, reaching thousands per cell in late life (Blokzijl et al., Nature, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature19768, PMID 27698416; clonal organoids; ~40 SBS/year per ASC; spectra tissue-specific).

📚 Across 29 human cell types from the same donors, monoclonal somatic structures accumulate SBS with age at tissue-specific rates, highest in intestinal crypts (~52 SBS/year, 95% CI 48–54) and lower in gastric glands (~25), prostate (~19), pancreatic acini (~15), and bile ductules (~9) (Moore et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03822-7, PMID 34433962; N=389 laser-microdissected patches from donors aged 47/54/78 plus additional testis/colon donors).

📚 Post-mitotic human cortical neurons accumulate somatic SBS at a constant ~2.5 substitutions per year (95% CI 1.7–3.3) without cell division, measured by NanoSeq at <5 errors per billion bp (Abascal et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03477-4; N=frontal-cortex neurons from 8 healthy + 9 Alzheimer donors).

📚 Selected mutant clones colonize physiologically normal human esophagus with age: by middle age more than half the epithelium is mutant-clone territory, with NOTCH1 affecting 12–80% and TP53 2–37% of cells and tens to hundreds of clones/cm² (Martincorena et al., Science, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aau3879, PMID 30337457; N=9 donors aged 20–75, 844 samples, median 870× targeted coverage).

📚 An independent Japanese series of 682 micro-scale esophageal samples from 139 people showed driver-mutant clones (mainly NOTCH1) emerging in childhood, expanding with age, and replacing almost the entire epithelium in the extremely elderly, accelerated by smoking and alcohol (Yokoyama et al., Nature, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0811-x, PMID 30602793).

📚 Detectable clonal mosaicism for large chromosomal anomalies in blood is rare before age 50 (<0.5%) and rises to ~2–3% in the elderly (Laurie et al., Nature Genetics, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2271, PMID 22561519; N=>50,000 GWAS subjects; SNP-array mosaic duplications/deletions/UPD at >5–10% cell fraction).

📚 In a separate GWAS mosaic analysis, autosomal mosaicism of >2 Mb rose from 0.23% under age 50 to 1.91% at ages 75–79 (P=4.8×10⁻⁸) among cancer-free people (Jacobs et al., Nature Genetics, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2270; N=31,717 cancer cases + 26,136 controls).

📚 CHIP as used in hematology is a working definition, not a whole-body genome-age score: a leukemia-driver somatic mutation at VAF ≥2% in blood without a WHO myeloid neoplasm; Steensma et al. set 2% because deeper sequencing finds mutations in nearly everyone and then-available outcome data used that floor (Steensma et al., Blood, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2015-03-631747, PMID 25931582).

📚 In 17,182 unselected people, detectable hematologic-cancer-gene mutations in blood were rare before age 40 and rose to 9.5% at 70–79 (219/2300), 11.7% at 80–89 (37/317), and 18.4% at 90–108 (19/103); presence associated with hematologic cancer HR 11.1 (95% CI 3.9–32.6), all-cause death HR 1.4 (1.1–1.8), incident CHD HR 2.0 (1.2–3.4), and ischemic stroke HR 2.6 (1.4–4.8) (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1408617, PMID 25426837; CVD subset N=3,353).

📚 Blood CHIP VAF is clone size in leukocytes, not a whole-body genome-integrity score (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014).

📚 Libby, Sidlow, and colleagues state they do not recommend CHIP-mutation screening of unselected individuals because there is no evidence base for managing the cardiovascular or other complications (Libby et al., JACC, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jacc.2019.06.007, PMID 31345432).

📜 Six months of metformin titrated to 1,500 mg/day did not significantly change CH VAF versus placebo for any gene, including among 30 participants with DNMT3A R882 (19 on metformin), in an ancillary of Reach for Health (Blood 2025 abstract, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2025-4954; N=332 non-diabetic female breast-cancer survivors; 1,028 CH mutations in 37 genes; baseline–6-month VAF R²=0.971).

### mtDNA in ordinary aged humans

📚 Individual pigmented substantia nigra neurons from ordinary aged humans carry clonally expanded mtDNA deletions that increase with age (P<0.0001, young vs old) and often exceed the ~60% heteroplasmy threshold; COX-deficient neurons from an 80-year-old had higher deletion fractions than COX-positive neighbors (P<0.0001) (Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1778, PMID 16604072; N=80 single neurons, donors 33–102 y).

📚 A companion single-neuron qPCR series found mean deleted mtDNA of 43.3% ± 9.3% in aged-control SN neurons and 52.3% ± 9.3% in Parkinson disease, with COX-deficient cells at 67% vs 48% in COX-positive cells (Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1038/ng1769, PMID 16604074).

🥼 Human SN-neuron mtDNA deletions and mouse Polg point-mutation burden are not the same operationalization; treating them as one “mtDNA aging clock” collapses two lesion classes (Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007).

### Lesion-marker moves and hard endpoints

📚 Six months of calorie restriction in overweight nonobese adults reduced whole-blood comet-assay DNA damage from baseline (CR −0.56 AU [SEM 0.11]; CR+exercise −0.45 [0.12]; VLCD −0.35 [0.12]; all P<.005 vs baseline); the combined interventions were not statistically different from controls (Heilbronn et al., JAMA, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.295.13.1539; N=48 randomized, 4 arms; overweight BMI 25–<30, men <50 y / women <45 y).

📚 A comet-tail-moment drop is a lesion-marker move in sampled blood, not a duplex-sequenced mutation-burden result and not a healthspan trial (Heilbronn et al., JAMA, 2006).

📚 Oral NR 500 mg twice daily for 6 weeks raised blood cellular NAD+ ~60% vs placebo in a crossover RCT of 24 healthy adults aged 55–79 (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03421-7, PMID 29599478; NIAGEN supplied by ChromaDex).

📚 Metabolite target engagement is not reduced tissue mutation burden, fewer persistent lesions, or healthspan via repair (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018).

📚 Oral NR 2,000 mg/day for 12 weeks did not improve clamp insulin sensitivity, endogenous glucose production, glucose disposal, REE, lipolysis, lipid oxidation, or body composition in obese insulin-resistant men, and the trial did not measure mutation burden, persistent lesions, or CHIP (Dollerup et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2018, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29992272/, NCT02303483; N=40 men BMI >30, age 40–70; M-value interaction P=0.71).

📚 A first therapeutic HBO session (100% O2 at 2.5 ATA) induced leukocyte strand breaks and FPG-sensitive oxidized bases in all tested subjects; the comet signal was gone by 6–24 h and did not appear as micronuclei (Dennog et al., Mutagenesis, 1996, https://doi.org/10.1093/mutage/11.6.605, PMID 8962431; Speit et al., Carcinogenesis, 1998, https://doi.org/10.1093/carcin/19.11.1913, PMID 9855002; Dennog n=10 for tail moment, n=7 with FPG).

📚 FDA’s 2019 safety communication states there is no proven clinical benefit of young-donor plasma infusions for aging, memory loss, dementia, Parkinson’s, MS, Alzheimer’s, heart disease, or PTSD, and lists infectious, allergic, TRALI, and circulatory-overload risks of plasma (FDA CBER, 2019, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/important-information-about-young-donor-plasma-infusions-profit).

☠︎︎ Daily beta-carotene 20 mg increased lung-cancer incidence 18% (95% CI 3–36%) and total mortality 8% (95% CI 1–16%) versus no beta-carotene in ATBC (ATBC Study Group, NEJM, 1994, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8127329/; N=29,133 male smokers aged 50–69, southwestern Finland; 5–8 years).

☠︎︎ CARET was stopped 21 months early after a mean 4.0 years: beta-carotene 30 mg plus retinyl palmitate 25,000 IU daily raised lung-cancer RR to 1.28 (95% CI 1.04–1.57; P=0.02) and all-cause death RR to 1.17 (95% CI 1.03–1.33) versus placebo (Omenn et al., NEJM, 1996, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602180/; N=18,314 smokers, former smokers, and asbestos-exposed workers; 388 lung cancers / 73,135 person-years).

☠︎︎ SELECT found vitamin E 400 IU/day increased prostate-cancer risk versus placebo (HR 1.17, 99% CI 1.004–1.36, P=0.008; 620 vs 529 cases; +1.6 cases per 1,000 person-years); selenium 200 µg/day did not prevent prostate cancer (HR 1.09, 99% CI 0.93–1.27) (Klein et al., JAMA, 2011, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21990298/; N=34,887 men in the primary analysis of 35,533 randomized).

📚 Cochrane meta-analysis of antioxidant supplements found no mortality benefit; in 56 low-bias-risk trials antioxidants increased mortality (RR 1.04, 95% CI 1.01–1.07; 18,833/146,320 vs 10,320/97,736 deaths), driven by beta-carotene (RR 1.05) and vitamin E (RR 1.03) (Bjelakovic et al., Cochrane Database Syst Rev CD007176, 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8407395/; N=78 trials / 296,707 participants in the full set).

⛔ High-dose beta-carotene as “DNA protection” in smokers is a garbage claim; the protection framing does not survive ATBC (ATBC Study Group, NEJM, 1994).

🐉 No compiled source is a completed human RCT whose primary endpoint is reduced duplex-sequenced somatic mutation burden or restored multi-tissue genome integrity (flight search result; absence is not a published disproof).

## Measurement

📚 NanoSeq duplex sequencing reports error rates below five errors per billion base pairs — two orders of magnitude below typical adult somatic burdens (~10⁻⁷/bp) — which is what makes tissue mutation-rate measurement possible (Abascal et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03477-4; Lawson et al., Nature, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-09584-w).

📚 Lawson et al. applied targeted NanoSeq to 1,042 oral-epithelium and 371 blood samples from a twin cohort and called >62,000 driver mutations / 46 genes under positive selection in oral epithelium (Lawson et al., Nature, 2025).

📚 NanoSeq is not a consumer “genome age” kit (Abascal et al., Nature, 2021; Lawson et al., Nature, 2025).

📚 OECD TG 489 alkaline comet measures % tail DNA (strand breaks and alkali-labile sites) in isolated nuclei after electrophoresis at pH ≥13; it does not report mutations, clone size, or organismal genome age, and enzyme-modified comet (FPG/OGG1) for oxidized bases is not in the validated TG (OECD, Test No. 489, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1787/9789264264885-en).

📚 OECD TG 487 micronucleus test scores MN in interphase cytoplasm as transmitted acentric fragments or lagged whole chromosomes (clastogen vs aneugen) in cells that divided; it is not a mutation-burden or aging clock (OECD, Test No. 487, 2023 correction, https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/test-no-487-in-vitro-mammalian-cell-micronucleus-test_9789264264861-en.html).

📚 HUMN standardized the lymphocyte cytokinesis-block micronucleus assay (scoring MN, nucleoplasmic bridges, nuclear buds, NDI) so labs can compare chromosomal damage rates (Fenech, Mutation Research, 2003, PMID 12504755; Fenech, Mutagenesis, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1093/mutage/geq051, PMID 21164208).

📚 A later HUMN compilation reported that higher lymphocyte MN frequency predicted cancer risk in a database of almost 7,000 subjects — still a blood chromosomal-damage biomarker, not whole-body SNV burden (Fenech, Mutagenesis, 2011).

📚 ESCODD found published baseline 8-oxoG / 8-oxodGuo in normal human-cell DNA varied over three orders of magnitude across labs and methods; chromatographic methods are prone to artifactual guanine oxidation during workup, while FPG-nicking methods read lower (ESCODD, Carcinogenesis, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1093/carcin/23.12.2129, PMID 12507938; N=25–27 labs).

📚 ESCODD’s later inter-lab round on identical HeLa/lymphocyte samples still left HPLC vs FPG-comet ~6–12-fold apart, with median lymphocyte background between 0.3 and 4.2 8-oxodGuo per 10^6 guanines (ESCODD / Gedik and Collins, FASEB J, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.04-1767fje, PMID 15533950).

📚 Urinary 8-oxodG is a repair-export / excretion marker requiring creatinine correction; ESCULA was stood up specifically because urine assays are not interchangeable with tissue lesion load (Guo et al., review of HPLC-ECD, 2022, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8911600/).

🐉 No compiled source validates a whole-body consumer genome-integrity score with treat-to-target outcome data (flight search result; inference from ESCODD/ESCULA/CHIP definition limits).

📚 Blood mutation or lesion assays are tissue-specific; they do not automatically represent brain, muscle, liver, skin, or stem-cell compartments (Moore et al., Nature, 2021; Abascal et al., Nature, 2021; Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014).

⛔ A clock movement or a leukocyte telomere change sold as genome repair is a vocabulary collision: methylation or telomere biology is not lesion load and not somatic mutation burden (NCT07500649 GrimAge primary; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

## What clinics and self-experimenters are doing

Practice is not efficacy. Doses below are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Hematology CHIP clinics measure selected mutant blood clones by targeted NGS (typical clinical LOD ~2–5% VAF) in people found incidentally on cytopenia, tumor, or germline workups; routine population screening is not the described practice (Weeks and Ebert, Blood, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood.2023022222, PMID 37931207; Ebert’s group developed CHRS and discloses industry ties in ASH papers).

🤔 Mito Health sells Lucence CardioHemeRISK, a 15-gene NGS CHIP panel (DNMT3A, TET2, ASXL1, JAK2, TP53, splicing factors, and others) with claimed LoD 0.05% VAF, SNV sensitivity >92% / specificity >99% vs commercial gDNA standards, and score bands that prescribe 1- or 3-year retesting; the same page states CH is not a guideline-endorsed population screen (Mito Health, https://mitohealth.com/guide/clonal-hematopoiesis-chip-complete-guide; dated 27 Aug 2026; Mito/Lucence sell the test).

🤼 Elective CHIP panels as a longevity product vs hematology-only use is an amateur/clinic split: Mito quotes Topol (*Super Agers*) favoring routine assessment in older adults, and simultaneously says no cardiology/hematology society endorses population screening (Mito Health CHIP guide; Steensma et al., Blood, 2015; Libby et al., JACC, 2019).

🤔 Doctor’s Data sells a first-morning urine 8-OHdG assay (CPT 82542) as “ongoing oxidative damage or stress in the body,” lists aging and cancer among uses, and tells clinicians to retest after identifying sources and assessing glutathione (Doctor’s Data, https://www.doctorsdata.com/DNA-Oxidative-Damage-Assay-urine; 5–7 day TAT; Doctor’s Data sells the assay).

🤔 Longevity IV clinics advertise NAD+ as cellular repair: Peach IV’s 2026 dosage guide lists IV NAD+ 100–1000 mg/session over 2–4 h (beginners 100–200 mg), loading ~weekly × 6 weeks then 500–1000 mg weekly/biweekly/monthly, oral NR/NMN commonly 100–500 mg/day (studies cited up to 1000–2000 mg), and SubQ NAD ~50–100 mg/injection (Peach IV, https://www.peachiv.com/blog-post/nad-dosage-frequency-guide; Peach IV sells the infusions).

🤔 DoNotAge sells SIRT6Activator® (*Fucus vesiculosus* fucoidan) as “the only proven activator of Sirtuin 6” for DNA repair; capsules are 400 mg; advertised daily intakes are 800, 1600 (recommended), or 2400 mg (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/sirt6-activator; DoNotAge sells the capsule).

🤔 NCT07500649 (recruiting) randomizes 60 prefrail men 50–80 to 2.4 g/day fucoidan vs placebo for 6 months with GrimAge as primary endpoint; collaborators include University of Rochester, L’Oréal, and DoNotAge.org; listed outcomes are clocks, inflammation, frailty, and skin — not duplex mutation burden (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07500649, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07500649; primary completion estimated 31 Mar 2027).

⛔ NCT07500649’s primary endpoint is change in blood GrimAge, not tissue mutation burden, persistent lesions, or CHIP (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07500649).

🤔 HBOT packages and young-plasma shops are sold as rejuvenation; the fetched neighbors are first-session leukocyte lesions that resolve by 24 h without micronuclei, and an FDA statement of no proven aging benefit for young-donor plasma (Dennog et al., Mutagenesis, 1996; Speit et al., Carcinogenesis, 1998; FDA CBER, 2019).

🤼 Weekly longevity sirolimus dose and whether to chase grapefruit-juice CYP3A4 boosting are split on Rapamycin News: Cohen 2012 in 138 advanced-cancer patients found 240 mL/day GFJ raised sirolimus AUC ~350% and dropped the weekly dose needed for a target AUC from 90 mg to 25–35 mg; HealthRX and Rapamune labeling treat GFJ as contraindicated, while forum users treat it as a dose-multiplier for 2–6 mg weekly longevity schedules (Cohen et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4410974/; Rapamycin News, https://www.rapamycin.news/t/rapamycin-and-grapefruit-juice/276; HealthRX, https://healthrx.com/rapamycin/food-supplement-interactions).

🤼 The sirolimus / grapefruit fight is nutrient-sensing and PK practice (hallmark 06), not a genomic-instability efficacy result (Cohen et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2012; Rapamycin News thread; HealthRX interaction page).

🤔 Hachmo 2020 / Aviv HBOT telomere marketing, Circulate/Buck TPE-as-longevity pages, TA-65 cycloastragenol product pages, and BioViva / Libella / Minicircle gene-therapy-tourism protocols were not re-fetched this round; they remain briefing-level slogans, not compiled practice doses (Phase 0 briefing; domain search log).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient restoration of youthful repair coordination — SIRT6-like chromatin/LINE1 control, NAD-supported PARP cycles, or mosaic clone clearance — without locking in a tumor program is a mechanistic hope, not a human result (no source for human genome restoration; inference from SIRT6 mouse genetics cited in hallmark reviews, the 2025 fucoidan preprint, and the lesion-vs-mutation fight).

🐉 Allele-specific mtDNA base editing (DdCBE/TALED) correcting selected substitutions in cells, organoids, and animals is not systemic aging therapy and not nuclear-genome restoration; delivery, heteroplasmy, and off-target/bystander edits are the live engineering problems (no compiled primary this round; inference from the briefing’s neighbor-fact and Vermulst’s point-mutation-threshold cut).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body genome-integrity score — duplex burden plus persistent-lesion load plus pathogenic-clone map, sampled from more than blood — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation project, not a stack (inference from NanoSeq method papers plus ESCODD/ESCULA/CHIP definition limits).

🐉 Late-life enhancement of NER or transcription-coupled repair in intact adults might unload Gyenis-style RNAPII stalling without changing SNV count; Dollé’s transcription-coupled-repair mutants dying early without extra lacZ mutants is the reason that experiment is interesting, and also the reason “more repair” can be the wrong knob if the proximal lesion is a transcription block (inference from Gyenis 2023 + Dollé 2006).

## Adjacent hallmarks

📚 Uncapped telomeres are persistent DDR / TIFs — that is hallmark 02, not a mutation-burden result (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 Repair remodels chromatin; LINE1 silencing and clocks live in hallmark 03; a clock move is not a lesion clearance (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; NCT07500649 GrimAge primary).

📚 NAD / PARP / sirtuins / mTOR sit in hallmark 06; raising blood NAD is target engagement there, not genome restoration here (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018).

📚 mtDNA mutations, deletions, and POLG biology overlap hallmark 07 (Kraytsberg et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Bender et al., Nature Genetics, 2006; Vermulst et al., Nature Genetics, 2007).

📚 Persistent DDR, DNA-SCARS, and micronuclei → cGAS are hallmark 08; MICSE reporting standards belong there, not to this assay stack (Schumacher et al., Nature, 2021; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 CHIP and HSC clonal collapse are the stem-cell-exhaustion overlap (hallmark 09) (Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014; Steensma et al., Blood, 2015).

📚 Cytosolic DNA / cGAS–STING and CHIP IL-1β are the chronic-inflammation overlap (hallmark 11) (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Jaiswal et al., NEJM, 2014).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Ordinary human tissues accumulate SNVs, epithelial driver clones, blood mosaicism, and SN-neuron mtDNA deletions with age; some NER and γH2AX kinetics slow; aged mouse liver shows massive RNAPII stalling on endogenous lesions (Blokzijl 2016; Moore 2021; Abascal 2021; Martincorena 2018; Yokoyama 2019; Laurie 2012; Jacobs 2012; Kraytsberg 2006; Bender 2006; Goukassian 2000; Sedelnikova 2008; Gyenis 2023).

🥼 Whether the limiter is ordinary SNV burden, persistent lesions / transcription stress, or selected clones is a live paper fight; extreme repair-defect progerias are not settled as a low-dose version of ordinary aging (Vijg 2021 vs Schumacher 2021 vs López-Otín 2023; Dollé 2006; Burtner and Kennedy 2010; Vermulst 2007).

🐉 No compiled human trial lowered ordinary tissue SNV or persistent-lesion burden and then extended healthspan (flight search result; absence is not a published disproof of the hallmark).

🤔 People are buying NAD IVs, SIRT6 fucoidan, urine 8-OHdG, CHIP panels, HBOT, and plasma under a DNA-repair slogan anyway (Peach IV; DoNotAge; Doctor’s Data; Mito Health; FDA 2019).

☠︎︎ The antioxidant-as-DNA-protection stack has a hard-endpoint record of lung cancer, prostate cancer, and excess death in ATBC, CARET, SELECT, and the Cochrane low-bias set (ATBC 1994; Omenn 1996; Klein 2011; Bjelakovic 2012).
