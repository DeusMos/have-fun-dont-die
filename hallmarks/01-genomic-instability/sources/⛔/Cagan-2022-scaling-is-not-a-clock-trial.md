# Cagan et al., 2022. Somatic mutation rates scale with lifespan across mammals. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-022-04618-z
Used for: Treating comparative crypt SNV-rate/lifespan scaling as master-clock proof or as an intervention result does not survive the paper’s design (one tissue, observational, authors: “may be a contributing factor”).
Mark: ⛔
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 208 intestinal crypts, 56 individuals, 16 mammalian species. Inverse of lifespan explained 82% of inter-species substitution-rate variance (P=2.9×10−9). Mean end-of-life burden 3,206.4 substitutions/genome/crypt (95% CI 2,683.9–3,728.9); ELB varied ~3-fold despite ~30-fold lifespan and ~40,000-fold body mass. Coding-rate analog: 85% variance, ELB 31 coding substitutions/crypt. No adult-human mutation-lowering arm.
Conflict if any: None product. Academic/Sanger comparative genomics; Fight Aging–style writeups launder the scaling into causality.
