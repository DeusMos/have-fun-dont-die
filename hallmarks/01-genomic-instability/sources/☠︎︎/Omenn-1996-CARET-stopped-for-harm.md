# Omenn et al., 1996. Effects of a combination of beta carotene and vitamin A on lung cancer and cardiovascular disease. New England Journal of Medicine.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/8602180/
DOI: 10.1056/NEJM199605023341802
Used for: CARET stopped 21 months early for excess lung cancer (RR 1.28) and all-cause death (RR 1.17) on beta-carotene plus retinol.
Mark: ☠︎︎
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 18,314 smokers, former smokers, asbestos-exposed workers. Beta-carotene 30 mg + retinyl palmitate 25,000 IU daily vs placebo. Mean follow-up 4.0 years; 388 lung cancers; 73,135 person-years. Lung-cancer RR 1.28 (95% CI 1.04–1.57, P=0.02); all-cause death RR 1.17 (1.03–1.33); lung-cancer death RR 1.46 (1.07–2.00). Active intervention stopped 11 Jan 1996, 21 months early.
Conflict if any: NCI-funded multicenter trial.
