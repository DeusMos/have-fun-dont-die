# Dollé et al., 2006. Increased genomic instability is not a prerequisite for shortened lifespan in DNA repair deficient mice. Mutation Research.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/16472827/
DOI: 10.1016/j.mrfmmm.2005.11.008
Used for: Ordinary SNV burden is not a prerequisite for shortened lifespan or premature-aging features in DNA-repair-deficient mice: Xpd^m/m and Csb−/− lacked elevated lacZ mutant frequencies despite aging phenotypes.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Four NER genotypes (Xpa−/−, Csb−/−, Xpd^m/m, Ercc1−/m) crossed to lacZ reporters; liver and kidney mutant frequencies to 52–104 weeks (Ercc1−/m lifespan ~6 months). Xpd^m/m: prominent premature aging, ~20% lifespan cut, no elevated lacZ. Csb−/−: mild features, no lifespan cut, no elevated lacZ. Xpa and Ercc1 did accumulate extra mutants (Xpa: 1-bp deletions then G:C→T:A; Ercc1: rearrangements).
