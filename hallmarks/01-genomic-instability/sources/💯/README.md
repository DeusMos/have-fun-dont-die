# 💯 Settled. Known and not contested.

Textbook-grade facts. Not a single paper. Not a hot fight. Rare.

A document lives here because the **claim** in `report.md` is marked 💯. Do not park a famous paper here to make it look settled. Consensus across the field, with no live dispute, is 💯. One paper, even in *Nature*, is 📚.

## What belongs here

Sources that back uncontested, textbook-grade statements: textbooks, field-standard reviews that state settled biology, consensus references that are not in a fight.

PDFs, html captures, and markdown notes of those sources.

## What does not belong here

- A single published paper or review, even a famous one → [📚](../📚/)
- Preprints → [📜](../📜/)
- Research literature that disagrees → [🥼](../🥼/)
- Forums, clinics, newsletters, podcasts, self-experiments, protocols-in-the-wild → [🤔](../🤔/)
- Amateur / biohacker disagreements → [🤼](../🤼/)
- Circulating claims that do not make sense → [⛔](../⛔/)
- Forward-looking leaps with little or no direct evidence → [🐉](../🐉/)

## File names

Short and source-identifying: `Author-Year-short-slug.ext` (or equivalent). Example: `Alberts-2015-molecular-biology-of-the-cell.pdf`.
