# Laurie et al., 2012. Detectable clonal mosaicism from birth to old age and its relationship to cancer. Nature Genetics.
https://doi.org/10.1038/ng.2271 PMID 22561519
Used for: 📚 Detectable clonal mosaicism for large chromosomal anomalies in blood is rare before age 50 (<0.5%) and rises to ~2–3% in the elderly.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: SNP-microarray data from >50,000 GWAS subjects; detection requires >5–10% cells with the same abnormal karyotype; frequency <0.5% from birth to 50, then 2–3% in the elderly; many anomalies resemble hematologic-cancer lesions; among mosaic subjects without prior hematologic cancer, subsequent hematologic-cancer risk ~10-fold (95% CI 6–18). Counted here as chromosomal-change accumulation with age, not as a screening program.
Conflict if any: None stated in retrieved materials.
