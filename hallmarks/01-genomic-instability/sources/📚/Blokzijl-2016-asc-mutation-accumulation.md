# Blokzijl et al., 2016. Tissue-specific mutation accumulation in human adult stem cells during life. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nature19768 PMID 27698416
Used for: 📚 Human adult stem cells of small intestine, colon, and liver accumulate somatic mutations linearly across life at ~40 novel mutations per year from ages 3–87, reaching thousands per cell in late life.
