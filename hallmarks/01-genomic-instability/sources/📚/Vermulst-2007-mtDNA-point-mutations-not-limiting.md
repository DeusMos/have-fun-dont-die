# Vermulst et al., 2007. Mitochondrial point mutations do not limit the natural lifespan of mice. Nature Genetics.
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17334366/
DOI: 10.1038/ng1988
Used for: Heterozygous Polg proofreading-deficient mice sustained ~30-fold (vs oldest WT) and ~500-fold (vs normal) higher mtDNA point-mutation burden without rapidly accelerated aging; Polg+/mut vs WT survival P=0.875.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: RMC assay at TaqI sites in brain/heart. Young WT 1–3 months n=4; old WT 24–33 months n=6; young Polg+/mut 2.5 months n=3; young Polg^mut/mut n=2. WT mutation frequency rose ~11-fold with age but was >10× lower than prior sequencing estimates. Median survival: Polg^mut/mut 423 d, Polg+/mut 758 d, WT 864 d (log-rank P=0.875 for +/mut vs WT). Authors note large deletions were not captured.
