# Yokoyama et al., 2019. Age-related remodelling of oesophageal epithelia by mutated cancer drivers. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0811-x PMID 30602793
Used for: 📚 An independent Japanese series showed driver-mutant clones (mainly NOTCH1) emerging in childhood, expanding with age, and replacing almost the entire epithelium in the extremely elderly, accelerated by smoking and alcohol.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 682 micro-scale esophageal samples from 139 individuals spanning <20 to >80 years; driver clones multifocal from early childhood; number and size rise with age; near-complete replacement in extreme elderly; smoking and alcohol accelerate; NOTCH1 and PPM1D overrepresented vs esophageal cancer.
Conflict if any: None stated in retrieved materials.
