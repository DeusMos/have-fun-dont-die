# ESCODD / Gedik and Collins, 2005. Establishing the background level of base oxidation in human lymphocyte DNA: results of an interlaboratory validation study. FASEB Journal.
https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.04-1767fje
Also: ESCODD 2002. Comparative analysis of baseline 8-oxo-7,8-dihydroguanine in mammalian cell DNA. Carcinogenesis. https://doi.org/10.1093/carcin/23.12.2129
Used for: 8-oxoGua estimates in human-cell DNA varied over three orders of magnitude; even after ESCODD, HPLC vs FPG-comet remained ~6–12-fold apart — not a validated organismal genome-integrity score.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 25–27 member labs; shared HeLa plus volunteer lymphocytes. Pre-ESCODD published estimates spanned ~1000-fold, largely artefact. Later medians: lymphocyte background between 0.3 (comet/FPG) and 4.2 (HPLC) 8-oxodGuo per 10^6 guanines. Urine 8-OHdG consumer kits inherit this chemistry-plus-handling problem and do not measure tissue mutations.
