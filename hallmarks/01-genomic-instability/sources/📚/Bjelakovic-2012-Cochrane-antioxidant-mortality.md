# Bjelakovic et al., 2012. Antioxidant supplements for prevention of mortality in healthy participants and patients with various diseases. Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews.
https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8407395/
CD007176
Used for: Systematic review: antioxidant supplements had no mortality benefit; low-bias-risk trials increased death (RR 1.04), driven by beta-carotene and vitamin E.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 78 trials, 296,707 participants. Random-effects RR 1.02 (0.98–1.05); fixed-effect RR 1.03 (1.01–1.05). 56 low-bias trials: 18,833/146,320 vs 10,320/97,736 deaths, RR 1.04 (1.01–1.07). Beta-carotene RR 1.05 (1.01–1.09); vitamin E RR 1.03 (1.00–1.05); vitamin C and selenium null. Related GI-cancer review CD004183: antioxidants without significant effect on GI cancers (RR 0.94, 0.83–1.06).
