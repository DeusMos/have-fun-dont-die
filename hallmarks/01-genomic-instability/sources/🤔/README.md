# 🤔 Guys on the internet said it, it makes sense, and it is not actively known to be false.

Forums, clinics, newsletters, podcasts, self-experiments, protocols-in-the-wild. Mechanistically coherent. Cite the thread, clinic page, or writeup.

A document lives here because the **claim** in `report.md` is marked 🤔. Clinic marketing and “protocol” blogs are 🤔 at best, or 🤼 if the scene is split. “What people are doing” can be 🤔 even when efficacy is 📚-null or 🐉. Forum and clinic sources are valid for *practice*, not for *efficacy*.

## What belongs here

Forum-thread captures, clinic pages, newsletters, podcast notes, self-experiment writeups, protocol pages in the wild. HTML, markdown, PDF saves of those.

## What does not belong here

- Settled textbook facts → [💯](../💯/)
- Peer-reviewed papers/reviews not in a fight → [📚](../📚/)
- Preprints → [📜](../📜/)
- Research literature that disagrees → [🥼](../🥼/)
- Amateur / biohacker fight (practitioners disagree; not research papers) → [🤼](../🤼/)
- Mechanism is garbage, or the claim is already contradicted → [⛔](../⛔/)
- Forward-looking leaps with little or no direct evidence → [🐉](../🐉/)

## File names

Short and source-identifying: `Author-Year-short-slug.ext` or `Venue-Year-short-slug.ext`. Example: `Kaeberlein-2023-sirolimus-survey-thread.md`.
