# Gonzales et al., 2023. Senolytic therapy in mild Alzheimer’s disease: a phase 1 feasibility trial. Nature Medicine.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02543-w PMID 37679434 PMC10875739
Used for: 📚 Intermittent oral D+Q for 12 weeks in mild symptomatic AD was feasible: plasma D 12.7–73.5 ng/ml and Q 3.29–26.3 ng/ml in all 5; CSF D 0.281–0.536 ng/ml in 4/5 (CSF/plasma 0.422–0.919%); Q undetectable in CSF; cognition and neuroimaging did not change; CSF IL-6 (p=0.008) and GFAP (p=0.028) rose.
