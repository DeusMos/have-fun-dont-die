# Waaijer et al., 2012. The number of p16INK4a positive cells in human skin reflects biological age. Aging Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2012.00837.x PMID 22612594 PMC3539756
Used for: 📚 p16INK4a-positive cell counts in sun-protected human skin are lower in middle-aged offspring of long-lived families than in their age-matched partners, and higher epidermal p16 tertiles associate with more cardiovascular disease.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=178 upper-inner-arm biopsies (89 Leiden Longevity offspring + 89 partners). Offspring shifted toward lower p16 tertiles in epidermis and dermis. Epidermal p16 tertiles associated with CVD count and medication use after sex/smoking adjustment. Cross-sectional. One stain, not MICSE concordance.
