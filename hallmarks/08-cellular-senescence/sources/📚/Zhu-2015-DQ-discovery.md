# Zhu et al., 2015. The Achilles’ heel of senescent cells: from transcriptome to senolytic drugs. Aging Cell.
https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344 PMID 25754370 PMC4531078
Used for: 📚 Dasatinib (≥50 nM) cut senescent human preadipocyte viability 30–40% by day 3 while proliferating cells expanded 2–5×; quercetin (10 μM) cut senescent HUVEC viability ~50% while non-senescent HUVECs expanded 2–3×; D+Q together cut senescent preadipocytes ~70%; a single D+Q dose in 24-month-old mice improved LV ejection fraction and vascular nitroprusside relaxation; one dose after unilateral 10 Gy leg irradiation restored treadmill endurance at 5 days and 7 months.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: In vitro triplicates, human preadipocytes and HUVECs. Cardiac/vascular n=8 aged males, assayed 5 days after one dose. Irradiation n=6–14, endurance at 5 days and 7 months. Ercc1−/Δ weekly D 5 mg/kg + Q 50 mg/kg for 10–12 weeks, n=7–8, composite aging score down. Endpoint=viability, EF, treadmill, healthspan score.
