## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that persistent senescent-cell burden accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives aging and age-related disease, can be scored, and can be selectively cleared (senolytics) or SASP-modulated (senomorphics) so people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity brands, Mayo-protocol copycats, and clock vendors sell that claim as a product: Qualia Senolytic 1400 mg fisetin + 750 mg Quercefit, six capsules on two consecutive days then ~28 days off; DoNotAge Pure Fisetin 400 mg capsules as 800 mg/day; Life Extension Senolytic Activator 56 mg Bio-Fisetin once weekly; forum D+Q at 100 mg + 1.2–1.5 g; Lee/Orlando cash-pay 50 mg D + 500 mg Q Mon–Wed monthly; TruDiagnostic clocks as the score you titrate against (Qualia Life, https://www.qualialife.com/shop/qualia-senolytic; DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin; Life Extension, https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item02301/senolytic-activator; Rapamycin News, https://www.rapamycin.news/t/has-anyone-used-dasatinib-quercetin-d-q-as-a-senolytic/14448; Lee et al., Aging, 2024; TruDiagnostic, https://www.trudiagnostic.com/truage). Those companies sell the capsules, clocks, or clinic visits they are citing.

📚 López-Otín et al. listed cellular senescence among the 2013 hallmarks and kept it in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark,” a Mayo NCT milligram, or a Unity slide as proof that ordinary human senescent-cell burden can be scored and flushed so people live longer is a taxonomy paper or a product page doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Qualia Life; DoNotAge).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — Hayflick culture arrest, Baker INK-ATTAC median-life extension, Xu transplant/D+Q mice, Hickson adipose-marker drop, Justice 6-minute-walk, Gonzales CSF dasatinib — into “buy the pulse / the clock / the fisetin bottle” (Hayflick and Moorhead, Experimental Cell Research, 1961; Baker et al., Nature, 2016; Xu et al., Nature Medicine, 2018; Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2019; Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019; Gonzales et al., Nature Medicine, 2023).

## What the words mean

💯 Cultured human fibroblasts undergo a finite number of population doublings before replicative arrest (Hayflick and Moorhead, Experimental Cell Research, 1961, https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-4827(61)90192-6).

⛔ Reading the Hayflick limit as “your tissues are full of zombie cells a flavonoid bottle clears” is dish arrest doing a job it was not measured for (Hayflick and Moorhead, 1961).

📚 The International Cell Senescence Association defines senescence as a stress-triggered state with prolonged generally irreversible arrest plus secretory features, macromolecular damage, and altered metabolism, and rejects any single biomarker as sufficient (Gorgoulis et al., Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2019.10.005, PMID 31675495).

📚 MICSE (2024) requires multiplexing: demonstrate arrest (typically p16 and/or p21) then at least two auxiliary features (LMNB1, HMGB1, SASP, DNA damage, β-gal), optionally then a senostatic or senolytic perturbation (Ogrodnik et al., Cell, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2024.05.059, PMID 39121846).

📚 A senolytic preferentially kills some senescent-cell states via senescent-cell anti-apoptotic pathways (SCAPs); dasatinib and quercetin hit different lineages in the 2015 screen and are used as an intermittent pulse, not a daily CML-style exposure (Zhu et al., Aging Cell, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344, PMID 25754370, PMC4531078).

📚 A senomorphic suppresses SASP without requiring cell death; rapamycin blunts SASP by suppressing MTOR-dependent IL1A translation and downstream NF-κB-driven IL-6/IL-8 without reversing the arrest (Laberge et al., Nature Cell Biology, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb3195, PMID 26147232).

⛔ Collapsing senolytic, senomorphic, p16-high, SA-β-gal, SASP cytokines, and a methylation clock into one “zombie-cell count” is a vocabulary collision (Ogrodnik et al., Cell, 2024; Saul et al., Nature Communications, 2022; TruDiagnostic TruAge).

🤔 Clinic English uses “senolytic” for a 100–500 mg daily fisetin capsule, a 1400 mg Qualia burst, or off-label dasatinib (Qualia Life; DoNotAge; Life Extension).

## Mechanism

📚 Genotoxic-stress-induced senescent human fibroblasts and epithelial cells secrete a delayed SASP of inflammatory and matrix-remodeling factors; IL-6 and IL-8 are required for paracrine EMT and invasiveness, and a similar SASP appears in prostate-cancer cells isolated after DNA-damaging chemotherapy (Coppé et al., PLoS Biology, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pbio.0060301, PMID 19053174).

📚 The SASP is heterogeneous — cytokines, chemokines, proteases, growth factors — and can reinforce arrest, recruit immune cells, or promote inflammation and tumor progression depending on context (Coppé, Desprez, Krtolica, Campisi, Annual Review of Pathology, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-pathol-121808-102144, PMID 20078217).

📚 Late-senescent human fibroblasts upregulate LINE-1 RNA 4–5-fold; cytoplasmic L1 cDNA drives IFN-I and SASP, and lamivudine downregulates IFN-I/inflammaging in aged mice (De Cecco et al., Nature, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-018-0784-9, PMID 30728521; already filed under hallmarks/03).

📚 Persistent telomeric DNA-damage foci activate a checkpoint that can lock in replicative senescence (d'Adda di Fagagna et al., Nature, 2003; already filed under hallmarks/02).

📚 Rodent tissue p16Ink4a rises ~10-fold with chronological age across 15 murine organs (geometric mean old/young ratio 9.7) and is attenuated by caloric restriction in kidney, ovary, and heart (Krishnamurthy et al., Journal of Clinical Investigation, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI22475, PMID 15520862).

📚 p16INK4a mRNA in human peripheral-blood T cells rises exponentially with chronological age, changing nearly 10-fold across six decades of adult life (Liu et al., Aging Cell, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2009.00489.x, PMID 19485966, PMC2752333; N=170 healthy donors aged 18–80; Pearson r=0.63 for log2 p16 vs age).

📚 p16INK4a-positive cell counts in sun-protected human skin are lower in middle-aged offspring of long-lived families than in their age-matched partners, and higher epidermal p16 tertiles associate with more cardiovascular disease (Waaijer et al., Aging Cell, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1474-9726.2012.00837.x, PMID 22612594; N=178 biopsies, 89 Leiden Longevity offspring + 89 partners).

📚 Fully senescent p16INK4A+ cells were rare (<1%) in parenchyma and structural stroma of ordinary elderly human colon, small intestine, lung, unexposed skin, liver, and thyroid, with no statistically significant young-vs-elderly difference on IHC; the modest age rise sat in immune cells at ~2% or less in most organs (Park et al., Experimental & Molecular Medicine, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s12276-024-01354-4, PMC11671602).

🥼 A large share of the p16Ink4a / SA-β-gal cells that accumulate with mouse age are F4/80+ macrophages, not locked mesenchymal senescent cells: they are attracted by senescent-cell secretions, killed by liposomal clodronate (which does not kill bona fide genotoxic senescent fibroblasts), and their p16/SA-β-gal is a reversible polarization response (Hall et al., Aging, 2016, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.100991, PMID 27391570, PMC4993332; Hall et al., Aging, 2017, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.101268, PMID 28768895, PMC5611982).

## Animal data

📚 Life-long AP20187 clearance of p16Ink4a-high cells via INK-ATTAC delayed lordokyphosis, cataracts, sarcopenia, and fat loss in BubR1H/H progeroid mice; late-life start at 5 months attenuated further muscle/fat decline without reversing established cataracts; overall survival was not substantially extended because cardiac phenotypes are p16-independent (Baker et al., Nature, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature10600, PMID 22048312, PMC3468323; lifelong function n=6 females/genotype/treatment; late-life n=5–9 females/arm; AP 0.2 μg/g i.p. every 3 days).

📚 Periodic AP20187 ablation of naturally accumulating p16Ink4a-positive cells from 12 months extended median lifespan 24% (C57BL/6, 5% fat) and 27% (mixed background, 9% fat) for both sexes combined, and 17–35% when sexes were split; maximum lifespan rose only in mixed-background sexes combined (p=0.0295); AP on non-transgenic mice did not extend life; tumor latency increased without changing tumor spectrum (Baker et al., Nature, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature16932, PMID 26840489, PMC4845101; AP twice weekly from 12 months until death).

🥼 Baker 2016 is p16-promoter-fragment ablation, not a census of senescent cells: Grosse notes the 2,617-bp promoter construct misses p16-high cells in liver, colon, and T cells, and Hall 2016/2017 show many p16/SA-β-gal cells are reversible macrophages — so the mouse “causal” file does not establish that senescent-cell clearance as opposed to p16-high-cell kill drives aging, and does not establish a human result (Grosse et al., Cell Metabolism, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2020.05.002, PMID 32485135; Hall et al., Aging, 2016; Hall et al., Aging, 2017).

🥼 Continuous or acute genetic ablation of endogenous p16High cells — mostly Cd31+ liver sinusoidal endothelium (>70% of liver p16High at 1 year), plus macrophages and adipocytes — disrupted blood–tissue barriers and produced liver and peri-vascular fibrosis; p16High LSECs were not replaced (Grosse et al., Cell Metabolism, 2020, PMID 32485135; continuous DTA morbidity by ~12 months; collagen rise 1 month after TAM in 1.5-year-old acute-kill mice).

📚 Transient wound-associated senescent fibroblasts and endothelial cells accelerate cutaneous closure via SASP PDGF-AA; ganciclovir kill of p16-3MR senescent cells delayed closure with a peak deficit at day 6, and p16/p21 double-knockout mice showed the same delay, rescued by topical PDGF-AA (Demaria et al., Developmental Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.devcel.2014.11.012, PMID 25482397).

📚 Transplanting 1×10^6 radiation-induced senescent preadipocytes into 6-month-old mice impaired walking speed, hanging endurance, and grip within 1 month; 0.5×10^6 senescent cells into 17-month-old mice raised 1-year mortality hazard 5.2-fold (p=0.006) versus non-senescent transplants (Xu et al., Nature Medicine, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-018-0092-9, PMID 29988130, PMC6082705; young function n=6–8/group; survival n=23 SEN vs 24 CON vs 33 non-transplanted).

📚 Intermittent oral D+Q starting at 24–27 months raised median post-treatment lifespan 36% and cut mortality hazard to 64.9% (p=0.01) versus vehicle, without increasing disease/tumor burden at death (Xu et al., Nature Medicine, 2018; N=71 D+Q vs 76 vehicle; D 5 mg/kg + Q 50 mg/kg, 3 days every 2 weeks).

📚 Dasatinib (≥50 nM) cut senescent human preadipocyte viability 30–40% by day 3 while proliferating cells expanded 2–5×; quercetin (10 μM) cut senescent HUVEC viability ~50%; D+Q together cut senescent preadipocytes ~70%; a single D+Q dose in 24-month-old mice improved LV ejection fraction (n=8 males) and vascular nitroprusside relaxation (Zhu et al., Aging Cell, 2015, PMID 25754370; D 5 mg/kg + Q 50 mg/kg).

📚 Among 10 flavonoids, fisetin was the strongest senolytic on oxidatively senescent Ercc1−/− MEFs and etoposide-senescent IMR90; 500 ppm dietary fisetin from 85 weeks extended median and maximum lifespan in WT C57BL/6:FVB mice (n=8–9/group, log-rank); 20 μM fisetin for 48 h on human omental explants (n=3, ages 55–66) reduced SA-β-gal+ cells and secreted IL-6, IL-8, and MCP-1 (Yousefzadeh et al., EBioMedicine, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2018.09.015, PMID 30279143, PMC6197652; exact % median extension is figure-only).

📚 In the NIA Interventions Testing Program, fisetin at 600 ppm from 20 months in UM-HET3 mice did not extend lifespan on either continuous or 3-days-on / 11-days-off schedules: males Fis_Cyc n=150 median +7% log-rank p=0.54, Fis_On n=142 median −5% p=0.85; females Fis_Cyc n=134 −1% p=0.75, Fis_On n=144 +1% p=0.37; p16 mRNA in kidney/brain/liver was not reduced after 2–4 months on either diet (Harrison et al., GeroScience, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-01011-0, PMID 38041783, PMC10828146; ~286–292 controls per sex, 3 sites).

📚 Genetic p16-3MR / GCV senolysis did not shrink aortic-root or descending-aorta plaque in fat-fed ApoE−/− chimeras; the paper notes prior D+Q had no effect on plaque development or composition; ABT-263 reduced plaque area but also kills macrophages that depend on BCL2-family survival (Garrido et al., Cardiovascular Research, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1093/cvr/cvab208).

📚 ABT-263 / navitoclax cleared senescent HSCs and muscle stem cells in irradiated and naturally aged mice (Chang et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4010, PMID 26657143).

📚 uPAR-directed CAR-T cells ablated senescent cells in vitro and in vivo, extended survival of mice with lung adenocarcinoma given senescence-inducing drugs, and restored tissue homeostasis in CCl4- and diet-induced liver fibrosis (Amor et al., Nature, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2403-9, PMID 32555459; supratherapeutic doses caused transient CRS-like hypothermia).

📚 A single 0.5×10^6 uPAR CAR-T dose in 18–20-month-old mice lowered fasting glucose, improved glucose tolerance (n=11–12), and increased exercise time-to-exhaustion (n=7–8) at 2.5 months; the same dose given at 3 months persisted ≥12 months and acted prophylactically against high-fat-diet metabolic dysfunction (Amor et al., Nature Aging, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-023-00560-5, PMID 38267705, PMC10950785).

📚 A GPNMB peptide vaccine cut Gpnmb-positive cells, improved metabolic and atherosclerotic phenotypes, and extended male progeroid-mouse median lifespan from 21 to 25 weeks (~20%) (Suda et al., Nature Aging, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-021-00151-2, PMID 37162503).

## Human data

📚 A single 3-day oral course of D 100 mg/day + Q 1000 mg/day in diabetic kidney disease reduced adipose p16INK4A+ cells 35% (p=0.001), p21CIP1+ cells 17% (p=0.009), and SA-β-gal+ cells 62% (p=0.005) at day 14 (11 days after last dose), with parallel drops in epidermal p16/p21 and circulating IL-1α, IL-6, MMP-9, and MMP-12; there was no placebo arm and no eGFR or hard kidney outcome (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2019.08.069, PMID 31542391, PMC6796530; N=9, mean age 68.7, eGFR 27.0; NCT02848131).

📚 A 2020 corrigendum states that re-analysis of the Hickson raw data changed some conclusions in that paper (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2019.12.004, PMC6994619; same n=9 dataset).

📚 Intermittent D 100 mg + Q 1250 mg, 3 consecutive days/week for 3 weeks, in open-label IPF improved 6-minute walk +21.5 m, plus 4-m gait speed, chair-stands, and SPPB (all p<0.05); FEV1/FVC and circulating SASP were unchanged (Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2018.12.052, PMID 30616998, PMC6412088; N=14, age 70.8±7.9, NCT02874989).

🥼 A later single-blind IPF RCT (n=12, 1:1) using the same D 100 / Q 1250, 3 days × 3 weeks, found intermittent D+Q feasible but 6-minute-walk and other function measures did not differ meaningfully from placebo (Nambiar et al., EBioMedicine, 2023, PMID 36857968, PMC10006434, NCT02874989; 6MWD change +16.6 ± 20.7 m D+Q vs −6.4 ± 15.9 m placebo).

📚 Intermittent oral D+Q for 12 weeks in mild symptomatic AD was feasible: plasma D 12.7–73.5 ng/ml and Q 3.29–26.3 ng/ml in all 5; CSF D 0.281–0.536 ng/ml in 4/5 (CSF/plasma 0.422–0.919%); Q undetectable in CSF; cognition and neuroimaging did not change; CSF IL-6 (p=0.008) and GFAP (p=0.028) rose (Gonzales et al., Nature Medicine, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-023-02543-w, PMID 37679434, PMC10875739; N=5, mean age 76±5; 6 cycles of 2 days on / 14 off; NCT04063124).

📚 Exploratory highest tertile of T-cell p16 mRNA in a 20-week open-label RCT of intermittent D+Q in postmenopausal women showed +2.7% radius BMD vs control at 20 weeks (p=0.004), +34% P1NP and −11% CTx at 2 weeks; the prespecified primary (CTx at 20 weeks) did not differ (D+Q −4.1% vs control −7.7%, p=0.611) (Farr et al., Nature Medicine, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-03096-2, PMID 38942993; N=60 women >60 y; D 100 mg × 2 days + Q 1000 mg × 3 days every 28 days × 5 cycles).

📚 Intra-articular UBX0101 (MDM2–p53 inhibitor) in 183 people with moderate-to-severe knee OA (single injection 0 / 0.5 / 2 / 4 mg) showed no significant WOMAC-pain reduction vs placebo; the observational follow-up NCT04349956 was terminated after the readout (Lane et al., Osteoarthritis and Cartilage, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.joca.2021.02.077; Brandt, Malone, and Kean, Biomedicines, 2025, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11853018/; NCT04129944). That is a local-translation miss, not a falsification of Baker/Xu.

📚 Intravitreal foselutoclax (UBX1325), a BCL-xL senolytic, was safe in a phase-1 single-ascending-dose study in advanced DME/nAMD no longer considered anti-VEGF candidates (Crespo-Garcia et al., Nature Medicine, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-024-02802-4, PMID 38321220; NCT04537884).

📚 In the sham-controlled BEHOLD phase 2a trial, a single 10 μg IVT UBX1325 dose in DME with prior suboptimal anti-VEGF response produced a +5.6 ETDRS-letter mean difference vs sham at week 48 (95% CI −1.5 to 12.7); primary endpoint was safety (no TEAE-driven discontinuations vs sham) (NEJM Evidence, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1056/evidoa2400009, PMID 40261111, NCT04857996; N=65 randomized 32 vs 33; funded by UNITY, which developed UBX1325). The week-48 BCVA CI crosses zero.

🤔 Unity’s 2025 ASPIRE phase 2b (NCT06011798; 10 μg UBX1325 vs 2 mg aflibercept q8w in previously treated DME) was reported by the company as missing the pre-specified 90% CI non-inferiority primary on BCVA average of weeks 20 and 24; the 2025 DEF 14A states the board ceased clinical development after ASPIRE and pursued dissolution (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06011798, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06011798; SEC DEF 14A, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1463361/000114036125030046/ny20051823x2_def14a.htm). GlobeNewswire mirrors of the 2020 OA and 2025 ASPIRE releases timed out on this run’s fetch; the trial-registry and SEC identities stay.

🤔 AFFIRM-LITE (NCT03675724), a Mayo Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled fisetin frailty trial (20 mg/kg/day × 2 days; estimated n=40, age ≥70), started 2018-11-15 and as of the 2026 ClinicalTrials.gov record is still ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION with primary completion 2026-11 and no posted results (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03675724, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT03675724).

📚 Unformulated oral fisetin has poor human bioavailability: 1000 mg UF gave Cmax 9.97 ng/mL and AUC0–12h 12.67 ng·h/mL, was unquantifiable below 1000 mg, and was only measurable in plasma for ~2 h; geraldol Cmax/AUC exceeded parent (Krishnakumar et al., Journal of Nutritional Science, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2022.72, PMID 36265100, PMC9574875; N=15 healthy crossover; Akay supplied the formulated product).

⛔ Qualia Life Sciences sells Qualia Senolytic (1400 mg fisetin + stack, 2 days / month) while the product page treats Mayo-style fisetin pulses as the human senolytic result; their own open-label proteomics pilot (NCT06953518; Qualia-funded) found no significant change in pre-specified primary SASP proteins TNF, IL-1β, CXCL8, VEGFA (p>0.38 after correction; n=53 paired of 71 enrolled) (Qualia Life, https://www.qualialife.com/shop/qualia-senolytic; Blomquist et al., International Journal of Molecular Sciences, 2026, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27104406; Qualia sells the capsule and funded the paper).

⛔ DoNotAge sells 400 mg fisetin capsules with a recommended daily 400–800 mg serving and copy that fisetin is “shown to support the selective removal of senescent cells”; that daily OTC milligram is not the Mayo 20 mg/kg × 2-day pulse and is not a completed human senolysis trial (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin; DoNotAge sells the capsule).

☠︎︎ Navitoclax (ABT-263) produced Grade III–IV thrombocytopenia in 41% of 39 relapsed-SCLC patients at 325 mg daily after a 150 mg 7-day lead-in — on-target Bcl-xL platelet apoptosis, the dose-limiting toxicity of the leading BCL senolytic (Rudin et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1158/1078-0432.CCR-11-3090, PMID 22496200, PMC3715059).

☠︎︎ Continuous oncology-dose dasatinib (Sprycel) has an established human harm file: myelosuppression; Grade 3/4 fluid retention 5% including 3% Grade 3/4 pleural effusion after 5 years in newly diagnosed CP-CML (n=258); at 100 mg once daily, pleural effusion 28% all-grades / 5% Grade 3/4 in one labeled arm (n=165); plus a labeled PAH warning that can appear after >1 year (Bristol Myers Squibb, SPRYCEL US PI, https://packageinserts.bms.com/pi/pi_sprycel.pdf). That file is continuous oncology exposure, not a published pulsed 2–3-day senolytic safety file.

📚 Justice still recorded one Grade 2 pleural effusion in the n=14 IPF open-label D+Q series (Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019).

## Measurement

📚 MICSE rejects one-stain identification; p16, SA-β-gal, and SASP proteins each occur outside senescence (Ogrodnik et al., Cell, 2024, PMID 39121846).

📚 SenMayo is a literature-derived 125-gene human set (118 mouse) dominated by SASP factors (n=83) plus transmembrane (n=20) and intracellular (n=22) proteins, intentionally leaving p16/p21 out so those can be used as independent validators; it enriched in aged human bone and fell after genetic or D+Q clearance in mouse/human adipose (Saul et al., Nature Communications, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-022-32552-1, PMID 35974106). It is a transcript signature, not a circulating cell census.

🤔 TruDiagnostic’s commercial catalog fetched 2026-09-02 sells OMICmAge / SYMPHONYAge / DunedinPACE methylation clocks and lists “Senolytic Trials (Dasatinib + Quercetin)” as a research service; it does not sell a validated whole-body p16 senescent-cell count (TruDiagnostic, https://www.trudiagnostic.com/truage; TruDiagnostic sells the clocks).

📚 In a Phase I open-label clock study at the Institute for Hormonal Balance (Orlando), 19 people took 50 mg dasatinib + 500 mg quercetin orally Mon–Wed each month for 6 months; first-generation epigenetic and mitotic clocks accelerated at 3 and 6 months and estimated telomere length fell, while second- and third-generation clocks did not move significantly (Lee et al., Aging, 2024, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.205581, PMID 38421953, PMC10929829). TruDiagnostic assayed the DNAm and sells the clocks used as endpoints.

⛔ A blood SASP multiplex, CRP, IL-6, T-cell p16, SenMayo, or a methylation-clock move after a pulse is not a body-wide senescent-cell count (Ogrodnik et al., Cell, 2024; Saul et al., Nature Communications, 2022; Lee et al., Aging, 2024; Park et al., Experimental & Molecular Medicine, 2024).

## What clinics and self-experimenters are doing

Practice is not efficacy. Doses below are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Mayo / Kirkland hit-and-run doses actually used in the published human pilots are D 100 mg + Q 1000 mg × 3 days (Hickson DKD), D 100 mg + Q 1250 mg × 3 days/week × 3 weeks (Justice IPF), and D 100 mg + Q 1000 mg × 2 days every 2 weeks × 6 (Gonzales AD); fisetin trial pulses on the Mayo NCT are 20 mg/kg × 2 days (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2019; Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019; Gonzales et al., Nature Medicine, 2023; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT03675724). Kirkland/Tchkonia do not sell a consumer SKU.

🤔 Qualia Senolytic’s product page lists 1400 mg fisetin + 750 mg Quercefit + a stack per 6-capsule serving, instructed as six capsules on two consecutive days then wait about four weeks; formulator pages cite Mayo NCT 20 mg/kg × 2 days as the 70 kg / 1400 mg alignment (Qualia Life, https://www.qualialife.com/shop/qualia-senolytic; https://www.qualialife.com/the-formulator-s-view-of-the-qualia-senolytic-ingredients). Qualia sells the capsule.

🤔 DoNotAge Pure Fisetin is 400 mg fisetin per capsule, recommended daily serving 800 mg (2 capsules) with fat, sold as senescent-cell clearance support (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin). DoNotAge sells the bottle. Daily 800 mg ≠ Mayo 20 mg/kg pulse.

## What is actually on the table

📚 Some senescent-cell / p16-high populations can be causal in mice: INK-ATTAC median-life +24–27%, senescent-cell transplant HR 5.2, intermittent D+Q +36% late-life survival (Baker et al., Nature, 2016; Xu et al., Nature Medicine, 2018).

🥼 Whether the mouse limiter is senescent cells as MICSE would call them, or p16-high macrophages and indispensable LSECs, is a live paper fight (Hall 2016/2017; Grosse 2020 vs Baker 2016).

📚 Human tissues show age-associated p16 in blood T cells and skin; ordinary elderly parenchyma p16+ cells can be <1% (Liu 2009; Waaijer 2012; Park 2024).

📚 Human senolytic evidence is open-label n=5–14 marker/feasibility (Hickson, Justice, Gonzales), a bone RCT that missed its primary CTx (Farr 2024), an IPF RCT that did not reproduce the walk signal (Nambiar 2023), a 183-person OA senolytic that missed WOMAC (Lane 2021; Brandt 2025), and a retinal BCL-xL shot whose +5.6-letter CI crosses zero (BEHOLD 2025).

📚 ITP fisetin did not extend UM-HET3 lifespan; Mayo AFFIRM-LITE remains unpublished since 2018; unformulated oral fisetin barely appears in plasma (Harrison 2024; NCT03675724; Krishnakumar 2022).

⛔ A Qualia burst, a daily DoNotAge capsule, a 56 mg weekly “activator,” a TruAge move, or “it is a hallmark” is not organismal senescent-cell restoration in healthy people (Qualia Life; DoNotAge; Life Extension; TruDiagnostic; López-Otín 2023).

🤔 People are running Mayo-copied D+Q pulses, Qualia 1400 mg × 2 days, DoNotAge 800 mg/day, Life Extension 56 mg weekly, and half-dose cash-pay D+Q under that slogan anyway (Hickson 2019 protocol; Qualia Life; DoNotAge; Life Extension; Lee 2024; Rapamycin News).

☠︎︎ Navitoclax’s on-target thrombocytopenia and continuous-dose Sprycel pleural effusion / myelosuppression / PAH are established oncology harms; they are not a pulsed-D+Q body count and they are not labeling theater (Rudin et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2012; BMS SPRYCEL PI).
