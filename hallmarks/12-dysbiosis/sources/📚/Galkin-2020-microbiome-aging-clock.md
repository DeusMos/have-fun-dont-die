# Galkin et al., 2020. Human gut microbiome aging clock based on taxonomic profiling and deep learning. iScience.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2020.101199 PMID 32534441 PMC7298543
Used for: 📚 A deep neural net trained on 1,165 healthy adults (3,663 metagenomes, ages 20–90) predicted chronological age from stool taxonomy with MAE 5.91 years on an independent healthy set (~400 profiles / 271 donors) versus 9.27 years for median-age assignment.
