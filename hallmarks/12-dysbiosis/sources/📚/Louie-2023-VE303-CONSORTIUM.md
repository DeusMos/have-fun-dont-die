# Louie et al., 2023. VE303, a Defined Bacterial Consortium, for Prevention of Recurrent Clostridioides difficile Infection. JAMA.
https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.4314 PMID 37060545 NCT03788434
Used for: 📚 Louie et al. randomized 79 high-risk CDI adults to VE303 high dose 8.0×10⁹ CFU/day (n=30), low dose 1.6×10⁹ CFU/day (n=27), or placebo (n=22) for 14 days; high-dose recurrence at 8 weeks was 13.8% vs 45.5% placebo.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Phase 2, 27 US/Canada sites, Feb 2019–Sep 2021. 8-week CDI recurrence. High-dose 8 Clostridia strains. Phase 3 RESTORATiVE303 ongoing; not approved.
