# Guillemard et al., 2010. Consumption of a fermented dairy product containing the probiotic Lactobacillus casei DN-114 001 reduces the duration of respiratory infections in the elderly in a randomised controlled trial. British Journal of Nutrition.
https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114509991395 PMID 19747410
Used for: 📚 In 1,072 free-living elderly (median age 76), 200 g/d fermented dairy with Lactobacillus casei DN-114 001 for 3 months cut mean CID episode duration (6.5 vs 8 days, P=0.008) and cumulative CID days (7 vs 8, P=0.009), especially URTI/rhinopharyngitis (P<0.001); the primary endpoint (cumulative CID count), severity, fever, meds, immune labs, and QoL did not differ.
