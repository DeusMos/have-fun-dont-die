# Boehme et al., 2021. Microbiota from young mice counteracts selective age-associated behavioral deficits. Nature Aging.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-021-00093-9 PMID 37117767
Used for: 📚 FMT from 3–4-month donors into 19–20-month mice (aged yFMT n≈19–21 vs aged oFMT n≈12–16 vs young yFMT n≈14–16 on behavior) reversed selected peripheral/brain immune and hippocampal metabolome/transcriptome differences and attenuated Morris-water-maze learning deficits and raised novel-object exploration (aged yFMT vs aged oFMT P=0.043).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Donors 3–4 mo or 19–20 mo into 19–20 mo recipients. Behavior ns vary by test (Y-maze n=16/15/21; NOR n=14/12/19). yFMT did not restore newborn-neuron survival (n=5/group) or intestinal transit. Selective rescue, not a full rejuvenation.
