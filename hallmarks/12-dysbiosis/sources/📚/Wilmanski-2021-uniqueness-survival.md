# Wilmanski et al., 2021. Gut microbiome pattern reflects healthy ageing and predicts survival in humans. Nature Metabolism.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-021-00348-0 PMID 33795896
Used for: 📚 Gut uniqueness rose with age and, in community-dwelling men 85+, higher uniqueness / lower Bacteroides predicted better ~4-year survival; observational association, not a restore trial.

N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: >9,000 people (Arivale, AGP, MrOS). Survival: MrOS n=706 all community-dwelling and n=256 aged 85+. Bacteroides abundance HR 1.31 (1.02–1.68) age/site/batch-adjusted in 85+. ~4-year follow-up. No intervention.
