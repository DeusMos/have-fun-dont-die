# Stebegg et al., 2019. Heterochronic faecal transplantation boosts gut germinal centres in aged mice. Nature Communications.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10430-7 PMID 31164642
Used for: 📚 Heterochronic faecal transfer or co-housing with 3-month adults increased Peyer’s-patch germinal-centre B cells (and Tfr) in ~21–22-month mice without restoring peripheral lymph-node GCs; cholera-toxin immunization produced a similar gut-restricted rescue.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Aged ~21–22-month C57BL/6 males gavaged with 3-month faeces; also reverse-direction and BALB/c→C57BL/6 adult-to-adult (no GC boost). Endpoint: PP GC B, Tfh, Tfr by flow ~23 days post-FMT. Same-age strain-mismatched FMT did not boost GC. Immune-structure endpoint, not lifespan. Mouse.
