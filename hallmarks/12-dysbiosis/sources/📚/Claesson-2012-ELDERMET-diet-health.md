# Claesson et al., 2012. Gut microbiota composition correlates with diet and health in the elderly. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11319 PMID 22797518
Used for: 📚 In ELDERMET, faecal microbiota from 178 Irish adults ≥65 clustered by residence (community / day-hospital / rehabilitation / long-stay) and by diet; long-stay individuals were less diverse than community dwellers, and loss of community-associated microbiota correlated with frailty, comorbidity, nutrition, and inflammation markers.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=178 elderly (83 community, 20 day-hospital, 15 rehab, 60 long-stay) + 13 young controls; 16S 454 + shotgun; FFQ diet. Diversity lower in long-stay vs community. Composition correlated with frailty, Charlson, MNA, inflammation, faecal metabolites. Cross-sectional. No antibiotics ≤1 month.
