# Wolter et al., 2024. Diet-driven differential response of Akkermansia muciniphila modulates pathogen susceptibility. Molecular Systems Biology.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s44320-024-00036-7 PMC11148096
Used for: 📚 On a fiber-deficient diet, adding A. muciniphila increased C. rodentium susceptibility in mice; the same organism reduced pathogen load on a fiber-sufficient diet.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Gnotobiotic mice, 14-member synthetic community with mucolytic strain dropouts. Context-dependent mucus penetrability and pathogen load. Animal data, not human aging or a DTC capsule trial.
