# Ghosh, Shanahan, and O’Toole, 2022. The gut microbiome as a modulator of healthy ageing. Nature Reviews Gastroenterology & Hepatology.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41575-022-00605-x PMID 35468952
Used for: 📚 Ghosh, Shanahan, and O’Toole review age-linked and disease-linked microbiome shifts as overlapping but distinct, and state that resetting “unhealthy ageing” microbial signals (lost core taxa, gained pathobionts) is a live research program; they also state that human microbiome therapeutics for older people remain a work in progress and that rodent restore results do not automatically export.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Review. No new trial N. Notes few human intervention studies with n>100. Promises targeted restoration of taxa associated with healthier ageing while flagging the export problem from rodents.
