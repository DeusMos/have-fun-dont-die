## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that age-associated microbial community disturbance (dysbiosis) accumulates with ordinary human aging, causally drives aging / frailty / inflammaging, can be scored (diversity, Akkermansia abundance, zonulin, Viome BioAge), and can be restored (probiotics, Akkermansia, FMT, fiber, “gut reset”) so people live longer or healthier.

🤔 Longevity clinics, DTC stool companies, and supplement brands sell that claim as a product: Viome RNA tests plus monthly Precision biotics; Pendulum live *Akkermansia*; The Akkermansia Company pasteurized MucT; Seed DS-01; Ombre / Tiny Health / Floré matched SKUs; GI-MAP / Doctor’s Data / Genova zonulin add-ons; MegaSporeBiotic; butyrate; ARMRA colostrum; oral BPC-157; CellCore parasite kits; Human Microbes and cash-pay FMT outside *C. difficile* (Viome products page; Pendulum; The Akkermansia Company; Seed; Human Microbes orders page; Purety; Sanoviv; RegenStep gut-reset pages). Those companies sell the test, the capsule, or the stool.

📚 López-Otín et al. parked microbiome talk inside intercellular communication in 2013 and added dysbiosis as an integrative hallmark among twelve in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349; already filed under hallmarks/10 and hallmarks/11).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark,” a *C. difficile* recurrence HR, a uniqueness-survival association, or a mouse FMT as proof that ordinary stool composition can be scored and restored so healthy people live longer is a taxonomy paper or a disease product doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; VOWST DailyMed label; Wilmanski et al., Nat Metab, 2021).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — age-linked composition change, Thevaranjan permeability, Bárcena progeria lifespan, Sato isoalloLCA, Vowst/Rebyota colonization resistance — into “buy the test and the stack” (Claesson 2012; Thevaranjan 2017; Bárcena 2019; Sato 2021; FDA Vowst/Rebyota indication language).

## What the words mean

💯 The human gut is colonized by bacteria, archaea, fungi, and viruses that ferment otherwise indigestible carbohydrates and produce metabolites the host can absorb (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Ghosh, Shanahan, and O’Toole, Nat Rev Gastroenterol Hepatol, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41575-022-00605-x, PMID 35468952).

📚 Shanahan, Ghosh, and O’Toole state that a universal taxonomic “healthy microbiome” does not exist; geography, age, diet, drugs, ethnicity, and lifestyle create legitimate variation, and a functional definition is more plausible than a core-taxa list (Shanahan, Ghosh, and O’Toole, Gastroenterology, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2020.09.057, PMID 33039466).

📚 Dysbiosis in the 2023 hallmark paper is an integrative, context-dependent disturbance of microbial ecology and host interaction — not one missing species (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 Hill et al. reaffirm the FAO/WHO probiotic definition as live microorganisms that, when administered in adequate amounts, confer a health benefit on the host, and require strain-level identity plus human evidence for the term (Hill et al., Nat Rev Gastroenterol Hepatol, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1038/nrgastro.2014.66, PMID 24912386).

📚 Knight et al. treat 16S marker-gene sequencing as a fast, cheap, low-resolution community survey and shotgun metagenomics as the higher-resolution route to species/strain and gene content; relative abundances are compositional (Knight et al., Nat Rev Microbiol, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41579-018-0029-9).

📚 Alpha diversity is routinely treated as a health meter; Reese and Dunn stated that this assumption “has not been tested outright” (Reese and Dunn, mBio, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1128/mBio.01294-18, PMID 29991588).

📚 Meta-analysis of 41 healthy-versus-disease comparisons from 27 microbiome studies found mean diversity-disease effect size indistinguishable from zero; 30/41 comparisons showed no significant alpha-diversity difference (Ma, Li, and Gotelli, ISME J, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41396-019-0395-y, PMID 30923323).

📚 “Leaky gut” as a measured property is intestinal permeability (dual-sugar lactulose–mannitol in people; FITC-dextran / ⁵¹Cr-EDTA in animals), not a commercial zonulin ELISA cutoff (Massier et al., Gut, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2020-323026, PMID 33408123; Thevaranjan et al., Cell Host Microbe, 2017).

🥼 Fasano’s Gut reply accepts commercial-ELISA unreliability but argues Massier et al. over-clears the zonulin field; the paper fight is assay identity, not whether dual-sugar tests exist (Fasano, Gut, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2020-323367; Massier et al., Gut, 2021).

📚 FDA indication language for Vowst (fecal microbiota spores, live-brpk; SER-109) is prevention of recurrent *Clostridioides difficile* infection in adults ≥18 after antibacterial treatment for rCDI; it is not indicated for treatment of CDI, let alone aging (VOWST DailyMed, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=99e5a37a-930c-4641-bcdd-7013ec1c15fe; FDA analysis in the approval record: recurrence 12.4% vs 39.8% at 8 weeks).

📚 FDA indication language for Rebyota (fecal microbiota, live-jslm; RBX2660) is prevention of recurrent CDI in individuals ≥18 following antibiotic treatment for recurrent CDI; single 150 mL rectal suspension (FDA Rebyota page / Ferring approval 30 Nov 2022).

🤔 Clinic English uses “dysbiosis” for low Shannon, missing *Akkermansia*, a zonulin tile, or a Viome Gut Dysbiosis score (Viome; GI-MAP; Pendulum).

## Mechanism

📚 Ghosh, Shanahan, and O’Toole review age-linked and disease-linked microbiome shifts as overlapping but distinct, and state that resetting “unhealthy ageing” microbial signals is a live research program; rodent restore results do not automatically export (Ghosh, Shanahan, and O’Toole, Nat Rev Gastroenterol Hepatol, 2022).

📚 DeJong, Surette, and Bowdish stated that human age–microbiome differences are not cleanly separable from diet, drugs, motility, immunosenescence, and comorbidity, and that identifying causal direction in humans has been the unsolved problem (DeJong, Surette, and Bowdish, Cell Host Microbe, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chom.2020.07.013, PMID 32791111).

📚 Bradley and Haran reviewed that gut composition is shaped from birth by delivery, diet, geography, drugs, and aging itself; chronological-age “dysbiosis” is not a single defect you can score off a stool kit (Bradley and Haran, Gut Microbes, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1080/19490976.2024.2359677, PMID 38813960, PMC11152108).

📚 In 1,106 Flemish plus 1,135 Dutch adults, stool consistency had the largest single-covariate microbiome effect size and medication explained the largest total variance and most covariate–microbiome interactions (Falony et al., Science, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aad3503, PMID 27126039; N=1,106 + 1,135; cross-sectional).

📚 Bristol stool scale, a transit-time proxy, strongly associated with richness, enterotype, and inferred bacterial growth rates in 53 women; looser stools tracked *Bacteroides* and harder stools tracked Ruminococcaceae (Vandeputte et al., Gut, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1136/gutjnl-2015-309618, PMID 26069274).

📚 In C57BL/6 mice, intestinal FITC-dextran permeability rose from 3 to 18 months (n=4–8/age; p<0.007); old SPF plasma had more muramyl dipeptide; old germ-free mice lacked the IL-6 rise; germ-free mice colonized ≥6 weeks with old SPF microbiota (n=23) had higher paracellular permeability than those given young microbiota (n=13) (Thevaranjan et al., Cell Host Microbe, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chom.2017.03.002, PMID 28407485; colonization ≥6 weeks). Hallmark 11 owns the cytokine broadcast; this hallmark owns the community-to-barrier step.

📚 Fiber-free gnotobiotic mice colonized with a 14-member human synthetic community expanded mucin degraders including *A. muciniphila*, thinned colonic mucus ~5-fold versus fiber-rich diet, and developed lethal *Citrobacter rodentium* colitis (60% euthanized by day 10 vs 0% on fiber-rich) (Desai et al., Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2016.10.043, PMID 27863247).

📚 On a fiber-deficient diet, adding *A. muciniphila* increased *C. rodentium* susceptibility in mice; the same organism reduced pathogen load on a fiber-sufficient diet (Wolter et al., Mol Syst Biol, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s44320-024-00036-7, PMC11148096). More *Akkermansia* is not a monotonic barrier fix.

## Animal data

📚 Wild-type faecal transplant into *Lmna*G609G/G609G progeria mice (n=11 vs 11 untransplanted) raised median lifespan 13.5% (160 vs 141 days; Cox HR 0.2, 95% CI 0.07–0.53, P=0.0012) and maximal survival 9% (P=0.04); the same direction held in *Zmpste24*−/− (median +13.4%, 279 vs 246 days, P=0.0092); oral *Akkermansia muciniphila* (n=9 vs 12) extended survival (log-rank P=0.0163; HR 0.31, 95% CI 0.11–0.86) (Bárcena et al., Nat Med, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0504-5, PMID 31332389).

📚 Recolonizing 9.5-week African turquoise killifish with 6-week-old gut contents after antibiotics raised median lifespan 41% vs same-age transplant (log-rank P=5.08×10⁻⁶), 37% vs untreated (P=4.04×10⁻⁹), and 21% vs antibiotics alone (P=5.89×10⁻⁵); young-microbiota fish stayed more active at 16 weeks (Smith et al., eLife, 2017, https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.27014, PMID 28826469).

📚 FMT from 3–4-month donors into 19–20-month mice reversed selected peripheral/brain immune and hippocampal metabolome/transcriptome differences and attenuated Morris-water-maze learning deficits and raised novel-object exploration (aged yFMT vs aged oFMT P=0.043) (Boehme et al., Nat Aging, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-021-00093-9, PMID 37117767; group n ≈12–21).

📚 Heterochronic faecal transfer or co-housing with 3-month adults increased Peyer’s-patch germinal-centre B cells in ~21–22-month mice without restoring peripheral lymph-node GCs (Stebegg et al., Nat Commun, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-10430-7, PMID 31164642; ~23 days post-gavage).

📚 FMT from aged donors into young adult mice raised Barnes-maze primary latency (41.7 ± 3.5 s vs 30.5 ± 3.9 s age-matched FMT and 23.5 ± 6.7 s untreated; P<0.05) and lowered novel-object discrimination (0.23 ± 0.04 vs 0.46 ± 0.05; P<0.01) without changing gut permeability or systemic cytokines (D’Amato et al., Microbiome, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1186/s40168-020-00914-w, PMID 33004079).

⛔ Reading Thevaranjan, Bárcena, Boehme, Stebegg, Smith, or D’Amato as proof that a young person’s stool extends an older person’s life fails the papers’ own design: animal transfer probes, not human aging RCTs.

## Human data

📚 In ELDERMET, faecal microbiota from 178 Irish adults ≥65 clustered by residence (community / day-hospital / rehabilitation / long-stay) and by diet; long-stay individuals were less diverse than community dwellers, and loss of community-associated microbiota correlated with frailty, comorbidity, nutrition, and inflammation markers (Claesson et al., Nature, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11319, PMID 22797518; N=178 elderly + 13 young; cross-sectional).

📚 In 728 community-dwelling female twins (mean age 63), frailty index negatively associated with gut alpha diversity after age, diet, alcohol, smoking, BMI, and technical covariates; 637 OTUs associated with FI (FDR 5%), and 60 of 96 mappable OTUs replicated in ELDERMET (n=280) (Jackson et al., Genome Med, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1186/s13073-016-0262-7, PMID 26822992).

📚 Across Arivale, MrOS, and American Gut (>9,000 people, ages 18–101), genus-level Bray–Curtis uniqueness rose from midlife; in community-dwelling MrOS men ≥85 (N=256, ~4-year follow-up), multivariable-adjusted all-cause mortality HR was 1.91 (95% CI 1.38–2.66) per SD higher relative *Bacteroides* and 0.70 (0.50–0.99) per SD higher genus uniqueness (Wilmanski et al., Nat Metab, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-021-00348-0, PMID 33795896). Observational association, not a restore trial; uniqueness is the opposite of “buy back a young core community.”

📚 Japanese centenarians (n=160, mean age 107) had a distinct faecal community enriched for secondary bile-acid producers and isoforms of lithocholic acid including isoalloLCA; *Odoribacteraceae* isolates made isoalloLCA, which inhibited Gram-positive pathogens including *C. difficile* and *E. faecium* in vitro and in mice (Sato et al., Nature, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-021-03832-5, PMID 34325466). Observational enrichment plus biochemistry, not an install-those-taxa trial.

📚 A deep neural net trained on 1,165 healthy adults (3,663 metagenomes, ages 20–90) predicted chronological age from stool taxonomy with MAE 5.91 years on an independent healthy set versus 9.27 years for median-age assignment (Galkin et al., iScience, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2020.101199, PMID 32534441; Insilico Medicine sells aging-clock products).

⛔ Viome employees’ stool-metatranscriptome “aging clock” (90,303 customer stools) predicted chronological age at R² ≈ 0.46; neither taxonomic richness nor diversity improved the model, and the paper frames diet/supplement “therapeutics” while Viome sells BioAge plus Precision Probiotics/Prebiotics (Gopu et al., iScience, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2023.108538, PMID 38213626). Age prediction is not healthspan.

📚 PROMOTe’s primary endpoint, 5× chair-rise time, did not differ after 12 weeks of inulin/FOS versus maltodextrin in 36 twin pairs aged ≥60 who all received BCAA plus resistance exercise (β = 0.579; 95% CI −1.080 to 2.239; p = 0.494 ITT) (Ni Lochlainn et al., Nat Commun, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-024-46116-y, NCT04309292; N=72).

📚 The same PROMOTe trial’s cognitive-factor move was a secondary endpoint (β = −0.482; 95% CI −0.813 to −0.141; p = 0.014) with higher missingness than physical outcomes (Ni Lochlainn et al., Nat Commun, 2024).

📚 In 60 adults ≥65 with frailty syndrome, 13 weeks of inulin + FOS vs maltodextrin did not change overall frailty rate but improved exhaustion (P<0.01) and handgrip (P<0.05); cognition, function, and sleep were null (Buigues et al., Int J Mol Sci, 2016, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms17060932, PMID 27314331, PMC4926465; Bonusan sells the product tested).

📚 In 1,072 free-living elderly (median age 76), 200 g/d fermented dairy with *Lactobacillus casei* DN-114 001 for 3 months cut mean common-infectious-disease episode duration (6.5 vs 8 days, P=0.008) but missed the primary endpoint (cumulative CID count); severity, fever, meds, immune labs, and QoL did not differ (Guillemard et al., Br J Nutr, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1017/S0007114509991395, PMID 19747410; N=537 vs 535; Danone funded).

📚 Meta-analysis of 15 RCTs (5,916 older adults, mean age 75.21) found probiotics no different from placebo for infection occurrence (RR 0.90, 95% CI 0.76–1.08) or mortality (RR 1.09, 0.70–1.72) (Wachholz et al., Age Ageing, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1093/ageing/afy006, PMID 29471390).

📚 A systematic review of 18 studies of probiotics/prebiotics/synbiotics in older people found little evidence of benefit on physical function, frailty, mood, mortality, or length of stay; cognition signals were limited to people who already had cognitive impairment (Coutts et al., Eur Geriatr Med, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1007/s41999-020-00396-x, PMID 33025313).

📚 Live AKK-WST01 for 12 weeks in 58 overweight/obese, drug-naïve T2D adults missed between-group body weight and HbA1c versus placebo; any signal sat in a post-hoc low-baseline-*Akkermansia* subgroup (Zhang et al., Cell Metab, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2024.12.010, NCT04797442).

📚 Daily 30 billion pasteurized *A. muciniphila* MucT for 4 months in 142 adults with metabolic syndrome missed the prespecified primary Matsuda whole-body insulin-sensitivity endpoint versus placebo in the ITT set; A-Mansia Biotech / The Akkermansia Company sponsored the trial and sells pasteurized MucT (Suenaert et al., Gut Microbes, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1080/19490976.2026.2690689, PMID 42343233, NCT05114018). Metabolic ITT miss, not a healthspan result.

📚 Louie et al. randomized 79 high-risk CDI adults to VE303 high dose 8.0×10⁹ CFU/day (n=30), low dose 1.6×10⁹ CFU/day (n=27), or placebo (n=22) for 14 days; high-dose recurrence at 8 weeks was 13.8% vs 45.5% placebo (Louie et al., JAMA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1001/jama.2023.4314, PMID 37060545, NCT03788434; Vedanta sells the candidate). Disease indication, not aging.

📚 VE303 is in Phase 3 RESTORATiVE303 for prevention of recurrent *C. difficile* (NCT06237452), not an aging or healthspan product (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06237452).

📚 ARMOR (NCT06649981) is an active-not-recruiting Phase I young-donor lyophilized oral FMT protocol in community-dwelling adults 65–84 (planned n=92; four 600 mg capsules, 2.4 g, one supervised session after short rifaximin); primary completion 30 Sep 2026; no efficacy tables posted (Astudillo-Guerrero et al., BMC Geriatr, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12877-025-06920-7; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06649981).

🐉 No compiled human trial lowered ordinary age-associated dysbiosis — as a scored stool defect — and then extended healthspan or lifespan (flight search result; ARMOR is the registered aging FMT trial and has no outcomes as of this pass).

☠︎︎ Oral FMT capsules from one unscreened-for-ESBL donor transmitted clonal ESBL *E. coli* bacteremia to two immunocompromised trial recipients; the 73-year-old allogeneic-HCT recipient died of severe sepsis 10 days after the last capsule (DeFilipp et al., NEJM, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1910437, PMID 31646575; 2 index bacteremias / 22 recipients of that donor; 40% of 12 additional tested recipients grew ESBL *E. coli*).

☠︎︎ FDA’s 13 June 2019 safety communication recorded MDRO transmission by investigational FMT, including the DeFilipp infections, and mandated ESBL / VRE / CRE / MRSA donor-stool testing for all IND FMT (FDA, 2019, https://www.fda.gov/safety/medical-product-safety-information/fecal-microbiota-transplantation-safety-communication-risk-serious-adverse-reactions-due).

☠︎︎ FDA’s March 2020 alerts recorded 6 OpenBiome-linked infections after FMT for *C. difficile* (2 EPEC, 4 STEC; 4 hospitalizations) plus 2 deaths after stool from the STEC-associated donor; one retained donor aliquot later tested STEC-positive by NAAT (FDA, 2020, https://www.fda.gov/vaccines-blood-biologics/safety-availability-biologics/update-march-12-2020-safety-alert-regarding-use-fecal-microbiota-transplantation-and-risk-serious).

📚 Two FMT recipients developed norovirus gastroenteritis after colonoscopic FMT for *C. difficile* despite asymptomatic donors and no sick contacts (Schwartz et al., Am J Gastroenterol, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/ajg.2013.164, PMID 23912408).

☠︎︎ ICU patients given *L. rhamnosus* GG probiotic had *Lactobacillus* bacteremia in 6/522 (1.1%) versus 2/21,652 (0.009%) untreated ICU patients (p = 4.8×10⁻⁹); whole-genome phylogeny made the six treated-patient blood isolates inseparable from capsule LGG (Yelin et al., Nat Med, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-019-0626-9, PMID 31700189; Boston Children’s ICU, typically tube-fed LGG; 5.5 years).

## Measurement

📚 Commercial CUSABIO and Immundiagnostik “zonulin” ELISAs did not capture recombinant zonulin/pre-haptoglobin-2; immunoprecipitation–MS matched haptoglobin and complement C3 (CUSABIO) and complement C3 (Immundiagnostik) (Ajamian et al., PLoS One, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0210728, PMID 30640933; N=36 NCWS / 37 celiac / 20 UC / 49 healthy plus assay biochemistry).

📚 Immundiagnostik zonulin ELISA signals in 376 Sorbs did not track HP genotype, failed to detect recombinant pre-HP2, and the captured protein was identified as properdin rather than zonulin (Scheffler et al., Front Endocrinol, 2018, https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2018.00022, PMID 29459849).

📚 Massier, Blüher, Chakaroun, and Kovács concluded that commercial zonulin ELISA measures unknown proteins, correlates poorly with lactulose–mannitol permeability, and cannot be used to infer zonulin/pre-HP2 biology or leaky-gut diagnosis (Massier et al., Gut, 2021).

🤔 Immundiagnostik still sells IDK Zonulin stool ELISA K 5600 and serum ELISA K 5601 (range 0.25–16 ng/ml); the serum IFU now says “zonulin family peptides (ZFP)” and still frames leaky gut (Immundiagnostik, https://www.immundiagnostik.com/en/portfolio/testkits/k-5600; https://www.immundiagnostik.com/en/portfolio/testkits/k-5601).

🤔 CUSABIO lists Human zonulin ELISA CSB-EQ027649HU as a sandwich assay, range 0.625–40 ng/ml — the kit class Ajamian tested (CUSABIO, https://www.cusabio.com/ELISA-Kit/Human-zonulin-ELISA-kit-154647.html).

📚 Seven unnamed DTC gut-microbiome services given the same NIST homogenized fecal standard returned taxonomic profiles whose between-company Bray–Curtis spread matched or exceeded between-donor biological spread; only 3 of 1,208 reported genera were shared across all seven, and one company called the identical material “healthy” on two kits and “unhealthy” on the third (Servetas et al., Commun Biol, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42003-025-09301-3; N=7 companies × 3 kits).

⛔ A consumer diversity / BioAge / *Akkermansia* / zonulin tile is not a validated treat-to-target aging score (Servetas 2026; Gopu 2024; Ajamian 2019; Wilmanski 2021 uniqueness vs “young core”).

## Speculative

🐉 Transient restoration of a missing microbial function — secondary bile-acid transformation, colonization resistance, or a defined metabolite — without transferring a resistome or an obesity-associated community is a mechanistic hope sitting on Sato biochemistry, VE303-class defined consortia, and ARMOR’s unanswered protocol, not a human aging result (inference from Sato 2021 + Louie 2023 + Astudillo-Guerrero 2026).

🐉 Young-donor oral FMT that moves GDLAM autonomy or grip in ARMOR-class adults would be the first human aging-adjacent functional signal; a diversity move or an *Akkermansia* bump would not be that signal (inference from NCT06649981 endpoints vs Servetas/Wilmanski measurement limits).

🐉 A treat-to-target whole-body microbiome-age score — strain plus function plus host permeability, sampled from more than one stool kit — does not exist as a validated product; building one is an instrumentation project, not a Viome subscription (inference from Servetas 2026 + Gopu 2024 + Shanahan 2021).

🐉 Context-dependent *Akkermansia* (mucus reinforcement vs erosion) plus a fiber-sufficient niche might reproduce Bárcena’s modest progeria-mouse lifespan move without a full FMT; Desai/Wolter are why “more MucT” can be the wrong knob (inference from Bárcena 2019 + Desai 2016 + Wolter 2024 + Suenaert 2026 ITT miss).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Gut composition and uniqueness change with age; frail/institutionalized adults often lose diversity; some centenarians carry distinctive bile-acid functions; uniqueness and lower *Bacteroides* predicted ~4-year survival in MrOS men ≥85 (Claesson 2012; Jackson 2016; Wilmanski 2021; Sato 2021).

📚 Animal transfers can move permeability, immunity, cognition, and — in progeria mice and killifish — lifespan (Thevaranjan 2017; Bárcena 2019; Smith 2017; Boehme 2021; Stebegg 2019; D’Amato 2020).

📚 Microbiota restoration is a genuine medical technology for recurrent *C. difficile* (Vowst 12.4% vs 39.8% recurrence; VE303 high-dose 13.8% vs 45.5%); those labels are not aging indications (VOWST DailyMed; Louie 2023).

🥼 Whether ordinary human aging is driven by a restorable stool defect is a live paper fight: uniqueness/centenarian-function vs frailty/diversity-loss readings of the same literature, plus cause-versus-consequence still unsolved in intact people (Wilmanski 2021 vs Claesson/Jackson; DeJong 2020; Shanahan 2021).

🐉 No compiled human trial restored a scored aged microbiome and then extended healthspan (flight search result).

🤔 People are buying Viome BioAge subscriptions, Pendulum / MucT / Seed / MegaSpore / butyrate / colostrum / BPC-157 / parasite kits, zonulin add-ons, and young-donor stool anyway (Viome; Pendulum; The Akkermansia Company; Seed; Human Microbes; GI-MAP; RegenStep).

☠︎︎ Investigational and donor-stool FMT has a hard-endpoint record of ESBL bacteremia and death, STEC/EPEC outbreaks, and — for LGG in ICU patients — clonal probiotic bacteremia (DeFilipp 2019; FDA 2019; FDA 2020; Yelin 2019).
