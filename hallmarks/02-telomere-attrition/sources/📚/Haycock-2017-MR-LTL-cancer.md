# Telomeres Mendelian Randomization Collaboration / Haycock et al., 2017. Association Between Telomere Length and Risk of Cancer and Non-Neoplastic Diseases: A Mendelian Randomization Study. JAMA Oncology.
https://doi.org/10.1001/jamaoncol.2016.5945 PMID 28241208
Used for: 📚 Mendelian randomization of germline LTL instruments associated longer telomeres with higher risk of several cancers — glioma OR 5.27 (3.15–8.81), lung adenocarcinoma OR 3.19 (2.40–4.22), melanoma OR 1.87 (1.55–2.26), bladder OR 2.19 (1.32–3.66), kidney OR 1.55 (1.08–2.23) per 1-SD genetically longer LTL — and lower CAD (OR 0.78, 0.67–0.90) and interstitial lung disease (OR 0.09, 0.05–0.15).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 420,081 cases + 1,093,105 controls; 35 cancers + 48 non-neoplastic diseases; OR per 1-SD genetically longer LTL; GWAS to Jan 2015; cross-sectional MR.
