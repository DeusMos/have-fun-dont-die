# Mannick et al., 2018. TORC1 inhibition enhances immune function and reduces infections in the elderly. Sci Transl Med.
https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aaq1564 PMID 29997249
Used for: 📚 In 264 adults ≥65, 6 weeks of RAD001 0.1 mg + BEZ235 10 mg daily cut fitted annualized self-reported infections to 1.49/person-year (95% CI 1.19–1.86) vs placebo 2.41 (2.00–2.90), P=0.001, and improved influenza vaccine GMT plus antiviral gene expression.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Phase 2a double-blind RCT; 12 sites; 6 weeks drug, infections captured for 1 year. BEZ235 monotherapy also reduced infections (1.61/py, P=0.008). Clinical infection rate, still not disability-free survival. RTB101 Phase 3 null is adjacent (invalidator).
