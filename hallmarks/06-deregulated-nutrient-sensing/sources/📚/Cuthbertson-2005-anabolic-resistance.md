# Cuthbertson, Smith, Babraj, et al., 2005. Anabolic signaling deficits underlie amino acid resistance of wasting, aging muscle. FASEB Journal.
https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.04-2640fje PMID 15596483
Used for: Aged human muscle activates mTOR/S6K1/4E-BP1 less after essential amino acids than young muscle; the tissue-level aging defect in muscle is too little anabolic mTOR response, not a license to suppress mTOR forever.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: N=44 healthy young vs old men. Basal MPS indistinguishable. Elderly: reduced MPS sensitivity/responsiveness to EAA and reduced mTOR-pathway expression/phosphorylation. Insulin clamped at basal. Acute feeding study.
Conflict if any: None. Does not say weekly rapamycin is proven sarcopenic in humans; it breaks the “turn mTOR off = restore youth” slogan.
