# Keys, Melbye, Hjelmborg, et al., 2022. Reassessing the evidence of a survival advantage in Type 2 diabetes treated with metformin compared with controls without diabetes: a retrospective cohort study. International Journal of Epidemiology.
https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyac200 PMID 36286820
Used for: Danish registers did not replicate Bannister 2014; metformin-monotherapy initiation associated with higher mortality vs no diabetes after comorbidity adjustment.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Danish National Health Registers 1996–2012, singletons + discordant twins. Unadjusted IRR 1.52 (1.37–1.68) singletons, 1.90 (1.35–2.67) twins. Adjusted 1.32 (1.16–1.50) and 1.64 (1.10–2.46). Nested case-control and matched cohort. Not an RCT in metabolically healthy adults.
