## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that nutrient-sensing — insulin/IGF-1, mTOR, AMPK, sirtuins/NAD, and the fed/fasted switches around them — becomes mistimed or tissue-wrong with ordinary human aging, that this deregulation causally drives aging, and that it can be scored and restored.

🤔 Longevity clinics, supplement brands, and protocol culture sell that claim as a product: AgelessRx weekly sirolimus (titrate 2→6 mg) plus metformin 500 mg plus NAD nasal 30 mg plus TruAge; Healthspan 3→5–8 mg weekly at $64/mo; HealthRX 1–6 / 3–10 mg weekly with GFJ listed as contraindicated; Kaeberlein-survey 6 mg modal weekly; Rapamycin News GFJ boosting; TAME press; Bannister “metformin outlives”; Elysium Basis NR; DoNotAge SIRT6/NMN/berberine; Peach IV NAD; Quicksilver AMPK Charge+; ProLon / 16:8 as the switch (AgelessRx rapamycin and TruAge pages; Healthspan Rapamycin Protocol; HealthRX weekly-dosing pages; Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023; Rapamycin News GFJ thread; AFAR TAME page; Bannister et al., 2014; Elysium Basis; DoNotAge; Peach IV; Quicksilver; ProLon). Those companies sell the prescriptions, clocks, kits, or capsules they are citing.

📚 López-Otín et al. listed deregulated nutrient sensing among the 2013 hallmarks and kept IIS, mTOR, AMPK, and sirtuins as the named axes in 2023; the listing criteria are age-association, experimental accentuation, and intervention opportunity — not a completed human causal trial (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.05.039, PMID 23746838; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2022.11.001, PMID 36599349).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that turning mTOR down, raising NAD, or swallowing metformin restores youthful sensing in intact people is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 The slogan launders nearby true facts — daf-2/FOXO genetics, ITP rapamycin/acarbose/canagliflozin, CALERIE cardiometabolic deltas, Mannick Phase 2 flu titers, indicated GLP-1/SGLT2 outcomes — into “buy the stack / weekly 6 mg / TAME already happened” (Kenyon et al., Nature, 1993; Harrison et al., Nature, 2009; Kraus et al., Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol, 2019; Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2014).

## What the words mean

💯 Nutrient sensing is the network that asks whether fuel, amino acids, and cellular energy are up or down and then allocates growth, storage, repair, or catabolism (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 Insulin/IGF-1 signaling (IIS) reports carbohydrate/energy and growth status through PI3K–AKT, inhibits FOXO, and feeds mTORC1 (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 mTORC1 integrates amino acids, insulin/growth factors, oxygen, and lysosomal status to drive protein/lipid/nucleotide synthesis and restrain autophagy; mTORC2 regulates AKT, cytoskeleton, and survival and is less acutely rapamycin-sensitive (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Lamming et al., Science, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1215135, PMID 22461615).

💯 AMPK detects high AMP/ADP relative to ATP, promotes catabolism and mitochondrial adaptation, and restrains mTORC1 (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

💯 Sirtuins are NAD-dependent deacylases in different compartments; NAD is a substrate, not a master on-switch (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 In the hallmark papers, “deregulation” is wrong amplitude, duration, timing, or tissue — including anabolic resistance in aged muscle — not simply “too much mTOR” (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

🤔 Clinic English uses “mTOR off,” “AMPK activator,” “sirtuin activator,” and “CR-mimetic” for weekly sirolimus, metformin, berberine, resveratrol, NR/NMN, and 16:8 (AgelessRx; Healthspan; Quicksilver AMPK Charge+; Elysium; Fasted app / ProLon).

🤔 “Biological age” sold next to these drugs is almost always a methylation clock or a questionnaire, not tissue p-S6 or a clamp (AgelessRx TruAge; Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023).

## Mechanism

📚 Persistent growth-pathway tone (IIS/mTOR) and weaker energy-stress tone (AMPK/sirtuins/FOXO) are the mechanistic cartoon of this hallmark: more translation and less autophagy/repair when the organism is old, plus the opposite problem in muscle that no longer responds to anabolic pulses (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023).

📚 After high-intensity resistance exercise, phosphorylation of mTOR, S6K1, 4E-BP1, and ERK1/2 and mixed-muscle protein synthesis rose in young adults (27±2 y) and did not in older adults (70±2 y) (Fry et al., PLoS One, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0022882, PMID 21829546; n=16/16). Essential-amino-acid feeding likewise activates mTOR/S6K1/4E-BP1 less in aged than young men (Cuthbertson et al., FASEB J, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.04-2640fje, PMID 15596483; n=44). The tissue-level aging defect in muscle is too little timed anabolism, not a license to suppress mTOR forever.

📚 Chronic rapamycin in mice disrupts mTORC2 as well as mTORC1; hepatic mTORC2 loss impairs insulin-mediated suppression of gluconeogenesis, which is how the drug can extend life and worsen glucose handling in the same animal (Lamming et al., Science, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1215135, PMID 22461615, PMC3324089; female mtor+/− mlst8+/− mice +14.4% lifespan with preserved glucose tolerance).

🥼 Whether ordinary human aging is “too much mTOR everywhere” (hyperfunction) or “too little timed anabolism in muscle/immune cells” is a live paper fight, not a settled direction (Lamming et al., Science, 2012; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Stanfield et al., J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, 2026).

📚 mTORC1 tonically restrains ULK1-dependent autophagy initiation; that branch is hallmark 05, not the whole drug (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 daf-2 reduction-of-function more than doubled fertile adult C. elegans lifespan and required daf-16/FOXO (Kenyon et al., Nature, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1038/366461a0, PMID 8247153). TOR deficiency more than doubled worm lifespan; dTsc/dTOR/dS6K genetics extended Drosophila life in a nutrition-dependent way; of 564 yeast deletion strains, 6 of 10 long-lived hits landed in TOR/Sch9 signaling (Vellai et al., Nature, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1038/426620a, PMID 14668850; Kapahi et al., Curr Biol, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cub.2004.03.059, PMID 15186745; Kaeberlein et al., Science, 2005, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1115535, PMID 16293764).

📚 Heterozygous Igf1r knockout mice lived a mean 26% longer than wild-type littermates (females +33%, P<0.001; males +16%, NS) (Holzenberger et al., Nature, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01298, PMID 12529643). Female Irs1−/− mice had median lifespan 738→971 days (+32%, log-rank P=0.001) despite mild lifelong insulin resistance; Irs2−/− were short-lived (Selman et al., FASEB J, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1096/fj.07-9261com, PMID 17928362).

📚 Encapsulated rapamycin started at 600 days of age extended UM-HET3 mouse lifespan at three independent NIA ITP sites: mean +9% in males and +14% in females; age at 90% mortality +9% / +14%; pooled log-rank p<0.0001 (Harrison et al., Nature, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08221, PMID 19587680, PMC2786175; N=1901 in the late-life dataset, 14 ppm microencapsulated rapamycin in chow).

📚 From 9 months, ITP rapamycin at 42 ppm raised UM-HET3 median lifespan 26% in females and 23% in males; 14 ppm reproduced an ~18% female benefit; 4.7 ppm worked in females, not in pooled males; females had higher blood levels at every dose; highest-dose males had 11–22% fight-cage removals vs ~1% controls (Miller et al., Aging Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12194, PMID 24341993).

📚 Three months of rapamycin starting at 20 months raised post-treatment life expectancy in C57BL/6 mice (8 mg/kg/day i.p.: male median 231→372 days, +61%, n=20/20; 126 ppm diet +44.6% males / +39% females); high-dose females shifted toward aggressive hematopoietic cancers (Bitto et al., eLife, 2016, https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.16351, PMID 27549339).

📚 Chronic rapamycin extended male C57BL/6J lifespan but ameliorated few aging phenotypes; several apparent rescues were aging-independent drug effects also seen in young mice (Neff et al., J Clin Invest, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI67674, PMID 23863708). Lifespan up is not “youthful sensing restored.”

📚 ITP acarbose 1000 ppm extended male UM-HET3 median lifespan 22% (p<0.0001) and female median 5%; maximum-lifespan effects were similar in both sexes (+11% / +9%) (Harrison et al., Aging Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12170, PMID 24245565).

📚 A later ITP acarbose dose series (400 / 1000 / 2500 ppm) moved lifespan in both sexes at every dose, still larger in males (Harrison / Strong / Miller, Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12898, PMID 30688027, PMC6413665).

📚 ITP canagliflozin 180 ppm from 7 months extended male UM-HET3 median lifespan 14% (log-rank p<0.001) and 90th-percentile age +9% at three sites; females got better glucose numbers and no extra years (Miller / Harrison et al., JCI Insight, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1172/jci.insight.140019, PMID 32841215).

📚 Rapamycin 14 ppm plus acarbose from 9 months raised UM-HET3 median lifespan 28% in females and 34% in males (both P<0.0001 pooled) and was the only combo in that paper that hit both sexes at all three sites (Strong et al., Aging Cell, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13724, PMID 36087005).

📚 Metformin 1000 ppm plus rapamycin 14 ppm from 9 months increased UM-HET3 median lifespan 23% in both sexes vs simultaneous controls; the add-on vs historical rapamycin-alone was not significant after site stratification (males P=0.12) (Strong et al., Aging Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12496, PMID 27312235).

📚 The same ITP cohort: metformin 1000 ppm alone from 9 months did not extend lifespan in either sex (male median +7%, P=0.35; site +13/−1/+10%, all NS; females null) (Strong et al., Aging Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12496, PMID 27312235; n=442 males / 421 females). That is the mouse analog of the TAME slogan, and it missed.

📚 ITP resveratrol did not extend UM-HET3 lifespan at 300 or 1200 ppm from 12 months, or 300 ppm from 4 months (Strong et al., J Gerontol A, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/gls070, PMID 22451473; Miller et al., J Gerontol A, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glq178, PMID 20974732). Sinclair is a co-author on the null.

📚 ITP nicotinamide riboside (~1000 ppm from 8 months) failed to extend lifespan in either sex; authors: “NR has no effect on lifespan” (Harrison et al., Aging Cell, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13328, PMID 33788371).

⛔ Reading ITP percentages as a completed human restoration product, or as a weekly 6 mg protocol, fails the papers’ own design: genetically heterogeneous mice, chow exposure, not a human healthspan RCT (Harrison et al., Nature, 2009; Miller et al., JCI Insight, 2020).

📚 Wisconsin adult-onset ~30% CR in rhesus macaques: at the 2009 cut, 80% of CR animals vs 50% of controls had survived age-related death; lower diabetes, cancer, CVD, and brain atrophy (Colman et al., Science, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1173635, PMID 19590001; N=76, ~20 years, WNPRC).

🥼 NIA rhesus CR over 23 years did not extend survival versus NIA controls, with some health benefits (Mattison et al., Nature, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature11432, PMID 22932268). The 2017 joint analysis attributed the Wisconsin/NIA split to diet composition, how controls were fed, age of onset, and sex — primate CR survival is context-dependent (Mattison et al., Nat Commun, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncomms14063, PMID 28094747).

## Human data

📚 CALERIE phase 2 randomized 218 healthy non-obese adults 21–50 y to prescribed 25% CR vs ad libitum for 2 years (143 CR / 75 AL); achieved restriction was 11.9% (SE 0.7); weight −7.5 kg vs +0.1 kg; LDL, TC/HDL, systolic and diastolic BP, CRP, insulin-sensitivity index, and metabolic-syndrome score improved (Kraus et al., Lancet Diabetes Endocrinol, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/S2213-8587(19)30151-2, PMID 31303390; NCT00427193).

📚 In the same trial, post-hoc blood DNAm: DunedinPACE fell 2–3% (24-month d=−0.25) while PC PhenoAge and PC GrimAge did not differ by arm (Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-022-00357-y, PMID 37118425). A clock decrement is not disability-free survival.

📚 When energy was matched in lean adults, 3-week alternate-day fasting was not metabolically superior to daily restriction and lost less fat (−0.74±1.32 vs −1.75±0.79 kg; P=0.01); isocaloric ADF did not lose fat (Templeman et al., Sci Transl Med, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.abd8034, PMID 34135111; n=12/arm).

📚 TREAT 16:8 (noon–8 pm) vs consistent meal timing for 12 weeks in 116 adults with overweight/obesity: between-group weight −0.26 kg (95% CI −1.30 to 0.78, P=0.63). The in-person subset (n=50) lost appendicular lean-mass index on 16:8 versus control (−0.16 kg/m², P=0.005); fat mass did not differ (Lowe et al., JAMA Intern Med, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamainternmed.2020.4153, PMID 32986097).

📚 Six weeks of everolimus (RAD001) 0.5 mg/day or 5 mg/week in 218 adults ≥65 y raised influenza-vaccine response about 20% and reduced PD-1+ T cells (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3009892, PMID 25540326). Novartis-sponsored. Immune surrogate, not lifespan.

📚 In 264 adults ≥65 y, 6 weeks of RAD001 0.1 mg + BEZ235 10 mg daily cut fitted annualized self-reported infections to 1.49/person-year (95% CI 1.19–1.86) vs placebo 2.41 (2.00–2.90), P=0.001 (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aaq1564, PMID 29997249). Still Novartis; still not disability-free survival.

📚 RTB101 10 mg daily for 16 winter weeks in 1024 adults ≥65 y did not cut clinically symptomatic respiratory illness versus placebo (26% vs 25%; OR 1.07, 90% CI 0.80–1.42, P=0.65); the indication was dropped (Mannick et al., Lancet Healthy Longev, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/S2666-7568(21)00062-3; resTORbio 15 Nov 2019). Phase 2b lab-confirmed RTI signal did not survive the Phase 3 endpoint.

📚 PEARL (NCT04488601): 48-week decentralized RCT, 114 completers aged 50–85 (compounded rapamycin 5 mg/week n=40, 10 mg/week n=35, placebo n=39). Primary visceral adiposity was null (p=0.942). Mid-trial PK showed compounded rapamycin at ~1/3 the 24 h blood level of generic, so listed 10 mg ≈ ~3.3 mg generic. Female 10 mg lean-mass secondary improved (ε²=0.202, p=0.013). Authors are at AgelessRx, which sells the drug (Moel, Harinath, Isman, Zalzala et al., Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206235, PMID 40188830).

📚 RAPA-EX-01: 40 sedentary adults 65–85 y, 6 mg generic sirolimus weekly plus home exercise for 13 weeks. ITT 30-s chair-stand difference −2.13 reps versus placebo (95% CI −4.61 to 0.34, P=0.089); complete-case −2.46 (P=0.045); per-protocol −3.44 (P=0.007). AE counts 99 vs 63; one possibly drug-related SAE pneumonia (Stanfield, Kaeberlein, Leroux et al., J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.70274, PMC13082878).

📚 Off-label weekly-rapamycin users ≥90 days reported more mouth ulceration than never-users (14.7% vs 4.7%, FDR P=0.0081); that was the only condition that cleared FDR in the survey (Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00818-1, PMID 37165209; 245 users vs 172 never-users; self-report, 3-month recall). Not transplant pneumonitis. Not nothing.

📚 DPP: 3234 high-risk adults; metformin 850 mg BID cut incident diabetes 31% (95% CI 17–43) versus placebo over 2.8 years (7.8 vs 11.0 cases/100 PY); intensive lifestyle cut 58% (Knowler et al., NEJM, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512, PMID 11832527). Indicated prediabetes.

📚 Konopka 2019: 12 weeks aerobic training in older adults (mean 62 y) plus metformin n=27 vs placebo n=26; metformin attenuated VO2max and whole-body insulin-sensitivity gains and blocked the mitochondrial-respiration increase (Konopka et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12880, PMID 30548390, PMC6351883).

🥼 A later pair of RCTs reported no metformin blunting of muscle mitochondrial or AMPK adaptations to exercise (Pilmark et al., Appl Physiol Nutr Metab, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1139/apnm-2021-0194, PMID 34784247; n=29 IGT + 15 lean men crossover). Konopka vs Pilmark is a live paper fight, not a license to call metformin an AMPK gerotherapy.

⛔ Bannister 2014 CPRD: 78,241 metformin-first T2D vs matched non-diabetics; adjusted median survival 15% longer on metformin (non-diabetic STR 0.85, 95% CI 0.81–0.90). Observational, indicated population, immortal-time and healthy-user bias — not a healthy-person geroprotection RCT (Bannister et al., Diabetes Obes Metab, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.12354, PMID 25041462).

🥼 Danish registers did not replicate that slogan: metformin-monotherapy initiation associated with higher, not lower, mortality vs no diabetes (adjusted IRR 1.32, 95% CI 1.16–1.50 in singletons; 1.64, 1.10–2.46 in discordant twins) (Keys et al., Int J Epidemiol, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyac200, PMID 36286820). A 20-year UK comparison found the year-1 metformin-vs-nondiabetic edge reversed by year 4 (STR 0.72, P=0.049); 20-year Cox HR 1.69 against the metformin patients (Stevenson-Hoare et al., BMC Public Health, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-023-15764-y, PMID 37131150).

📚 AFAR’s TAME page still lists “visionary donors to help launch” a 6-year, 14-site, 3,000-person metformin vs placebo composite (CVD, cancer, dementia, death) in ages 65–79; Wake Forest is the coordinating center; no efficacy results (AFAR, https://www.afar.org/tame-trial/). Existence of a protocol is not a result. NCT02432287 is Barzilai/Einstein MILES (completed 2017; n=16 actual; 6-week metformin 1,700 mg/day crossover RNA-seq in older IGT adults), not TAME (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02432287). NCT04370067 is Hungarian COVID screening. TAME has no launched interventional NCT with results.

📚 Ecuadorian GHR-deficient (Laron) follow-up: 99 related individuals over 22 years; one nonlethal malignancy and no diabetes versus 17% cancer and 5% diabetes in relatives; fasting insulin 1.4 vs 4.4 μU/ml; HOMA-IR 0.34 vs 0.96 (Guevara-Aguirre et al., Sci Transl Med, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3001845, PMID 21325617, PMC3357623).

🥼 Extra years in that Laron cohort were not shown; contemporaneous commentary blamed accident and alcohol deaths in a ~3.5-foot population (Guevara-Aguirre et al., 2011; Longo comments in Scientific American / MIT Technology Review, 2011). Disease-sparing ≠ lifespan.

📚 High-dose resveratrol 500 mg TID for 4 weeks in 24 obese men did not move clamp insulin sensitivity, BP, REE, ectopic fat, or inflammatory markers (Poulsen et al., Diabetes, 2013, https://doi.org/10.2337/db12-0975, PMID 23139350).

📚 Sixteen weeks of 150 or 1000 mg resveratrol in men with metabolic syndrome (74 enrolled, 66 completers) did not improve hsCRP, glucose, BP, or hepatic fat; 1000 mg raised LDL and fructosamine (Kjær et al., J Clin Endocrinol Metab, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2016-2160).

🥼 Howitz/Sinclair 2003 reported resveratrol as a SIRT1 activator that extended yeast lifespan; Pacholec et al. found resveratrol and Sirtris compounds activate fluorophore-tagged peptides, not native substrates, and SRT1720 did not lower glucose in their HFD mice (Howitz et al., Nature, 2003, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature01960; Pacholec et al., J Biol Chem, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1074/jbc.M109.088682). Consumer trans-resveratrol as a CR-mimetic sirtuin drug is the launder.

📚 SRT2104, the lead Sirtris/GSK SIRT1 activator, did not improve glucose or insulin in T2D; oral bioavailability was ~14% and not dose-proportional; five of eight outcome trials were neutral; SRT3025 stopped for QTc; SRT2379 for preclinical toxicity; GSK closed the Cambridge Sirtris site in 2013 (Baksi et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/bcp.12327, PMID 24446723; Hoffmann et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol, 2013; Dai et al., Front Physiol, 2021, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2021.752117).

☠︎︎ Micronized resveratrol SRT501 was terminated in relapsed myeloma after nephrotoxicity — established harm at that exposure, not drugstore 100 mg capsules and not weekly rapamycin (Popat et al. via Dai et al., Front Physiol, 2021, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2021.752117; Sirtris/GSK sold SRT501).

📚 Oral NR can raise blood NAD (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). REUSE hallmark 01. Substrate move ≠ sirtuin restoration ≠ healthspan.

📚 NR 2000 mg/d × 12 weeks did not improve clamp insulin sensitivity, glucose disposal, body composition, or hepatic fat in obese insulin-resistant men; the same cohort showed no change in muscle mitochondrial respiration, content, or morphology, and muscle NAD+ did not rise (Dollerup et al., Am J Clin Nutr, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/nqy132, PMID 29992272; Dollerup et al., J Physiol, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1113/JP278752, PMID 31828822; n=40).

📚 Oral NMN 250 mg/d × 10 weeks raised clamp-measured muscle insulin sensitivity and muscle p-AKT/mTOR signaling in overweight/obese postmenopausal women with prediabetes; tissue NAD+ change was not detected (Yoshino et al., Science, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe9985, PMID 33888596). Signaling move, not sirtuin restoration.

📚 SELECT: weekly semaglutide 2.4 mg cut MACE in people with obesity and established CVD (HR 0.80, 95% CI 0.72–0.90; 6.5% vs 8.0%; n=17,604; mean 39.8 months) (Lincoff et al., NEJM, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2307563, PMID 37952131). Novo Nordisk funded. Indicated secondary prevention, not hallmark-06 restoration in metabolically healthy buyers.

⛔ Relabeling SELECT / SGLT2 / acarbose disease outcomes as nutrient-sensing restoration for the cash-pay clinic is a definition swap (Lincoff et al., NEJM, 2023; Miller et al., JCI Insight, 2020).

📚 TRIIM: n=9 men, rhGH + DHEA + metformin, ~2.5-year epigenetic-minus-chronological drop, no placebo (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019). REUSE hallmark 03. That is GH addition, the opposite direction from Laron/IIS reduction.

## Measurement

💯 There is no single assay of “nutrient-sensing age.” Insulin, C-peptide, IGF-1/IGFBPs, clamps, and OGTTs score IIS tone; p-S6 / p-4E-BP1 score mTORC1 in the tissue you biopsied; p-AMPK / p-ACC score AMPK there; a NAD metabolome is not compartmental sirtuin activity; CGM is glucose (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Kraus et al., 2019).

📚 Blood-cell phospho-S6 after a pill is exposure in those cells, not rejuvenation of muscle, liver, or brain (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; McCormick et al., Physiology International, 2018 — old-PBMC rapamycin flux null already filed under hallmark 05).

⛔ A commercial clock decrement sold next to rapamycin or metformin is not a qualified nutrient-sensing surrogate (Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023; AgelessRx TruAge). CALERIE moved one clock and nullified two in the same paper.

## What clinics and self-experimenters are doing

Practice is not efficacy. Doses below are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Kaeberlein et al. surveyed 333 off-label rapamycin users: 95% “healthy longevity/anti-ageing”; weekly dosing in ~88–92%; 6 mg was the modal weekly dose (101/229 men, 25/67 women); range 1–20 mg (Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00818-1, PMID 37165209). ## Speculative

🐉 Pulse mTORC1 restraint that spares mTORC2 — intermittent rapamycin or a selective TORC1 inhibitor — might keep the ITP lifespan branch without Lamming-style hepatic insulin resistance; that is a schedule/chemistry hope, not a completed human result (inference from Lamming 2012 + Harrison 2009 + Mannick P3 null; no source for the human uncoupling).

🐉 Tissue-restricted IIS reduction (not whole-body GH suppression, not TRIIM GH addition) might copy Laron cancer/diabetes sparing without the stature and accident load; no intact-adult human experiment exists (inference from Guevara-Aguirre 2011 + Kenyon 1993).

🐉 A treat-to-target nutrient-sensing score — clamp or mixed-meal plus tissue p-S6/p-AMPK in the organ you care about, not TruAge, not fasting insulin alone — does not exist as a validated product (inference from Kraus 2019 panel + López-Otín measurement caveats + no consumer SKU with a methods paper).

🐉 Sex-split ITP hits (acarbose, canagliflozin male-only years) imply the human protocol, if any, is not “one AMPK/SGLT2 pill for everyone”; that is a translation problem, not a result (inference from Harrison 2014 + Miller 2020).

🐉 GLP-1/GIP agonists have large indicated metabolic-outcome data and are being dropped into the same storefront as rapamycin; whether that is nutrient-sensing restoration or weight-loss pharmacology wearing a hallmark badge is the live leap (AgelessRx XPRIZE finals copy; no healthy-person aging RCT).

## What is actually on the table

📚 IIS reduction and TOR inhibition extend life in worms, flies, yeast, and genetically heterogeneous mice; ITP rapamycin is the most reproduced pharmacological lifespan hit in that system; rapamycin+acarbose was the dual-sex combo; acarbose and canagliflozin add sex-split glucose-peak hits; ITP metformin, resveratrol, and NR did not add years; Wisconsin CR helped rhesus survival and NIA CR did not (Kenyon 1993; Harrison 2009/2014; Miller 2014/2020; Strong 2013/2016/2022; Harrison 2021; Colman 2009; Mattison 2012/2017).

📚 Human CR at ~12% achieved intake improves cardiometabolic risk factors; one post-hoc clock moved and two did not; isocaloric fasting and 16:8 are not free upgrades (Kraus 2019; Waziry 2023; Templeman 2021; Lowe 2020).

🥼 Human rapalog data are split: Mannick Phase 2 vaccine titers up, Phase 3 respiratory illness null; PEARL primary VAT null at under-bioavailable compounded doses; weekly 6 mg plus exercise did not beat placebo on chair-stands and favored placebo on sensitivity analyses (Mannick 2014/2021; Moel 2025; Stanfield 2026).

⛔ A weekly sirolimus capsule, a metformin-for-the-healthy prescription, an NAD/resveratrol/berberine bottle, a 16:8 app, or “it is a hallmark” is not organismal nutrient-sensing restoration in healthy people. TAME has not launched; NCT02432287 is MILES n=16, not TAME. Bannister did not replicate (AFAR TAME; Keys 2022; Stevenson-Hoare 2023; López-Otín 2023).

🤔 People are buying weekly 3–8 mg sirolimus (Healthspan 5–8 mg target; Kaeberlein modal 6 mg; AgelessRx 2→6 mg), metformin 500–1500 mg, NAD IV/nasal/NR/NMN, berberine, FMD kits, and clock-guided stacks under that slogan anyway (Kaeberlein 2023; Healthspan; HealthRX; AgelessRx; Peach IV; Elysium; DoNotAge; Quicksilver; ProLon).

☠︎︎ Daily transplant-dose sirolimus has an established infection and pneumonitis body count; weekly geroscience exposure was not equated (FDA Rapamune 2013).
