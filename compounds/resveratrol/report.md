## Animal data

📚 ITP resveratrol did not extend UM-HET3 lifespan at 300 or 1200 ppm from 12 months, or 300 ppm from 4 months (Strong et al., J Gerontol A, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/gls070, PMID 22451473; Miller et al., J Gerontol A, 2011, PMID 20974732). Sinclair is a co-author on the null. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 High-dose resveratrol 500 mg TID for 4 weeks in 24 obese men did not move clamp insulin sensitivity, BP, REE, ectopic fat, or inflammatory markers (Poulsen et al., Diabetes, 2013, https://doi.org/10.2337/db12-0975, PMID 23139350). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 Sixteen weeks of 150 or 1000 mg resveratrol in men with metabolic syndrome (74 enrolled, 66 completers) did not improve hsCRP, glucose, BP, or hepatic fat; 1000 mg raised LDL and fructosamine (Kjær et al., J Clin Endocrinol Metab, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2016-2160). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 SRT2104, the lead Sirtris/GSK SIRT1 activator, did not improve glucose or insulin in T2D; oral bioavailability was ~14% and not dose-proportional; five of eight outcome trials were neutral; SRT3025 stopped for QTc; SRT2379 for preclinical toxicity; GSK closed the Cambridge Sirtris site in 2013 (Baksi et al., Br J Clin Pharmacol, 2014, PMID 24446723; Dai et al., Front Physiol, 2021). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

## Toxicity and hazards

☠︎︎ Micronized resveratrol SRT501 was terminated in relapsed myeloma after nephrotoxicity — established harm at that exposure, not drugstore 100 mg capsules and not weekly rapamycin (Popat et al. via Dai et al., Front Physiol, 2021, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2021.752117; Sirtris/GSK sold SRT501). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

## Fights

🥼 Howitz/Sinclair 2003 reported resveratrol as a SIRT1 activator that extended yeast lifespan; Pacholec et al. found resveratrol and Sirtris compounds activate fluorophore-tagged peptides, not native substrates, and SRT1720 did not lower glucose in their HFD mice (Howitz et al., Nature, 2003; Pacholec et al., J Biol Chem, 2010). Consumer trans-resveratrol as a CR-mimetic sirtuin drug is the launder. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

## Legal / access status

🤔 Resveratrol is a storefront SIRT1-activator SKU; SRT501 was an investigational high-exposure formulation with established myeloma nephrotoxicity at that exposure ([06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md)).
