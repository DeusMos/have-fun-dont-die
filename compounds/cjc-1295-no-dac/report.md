## Identity

Parked home: [06 deregulated nutrient sensing](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md) for the GHRH axis. Adjacent GH-axis SKUs: [sermorelin](../sermorelin/report.md), [tesamorelin](../tesamorelin/report.md), [ipamorelin](../ipamorelin/report.md), [hexarelin](../hexarelin/report.md). Aliases: Mod GRF (1-29), tetrasubstituted GHRH(1-29).

💯 The 29-mer sold as “CJC-1295 no DAC” / Mod GRF (1-29) is tetrasubstituted GHRH(1-29) amide (D-Ala², Gln⁸, Ala¹⁵, Leu²⁷). CJC-1295 *with* DAC is that backbone plus a lysine-linked maleimidopropionic acid that covalently binds albumin (Teichman et al., *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2005-1536).

📚 PubChem CID 56841945 is the 29-aa sequence title (Tyr-D-Ala-Asp-Ala-…-Arg-NH₂), formula C152H252N44O42, MW 3367.9; the CID RN dump lists 446036-97-1, 446262-90-4, and 863288-34-0 (PubChem PUG CID/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/56841945).

## Mechanism

📚 Teichman et al. describe the shared pharmacophore as GHRH-(1-29)NH₂ with four substitutions that resist proteolysis; the *DAC* construct then links that GRF by lysine to maleimidopropionic acid so ≥90% binds albumin Cys34 after SC injection (Teichman et al., 2006, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2005-1536). The no-DAC SKU is the first half of that sentence.

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 After single SC CJC-1295 *with DAC*, mean t½ was 5.8–8.1 days; Cmax 1–1.5 h; mean GH rose 2- to 10-fold for ≥6 days and mean IGF-I 0.5- to 3-fold for 9–11 days (Teichman et al., 2006, study 1). That table is the albumin-bound construct.

🐉 Human t½ for the 29-mer / no-DAC vial is not compiled on this page. Clinic copy of “~30 minutes” is not a paper extract. no source; inference from the missing-no-DAC-PK file.

## Animal data

📚 Teichman et al. cite dog and pig work in which a single DAC-GRF dose kept GH pulsatile and raised IGF-I for several days (Teichman et al., 2006, citing that animal file). DAC construct, not the Mindful 29-mer.

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Teichman study 1: healthy adults 21–61 y, BMI ≤30, four single SC doses 30 / 60 / 125 / 250 µg/kg (n=6 per first four cohorts, 5:1 active:placebo) plus an extra 125 µg/kg cohort of 18 (15 active); GH AUC₀–₇d significant vs placebo at 60, 125, and 250 µg/kg; IGF-I exceeded age/sex norms only at 250 µg/kg (Teichman et al., 2006). Not aging. Not no-DAC.

📚 Teichman study 2: 24 subjects, two or three weekly/biweekly SC doses (20–60 µg/kg); IGF-I stayed above baseline through day 28; cumulative predose IGF-I and a shorter Tmax after later shots (Teichman et al., 2006). Still DAC.

🤔 A ConjuChem Phase II HIV-lipodystrophy trial of weekly DAC:GRF (CJC-1295) in 192 participants was halted in July 2006 after a death; AIDSMap reported an Argentine site and an unconfirmed eleventh-injection timing (AIDSMap, 2006-07-31, https://www.aidsmap.com/news/jul-2006/lipodystrophy-study-halted-after-patient-death). News, DAC construct, relationship to drug not adjudicated in that piece.

🐉 No published human aging / body-composition RCT of the no-DAC 29-mer is compiled on this page. no source; inference from the empty no-DAC human file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Teichman (DAC): single-dose AEs in 33/35 (94%) active vs 2/7 (29%) placebo, all mild–moderate; injection-site reactions ~70%; transient site urticaria ~30%; headache 63%, diarrhea 43%, flushing/warmth/transient hypotension 30% (higher at 125–250 µg/kg); two withdrawals for mild site reactions; no serious AEs and no significant antibodies in those studies (Teichman et al., 2006). DAC, not the RUO 29-mer.

🤔 The 2006 HIV-lipodystrophy halt is one death under investigation in a news account, not a labeled harm series for a no-DAC vial (AIDSMap, 2006-07-31).
