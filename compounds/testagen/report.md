## Identity

Parked home: [03 epigenetic alterations](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md) for the Khavinson DNA-binding slogan. Adjacent Khavinson SKUs: [epithalon](../epithalon/report.md), [pinealon](../pinealon/report.md). Aliases: KEDG, Lys-Glu-Asp-Gly.

💯 Testagen is the synthetic tetrapeptide Lys-Glu-Asp-Gly (KEDG). It is not epithalon (AEDG), not pinealon (EDR), and not testosterone.

📚 Fedoreyeva, Kireev, Khavinson, and Vanyushin name testagen as Lys-Glu-Asp-Gly in the same HeLa-nuclear paper as epithalon and pinealon (Fedoreyeva, Kireev, Khavinson, and Vanyushin, *Biochemistry (Mosc.)*, 2011;76:1210–1219, PMID 22117547, https://doi.org/10.1134/s0006297911110022).

## Mechanism

📚 FITC-labelled testagen, epithalon, and pinealon produced cytoplasmic, nuclear, and nucleolar fluorescence in HeLa cells. In vitro, the peptides quenched labelled oligonucleotide / DNA–ethidium fluorescence with sequence-dependent Stern–Volmer constants; testagen, epithalon, and pinealon preferred CAG-containing stretches (Fedoreyeva et al., 2011, PMID 22117547).

🐉 Nuclear DNA-binding as a Leydig-cell testosterone program is the Khavinson leap. HeLa fluorescence plus oligonucleotide quenching is not a steroidogenic-gene occupancy result and not a testis assay. no source; inference from the docking/quench-only file.
