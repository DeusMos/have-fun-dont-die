## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 RTB101 10 mg daily for 16 winter weeks in 1024 adults ≥65 y did not cut clinically symptomatic respiratory illness versus placebo (26% vs 25%; OR 1.07, 90% CI 0.80–1.42, P=0.65); the indication was dropped (Mannick et al., Lancet Healthy Longev, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1016/S2666-7568(21)00062-3; resTORbio 15 Nov 2019). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 The same Phase 3 respiratory-illness null is reused in hallmark 11 (Mannick et al., Lancet Healthy Longev, 2021; already in hallmark 06). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).
