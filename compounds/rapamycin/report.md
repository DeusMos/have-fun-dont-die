## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity clinics sell weekly sirolimus as a CR-mimetic / nutrient-sensing restore: AgelessRx titrates 2→6 mg/week; Healthspan starts at 3 mg and titrates to 5–8 mg weekly ($64/mo); HealthRX lists 1–6 or 3–10 mg; Kaeberlein-survey modal is 6 mg weekly (AgelessRx rapamycin pages; Healthspan Rapamycin Protocol; HealthRX weekly-dosing pages; Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023). Those companies sell the prescriptions they are citing. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that turning mTOR down restores youthful sensing in intact people is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

## Mechanism

💯 mTORC1 integrates amino acids, insulin/growth factors, oxygen, and lysosomal status to drive protein/lipid/nucleotide synthesis and restrain autophagy; mTORC2 regulates AKT, cytoskeleton, and survival and is less acutely rapamycin-sensitive (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Lamming et al., Science, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1215135, PMID 22461615). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-the-words-mean).

📚 Chronic rapamycin in mice disrupts mTORC2 as well as mTORC1; hepatic mTORC2 loss impairs insulin-mediated suppression of gluconeogenesis (Lamming et al., Science, 2012, PMID 22461615). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#mechanism).

📚 A senomorphic suppresses SASP without requiring cell death; rapamycin blunts SASP by suppressing MTOR-dependent IL1A translation and downstream NF-κB-driven IL-6/IL-8 without reversing the arrest (Laberge et al., Nature Cell Biology, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1038/ncb3195, PMID 26147232). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#mechanism).


## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 PEARL mid-trial PK showed compounded rapamycin at ~1/3 the 24 h blood level of generic, so the listed 10 mg arm ≈ ~3.3 mg generic (Moel, Harinath, Isman, Zalzala et al., Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206235, PMID 40188830). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 From 9 months, ITP females had higher blood rapamycin levels than males at every chow dose (Miller et al., Aging Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12194, PMID 24341993). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

🤼 Cohen 2012 in 138 advanced-cancer patients found 240 mL/day grapefruit juice raised sirolimus AUC ~350% and dropped the weekly dose for a target AUC from 90 mg to 25–35 mg (Cohen et al., Clinical Cancer Research, 2012, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4410974/). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🐉 Terminal half-life, CYP3A4/P-gp parameter tables, and a compiled trough-vs-AUC schedule for weekly geroscience doses are not on this page. Do not invent them. no source; inference from the missing-PK rule.

## Animal data

📚 Encapsulated rapamycin started at 600 days of age extended UM-HET3 mouse lifespan at three independent NIA ITP sites: mean +9% in males and +14% in females; age at 90% mortality +9% / +14%; pooled log-rank p<0.0001 (Harrison et al., Nature, 2009, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature08221, PMID 19587680; 14 ppm microencapsulated rapamycin in chow). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

📚 From 9 months, ITP rapamycin at 42 ppm raised UM-HET3 median lifespan 26% in females and 23% in males; females had higher blood levels at every dose (Miller et al., Aging Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12194, PMID 24341993). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

📚 Chronic rapamycin extended male C57BL/6J lifespan but ameliorated few aging phenotypes; several apparent rescues were aging-independent drug effects also seen in young mice (Neff et al., J Clin Invest, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI67674, PMID 23863708). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 PEARL (NCT04488601): 48-week decentralized RCT, 114 completers aged 50–85 (compounded rapamycin 5 mg/week n=40, 10 mg/week n=35, placebo n=39). Primary visceral adiposity was null (p=0.942). Mid-trial PK showed compounded rapamycin at ~1/3 the 24 h blood level of generic, so listed 10 mg ≈ ~3.3 mg generic. Female 10 mg lean-mass secondary improved (ε²=0.202, p=0.013). Authors are at AgelessRx, which sells the drug (Moel, Harinath, Isman, Zalzala et al., Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.18632/aging.206235, PMID 40188830). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 RAPA-EX-01: 40 sedentary adults 65–85 y, 6 mg generic sirolimus weekly plus home exercise for 13 weeks. ITT 30-s chair-stand difference −2.13 reps versus placebo (95% CI −4.61 to 0.34, P=0.089). AE counts 99 vs 63; one possibly drug-related SAE pneumonia (Stanfield, Kaeberlein, Leroux et al., J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, 2026, https://doi.org/10.1002/jcsm.70274). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 Off-label weekly-rapamycin users ≥90 days reported more mouth ulceration than never-users (14.7% vs 4.7%, FDR P=0.0081); that was the only condition that cleared FDR in the survey (Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-00818-1, PMID 37165209; 245 users vs 172 never-users). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 PBMCs from older adults failed to raise LC3-II after rapamycin plus bafilomycin the way young PBMCs did (McCormick et al., Physiology International, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1556/2060.105.2018.3.20, PMID 30269563). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md#human-data).

## Toxicity and hazards

☠︎︎ Transplant-dose daily sirolimus causes established, sometimes fatal, human infection and interstitial pneumonitis; that is not labeling theater, and it is not evidence that weekly geroscience doses are the same exposure (FDA Rapamune label, 2013, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/021083s053,021110s067lbl.pdf; boxed warning: infection, opportunistic infection, sepsis, lymphoma; §5.11 ILD/pneumonitis, some fatal). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

## Interactions

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Kaeberlein et al. surveyed 333 off-label rapamycin users: 95% “healthy longevity/anti-ageing”; weekly dosing in ~88–92%; 6 mg was the modal weekly dose (101/229 men, 25/67 women); range 1–20 mg (Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 Healthspan starts weekly sirolimus at 3 mg and titrates to a “5 to 8 mg” weekly optimum (Healthspan, https://gethealthspan.com/protocols/rapamycin-protocol). Healthspan sells the prescriptions. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

## Fights

🥼 Whether ordinary human aging is “too much mTOR everywhere” or “too little timed anabolism in muscle/immune cells” is a live paper fight (Lamming et al., Science, 2012; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Stanfield et al., J Cachexia Sarcopenia Muscle, 2026). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#mechanism).

🤼 Blagosklonny treats 5–7 mg once weekly as the popular longevity schedule; RAPA-EX-01 is the amateur/clinic counter that weekly 6 mg can blunt short-term exercise gains (Blagosklonny, Oncotarget / Aging 2023 dosing essay; Stanfield et al., 2026). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

## Legal / access status

📚 Rapamune (sirolimus) is an FDA-labeled transplant immunosuppressant with a boxed warning for infection, opportunistic infection, sepsis, and lymphoma, plus labeled ILD/pneumonitis, some fatal (FDA Rapamune label, 2013, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2013/021083s053,021110s067lbl.pdf). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 Weekly compounded or generic sirolimus for “longevity” is off-label clinic / survey practice, not an aging indication (Kaeberlein et al., GeroScience, 2023; AgelessRx; Healthspan; HealthRX). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).
