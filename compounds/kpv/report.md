## Identity

Parked home: [11 chronic inflammation](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md) for the colitis / NF-κB file. That hallmark report does not name this SKU. Aliases: α-MSH (11-13), MSH (11-13), ACTH (11-13), Lys-Pro-Val.

💯 KPV is the C-terminal tripeptide of α-melanocyte-stimulating hormone: L-lysyl-L-prolyl-L-valine.

📚 PubChem CID 125672 is titled Msh (11-13), formula C16H30N4O4, MW 342.43; name “lysyl-prolyl-valine” maps that CID to CAS 67727-97-3 (PubChem PUG name/CID fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/125672).

📚 FDA’s July 2026 PCAC briefing lists KPV free base CAS 67727-97-3 and states UNII codes are not available for free base or acetate; staff treat free base and acetate as distinct nominated BDSs (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193346/download).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO and clinic copy sells KPV as a gut / skin anti-inflammatory α-MSH fragment, often stacked with BPC-157 in KLOW (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/kpv). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating mouse DSS/TNBS colitis papers as a human IBD or aging restore, or treating a lyophilized RUO vial as a 503A cream, is a definition swap (Dalmasso et al., *Gastroenterology*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2007.10.026; Kannengiesser et al., *Inflamm. Bowel Dis.*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1002/ibd.20334; FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193346/download).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Oral KPV is taken up via the intestinal peptide transporter PepT1; in DSS and TNBS mouse colitis that reduced proinflammatory cytokine mRNA (IL-6, IL-12, and others by model) without raising IL-10 (Dalmasso, Charrier-Hisamuddin, Thu Nguyen, Yan, Sitaraman, and Merlin, *Gastroenterology*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2007.10.026, PMID 18061177).

📚 Kannengiesser et al. reported anti-inflammatory activity of KPV in DSS and CD45RBhi transfer colitis, including rescue of MC1Re/e mice from DSS death, and argued the effect is at least partly independent of MC1R (Kannengiesser et al., *Inflamm. Bowel Dis.*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1002/ibd.20334).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 A University of Michigan dissertation reported PEPT1 accounting for at least 90% of small-intestinal Lys-Pro-Val uptake in wild-type vs Pept1-knockout perfusion (Km 0.11 mM) and that the tripeptide is not enzymatically stable in jejunum/ileum lumen (https://hdl.handle.net/2027.42/89738). ## Animal data

📚 In DSS colitis, drinking-water KPV (100 µmol/L in the Dalmasso protocol) cut disease activity, colonic shortening, and selected cytokine mRNAs; in TNBS, KPV cut weight loss and MPO ~30% at 48 h (Dalmasso et al., *Gastroenterology*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1053/j.gastro.2007.10.026).

📚 In DSS and transfer-colitis models, KPV sped weight regain and cut histologic infiltrate / MPO; MC1Re/e DSS mice in the treatment arm all survived in that report (Kannengiesser et al., *Inflamm. Bowel Dis.*, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1002/ibd.20334).
