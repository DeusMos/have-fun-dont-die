## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Elysium Basis NR and DoNotAge NAD bottles are sold as sirtuin activation (Elysium Basis; DoNotAge). Those companies sell the capsules they are citing. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

🤔 Clinic English uses “sirtuin activator” and “CR-mimetic” for NR/NMN (Elysium; AgelessRx). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).

📚 Raising blood NAD is target engagement, not sirtuin restoration and not genome restoration (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).


## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Martens et al. reported a blood NAD+ rise after oral NR in older adults; that is target engagement in blood, not a compiled oral bioavailability or tissue-PK table (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018 — cited in [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#human-data) and [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md)).

## Animal data

📚 ITP nicotinamide riboside (~1000 ppm from 8 months) failed to extend lifespan in either sex; authors: “NR has no effect on lifespan” (Harrison et al., Aging Cell, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13328, PMID 33788371). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

📚 Raising NAD in C. elegans via PARP loss/inhibitors or NR/NAM extended mean lifespan 15–29% in a sir-2.1- and UPRmt-dependent manner (Mouchiroud et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.06.016, PMID 23870130). That is worm NAD→mito-stress signaling, not human blood-NAD restoration. Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Oral NR 500 mg twice daily for 6 weeks raised blood cellular NAD+ ~60% vs placebo in a crossover RCT of 24 healthy adults aged 55–79 (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03421-7, PMID 29599478; NIAGEN supplied by ChromaDex). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

📚 Metabolite target engagement is not reduced tissue mutation burden, fewer persistent lesions, or healthspan via repair (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

📚 Oral NR 2,000 mg/day for 12 weeks did not improve clamp insulin sensitivity, endogenous glucose production, glucose disposal, REE, lipolysis, lipid oxidation, or body composition in obese insulin-resistant men, and the trial did not measure mutation burden, persistent lesions, or CHIP (Dollerup et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2018, PMID 29992272; N=40 men BMI >30, age 40–70; M-value interaction P=0.71). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

📚 NR 2000 mg/d × 12 weeks did not improve clamp insulin sensitivity, glucose disposal, body composition, or hepatic fat in obese insulin-resistant men; the same cohort showed no change in muscle mitochondrial respiration, content, or morphology, and muscle NAD+ did not rise (Dollerup et al., Am J Clin Nutr, 2018, PMID 29992272; Dollerup et al., J Physiol, 2020, PMID 31828822; n=40). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 NR 1 g/day × 21 days in 12 men (median 75 y) raised muscle NAAD and nicotinamide clearance products but did not raise muscle NAD+ and did not change complex I/II OXPHOS, max respiratory capacity, citrate synthase, mtDNA copy number, or grip (Elhassan et al., Cell Reports, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.07.043, PMID 31412242; ChromaDex supplied Niagen). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Freeberg 2023 synthesizes that oral NAD precursors are generally safe and raise NAD-related metabolites, while “the clinical evidence that raising NAD+ concentrations can improve physiological function is unclear” (Freeberg et al., Journals of Gerontology Series A, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glad106, PMC10692436). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Elysium Basis is sold as NR + pterostilbene NAD/sirtuin support at $65 / 30-day pouch, two capsules morning (Elysium Basis, https://www.elysiumhealth.com/products/basis). Elysium sells the capsule. Extracted from [03](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md).

🤔 DoNotAge sells Pure NR 350 mg/capsule (DoNotAge NR, https://donotage.org/pure-nr). DoNotAge sells the capsules. Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🤔 Peach IV lists oral NR/NMN commonly 100–500 mg/day (studies cited up to 1000–2000 mg) (Peach IV NAD protocol). Peach IV sells the infusions. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

## Fights

🤼 Clinic and protocol blogs split NAD IV vs daily oral NR/NMN vs nasal; oral has the RCTs (Martens/Dollerup/Elhassan); IV/nasal are cash-pay PK slogans without an aging-outcomes trial on those pages (Peach IV; AgelessRx; DoNotAge; Martens 2018; Dollerup 2018; Elhassan 2019). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Legal / access status
