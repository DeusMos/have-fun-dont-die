## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Senin et al.: double-blind RCT vs placebo; N=109 elderly with mild-to-moderate impairment meeting NINCDS-ADRDA probable Alzheimer-type dementia; aniracetam (Ro 13-5057) × 6 months. Groups comparable at baseline. Aniracetam differed from placebo by study end and improved vs baseline on psychobehavioural parameters; placebo deteriorated. Authors called tolerability excellent. Roche Italy author on the paper (Senin, Abate, Fieschi, Gori, Guala, Marini, Villardita, and Parnetti, *Eur. Neuropsychopharmacol.*, 1991;1:511–517, https://doi.org/10.1016/0924-977x(91)90004-e).

📚 Parnetti et al.: double-blind RCT vs piracetam; N=115 probable SDAT; 6 months. Aniracetam improved over most psychobehavioural parameters; piracetam improved to a lesser extent (Parnetti et al., *Dementia*, 1991;2:262–267, https://doi.org/10.1159/000107214).

⛔ An SDAT psychometric battery is not a healthy-aging RCT.

🐉 No compiled healthy-volunteer cognition or lifespan trial of aniracetam is on this page. no source; inference from the empty healthy-aging file.
