## Mechanism

💯 GSH is synthesized from Glu, Cys, and Gly by glutamate-cysteine ligase then GSH synthetase; Cys is usually rate-limiting. It is the main intracellular thiol buffer for peroxide detox (GPx), xenobiotic conjugation (GST), and glutathionylation.

📚 Richie et al. restate that almost all tissues make GSH, that even partial depletion impairs immune function, and that oral GSH in animals can raise plasma and tissue stores (Richie et al., *Eur. J. Nutr.*, 2015;54:251–263, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-014-0706-z).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 A single oral GSH dose (150 µmol/kg) in 7 healthy adults did not move plasma GSH over 4.5 h — the Witschi 1992 observation Richie cites as the reason a 6-month store study was needed (Richie et al., 2015, citing Witschi et al., 1992).

📚 Richie 2015: after 6 months of daily oral GSH, compartment stores rose and then returned to baseline after a 1-month washout (Richie et al., 2015, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-014-0706-z).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Richie et al.: RCT, N=54 completers (41 F / 13 M, mean age 46.6), oral Setria 250 or 1,000 mg/day vs placebo × 6 months, then 1-month washout; NCT01044277. High-dose at 6 months: GSH +30–35% in erythrocytes, plasma, and lymphocytes and +260% in buccal cells (p < 0.05). Low-dose: +17% whole blood and +29% erythrocytes (p < 0.05). GSSG/GSH in whole blood fell. NK cytotoxicity more than doubled vs placebo at 3 months in the high-dose arm (p < 0.05). Levels reverted after washout. Kyowa Hakko USA supplied Setria (Richie, Nichenametla, Neidig, Calcagnotto, Haley, Schell, and Muscat, *Eur. J. Nutr.*, 2015;54:251–263, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00394-014-0706-z).

📚 Allen and Bradley: RCT, N=40 healthy adults, oral GSH 500 mg BID × 4 weeks. Urinary F2-isoprostanes, 8-OHdG, and erythrocyte GSH / GSSG / ratio did not change vs placebo (Allen and Bradley, *J. Altern. Complement. Med.*, 2011;17:827–833, https://doi.org/10.1089/acm.2010.0716, PMC3162377).

📚 Hauser et al.: randomized, double-blind pilot, PD with inadequately controlled motor symptoms; IV GSH 1,400 mg vs placebo TIW × 4 weeks; N=21 randomized (11 GSH / 10 placebo). No significant UPDRS difference. ADL+motor improved 2.8 units more on GSH during dosing (p = 0.32) and worsened 3.5 units more in the 8 weeks after stop (p = 0.54). Well tolerated; no AE withdrawals (Hauser, Lyons, McClain, Carter, and Perlmutter, *Mov. Disord.*, 2009;24:979–983, https://doi.org/10.1002/mds.22401).

⛔ A blood GSH percentage, an NK-assay fold, or a non-significant UPDRS delta is not a human aging RCT.
