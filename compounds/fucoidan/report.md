## The claim and the slogan

🤔 *Fucus vesiculosus* fucoidan is sold as a SIRT6 “genomic guardian” (DoNotAge SIRT6Activator®; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07500649 collaborator list naming DoNotAge.org). DoNotAge sells the capsule. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

🤔 Clinic English uses “DNA repair” for fucoidan (DoNotAge). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

⛔ Selling the 2025 preprint plus a brand assay as human SIRT6-in-a-bottle genome restoration does not survive the missing human tissue target-engagement and mutation-burden data (Biashad / Gorbunova et al., bioRxiv, 2025; DoNotAge SIRT6Activator® product page, https://donotage.org/sirt6-activator). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

## Animal data

📜 A 2025 preprint reports *F. vesiculosus* fucoidan activates SIRT6 mono-ADP-ribosylation, represses LINE1, and extended median lifespan 13% in aged male C57BL/6 mice (Gehan-Breslow p=0.009) with a non-significant 3% female change (p=0.12) (Biashad / Gorbunova et al., bioRxiv, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.03.24.645072; chow/water fucoidan starting ~19–25 months). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 DoNotAge sells SIRT6Activator® (*Fucus vesiculosus* fucoidan) as “the only proven activator of Sirtuin 6” for DNA repair; capsules are 400 mg; advertised daily intakes are 800, 1600 (recommended), or 2400 mg (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/sirt6-activator). DoNotAge sells the capsule. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 The same 800–2400 mg/day SKU is reused in hallmark 06 as a sirtuin product; the DNA-repair slogan lives in 01 (DoNotAge). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).

🤔 NCT07500649 (recruiting) randomizes 60 prefrail men 50–80 to 2.4 g/day fucoidan vs placebo for 6 months with GrimAge as primary endpoint; collaborators include University of Rochester, L’Oréal, and DoNotAge.org; listed outcomes are clocks, inflammation, frailty, and skin — not duplex mutation burden (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07500649; primary completion estimated 31 Mar 2027). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

⛔ NCT07500649’s primary endpoint is change in blood GrimAge, not tissue mutation burden, persistent lesions, or CHIP. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).
