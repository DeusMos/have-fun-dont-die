## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Oral NMN 250 mg/d × 10 weeks raised clamp-measured muscle insulin sensitivity and muscle p-AKT/mTOR signaling in overweight/obese postmenopausal women with prediabetes; tissue NAD+ change was not detected (Yoshino et al., Science, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.abe9985, PMID 33888596). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).
