## Identity

Parked home: [06 deregulated nutrient sensing](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md) for the GH/IGF axis. Adjacent SKUs: [tesamorelin](../tesamorelin/report.md), [sermorelin](../sermorelin/report.md), [ipamorelin](../ipamorelin/report.md), [cjc-1295-no-dac](../cjc-1295-no-dac/report.md). Aliases: Long Arg3-IGF-1, Long R3-IGF-I, LR3-IGF-I.

💯 IGF-1 LR3 is an 83-residue analogue of human IGF-1: a 13-aa N-terminal extension plus Arg in place of Glu at position 3 of the mature sequence. Mecasermin (Increlex) is recombinant *native* 70-aa IGF-1, a different molecule (DailyMed INCRELEX, setid a8b27a1b-a611-4f91-ad22-76d4b390c3ae).

📚 DailyMed names the labeled drug substance mecasermin (rhIGF-1), 10 mg/mL, indicated for growth failure in pediatric patients ≥2 y with severe primary IGF-1 deficiency or GH-gene-deletion neutralizing antibodies — not adult aging, not LR3 (DailyMed INCRELEX, INDICATIONS AND USAGE).

⛔ PubChem name/CAS lookup for 946870-92-4 this flight returned CID 168009904, titled a C80H154N4O6S2 lipid (MW 1332.2) — not an 83-aa growth factor (PubChem PUG name 946870-92-4 fetched 2026-09-04). Vendor pages that print CID 168009904 next to IGF-1 LR3 are pairing the wrong CID.

🐉 Extra CAS 143045-27-6 and UNII M9L22Y19H9 circulate for Long R3 IGF-1; this flight did not independently title them on PubChem/NCATS (inference from the untitled-registry file).

## Mechanism

📚 Native IGF-1 in the Increlex label: most circulating IGF-1 is bound, >80% as a ternary complex with IGFBP-3 and ALS; free IGF-1 has insulin-like metabolic effects (DailyMed INCRELEX, CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY).

🐉 The LR3 claim — ~100- to 600-fold lower IGFBP affinity and a longer free-fraction half-life — is the design story for the analogue. A primary binding-constant paper was not extracted this flight. no source; inference from the missing-LR3-Kd file.

## Animal data

📚 Increlex was tumorigenic in a 2-year rat SC study (adrenal medullary hyperplasia/pheochromocytoma, skin keratoacanthoma, male mammary carcinoma at high dose); only doses above the MTD from IGF-1 hypoglycemia caused the skin and mammary tumors (DailyMed INCRELEX, PRECAUTIONS / carcinogenesis). Mecasermin rats, not LR3.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Increlex: severe hypoglycemia including hypoglycemic seizures; 30/72 (42%) of the pediatric IGFD series reported hypoglycemia, 5 severe, 4 with seizure/LOC; administer ±20 minutes of a meal; do not dose if the meal is omitted (DailyMed INCRELEX, WARNINGS AND PRECAUTIONS and ADVERSE REACTIONS). Native rhIGF-1 in children with IGFD — not a license to dose an RUO analogue, and not ☠︎ for the vial without a compiled RUO harm series.
