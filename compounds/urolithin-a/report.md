## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Timeline Mitopure 500–1000 mg is sold as “directly activates mitophagy” / cellular recycling (Timeline Mitopure). Timeline/Amazentis sells Mitopure. Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

⛔ Collapsing selective mitophagy into one “mitochondrial age” is a vocabulary collision (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Urolithin A’s published human work is a Parkin-axis / mitochondrial program (hallmark 07), not general macroautophagy restoration (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019; Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 Urolithin A 50 μM extended C. elegans mean lifespan 45.4% (pink-1/dct-1 dependent) and improved grip/running in aged mice (Ryu et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4132, PMID 27400265; Amazentis-associated authors). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Four weeks of oral urolithin A 500–1000 mg/day in sedentary elderly changed skeletal-muscle mitochondrial gene expression and lowered plasma acylcarnitines (Andreux et al., Nature Metabolism, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1038/s42255-019-0073-4, PMID 32694802; Part B N=36, 9/arm, 28 days; Amazentis/Timeline sells Mitopure). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Four months of Mitopure urolithin A 500 or 1000 mg/day in 88 overweight middle-aged adults enriched Parkin-pathway / mitophagy-linked muscle proteins and raised some strength/endurance secondaries (~12% hamstring at 500 mg, p=0.027) but missed the primary peak-power endpoint (Singh et al., Cell Reports Medicine, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.xcrm.2022.100633; N=88, 79 completers, 40–64 y). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 In 66 older adults (mean 71.7 y, 75.8% women, all White), UA 1000 mg/day × 4 months improved FDI and TA contractions-to-fatigue at 2 months vs placebo (FDI +95.3 vs +11.6; TA +41.4 vs +5.7) and lowered several acylcarnitines, ceramides, and CRP; the 6-minute-walk primary (+60.8 vs +42.5 m) and MRS ATP max were not significant vs placebo (Liu et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2021.44279, NCT03283462; Amazentis authors/funding). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Denk 2025 (MitoImmune): UA 1000 mg/day × 28 days in 50 healthy adults 45–70 expanded naive-like, less terminally exhausted CD8+ cells (treatment difference 0.50 percentage points; 95% CI 0.16–0.83; P=0.0437) and CD8+ fatty-acid-oxidation capacity (+14.72 pp; 95% CI 6.46–22.99; P=0.0061); no multiple-comparison adjustment on the cytometry panel (Denk et al., Nature Aging, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s43587-025-00996-x, NCT05735886). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).
