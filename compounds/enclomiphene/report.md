## Identity

Home hallmark: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md) for the HPG-axis SERM claim. Aliases: trans-clomiphene, enclomid, Androxal (failed US name), EnCyzix (refused EU name).

💯 Enclomiphene is the *trans* (E) geometric isomer of clomiphene, a non-steroidal SERM. Racemic clomiphene citrate (Clomid / Serophene) is a mixture of enclomiphene and zuclomiphene.

## Mechanism

📚 Enclomiphene is the *trans* isomer of clomiphene and acts as an estrogen-receptor antagonist at the hypothalamic–pituitary axis, raising LH/FSH and thereby endogenous testicular testosterone. Zuclomiphene (the *cis* isomer in racemic Clomid) is the longer-lived, more estrogen-agonist isomer (Kim, McCullough, and Kaminetsky, *BJU Int.*, 2016;117:677–685, https://doi.org/10.1111/bju.13337).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 In ZA-304/305, men not at TT >450 ng/dL on 12.5 mg/day at week 4 were up-titrated to 25 mg/day. Pooled enclomiphene raised TT into the 300–1040 ng/dL window and raised LH/FSH; topical testosterone gel raised TT and suppressed LH/FSH (Kim et al., 2016).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Kim 2016: two parallel Phase 3 RCTs (ZA-304, ZA-305) in overweight men aged 18–60 with secondary hypogonadism (morning TT ≤300 ng/dL, LH <9.4 IU/L), 16 weeks, enclomiphene citrate 12.5 or 25 mg vs AndroGel 1.62% vs placebo. ZA-304 week-16 mean TT: enclomiphene 445.8 (SD 186.4) vs gel 350.6 (338.1) vs placebo 236.4 (144.7) ng/dL. Sperm-concentration change: enclomiphene +11.7% vs gel −56.6% vs placebo −4.1%. Pooled composite success (sperm ≥10 million/mL and TT 300–1040 ng/dL): enclomiphene 63.5%, gel 24.7%, placebo 5.8% (Kim et al., 2016).

📚 FDA issued a Complete Response Letter on 1 December 2015 for Repros’s enclomiphene NDA (secondary hypogonadism in overweight men). The letter said the Phase 3 designs were no longer adequate to demonstrate clinical benefit and asked for an additional Phase 3 study; it also flagged entry criteria, titration, and bioanalytical validation (Repros Therapeutics press release / SEC exhibit, 1 Dec 2015, https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/897075/000117184315006596/newsrelease.htm).

📚 EMA CHMP adopted a negative opinion on EnCyzix (enclomifene) on 25 January 2018. The committee said testosterone rose but the trials did not show improvement in symptoms (bone strength, weight gain, impotence, libido) and cited a venous-thromboembolism risk; benefit did not outweigh risk (EMA EnCyzix EPAR, https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/medicines/human/EPAR/encyzix).

🐉 No published enclomiphene RCT with an aging or healthspan primary is compiled on this page. Hormone restoration is not that endpoint. no source; inference from the empty aging file.

## Fights

🤼 Clinic copy treats enclomiphene as “Clomid without the zuclomiphene.” The isomer split is real. The approved-drug substitution is not: there is no US or EU marketing authorization for the single isomer.

## Legal / access status

📚 Enclomiphene is not FDA-approved. The Androxal NDA received a CRL (Repros, 1 Dec 2015).
