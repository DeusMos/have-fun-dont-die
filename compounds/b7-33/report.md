## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells a 27-residue single-chain relaxin mimetic as a selective RXFP1 agonist that prefers pERK over cAMP (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/b7-33). Mindful sells the SKU. The papers are Hossain / Bathgate / Samuel (University of Melbourne) and later Devarakonda / Salloum (VCU).

⛔ Treating mouse/rat MI, isoproterenol cardiomyopathy, or ovalbumin-airway fibrosis as a completed human aging or fibrosis restore is a definition swap (Hossain et al., 2016; Devarakonda et al., 2020).

⛔ A 10 mg research-chemical listing is not serelaxin (RLX030) and is not a human RCT (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 B7-33 is H2 relaxin B-chain 7–29 plus C-terminal KRSL from the B1–33 isoform, with Cys11/23 → Ser to block dimerization. It binds RXFP1 (HEK-RXFP1 pKi 5.54 ± 0.13 vs H2 8.96 ± 0.03) and is weak on cAMP in overexpression and THP-1 cells (pEC50 5.12 ± 0.06 vs H2 10.49 ± 0.13). In fibroblasts that natively express RXFP1 it matches H2 on pERK1/2 and MMP-2. MMP-2 promotion is blocked by an RXFP1 antagonist and by the AT2 antagonist PD123319 — the authors read that as RXFP1–AT2R heterodimer signaling. No RXFP2 cAMP at micromolar concentrations. Unlike H2, it did not promote prostate-tumor growth in the in-vivo model they ran (Hossain, Kočan, Yao, Royce, et al., *Chem. Sci.*, 2016;7:3805–3819, https://doi.org/10.1039/c5sc04754d, PMC6013806).

📚 Praveen et al. restate: poor affinity/cAMP in HEK-RXFP1; equipotent to H2 in native fibroblasts; anti-fibrotic in preclinical organs (Praveen et al., *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023;24:12670, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms241612670, PMC10454739).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Hossain 2016 used 0.5 mg/kg/day B7-33 (same mass dose as H2) in post-MI rats, citing prior H2 circulating levels of 20–40 ng/mL at that H2 dose — not a compiled B7-33 plasma table (Hossain et al., 2016, PMC6013806).

📚 Devarakonda et al. dosed 0.25 mg/kg IP at reperfusion, then 0.25 mg/kg BID in the 7-day arm; in vitro 50 and 100 nM on isolated cardiomyocytes (Devarakonda et al., *J. Am. Heart Assoc.*, 2020;9:e015748, https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.119.015748).

## Animal data

📚 Hossain et al.: rat post-MI, B7-33 or H2 0.5 mg/kg/day weeks 8–12 reduced LV collagen vs saline (both p < 0.01) and lowered LVEDP (both p < 0.05). Mouse isoproterenol cardiomyopathy: B7-33 0.25 mg/kg/day vs H2 0.5 mg/kg/day (equimolar) cut interstitial collagen ~51% and ~57% and total LV collagen ~47% and ~53% (both p < 0.01 vs ISO); SBP unchanged. Mouse chronic OVA airways: IN B7-33 0.25 mg/kg/day or H2 0.5 mg/kg/day × 2 weeks reversed epithelial thickening (p < 0.001), normalized lung collagen, and partially reversed AHR at the three highest methacholine doses (p < 0.05 vs OVA; still worse than saline) (Hossain et al., 2016, PMC6013806).

📚 Devarakonda et al.: male CD1 mice, 30 min LAD occlusion; B7-33 0.25 mg/kg IP 5 min before reperfusion. Infarct/AAR 21.99% vs 45.32% (p = 0.02). Fractional shortening 29% vs 23% at 24 h (p = 0.02) and 29% vs 20% at 7 days. Isolated cardiomyocytes: 50 and 100 nM improved survival and cut GRP78 after simulated IR; 100 nM cut tunicamycin-induced GRP78 in an ERK1/2-dependent way (Devarakonda et al., 2020, https://doi.org/10.1161/JAHA.119.015748).
