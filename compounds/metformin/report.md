## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity clinics sell metformin-for-the-healthy next to weekly sirolimus: AgelessRx plus metformin 500 mg; Healthspan lists metformin $27/mo; HealthRX protocol pages name metformin 500 mg; TAME is spoken as if metformin already proved aging-as-indication; Bannister-class headlines say diabetics on metformin outlive non-diabetics (AgelessRx; Healthspan; HealthRX; AFAR TAME; Bannister et al., 2014). Those companies sell the prescriptions they are citing. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

⛔ Treating “it is a hallmark” as proof that swallowing metformin restores youthful sensing in intact people is a taxonomy paper doing a job it was not written for (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2013; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

⛔ A commercial clock decrement sold next to metformin is not a qualified nutrient-sensing surrogate (Waziry et al., Nature Aging, 2023; AgelessRx TruAge). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 ITP metformin 1000 ppm alone from 9 months did not extend lifespan in either sex (male median +7%, P=0.35; site +13/−1/+10%, all NS; females null) (Strong et al., Aging Cell, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12496, PMID 27312235; n=442 males / 421 females). That is the mouse analog of the TAME slogan, and it missed. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#animal-data).

## Human data — labeled / indicated use

📚 DPP: 3234 high-risk adults; metformin 850 mg BID cut incident diabetes 31% (95% CI 17–43) versus placebo over 2.8 years (7.8 vs 11.0 cases/100 PY); intensive lifestyle cut 58% (Knowler et al., NEJM, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa012512, PMID 11832527). Indicated prediabetes. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Konopka 2019: 12 weeks aerobic training in older adults (mean 62 y) plus metformin n=27 vs placebo n=26; metformin attenuated VO2max and whole-body insulin-sensitivity gains and blocked the mitochondrial-respiration increase (Konopka et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12880, PMID 30548390, PMC6351883). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 The same Konopka attenuation of VO2max / insulin-sensitivity / mitochondrial-respiration gains is filed under hallmark 07 as a training interaction, not mito restoration (Konopka et al., Aging Cell, 2019). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🥼 A later pair of RCTs reported no metformin blunting of muscle mitochondrial or AMPK adaptations to exercise (Pilmark et al., Appl Physiol Nutr Metab, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1139/apnm-2021-0194, PMID 34784247; n=29 IGT + 15 lean men crossover). Konopka vs Pilmark is a live paper fight, not a license to call metformin an AMPK gerotherapy. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

⛔ Bannister 2014 CPRD: 78,241 metformin-first T2D vs matched non-diabetics; adjusted median survival 15% longer on metformin (non-diabetic STR 0.85, 95% CI 0.81–0.90). Observational, indicated population, immortal-time and healthy-user bias — not a healthy-person geroprotection RCT (Bannister et al., Diabetes Obes Metab, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.12354, PMID 25041462). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

🥼 Danish registers did not replicate that slogan: metformin-monotherapy initiation associated with higher, not lower, mortality vs no diabetes (adjusted IRR 1.32, 95% CI 1.16–1.50 in singletons; 1.64, 1.10–2.46 in discordant twins) (Keys et al., Int J Epidemiol, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1093/ije/dyac200, PMID 36286820). A 20-year UK comparison found the year-1 metformin-vs-nondiabetic edge reversed by year 4 (STR 0.72, P=0.049); 20-year Cox HR 1.69 against the metformin patients (Stevenson-Hoare et al., BMC Public Health, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-023-15764-y, PMID 37131150). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 AFAR’s TAME page still lists “visionary donors to help launch” a 6-year, 14-site, 3,000-person metformin vs placebo composite (CVD, cancer, dementia, death) in ages 65–79; Wake Forest is the coordinating center; no efficacy results (AFAR, https://www.afar.org/tame-trial/). Existence of a protocol is not a result. NCT02432287 is Barzilai/Einstein MILES (completed 2017; n=16 actual; 6-week metformin 1,700 mg/day crossover RNA-seq in older IGT adults), not TAME. TAME has no launched interventional NCT with results. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 TRIIM: n=9 men, rhGH + DHEA + metformin, ~2.5-year epigenetic-minus-chronological drop, no placebo (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019). That is GH addition, the opposite direction from Laron/IIS reduction. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 TRIIM reported a mean ~2.5-year drop in epigenetic-minus-chronological age after 12 months of rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men aged 51–65 with no placebo arm (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, PMID 31496122). Corresponding author Fahy is at Intervene Immune, which markets the protocol. Extracted from [03](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md).

📜 Six months of metformin titrated to 1,500 mg/day did not significantly change CH VAF versus placebo for any gene, including DNMT3A R882 (Blood 2025 abstract, https://doi.org/10.1182/blood-2025-4954; N=332 non-diabetic female breast-cancer survivors; already filed under hallmarks/01). Extracted from [09](../../hallmarks/09-stem-cell-exhaustion/report.md).

## Legal / access status

📚 DPP metformin 850 mg BID is indicated diabetes-delay evidence in high-risk adults, not a healthy-aging label (Knowler et al., NEJM, 2002). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

🤔 Longevity-clinic metformin-for-the-healthy is off-label storefront practice (AgelessRx; Healthspan; HealthRX). TAME has not launched as an aging-indication trial with results (AFAR TAME page). Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).
