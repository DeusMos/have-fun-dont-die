## Animal data

📚 Oral spermidine (0.3 mM lifelong from 4 months, or 3 mM from 18 months) extended C57BL/6 median lifespan by ~10% when started late, improved diastolic function, and failed to protect Atg5-null cardiomyocytes (Eisenberg et al., Nature Medicine, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.4222, PMID 27841876; Madeo and Eisenberg later disclosed equity in The Longevity Labs, which sells spermidineLIFE). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

📚 Excess autophagy can kill cells by autosis — a ceiling on “more flux is always better,” not a documented harm of 1–8 mg spermidine or 16:8 (Liu et al., PNAS, 2013, PMID 24277826). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Twelve-month spermidine-rich wheat-germ extract at 0.9 mg/day did not beat placebo on the prespecified primary memory endpoint in older adults with subjective cognitive decline (adjusted MST difference −0.03, 95% CI −0.11 to 0.05, P=0.47); ITT secondaries were also null (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1001/jamanetworkopen.2022.13875, PMID 35616942; N=100 ITT, 89 completers, mean age 69; capsules from The Longevity Labs). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

📚 Madeo, Eisenberg, Sigrist, and Stekovic disclosed equity, advisory, CEO, and/or patent ties to The Longevity Labs on SmartAge and related spermidine papers; TLL sells the extract they cite (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022; Madeo et al., Autophagy, 2018, PMID 30306826; Hofer et al., Nature Aging, 2022). Extracted from [05](../../hallmarks/05-disabled-macroautophagy/report.md).

📚 The same SmartAge 0.9 mg MST-primary null is reused in hallmark 04 (Schwarz et al., JAMA Network Open, 2022). Extracted from [04](../../hallmarks/04-loss-of-proteostasis/report.md).
