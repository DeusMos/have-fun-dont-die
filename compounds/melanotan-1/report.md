## Identity

Home hallmark: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md) for the MC1R / pigmentation claim. Aliases: MT-1, afamelanotide, [Nle4,D-Phe7]-α-MSH, Scenesse, CUV1647. Adjacent cyclic analogs: [melanotan-2](../melanotan-2/report.md), [pt-141](../pt-141/report.md).

💯 Afamelanotide (melanotan-I) is a linear 13-residue [Nle4,D-Phe7] analog of α-MSH. It is not the cyclic heptapeptide melanotan-II and it is not bremelanotide.

📚 PubChem name “afamelanotide” maps CID 16197727, titled Afamelanotide, formula C78H111N21O19, MW 1646.8, CAS 75921-69-6 and 1566590-77-9 (PubChem PUG name/CID fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16197727).

📚 Scenesse lists UNII QW68W3J66U and free-base MW 1646.85 (Scenesse USPI, DailyMed setid 94f53286-11dd-7fbb-e053-2a95a90a7c48).

🤔 Mindful Research lists MT-1, CAS 75921-69-6, CID 16197727, C78H111N21O19, MW 1646.84 g/mol (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/mt-1.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). CID, CAS, formula, and MW match CID 16197727.

⛔ Mindful’s product description calls MT-1 a “synthetic cyclic α-MSH analog.” The titled afamelanotide record and the Scenesse label are the linear 13-mer. Cyclic is melanotan-II / bremelanotide (Mindful JSON, 2026-09-04; Scenesse USPI).

🐉 Extra CAS 1566590-77-9 sits on the same titled CID as 75921-69-6. Extra RN, not a wrong-molecule collision. no source; inference from the extra-RN dump.

## Mechanism

📚 Scenesse is labeled a melanocortin-1 receptor (MC1-R) agonist. The indication is to increase pain-free light exposure in adults with a history of phototoxic reactions from erythropoietic protoporphyria (Scenesse USPI, DailyMed).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Single 16 mg implant in 12 healthy adults: mean ± SD Cmax 3.7 ± 1.3 ng/mL, AUC0-inf 138.9 ± 42.6 h·ng/mL; last measurable concentration at 96 h in 9/12 subjects. Apparent t½ ~15 h from the controlled-release implant (Scenesse USPI, DailyMed).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Langendonk 2015: two multicenter RCTs of 16 mg SC implants every 60 days. US (N=94, 3 implants, 180 days): median pain-free direct-sunlight hours 69.4 vs 40.8, p=0.04. EU (N=74, 5 implants, 270 days): 6.0 vs 0.8 hours, p=0.005; phototoxic reactions 77 vs 146, p=0.04. QoL improved; pigmentation partially unblinded the trials (Langendonk, Balwani, Anderson, Bonkovsky, Anstey, Bissell, Bloomer, et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2015;373:48–59, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1411481, PMID 26132941).

📚 Scenesse label Study CUV039 (N=93, 180 days): median hours in direct sunlight 10 am–6 pm on pain-free days 64.1 vs 40.5. Study CUV029 (N=74, 270 days): median hours outdoors 10 am–3 pm on pain-free “most of day in direct sunlight” days 6.0 vs 0.8 (Scenesse USPI, DailyMed).

🐉 No published afamelanotide RCT with an aging or healthspan primary is compiled on this page. EPP photoprotection is not that endpoint. no source; inference from the empty aging file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Common AEs (>2%): implant-site reaction (21% vs 10% vehicle), nausea, oropharyngeal pain, cough, fatigue, dizziness, skin hyperpigmentation, somnolence, melanocytic nevus, respiratory-tract infection, non-acute porphyria, skin irritation. Post-market anaphylaxis reported. Pharmacologic darkening of pre-existing nevi and ephelides; twice-yearly full-body skin exam is on the label (Scenesse USPI, DailyMed).
