## Identity

Home hallmark: [06 deregulated nutrient sensing](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md) for the appetite / weight-loss claim. Aliases: NS-2330, NS2330.

💯 Tesofensine is a phenyltropane triple monoamine reuptake inhibitor (serotonin, norepinephrine, dopamine). It is not a peptide.

## Mechanism

📚 Tesofensine (NS2330) is a centrally acting inhibitor of norepinephrine, dopamine, and serotonin reuptake, developed first for Alzheimer’s / Parkinson’s and then for obesity after weight loss showed up in those programs (Astrup, Madsbad, Breum, Jensen, Kroustrup, and Larsen, *Lancet*, 2008;372:1906–1913, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(08)61525-1, PMID 18950853).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Lehr 2007 population PK in Alzheimer’s patients: tesofensine t½ 234 h (SD 89); major desalkyl metabolite M1 t½ 374 h (SD 98). Absolute bioavailability estimated >90%. Tmax 6–8 h. Vd ~600 L. Oral clearance 30–40 mL/min. Mainly CYP3A4 to M1, the only metabolite detectable in human plasma (Lehr, Staab, Tillmann, Trommeshauser, Raschig, and Schaefer, *Br. J. Clin. Pharmacol.*, 2007;64:36–48, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1365-2125.2007.02855.x, PMID 17324246).

## Animal data

📚 Axel 2010: tesofensine induced hypophagia in DIO rats via indirect α1-adrenoceptor and dopamine D1 pathways; the paper treats the human weight-loss package as already published in Astrup 2008 (Axel, Mikkelsen, and Hansen, *Neuropsychopharmacology*, 2010, PMID 20200509).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Astrup 2008 (TIPO-1): Phase 2, five Danish centers, N=203, BMI 30–≤40, energy-restricted diet, tesofensine 0.25 / 0.5 / 1.0 mg or placebo once daily for 24 weeks, NCT00394667. Completers 161/203 (79%). Mean weight loss: placebo 2.0% (SE 0.60); 0.25 mg 4.5% (0.87); 0.5 mg 9.2% (0.91); 1.0 mg 10.6% (0.84); all active doses greater than placebo, p<0.0001. Common AEs: dry mouth, nausea, constipation, hard stools, diarrhoea, insomnia. Heart rate +7.4 bpm at 0.5 mg (p=0.0001). 0.25 and 0.5 mg did not significantly raise systolic or diastolic BP vs placebo (Astrup et al., 2008).

📚 Saniona announced 17 December 2018 that its partner Medix’s Mexico Phase 3 (N=372, 0.25 and 0.50 mg once daily, 24 weeks, diet + exercise) met the primary endpoint of percent and absolute body-weight change vs placebo (Saniona / GlobeNewswire, 17 Dec 2018, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2018/12/17/1667781/0/en/Saniona-s-tesofensine-meets-primary-and-secondary-endpoints-in-Phase-3-obesity-registration-trial.html).

🐉 No published tesofensine RCT with an aging or healthspan primary is compiled on this page. Twenty-four-week weight loss is not that endpoint. no source; inference from the empty aging file.

## Interactions

📚 Lehr 2007: tesofensine is mainly metabolized by CYP3A4 to M1 (Lehr et al., 2007). That is a clearance path, not a labeled DDI table.

🐉 No US prescribing-information DDI table exists. Piperine on the capsule is a storefront adjuvant, not a CYP3A4 interaction study. no source; inference from the no-US-label file.
