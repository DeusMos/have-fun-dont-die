## Identity

No parked home hallmark: the slogan is sleep, and sleep is not a hallmark. Adjacent Khavinson / insomnia-nomination SKU: [epithalon](../epithalon/report.md). Aliases: emideltide (INN), delta sleep-inducing peptide, WAGGDASGE.

💯 DSIP / emideltide is the synthetic nonapeptide Trp-Ala-Gly-Gly-Asp-Ala-Ser-Gly-Glu, first isolated from sleeping-rabbit blood and later made as a defined sequence.

📚 PubChem name “DSIP” maps CID 68816, titled Delta Sleep-Inducing Peptide, formula C35H48N10O15, MW 848.8; synonyms include Emideltide, DSIP, Trp-Ala-Gly-Gly-Asp-Ala-Ser-Gly-Glu, CAS 62568-57-4, and UNII YN28Z5YZ73. Extra RNs on the CID: 69431-45-4 and 86349-55-5. A same-formula second hit is CID 3623358, titled the expanded IUPAC string — not a wrong-molecule collision (PubChem PUG name/CID/synonyms/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/68816).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells DSIP as a rabbit-derived sleep peptide and a lab neuromodulator of corticotropin / LH / somatostatin (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/dsip). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating Schneider-Helmert IV sleep series, or the name “delta sleep-inducing,” as a completed aging or insomnia restore is a definition swap. Bes 1992 and Monti 1987 did not replicate the sleep win (FDA, citing those papers, https://www.fda.gov/media/193344/download).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Schneider-Helmert / Schoenenberger 1980s IV series reported sleep-continuity moves in unstable sleepers. Bes, Hofman, Schuur, and Van Boxtel 1992 and Monti et al. 1987, double-blind / placebo-controlled, did not reach the same conclusion. FDA staff: insufficient evidence for IV emideltide in chronic insomnia; no study on the nominated SC route; narcolepsy and opioid-withdrawal files were small, unblinded, or case-level (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193344/download; Bes et al., 1992; Monti et al., 1987; Schneider-Helmert and Schoenenberger, 1981).

🐉 No human aging RCT of DSIP is compiled on this page. An IV sleep-lab night is not a healthspan result. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 FDA staff: insomnia papers reported no significant AEs in the extracts they used; withdrawal-symptom studies reported transient headache, nausea, vertigo, and “progressive” hypotension after a second IV dose. Nine-residue peptide: immunogenicity concern for IV/SC, unassessed (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193344/download).

🥼 Schneider-Helmert positive IV sleep series vs Bes 1992 and Monti 1987 nulls. FDA staff called the insomnia file inconclusive and methodologically thin. That is a live published fight, not an amateur split (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193344/download).

## Legal / access status

📚 FDA staff, 11 May 2026: no USP/NF monograph; neither free base nor acetate is a component of an FDA-approved drug; both judged not well-characterized; insufficient evidence for chronic insomnia, narcolepsy, and opioid withdrawal. Staff proposed that emideltide free base and acetate NOT be included on the 503A Bulks List (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193344/download; intro https://www.fda.gov/media/193342/download).

🤔 FDA PCAC met 24 July 2026 and, per contemporaneous law-firm tallies, voted 6–7–1 **against** recommending emideltide / DSIP; it was the only one of the seven peptides not recommended that meeting (McDermott Will & Emery, 2026, https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/bulk-list-bound-pcac-backs-majority-of-peptides-in-two-day-public-meeting/).

📚 This flight did not find DSIP / emideltide named on the WADA 2026 Prohibited List. Do not invent an S0 listing (WADA 2026 list, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).
