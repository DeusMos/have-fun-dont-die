## Identity

Home hallmark: [03 epigenetic alterations](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md). Aliases: thymosin α1, Tα1, thymalfasin, Zadaxin. Adjacent TRIIM-stack SKUs: [tesamorelin](../tesamorelin/report.md), [tb-500](../tb-500/report.md) (different peptide — thymosin-β4 fragment).

💯 Thymalfasin is synthetic N-acetyl thymosin α1, a 28-residue acetylated fragment of prothymosin α; Zadaxin is that peptide as a labeled injection in some countries outside the US (PubChem CID 16130571 title Thymalfasin; Singapore NDF ZADAXIN SIN07483P; FDA PCAC staff, 4 Dec 2024).

📚 PubChem CID 16130571 lists Thymalfasin, formula C129H215N33O55, MW 3108.3 g/mol. ChemIDplus / DrugBank / DSSTox on that record print CAS 62304-98-7 as Thymalfasin [USAN:INN]. CAS Common Chemistry prints 69521-94-4 as Thymosin alpha-1. The name-search RN dump also includes 1337515-90-8 and 69440-99-9 (PubChem PUG name/CID/RN and pug_view CAS fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16130571).

📚 FDA’s December 4, 2024 PCAC staff briefing states CAS 62304-98-7 is the free-base number and that SciClone’s Zadaxin is Ta1 free base 1.6 mg/mL; Ta1 acetate was evaluated as a separate, poorly characterized nomination (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/183892/download).

📚 NCATS UNII W0B22ISQ1C preferred name THYMALFASIN, primary CAS 62304-98-7; the record says the drug is not FDA-approved and is used in China and some other countries as Zadaxin (NCATS, https://drugs.ncats.io/substance/W0B22ISQ1C).

🤔 Mindful Research lists CAS 62304-98-7, CID 16130571, formula C129H215N33O55, MW 3108.32 g/mol, alias thymalfasin (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/thymosin-alpha-1.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). That table pairing matches the INN record.

⛔ The same Mindful page’s “Further Information” href points at CID 16132172, which PubChem titles Tachykinin neuropeptide gamma (C99H158N34O29S, MW 2320.6) — not thymalfasin (Shopify product JSON; PubChem PUG CID 16132172).

🐉 CAS 1337515-90-8 and 69440-99-9 on the thymalfasin RN dump were not independently titled this round; do not file them as acetate (PubChem PUG xrefs).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Peptide-clinic and gray-market copy sells a 10 mg RUO vial, or “Zadaxin / thymalfasin,” as thymus / immunity / TRIIM-shaped aging restore in ordinary adults (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/thymosin-alpha-1; Rapamycin News thread 25573). Those pages sell or discuss the peptide.

⛔ Treating Zadaxin’s chronic-hepatitis indication as proof that ordinary-adult aging or thymic involution can be restored is an ex-US antiviral/immunomodulator label doing a job it was not written for (Singapore NDF SIN07483P; FDA PCAC staff 2024).

⛔ Treating thymosin α1 as TRIIM ignores that TRIIM used rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men, not this peptide (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028, PMID 31496122).

## Mechanism

📚 The SciClone monograph: mechanism is not completely understood; framed as T-cell augmentation (differentiation/maturation of CD3/CD4/CD8, IFN-γ / IL-2 / IL-3 / IL-2R after mitogen, NK activity) (RxList reprint of Zadaxin PI, https://www.rxlist.com/zadaxin-drug.htm).

📚 FDA staff: immunomodulatory activity mediated at least in part by TLR9 on dendritic and lymphoid progenitor cells (FDA PCAC staff, 4 Dec 2024, https://www.fda.gov/media/183892/download).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 SciClone monograph: single SC doses 0.8–6.4 mg and multiple-dose 1.6–16 mg × 5–7 days in adult volunteers; peak serum ~2 hours; Cmax and AUC dose-proportional; back to basal by 24 hours; t1/2 ~2 hours; no accumulation; urine excretion 31–60% of the dose (RxList Zadaxin PI, Clinical Pharmacology). That is labeled-product PK, not a gray-market vial table.

## Animal data

📚 FDA staff, from the Zadaxin monograph summaries (underlying data not in the deck): single SC up to 20 mg/kg no drug-related signals in mice, rats, marmosets; repeat SC up to 6 mg/kg/day × 13 weeks or 1 mg/kg/day × 26 weeks no drug-related signals; genotoxicity assays negative; no carcinogenicity studies identified (FDA PCAC staff, 4 Dec 2024).

📚 SciClone: mouse and rabbit teratology showed no difference in fetal abnormalities vs controls; highest single animal dose cited as 800× the clinical 1.6 mg (RxList Zadaxin PI, Precautions / Overdose). Label developmental notes, not an aging lifespan study.

🐉 No compiled Ta1 rodent lifespan or healthy-aging dataset was fetched this round (inference from the labeled-product / 503A file).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Singapore NDF / SciClone: Zadaxin 1.6 mg SC twice weekly × 6 months in chronic HBV; pooled 3 RCTs, n=223, response = HBV DNA and HBeAg negative at 12-month follow-up, 36% Ta1 vs 19% placebo/no treatment (US Phase 2 83% vs 25%; US Phase 3 24% vs 12%; Taiwan Phase 3 37% vs 25%) (Singapore NDF SIN07483P; RxList Zadaxin PI tables; Chien et al., Hepatology, 1998 cited on the label).

📚 SciClone HCV combination tables: pooled sustained ALT response 22.4% Ta1+interferon vs 9.3% interferon alone (RxList Zadaxin PI). That is hepatitis biochemistry, not aging.

📚 TRIIM reported a mean ~2.5-year drop in epigenetic-minus-chronological age after 12 months of rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men with no placebo arm (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028). Restore from hallmark 03. That protocol is not thymosin α1.

🐉 No compiled Ta1 RCT in ordinary adults with a healthspan, mortality, thymic-MRI, or epigenetic-age primary endpoint was found this round (FDA PCAC staff 2024 evaluated HBV/HCV/HIV/COVID/vaccine-adjuvant/cancer/sepsis/HSCT/COPD/ME-CFS and did not list healthy aging).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Zadaxin is contraindicated in hypersensitivity to thymosin α1 or any injection component, and should be considered contraindicated in deliberate immunosuppression (e.g. organ transplant) unless benefits clearly outweigh risks (Singapore NDF SIN07483P, Contraindications; RxList Zadaxin PI).

📚 A transient ALT rise to more than twice baseline (flare) can occur during Zadaxin HBV therapy; the label says continue unless signs of liver failure appear (Singapore NDF SIN07483P).

📚 SciClone: over 2000 individuals, <1% drug-related adverse events claimed; infrequent mild ISR, rare erythema, transient muscle atrophy, polyarthralgia with hand edema, and rash; not for IV use (RxList Zadaxin PI; Singapore NDF dosing).

📚 Pregnancy Category C in the monograph: animal teratology unremarkable; unknown whether it harms a human fetus; pediatric safety/effectiveness not established under 18 (RxList Zadaxin PI).

📚 FDA staff: insufficient safety information and a lack of immunogenicity-risk data weigh against 503A listing for both free base and acetate (FDA PCAC staff, 4 Dec 2024). That is a compounding-list weighing, not a completed harm series.

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market Ta1 in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Labeled ISR / hypersensitivity / transplant caution are not ☠︎︎ for the RUO vial. Stay-away is not issued.

## Interactions

📚 Interactions between Zadaxin and other drugs have not been fully evaluated; the monograph says caution with other immunomodulating drugs and do not mix Zadaxin with any other drug (RxList Zadaxin PI, Drug Interactions).

📚 Labeled HBV/HCV use is often with interferon at the interferon label’s dose and schedule (Singapore NDF SIN07483P). That is combination-indication copy, not a CYP table.

## Fights

## Legal / access status

📚 Thymalfasin / Zadaxin is not FDA-approved (NCATS; FDA PCAC staff 2024). It is a prescription product in at least Singapore (SIN07483P, last updated 28 July 2026) for chronic hepatitis B ± interferon (Singapore NDF).

📚 FDA staff, 4 December 2024 PCAC: nomination for 503A was withdrawn; FDA evaluated Ta1 free base and acetate at its discretion and concluded the four criteria weigh against adding either to the 503A Bulks List (physicochemical characterization, historical use, effectiveness, safety/immunogenicity) (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/183892/download).

🤔 Mindful Research LLC states it is a chemical supplier and that products are sold only as research chemicals for in vitro use (https://happypeptides.com/policies/terms-of-service).

⛔ Calling a 10 mg RUO vial “Zadaxin” or “FDA-approved thymalfasin for aging” collapses an ex-US hepatitis injection into a research-chem SKU (Singapore NDF; FDA 2024; Mindful JSON).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Thymalfasin is real acetylated thymosin α1. CAS 62304-98-7 / CID 16130571 is the INN free-base pairing. Zadaxin is an ex-US 1.6 mg SC BIW hepatitis product with labeled hypersensitivity / transplant cautions, ALT flare, and an incomplete interaction table. FDA staff argued against 503A listing. TRIIM did not use this peptide (Singapore NDF; FDA 2024; Fahy 2019).

⛔ That file is not organismal aging restore, and a Mindful vial is not Zadaxin. The page’s footer CID is a tachykinin (Mindful JSON; PubChem 16132172).

🤔 People can buy 10 mg RUO vials and, in some countries, labeled 1.6 mg HBV injections today. That is practice plus an ex-US label, not a healthspan result.
