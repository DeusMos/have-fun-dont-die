## Identity

Home hallmark: [08 cellular senescence](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md). Aliases in the extracted reports: Quercefit. Pair: [dasatinib](../dasatinib/report.md). Also sold inside Qualia / Life Extension / AMPK stacks.

**CAS:** 117-39-5 (📚 PubChem Quercetin CID page; CAS 117-39-5. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Quercetin).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Qualia Senolytic lists 1400 mg fisetin + 750 mg Quercefit; Mayo-copied D+Q pulses use 1000–1250 mg quercetin × 2–3 days; forum D+Q is 100 mg + 1.2–1.5 g (Qualia Life; Hickson 2019; Rapamycin News). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

## Mechanism

📚 Dasatinib and quercetin hit different lineages in the 2015 screen: quercetin (10 μM) cut senescent HUVEC viability ~50%; D+Q together cut senescent preadipocytes ~70% (Zhu et al., Aging Cell, 2015, PMID 25754370). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#mechanism).


## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 CSF quercetin was undetectable in the Gonzales AD D+Q pilot (Gonzales et al. — filed under [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md)).

🐉 Oral quercetin bioavailability / t½ are not compiled on this page. no source; inference from the missing-PK rule.

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Intermittent oral D+Q for 12 weeks in mild symptomatic AD: plasma Q 3.29–26.3 ng/ml in all 5; Q undetectable in CSF (Gonzales et al., Nature Medicine, 2023, PMID 37679434; N=5). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 Hickson, Justice, Nambiar, Farr, and Lee D+Q human series are compiled on [dasatinib](../dasatinib/report.md); quercetin doses in those pilots were 1000 mg, 1250 mg, 1000 mg, and 500 mg respectively. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).
