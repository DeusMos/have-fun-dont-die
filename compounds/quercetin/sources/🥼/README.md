# 🥼 Papers in a live fight.

Research literature disagrees — published, preprint, or both. Cite both sides. Do not pick a winner to look tidy.

A document lives here because the **claim** in `report.md` is marked 🥼. If the paper is in a live fight, use this dir — not 📚, not 📜, not 🤼. That includes paper vs paper and preprint vs paper.

## What belongs here

Both sides of a live research disagreement. Journal PDFs, preprint PDFs, html, and markdown notes. Keep the opposing papers together here.

## What does not belong here

- Settled, uncontested textbook facts → [💯](../💯/)
- A published finding that is not currently in a serious fight → [📚](../📚/)
- A preprint that is not in a literature fight → [📜](../📜/)
- Forums, clinics, newsletters, podcasts, self-experiments, protocols-in-the-wild → [🤔](../🤔/)
- Forum / Discord / blog / clinic-marketing disagreements. **Not research papers.** → [🤼](../🤼/)
- Circulating claims that do not make sense → [⛔](../⛔/)
- Wild speculation with little or no direct evidence → [🐉](../🐉/)

## File names

Short and source-identifying: `Author-Year-short-slug.ext` (or equivalent). Example: `Lopez-Otin-2023-hallmarks-rebuttal.pdf`.
