## Identity

Home hallmark: [13 extracellular matrix changes](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md). Aliases: GHK-Cu, copper tripeptide-1, prezatide copper. Related copper peptide already split out: [ahk-cu](../ahk-cu/report.md). GLOW/KLOW stack-mates: [bpc-157](../bpc-157/report.md), [tb-500](../tb-500/report.md), [kpv](../kpv/report.md).

💯 GHK is the tripeptide Gly-His-Lys; GHK-Cu is that ligand plus copper(II) (PubChem CID 73587 title glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine; CID 71587328 title Prezatide copper).

📚 PubChem CID 71587328 lists prezatide copper (copper tripeptide-1 / GHK copper), CAS 89030-95-5, formula C14H23CuN6O4+, MW 402.92 g/mol (PubChem, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/71587328; modify date 2026-08-31).

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII 6BJQ43T1I9 is PREZATIDE COPPER and maps CAS 89030-95-5; UNII availability does not imply review or approval (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/6BJQ43T1I9).

📚 PubChem CID 73587 is free GHK (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine), formula C14H24N6O4, MW 340.38 g/mol, CAS 49557-75-7, UNII 39TG2H631E — no copper in the structure (PubChem, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/73587).

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII A3LEI4P1NB is PREZATIDE COPPER ACETATE, CAS 130120-57-9, with synonyms IAMIN, PC-1020 — a distinct salt listing from the complex CAS 89030-95-5 (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/A3LEI4P1NB).

⛔ Mindful Research lists CAS 89030-95-5 next to PubChem CID 73587 and MW 340.384 g/mol while writing copper into the formula — that paste pairs the copper-complex CAS with the free-GHK CID and free-GHK mass (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/ghk-cu.json; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

⛔ CAS 49557-75-7 is free GHK / prezatide on CID 73587; [ahk-cu](../ahk-cu/report.md) currently parks the same number — that collision is an ahk-cu problem, not a GHK-Cu identity (PubChem CID 73587; compounds/ahk-cu/report.md).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity and peptide-clinic copy sells 1–2 mg/day SubQ GHK-Cu, 50–100 mg RUO vials, and ~1% copper-peptide serums as ECM / collagen / skin-youth / hair restore (Peptide Protocol Wiki, https://www.peptideprotocolwiki.com/peptides/ghk-cu/dosing; The Ordinary, https://theordinary.com/en-us/multi-peptide-copper-peptides-1-serum-100625.html; Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/ghk-cu). Those pages sell or refer the peptide or the serum.

⛔ Treating Maquart 1988 nM dish collagen or Maquart 1993 rat wound-chamber collagen as proof that 1–2 mg human SubQ or a topical serum restores organismal ECM is a definition swap (Maquart et al., FEBS Letters, 1988, https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(88)80509-X; Maquart et al., JCI, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI116842).

⛔ Filing Miller 2006 as a positive human aging / ECM-restore trial ignores the paper’s own objective endpoints (Miller et al., Archives of Facial Plastic Surgery, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1001/archfaci.8.4.252, PMID 16847171).

⛔ Reading Pickart Connectivity Map / “younger profile” reviews as organismal rejuvenation treats a conflicted review of cell-line microarray matches as a healthspan trial (Pickart and Margolina, IJMS, 2018, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms19071987; Skin Biology sells GHK-Cu products).

⛔ Reading Teuscher 2024 worm collagen / matreotype as GHK-Cu human restore fails the paper’s organism and its intervention list (Teuscher, Statzer et al., Nature Communications, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44409-2).

## Mechanism

📚 Pickart, Thayer, and Thaler reported a synthetic glycyl-histidyl-lysine that, at nanomolar concentrations, increased survival of normal hepatocytes from regenerating rat liver and enhanced growth of cultured hepatoma cells (Pickart, Thayer, Thaler, BBRC, 1973, https://doi.org/10.1016/0006-291X(73)91459-9).

📚 GHK-Cu stimulated collagen synthesis in fibroblast cultures beginning between 10⁻¹² and 10⁻¹¹ M, maximizing at 10⁻⁹ M, independent of cell number; Pickart was a Procyte-affiliated coauthor (Maquart, Pickart, Laurent, Gillery, Monboisse, Borel, FEBS Letters, 1988, https://doi.org/10.1016/0014-5793(88)80509-X, PMID 3169264).

📚 GHK enhanced copper uptake into cultured HTC4 hepatoma cells when the peptide was added with copper (Pickart, Freedman, Loker et al., Nature, 1980, https://doi.org/10.1038/288715a0, PMID 7453802).

📚 Pickart, Vasquez-Soltero, and Margolina review GHK-Cu as coordinating Cu(II) via histidine imidazole, glycine α-amino, and the deprotonated Gly-His amide nitrogen; all three authors list Skin Biology, Bellevue WA (Pickart et al., BioMed Research International, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1155/2015/648108, PMID 26236730).

📚 Pickart and Margolina (IJMS, 2018) report Connectivity Map profiles in which 31.2% of genes changed ≥50% (59% up, 41% down); authors are R&D Skin Biology and declared no conflict of interest (Pickart and Margolina, IJMS, 2018, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms19071987, PMID 29986520). Skin Biology sells copper-peptide products.

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 CIR extracts Endo 1997: rat IV GHK 10 mg/kg was rapidly degraded to His-Lys and cleared from circulating blood within a 60-minute sampling window; GHK is unstable in human plasma and rapidly degraded by aminopeptidases (CIR Expert Panel, 2014 / IJT 2018, https://www.cir-safety.org/sites/default/files/tripep062014final.pdf; Endo et al., J Chromatogr B, 1997, 692:37–42). That is free GHK IV in rats, not a human SubQ GHK-Cu table.

🐉 No published human pharmacokinetic table for exogenous subcutaneous GHK-Cu (t½, Vd, CL, F) was compiled this round (Peptide Protocol Wiki; Dosagepeptide; CIR 2014). Do not invent one.

⛔ HealthRX prints estimated plasma t½ 0.5–1 h and Cmax 15–30 min as if those were measured SubQ GHK-Cu PK; the same page admits no formal FDA PK study (HealthRX, https://healthrx.com/ghk-cu/pharmacokinetics).

## Animal data

📚 Serial 2.0 mg injections of Procyte PC 1020 (GHK-Cu) into rat Schilling wound chambers raised day-29 collagen to 344% of DPBS controls, dry weight 223%, total protein 230%, and GAGs 208% (all P<0.01), with collagen rising about twice as much as other proteins (Maquart, Bellon, Chaqour, Wegrowski, Patt, Trachy et al., JCI, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI116842, PMID 8227353; supported in part by Procyte, which supplied PC 1020).

📚 In the Maquart 1993 specificity arm (n=10 chambers each, harvest day 21), only GHK-Cu raised collagen versus DPBS (P<0.01); equimolar free GHK slightly decreased collagen and CuCl₂ had no significant effect (Maquart et al., JCI, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI116842).

📚 Maquart 1993 dose-response (0.2–4.0 mg/injection) was significant from 0.5 mg (Maquart et al., JCI, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI116842).

📚 Those Maquart results are local wound-chamber ECM in rats after serial chamber injections — not systemic human ECM restore and not a 1–2 mg SubQ healthspan result (Maquart et al., JCI, 1993).

📚 Teuscher, Statzer et al. 2024 mapped *C. elegans* matreotype / collagen dynamics; the paper does not study GHK-Cu (Teuscher, Statzer et al., Nature Communications, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-023-44409-2).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Miller 2006 randomized patients after circumoral CO₂ laser resurfacing to a post-treatment regimen with or without topical GHK-Cu; 13 completed; computer analysis and blinded evaluators found no difference in erythema resolution, wrinkles, or overall skin quality at 12 weeks; the patient questionnaire for overall skin quality was P=0.04 (Miller, Wagner, Baack, Eisbach, Archives of Facial Plastic Surgery, 2006, https://doi.org/10.1001/archfaci.8.4.252, PMID 16847171).

📚 Bishop 1992 randomized venous-stasis ulcers to 0.4% GHK-Cu cream (n=29 evaluable), 1% silver sulfadiazine (n=28), or vehicle (n=29); last-visit mean lesion-size improvement was 18.7% (GHK-Cu) vs 22.5% (placebo) vs 44.0% (SSD); GHK-Cu vs placebo P=0.82; zero GHK-Cu ulcers completely healed vs 1/29 placebo and 6/28 SSD (Bishop, Phillips, Mustoe et al., Journal of Vascular Surgery, 1992, https://doi.org/10.1016/0741-5214(92)90115-o).

📚 Mulder 1994, with ProCyte coauthors, reported that Iamin Gel (GHK:Cu) raised median plantar-ulcer area closure to 98.5% vs 60.8% vehicle (P<0.05) and cut infection 7% vs 34% (P<0.05) under standardized debridement and footwear (Mulder, Patt, Sanders et al., Wound Repair and Regeneration, 1994, https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1524-475x.1994.20406.x, PMID 17147644).

🤔 Contemporaneous reporting said ProCyte’s 511-patient, 30-center Phase III Iamin program missed primary endpoints versus placebo (Seattle Times, 17 Oct 1994, https://archive.seattletimes.com/archive/19941017/1936453/procyte-drug-fails-test-stock-plunges----clinical-trials-cast-doubt-on-companys-future). A peer-reviewed Phase III paper was not fetched this round.

📚 FDA 510(k) K953853 cleared “IAMIN GEL WOUND DRESSING” for Procyte on 5 Feb 1996 as substantially equivalent — a hydrogel dressing, not an NDA drug approval (FDA 510(k), https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K953853).

⛔ Calling Iamin “FDA-approved GHK-Cu” as if that were a drug restore for aging ECM collapses a 510(k) dressing clearance after a reported Phase III miss into efficacy (FDA K953853; Seattle Times, 17 Oct 1994).

📚 NCT07437586 is a recruiting Phase 2 split-wound topical 0.1% GHK-Cu gel vs vehicle for 5 mm punch-biopsy wounds, estimated n=60, primary time to re-epithelialization; hasResults=false — acute wound, not aging, not SubQ (ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07437586, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07437586; sponsor Hudson Biotech).

📚 NCT07706361 measures circulating GHK and GHK-Cu after a marketed acupuncture-point patch versus placebo; it does not administer GHK-Cu as a drug (ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07706361, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07706361).

📚 NCT05932732 is a completed open-label HydraFacial series (n=27) whose ReGen-GF booster copy names Cu-GHK among many serum ingredients — not a GHK-Cu drug trial (ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT05932732, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT05932732).

🐉 A ClinicalTrials.gov hunt on 2026-09-03 returned no completed subcutaneous GHK-Cu aging, ECM-restore, or injectable-PK RCT (ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 query GHK-Cu OR prezatide OR “copper tripeptide”).

📚 Mortazavi, Vadoud, and Moghimi 2025 reviewed topical GHK / GHK-Cu / Pal-GHK as anti-wrinkle peptides and wrote there is a “surprising absence of clinical studies using them” (Mortazavi et al., BioImpacts, 2025, https://doi.org/10.34172/bi.30071).

## Toxicity and hazards

💯 Copper is an essential cofactor; Wilson disease (ATP7B) is hereditary copper-overload with neurologic and liver injury (NIH ODS Copper fact sheet, https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Copper-HealthProfessional/).

📚 Adult copper RDA is 900 mcg/day; FNB UL from food and supplements is 10 mg/day based on liver damage; the UL does not apply to medically supervised copper (NIH ODS, same fact sheet).

📚 CIR 2014/2018 concluded tripeptide-1, copper tripeptide-1, and bis(tripeptide-1) copper acetate are safe in present cosmetic practices of use and concentration; typical use <10 ppm; Council survey max 0.002% leave-on for the class (CIR Final Report, https://www.cir-safety.org/sites/default/files/tripep062014final.pdf; IJT 2018, https://doi.org/10.1177/1091581818807863). That is ppm cosmetics, not a 50–100 mg injectable vial.

📚 FDA’s withdrawn-nomination table states compounded injectable GHK-Cu may pose immunogenicity risk from aggregation and peptide-related impurities, and that human safety data are limited — Category 2 language later withdrawn with the nomination, not a completed harm series (FDA, Certain Bulk Drug Substances… Significant Safety Risks, https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks).

🤔 Peptide Protocol Wiki contraindicates SC injection in Wilson disease or copper-overload disorders and suggests serum copper / ceruloplasmin monitoring for injection use (https://www.peptideprotocolwiki.com/peptides/ghk-cu/dosing). That is clinic copy, not a GHK-Cu case series.

🤔 The Ordinary tells users irritation → rinse off / cease use, and to patch-test (https://theordinary.com/en-us/multi-peptide-copper-peptides-1-serum-100625.html).

🐉 No published case series or trial establishing copper toxicity, Wilson decompensation, or serious systemic harm from GHK-Cu itself was found this round; Wilson is ATP7B copper disease, not a GHK-Cu outcome. That gap is not ☠︎︎ and is not proof of safety (NIH ODS; FDA withdrawn injectable text; no primary GHK-Cu Wilson case located).

## Interactions

📚 NIH ODS: copper is not known to have clinically relevant interactions with medications; high zinc can induce copper deficiency (NIH ODS Copper fact sheet, https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Copper-HealthProfessional/).

🤔 The Ordinary tells users not to combine the 1% copper-peptide serum with direct acids, retinoids, or vitamin C (https://theordinary.com/en-us/multi-peptide-copper-peptides-1-serum-100625.html). DECIEM sells the serum.

🐉 There is no FDA-labeled systemic GHK-Cu drug and no compiled CYP/transporter interaction table was found this round (CLYR; Dosagepeptide; CIR).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Peptide Protocol Wiki lists topical GHK-Cu 0.01–1% once or twice daily for 8–12 weeks and subcutaneous 1–2 mg once daily, 4–8 weeks on / 2–4 weeks off, and states no FDA-approved injectable dose exists (https://www.peptideprotocolwiki.com/peptides/ghk-cu/dosing; updated 29 Jan 2026).

🤔 Dosagepeptide prints reconstitution arithmetic for 50/100 mg vials and states no approved injectable product and no published human injection trial (https://www.dosagepeptide.com/ghk-cu-dosage-injection-chart-reconstitution/).

🤔 HealthRX prints compounded off-label 1–2 mg subcutaneous daily or every other day, some titrating toward 5 mg three times weekly, cycles 8–12 weeks then 4 weeks off (https://healthrx.com/peptides-performance/ghk-cu-injection-dose). HealthRX traffics compounding copy.

🤔 Gray-market / research-chem buyers can source GHK-Cu from Mindful Research at 50 mg $42 and 100 mg $79.50, and as a constituent of GLOW, KLOW, and REJUV-3X (https://happypeptides.com/products/ghk-cu.json; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

📚 FDA’s 503A nominations document states GHK-Cu (except for injectable routes) is in Category 1 after a May 2026 clarification; FDA intends to consult PCAC before the end of February 2027; injectable GHK-Cu is not on that Category 1 line (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/94155/download).

## Fights

🤼 Topical versus injectable GHK-Cu is an amateur/clinic split: wiki and compounding pages traffic 1–2 mg/day SubQ as systemic ECM restore; CLYR treats topical as the evidence-bearing route and injection as under-proven (Peptide Protocol Wiki; CLYR Health, https://www.clyr.health/journal/ghk-cu-injectable-copper-peptide/; CLYR sells a 3% topical cream).

🤼 HealthRX 1–2 mg daily-or-EOD versus wiki 1–2 mg daily cycle versus Dosagepeptide’s refusal to print a sourced injectable mg is a protocol-number split, not a paper fight (same URLs).

📚 Miller 2006 (topical post-laser objective-null) and Mulder 1994 (topical diabetic-ulcer signal) are different indications and are not a same-endpoint literature fight (Miller et al., 2006; Mulder et al., 1994).

## Legal / access status

🤔 Mindful Research LLC states products are for in vitro laboratory research only (https://happypeptides.com/policies/terms-of-service).

🤔 CLYR and Dosagepeptide state there is no FDA-approved GHK-Cu drug product for any indication (CLYR journal; Dosagepeptide).

📚 Iamin wet/gel dressings were 510(k)-cleared hydrogel wound dressings, not NDA drugs (FDA K953853; FDA K964468, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfpmn/pmn.cfm?ID=K964468).

📚 Cosmetic copper tripeptide-1 sits under CIR “safe as used” at surveyed ppm-class concentrations (CIR 2014).

⛔ A 503A Category 1 parking spot or a withdrawn Category 2 immunogenicity sentence is compounding policy, not a demonstration that injected or topical GHK-Cu restores ECM (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/94155/download).

## Open questions

🐉 Whether NCT07437586 topical 0.1% gel shortens punch-biopsy re-epithelialization is not a result yet (ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07437586).

🐉 Whether any salt sold as “GHK-Cu” matches Maquart PC 1020, Iamin gel, or CID 71587328 vs the acetate listing is not compiled (FDA SRS; Mindful CID 73587 paste).

🐉 Whether 1–2 mg/day SubQ copies rat wound-chamber collagen into human aging ECM is a leap, not a trial (Maquart 1993; Peptide Protocol Wiki).

## What is actually on the table

📚 GHK-Cu is a real copper(II) complex with nM fibroblast collagen (Maquart 1988) and rat wound-chamber ECM accumulation (Maquart 1993, Procyte PC 1020).

📚 The compiled human topical file is Miller 2006 objective-null after CO₂ laser, Bishop 1992 venous-ulcer null vs vehicle, and Mulder 1994 diabetic-ulcer gel signal that did not become an NDA (Miller 2006; Bishop 1992; Mulder 1994; FDA K953853).

⛔ That biology is not a completed human ECM-restore or healthspan result, and CID 73587 is not the copper complex (PubChem CID 71587328 vs 73587; Miller 2006).

🐉 No subcutaneous GHK-Cu aging RCT was on the registry this round (ClinicalTrials.gov API, 2026-09-03).

🤔 People can buy 50/100 mg RUO vials, GLOW/KLOW/REJUV-3X blends, 1% OTC serum, and 1–2 mg compounded SubQ protocols today. That is practice, not healthspan (Mindful Research; The Ordinary; Peptide Protocol Wiki).
