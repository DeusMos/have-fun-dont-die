## Animal data

📚 Serial 2.0 mg GHK-Cu injections into rat wound chambers raised day-29 collagen to 344% of saline controls, dry weight 223%, total protein 230%, and GAGs 208% (all P<0.01) (Maquart et al., JCI, 1993, https://doi.org/10.1172/JCI116842, PMID 8227353). Extracted from [13](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Miller 2006 randomized 13 patients after circumoral CO2 laser resurfacing to GHK-Cu skincare versus control; blinded computer and evaluator scores found no difference in erythema resolution, wrinkles, or overall skin quality at 12 weeks (patient satisfaction P=0.04 only) (Miller et al., Archives of Facial Plastic Surgery, 2006, PMID 16847171). Extracted from [13](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md).

🐉 No published controlled human trial has tested subcutaneous GHK-Cu for aging-ECM restore, pharmacokinetics, or healthspan; 1–2 mg/day injectable protocols are clinic/wiki convention (Peptide Protocol Wiki; Miller 2006 is topical and objective-null). Extracted from [13](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md).
