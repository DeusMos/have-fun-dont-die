# Yuan et al., 2021. The mitochondrial signaling peptide MOTS-c improves myocardial performance during exercise training in rats. Scientific Reports.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-021-99568-3
Used for: 📚 IP MOTS-c 0.5 mg/kg/day × 12 weeks plus treadmill changed rat echocardiographic EF/HR and P–V indices versus sedentary control.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Male SD rats, 6 wk old, n=8/group (control, exercise, exercise+MOTS-c). MOTS-c 0.5 mg/kg/day IP 30 min before exercise, 12 weeks; treadmill 20 m/min, +10°, 60 min, 5 d/wk. ME vs C: EF p<0.001, HR p=0.021, EDV p=0.015. ME vs E: SW, CO, Powmax, dP/dtmin, Tau lower (p<0.001). Animal cardiac performance, not human mito restore.
Conflict if any: None noted.
