# Live literature fight: circulating MOTS-c concentration by assay.
Knoop et al., Rapid Commun Mass Spectrom, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1002/rcm.8337
Cuyàs et al., Aging, 2023, https://www.aging-us.com/article/204423/text
Reynolds et al., Nat Commun, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20790-0
Lee et al., Cell Metab, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cmet.2015.02.009
Used for: 🥼 Papers do not agree on what “plasma MOTS-c” is. Reynolds reports circulating MOTS-c in the ~125–190 pg/mL range after a bike test. Commercial competitive ELISAs (Cloud-Clone CEX132Hu and the kit Knoop compared) read tens to hundreds of ng/mL. Knoop’s WADA LC/MS (LLOD 100 pg/mL) did not confirm the ELISA ng/mL values in n=20. Cuyàs states kit immunoreactive species are not identical and literature spans 154 pg/mL to 584 ng/mL. Lee 2015 used a MOTS-c ELISA for circulation. This is a methods fight, not settled measurement.
