## Animal data

📚 Late-life intermittent MOTS-c (3×/week from 23.5 months) increased physical capacity in mice; daily 5–15 mg/kg IP improved treadmill performance in young mice on chow or high-fat diet (Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-020-20790-0). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 In 10 sedentary healthy young men, bicycle exercise raised skeletal-muscle MOTS-c ~11.9-fold and circulating MOTS-c ~1.5–1.6-fold, returning toward baseline after 4 h rest (Reynolds et al., Nature Communications, 2021; n=10; Wilcoxon). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🐉 No completed native-MOTS-c anti-aging RCT was compiled in the 07 extract. no source; inference from the extract — the fact is not compiled as a human restore trial.
