## Mechanism

📚 Cagrilintide is a long-acting amylin analogue; amylin slows gastric emptying and suppresses appetite. Phase 2 and 3 programs dosed it once weekly, alone or with semaglutide as CagriSema (Lau et al., *Lancet*, 2021;398:2160–2172, PMID 34798060, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)01751-7; Garvey et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2502081).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Lau et al.: Phase 2, n=706 overweight/obesity without diabetes, 26 weeks; cagrilintide 0.3–4.5 mg weekly vs liraglutide 3.0 mg vs placebo. Trial-product estimand: all cagrilintide doses 6.0–10.8% weight loss vs placebo 3.0%; 4.5 mg 10.8% (11.5 kg) vs liraglutide 3.0 mg 9.0% (9.6 kg), ETD 1.8%, p=0.03. NCT03856047. Novo-funded (Lau, Erichsen, Francisco, Satylganova, le Roux, McGowan, et al., *Lancet*, 2021;398:2160–2172, PMID 34798060, https://doi.org/10.1016/S0140-6736(21)01751-7).

📚 REDEFINE 1 (NCT05567796): Phase 3a, 68 weeks, n=3417, BMI ≥30 or ≥27 plus one comorbidity, no diabetes; 21:3:3:7 to CagriSema 2.4/2.4 mg, semaglutide 2.4 mg, cagrilintide 2.4 mg, or placebo. Treatment-policy estimand: CagriSema −20.4% vs placebo −3.0% (P<0.001). GI AEs 79.6% vs 39.9% placebo. Novo-funded (Garvey, Blüher, et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2025;393:635–647, PMID 40544433, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2502081). Obesity trial, not an aging RCT.

🤔 Novo’s 20 Dec 2024 REDEFINE 1 headline, trial-product estimand if all adhered: CagriSema −22.7%, cagrilintide 2.4 mg −11.8%, semaglutide 2.4 mg −16.1%, placebo −2.3%; ≥25% loss 40.4% / 6.0% / 16.2% / 0.9% (Novo Nordisk / GlobeNewswire, https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/12/20/3000444/0/en/Novo-Nordisk-A-S-CagriSema-demonstrates-superior-weight-loss-in-adults-with-obesity-or-overweight-in-the-REDEFINE-1-trial.html). ## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Lau 2021: GI disorders (nausea, constipation, diarrhea) and injection-site reactions; GI AEs 41–63% on cagrilintide vs 32% placebo; nausea 20–47% vs 18%; permanent discontinuation ~10% across arms, mostly AEs (Lau et al., 2021).

📚 REDEFINE 1: GI AEs 79.6% on CagriSema vs 39.9% placebo, mostly transient mild-to-moderate (Garvey et al., 2025).

🐉 No US Prescribing Information exists for cagrilintide as of this cutoff. no source; inference from the no-US-label file.
