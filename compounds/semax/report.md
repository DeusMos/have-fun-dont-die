## Identity

Parked home: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md) for the BDNF / neurotrophin slogan. Adjacent Russian-nasal SKUs: [selank](../selank/report.md), [pinealon](../pinealon/report.md). Aliases: MEHFPGP, Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro, ACTH(4-7)-PGP.

💯 Semax is the synthetic heptapeptide Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro. Sequence is ACTH(4-7) plus a Pro-Gly-Pro C-terminus, not native ACTH(4-10) MEHFRWG.

📚 PubChem name “semax” maps CID 9811102, titled “ACTH (4-7), Pro-Gly-Pro-”, formula C37H51N9O10S, MW 813.9; synonyms include Semax, MEHFPGP, Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro, CAS 80714-61-0, and UNII I5FAL2585H (PubChem PUG name/CID/synonyms/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9811102).

📚 FDA’s May 2026 Semax BDS briefing lists free base UNII I5FAL2585H, CAS 80714-61-0, C37H51N9O10S / 813.93, and acetate as C39H55N9O12S / 874.0 with RN 2828433-33-4 “or use the CAS number same as free base”; acetate UNII “not available” (FDA, Semax BDS briefing, 2026-05-11, https://www.fda.gov/media/193348/download; slides https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

🐉 Extra RN 2828433-33-4 sits on the FDA acetate row; this flight did not independently title that number on PubChem. Do not invent a second molecule (FDA briefing, 2026-05-11).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells Semax as an ACTH(4-10) analogue tool for neurotrophin expression in neuronal cultures (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/semax). Mindful sells the SKU.

📚 The ACTH(4-10) slogan is the usual literature line; PubChem titles the same CID as ACTH(4-7)+PGP. Sequence MEHFPGP is what is sold (Dolotov et al., *J. Neurochem.*, 2006; PubChem CID 9811102).

📚 Kolomin et al. describe Russian nasal **Semax 0.1%** as a nootropic / neuroprotective drop and **Semax 1%** (by 2001) as the stroke-strength drop; both from the Institute of Molecular Genetics RAS / Peptogen line (Kolomin, Shadrina, Slominsky, Limborska, and Myasoedov, *Neurosci. Med.*, 2013;4:223–252, https://doi.org/10.4236/nm.2013.44035).

⛔ Treating Gusev 2018 plasma-BDNF / Barthel moves, or Russian 0.1%/1% drops, as a completed US aging or nootropic restore is a definition swap (Gusev, Martynov, Kostenko, Petrova, and Bobyreva, *Zh. Nevrol. Psikhiatr. im. S.S. Korsakova*, 2018;118(3-2):61–68, https://doi.org/10.17116/jnevro20181183261-68).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Dolotov et al.: tritium-labelled Semax bound rat basal-forebrain membranes, Ca2+-dependent, KD 2.4±1.0 nM, Bmax 33.5±7.9 fmol/mg. Intranasal 50 and 250 µg/kg raised BDNF protein at 3 h in basal forebrain, not cerebellum (Dolotov, Karpenko, Seredenina, Inozemtseva, Levitskaya, Zolotarev, Kamensky, Grivennikov, Engele, and Myasoedov, *J. Neurochem.*, 2006;97:82–86, PMID 16635254, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1471-4159.2006.03658.x).

📚 FDA staff cited Kost 2001 enkephalinase inhibition at an apparent ~10 µM and Eremin 2005 potentiation of amphetamine locomotor activity and striatal dopamine release in mice (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193348/download; https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 FDA staff: Semax given IV or IN to rats reaches brain; IN brain/blood ratio was higher than IV; the main metabolite in blood and brain was Pro-Gly-Pro. No published human PK table via any route was identified. No nominated-SC PK was identified (FDA, citing Potaman 1991b and Shevchenko 2006b, https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

🐉 A compiled human plasma t½ for the RUO vial is not on this page. no source; inference from the FDA missing-human-PK file.

## Animal data

📚 Dolotov 2006: IN 50 and 250 µg/kg, BDNF up at 3 h in rat basal forebrain, not cerebellum (Dolotov et al., 2006).

📚 Filippenkov et al.: rat tMCAO; Semax 10 µg/100 g IP (100 µg/kg) at 90 min, 2.5 h, and 6.5 h. RNA-seq of subcortex: no DEGs >1.5-fold vs saline at 4.5 h; 394 DEGs (191 up, 203 down) at 24 h. Semax suppressed inflammation-related transcripts and activated neurotransmission-related transcripts relative to ischaemia–reperfusion alone (Filippenkov, Stavchansky, Denisova, Yuzhakov, et al., *Genes*, 2020;11:681, https://doi.org/10.3390/genes11060681).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Gusev, Martynov, Kostenko, Petrova, and Bobyreva: N=110 ischemic stroke (43 men, 67 women; mean age 58.0±9.7, median 63). Early rehab 89±9 d vs late 214±22 d; each split Semax+ / Semax−. Semax 6000 mcg/day × 10 d × 2 courses, 20 d interval. Plasma BDNF rose and stayed up on Semax regardless of rehab timing; Semax plus high BDNF sped Barthel improvement. Exact Barthel deltas and p-values are not in the English abstract this flight used (Gusev et al., 2018, https://doi.org/10.17116/jnevro20181183261-68).

📚 FDA staff did not treat the Russian-language stroke set (including Gusev 1997, 2005, 2018) as part of the 503A effectiveness file because full English texts were not available; staff conclusion for the nominated uses (cerebral ischemia, migraine, trigeminal neuralgia) was insufficient evidence. Koroleva 1996: IN 0.5 mg/kg once; migraine headache ceased in 4/12 at 90–120 min, “less severe” in 8/12; typical trigeminal neuralgia pain characteristics did not move (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193348/download; https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

🐉 No Western-standard human aging / cognition RCT of Semax is compiled on this page. Plasma BDNF and Barthel after stroke are not a healthspan result. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 FDA staff: one FAERS report (May 2025) of eye pain and burning after Semax 0.1% nasal drops bought online from CosmicNootropic, with hospitalization and symptoms lasting at least a year. FAERS is voluntary; one report is not a labeled risk table (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

📚 FDA’s compounding-risk page flags Semax heptapeptide immunogenicity for some routes because of aggregation and peptide-related impurities, and says the agency lacks enough safety information to know whether harm would follow human administration (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks).

📚 FDA staff also flagged Cherkasova 2002 clotting-lab chatter as a bleeding-risk concern and Eremin 2005 striatal-dopamine potentiation as an abuse-class signal. Neither is a completed human tox series (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193774/download).

🐉 A compiled RUO-vial harm series is not on this page. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-tox file.

## Legal / access status

📚 FDA staff, 11 May 2026: no USP/NF monograph; neither free base nor acetate is a component of an FDA-approved drug; both judged not well-characterized; insufficient evidence for cerebral ischemia, migraine, and trigeminal neuralgia; immunogenicity unassessed. Staff proposed that Semax free base and Semax acetate NOT be included on the 503A Bulks List (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193348/download; intro https://www.fda.gov/media/193342/download).

🤔 FDA PCAC met 24 July 2026 and, per contemporaneous law-firm tallies, voted 8–5–1 to recommend Semax free base and acetate for the 503A Bulks List; FDA staff had recommended against; the vote is advisory, not a compounding authorization (McDermott Will & Emery, 2026, https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/bulk-list-bound-pcac-backs-majority-of-peptides-in-two-day-public-meeting/).

📚 This flight did not find Semax named on the WADA 2026 Prohibited List. Do not invent an S0 or S2 listing from the ACTH slogan (WADA 2026 list, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).
