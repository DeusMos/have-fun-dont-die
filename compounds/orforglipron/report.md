## Mechanism

📚 Orforglipron is a small-molecule, nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist designed for once-daily oral use without food or liquid restrictions — i.e. without a SNAC gastric-absorption trick (Wharton et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2025;393:1796–1806, PMID 40960239; Wharton et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2023;389:877–888, https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2302392).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 ATTAIN-1: once-daily oral 6 / 12 / 36 mg after 4-week step-ups from 1 mg (via 3 mg, then 6 / 12 / 24 mg as needed); 72 weeks; no reduced-calorie diet mandate (Wharton et al., 2025; Lilly, https://investor.lilly.com/news-releases/news-release-details/lillys-oral-glp-1-orforglipron-demonstrated-meaningful-weight).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 ATTAIN-1 (NCT05869903): Phase 3, n=3127, obesity without diabetes, 72 weeks, 6 / 12 / 36 mg vs placebo (3:3:3:4). Treatment-regimen estimand percent weight change: −7.5% (95% CI −8.2 to −6.8), −8.4% (−9.1 to −7.7), −11.2% (−12.0 to −10.4) vs placebo −2.1% (−2.8 to −1.4); all P<0.001. At 36 mg: ≥10% in 54.6%, ≥15% in 36.0%, ≥20% in 18.4% vs 12.9 / 5.9 / 2.8% placebo. Lilly-funded (Wharton, Aronne, Stefanski, et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2025;393:1796–1806, PMID 40960239).

📚 Wharton 2023 Phase 2: daily oral orforglipron in adults with obesity; the 2023 paper is the dose-finding predecessor, not ATTAIN-1 (Wharton et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2023;389:877–888, https://doi.org/10.1056/nejmoa2302392).

🐉 No published human aging RCT of orforglipron is compiled on this page. Weight loss is not healthspan. no source; inference from the empty aging file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 ATTAIN-1: most common AEs gastrointestinal, mostly mild to moderate; discontinuation for AEs 5.3–10.3% on orforglipron vs 2.7% placebo (Wharton et al., 2025).
