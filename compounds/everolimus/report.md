## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Six weeks of everolimus (RAD001) 0.5 mg/day or 5 mg/week in 218 adults ≥65 y raised influenza-vaccine response about 20% and reduced PD-1+ T cells (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.3009892, PMID 25540326). Novartis-sponsored. Immune surrogate, not lifespan. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 In 264 adults ≥65 y, 6 weeks of RAD001 0.1 mg + BEZ235 10 mg daily cut fitted annualized self-reported infections to 1.49/person-year (95% CI 1.19–1.86) vs placebo 2.41 (2.00–2.90), P=0.001 (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1126/scitranslmed.aaq1564, PMID 29997249). Still Novartis; still not disability-free survival. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md#human-data).

📚 The same everolimus 0.5 mg/day or 5 mg/week vaccine-titer / PD-1+ result is reused in hallmark 11 as an immune surrogate, not lifespan (Mannick et al., Sci Transl Med, 2014; already in hallmark 06). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).
