## Mechanism

📚 Khavinson, Bondarev, and Butyugov added epithalon to telomerase-negative human fetal fibroblast cultures and reported hTERT induction, telomerase enzymatic activity, and telomere elongation (Khavinson, Bondarev, and Butyugov, *Bull. Exp. Biol. Med.*, 2003;135:590–592, PMID 12937682, https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12937682/).

📚 Al-dulaimi, Thomas, Matta, and Roberts: 1 µg/mL daily for 3 weeks elongated qPCR telomere length and raised hTERT mRNA and TRAP activity in IBR.3 fibroblasts and HMEC; 0.2–1 µg/mL for 4 days elongated telomeres in 21NT and BT474 breast-cancer lines. Cancer-cell elongation ran with ALT (C-circle) activation; normal cells showed only a minor ALT bump. Peptide was purchased from UK Peptides (Al-dulaimi, Thomas, Matta, and Roberts, *Biogerontology*, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x).

## Animal data

📚 Anisimov et al.: female Swiss-derived SHR mice, n=54/group, from 3 months to natural death; 1.0 µg/mouse SC (~30–40 µg/kg) on 5 consecutive days each month vs saline. Food intake, body weight, and *mean* lifespan did not move. Last-10% survivor lifespan +13.3% (P<0.01); maximum lifespan +12.3%. Bone-marrow chromosome aberrations −17.1% (P<0.05). Total spontaneous tumors unchanged; leukemia 6.0-fold lower (Anisimov, Khavinson, Popovich, Zabezhinski, Alimova, Rosenfeld, Zavarzina, Semenchenko, and Yashin, *Biogerontology*, 2003;4:193–202, https://doi.org/10.1023/a:1025114230714).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 FDA staff, searching for the nominated insomnia use, found three human administrations: sublingual AEDG in night-shift workers for circadian-gene expression (Khavinson et al., 2021) and 6-sulfatoxymelatonin excretion (Ivko et al., 2021), and a parabulbar-injection retinitis-pigmentosa series (Khavinson et al., 2002). None used the proposed SC compounded product. None measured insomnia outcomes. An orphan designation for retinitis pigmentosa (2 Sep 2010) was withdrawn/revoked 6 Jan 2016 (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193345/download).

📚 FDA staff conclusion for the nominated use: lack of evidence that epitalon free base or acetate treats insomnia; melatonin-metabolite moves are not sleep outcomes; multiple FDA-approved insomnia drugs already exist (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193345/download).

🐉 No Western-standard human aging / telomere RCT of epithalon is compiled on this page. The Khavinson human file is pineal/circadian/retina, not a healthspan trial. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Al-dulaimi 2025 reported ALT activation in breast-cancer cell lines at the same concentrations that elongated telomeres. That is a cell-line recombination signal, not a human tumor series (Al-dulaimi et al., 2025, https://doi.org/10.1007/s10522-025-10315-x).

📚 Anisimov 2003: long-term monthly SC dosing in female SHR mice did not raise total spontaneous tumor incidence; leukemia incidence fell (Anisimov et al., 2003). Mouse, not human.
