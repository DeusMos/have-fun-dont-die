## Identity

Home hallmark: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md) for the MC1/MC4 pigmentation and erection claims. Aliases: MT-2, MT-II, melanotan-II. Acid analog: [pt-141](../pt-141/report.md). Linear 13-mer: [melanotan-1](../melanotan-1/report.md).

💯 Melanotan-II is a cyclic heptapeptide α-MSH analog (lactam-bridged). It is not the linear 13-mer afamelanotide and it is not bremelanotide (the C-terminal acid).

📚 PubChem name “melanotan-II” maps CID 92432, titled melanotan-II, formula C50H69N15O9, MW 1024.2, CAS 121062-08-6 and 75921-69-6 (PubChem PUG name/CID fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/92432).

🤔 Mindful Research lists MT-2, CAS 121062-08-6, CID 92432, C50H69N15O9, MW 1024.198 g/mol (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/ml-2.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). CID, primary CAS, formula, and MW match CID 92432.

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells MT-2 as a cyclic α-MSH analog / non-selective melanocortin agonist for in-vitro MC1R work (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/ml-2). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating Dorr 1996 pigmentation or Wessells erection data as a completed tanning or ED drug — or as interchangeable with Vyleesi or Scenesse — is a definition swap (Dorr et al., *Life Sci.*, 1996; Wessells et al., *J. Urol.*, 1998).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Melanotan-II is a superpotent cyclic melanotropic peptide. Dorr 1996 treated it as an α-MSH analog that tans and, as an unexpected AE, produced spontaneous erections (Dorr, Lines, Levine, et al., *Life Sci.*, 1996;58:1777–1784).

📚 Bremelanotide was developed as a likely metabolite / C-terminal acid analog after Palatin dropped MT-II (Hadley and Dorr, *Peptides*, 2006;27:921–930, PMID 16412534). That is history, not a second identity.

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Dorr 1996: pilot Phase 1 of melanotan-II in humans; dose-dependent skin pigmentation with dose-limiting nausea, decreased appetite, and spontaneous penile erections (Dorr et al., *Life Sci.*, 1996;58:1777–1784).

📚 Wessells 1998: double-blind placebo-controlled crossover, 10 men with psychogenic ED, SC MT-II 0.025–0.157 mg/kg. Clinically apparent erections in 8/10; mean tip rigidity >80% duration 38.0 min vs 3.0 min placebo, p=0.0045 (Wessells, Fuciarelli, Hansen, Hadley, Levine, Dorr, et al., *J. Urol.*, 1998;160:389–393).

📚 Wessells 2000: organic and mixed ED, 20 men; erections in 17/20 without sexual stimulation; sexual desire increased after 68% of MT-II doses vs 19% of placebo, p<0.01 (Wessells et al., 2000, cited in Giuliano, *J. Androl.*, 2004).

🐉 No published melanotan-II RCT with an aging or healthspan primary is compiled on this page. Pigment and erection are not that endpoint. no source; inference from the empty aging file.
