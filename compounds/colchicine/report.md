## Human data — labeled / indicated use

📚 In COLCOT, colchicine 0.5 mg daily started within 30 days after MI cut the composite of CV death, resuscitated arrest, MI, stroke, or urgent angina hospitalization leading to revascularization (5.5% vs 7.1%; HR 0.77, 95% CI 0.61–0.96, P=0.02) over median 22.6 months; pneumonia SAE 0.9% vs 0.4% (P=0.03) (Tardif et al., NEJM, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa1912388, PMID 31733140, NCT02551094; N=4,745). Disease events, not aging. Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

📚 In LoDoCo2, colchicine 0.5 mg daily in chronic coronary disease cut CV death, spontaneous MI, ischemic stroke, or ischemia-driven revascularization (6.8% vs 9.6%; 2.5 vs 3.6 /100 person-years; HR 0.69, 95% CI 0.57–0.83, P<0.001) over median 28.6 months (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2021372, PMID 32865380; N=5,522, mean age 66, 15.3% women). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

📚 LoDoCo2 noncardiovascular death was 53/2,762 vs 35/2,760 (0.7 vs 0.5 / 100 person-years; HR 1.51, 95% CI 0.99–2.31); all-cause death 73 vs 60 (HR 1.21, 0.86–1.71) (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

🥼 The LoDoCo2 non-CV death HR 1.51 CI includes 1.00; a later cause-of-death paper (133 deaths; cancer 26 vs 22, infection 4 vs 4) did not pin a mechanism and treated the split as chance plus comorbidity (Opstal et al., Int J Cardiol, 2023, PMID 36535561). Live safety ambiguity in the same trial quoted as colchicine-for-inflammaging. Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

📚 CLEAR SYNERGY is the colchicine population-specificity null: 7,062 patients with acute MI at 104 centers, colchicine vs placebo (2×2 with spironolactone), median 3 years; primary (CV death, recurrent MI, stroke, or unplanned ischemia-driven revascularization) 322/3,528 (9.1%) vs 327/3,534 (9.3%); HR 0.99 (95% CI 0.85–1.16, P=0.93) (Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2405922, PMID 39555823, NCT03048825). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

📚 CLEAR SYNERGY still moved CRP (least-squares mean difference −1.28 mg/L at 3 months, 95% CI −1.81 to −0.75) and still missed the outcome; diarrhea 10.2% vs 6.6% (P<0.001); serious infections did not differ (Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

📚 DailyMed LODOCO (colchicine 0.5 mg) is labeled to reduce MI, stroke, coronary revascularization, and CV death in adults with established atherosclerotic disease or multiple CV risk factors, dose 0.5 mg once daily; contraindicated with strong CYP3A4 or P-gp inhibitors and in renal failure / severe hepatic impairment (AGEPHA / FDA label, revised 8/2024). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

## Interactions

🤼 Rapamycin News longevity threads treat 0.5 mg/day colchicine as an inflammaging / CRP tool (targets hs-CRP <0.5–1.0) and also as a CYP3A4/P-gp bomb next to weekly sirolimus; one 2026 stack-review post calls the combo “potentially life-threatening” and tells a high-CAC user to keep colchicine and drop rapamycin; another inflammation FAQ says no pharma add-on if hs-CRP is already <0.5 (Rapamycin News threads 22322 / 25357 / 23007). Amateur split, not a paper fight. Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

## Fights

🥼 Low-dose colchicine is not a settled CAD drug across populations and is not a healthy-person inflammaging pill: LoDoCo2 HR 0.69 in chronic CAD versus CLEAR SYNERGY HR 0.99 in post-MI PCI (Nidorf et al., NEJM, 2020; Jolly et al., NEJM, 2025). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).
