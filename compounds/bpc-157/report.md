## Identity

Home hallmark: [12 dysbiosis](../../hallmarks/12-dysbiosis/report.md). Aliases: BPC-157, Bepecin, PL-14736, PL-10, gastric pentadecapeptide. GLOW/KLOW stack-mates: [tb-500](../tb-500/report.md), [ghk-cu](../ghk-cu/report.md), [kpv](../kpv/report.md).

💯 BPC-157 is the synthetic 15-mer Gly-Glu-Pro-Pro-Pro-Gly-Lys-Pro-Ala-Asp-Asp-Ala-Gly-Leu-Val (PubChem CID 9941957 title Bpc-157; FDA SRS UNII 8ED8NXK95P).

📚 PubChem CID 9941957 lists Bpc-157, CAS 137525-51-0, formula C62H98N16O22, MW 1419.5 g/mol (PubChem, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/9941957; PUG fetched 2026-09-04).

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII 8ED8NXK95P is BPC-157 and maps CAS 137525-51-0 plus synonyms BEPECIN, PL-14736, PL-10; UNII availability does not imply review or approval (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/8ED8NXK95P).

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII PAR2FC72XP is BPC-157 ACETATE, CAS 216441-37-1, formula C62H98N16O22·C2H4O2 — a distinct salt listing from free-base CAS 137525-51-0 (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/PAR2FC72XP).

⛔ Mindful Research’s lyophilized product JSON table lists CID 9941957 next to CAS 137525-51-0, but the same page’s “Further Information” href points at CID 9941376, which PubChem titles H-Cys-Arg-Tyr-Gly-Tyr-Leu-Phe-Cys-OH (C47H65N11O11S2, MW 1024.2) — not BPC-157 (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/bpc-157.json; PubChem PUG CID 9941376; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity, gut, and injury-clinic copy sells 200–500 µg oral or SubQ BPC-157, 10 mg RUO vials, capsules, and GLOW/KLOW stacks as leaky-gut restore, tendon/soft-tissue heal, and systemic repair (AutoimmuneFinder, https://www.autoimmunefinder.com/blog/bpc-157-gut-healing; Peptide Protocol Wiki, https://www.peptideprotocolwiki.com/peptides/bpc-157/dosing; Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/bpc-157). Those pages sell or refer the peptide.

⛔ Treating the Sikiric/Seiwerth rodent GI/tendon/muscle file as proof that a 250–500 µg human capsule or SubQ vial restores barrier, tendon, or aging is a definition swap (Józwiak et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18020185; FDA BPC-157 PCAC briefing, 2026-05-11, https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download).

⛔ A research-chemical CAS/HPLC listing or a PCAC advisory vote is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (Mindful Research terms, https://happypeptides.com/policies/terms-of-service; McDermott Will & Emery, 2026, https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/bulk-list-bound-pcac-backs-majority-of-peptides-in-two-day-public-meeting/).

## Mechanism

📚 Józwiak, Bauer, Kamysz, and Kleczkowska review BPC-157 as a 15-aa gastric-juice fragment first described by Sikiric and colleagues in 1993, with a literature dominated by cytoprotection, angiogenesis, and nitric-oxide-system language (Józwiak et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18020185, PMID 40005999).

📚 Chang, Tsai, Lin et al. reported that BPC-157 promoted outgrowth, survival under stress, and migration of cultured tendon fibroblasts via the FAK–paxillin pathway (Chang et al., Journal of Applied Physiology, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1152/japplphysiol.00945.2010, PMID 21030672). That is a cell paper, not a human tendon RCT.

📚 Hsieh, Lee, Chueh et al. reported that BPC-157 modulated vasomotor tone and activated a Src–Caveolin-1–eNOS path in isolated aortic tissue (Hsieh et al., Scientific Reports, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-74022-y). Isolated vessel, not organismal restore.

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 He, Feng, Guo et al. reported rat and dog IV/IM PK: parent plasma half-life under 30 minutes; IM bioavailability about 14–19% in rats and 45–51% in beagles; authors scaled a 200 µg/person/day target to 20 µg/kg rat and 6 µg/kg dog by body-surface area (He et al., Frontiers in Pharmacology, 2022, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2022.1026182, PMID 36438806). That is not a human SubQ or oral table.

🐉 No published human pharmacokinetic table for exogenous oral or subcutaneous BPC-157 (t½, Vd, CL, F) was compiled this round (NCT02637284 unknown/no results; He 2022 is rat/dog). Do not invent one.

## Animal data

📚 The compiled animal file is a large Sikiric/Seiwerth rodent GI, tendon, muscle, fistula, and CNS injury series, typically 10 ng/kg–10 µg/kg (sometimes pg/kg to mg/kg), oral or parenteral, with cytoprotection and healing endpoints (Józwiak et al., 2025; Peptide Protocol Wiki extracts of those models, https://www.peptideprotocolwiki.com/peptides/bpc-157/dosing). Those are injury models in rats, not human aging.

📚 Józwiak 2025 states that a PubMed search (20 May 2025) retrieved >190 “BPC 157” articles and that >80% list P. Sikiric or S. Seiwerth as first or senior author (Józwiak et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18020185).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Lee and Padgett reported a retrospective phone survey after intra-articular BPC-157 at a Florida clinic: 17 charts, 16 reached; 11/12 BPC-157-only patients reported “significant improvement”; overall 14/16 (87.5%) reported relief when BPC-157 ± TB4 was used; no functional scale, no control, no MRI; most injections were 6–12 months earlier (Lee and Padgett, Alternative Therapies in Health and Medicine, 2021, 27:8–13, PMID 34324435). Institute for Hormonal Balance used the peptide.

📚 Lee and Burgess reported an IV safety pilot of 1–20 mg BPC-157 in two healthy adults with no adverse events on vitals, ECG, or labs (Lee and Burgess, Alternative Therapies in Health and Medicine, 2025, 31:20–24; cited in Józwiak et al. reply, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12567171/). N=2 is not a safety program and not restore.

📚 FDA’s 2026 briefing treats the PL 14736 / Ruenzi ulcerative-colitis program as historical-use material, not as a published primary RCT the agency could evaluate for effectiveness; Veljaca 2003 is a Gut meeting abstract on healthy-volunteer PL 14736 PK (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download; Veljaca, Sladoljev, Mildner, Gut, 2003, 51:A309).

📚 NCT02637284 (PharmaCotherapia, oral Bepecin / PCO-02 Phase I in healthy volunteers) has overallStatus UNKNOWN and no posted results (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT02637284).

📚 NCT07437547 is a recruiting Phase 2 randomized SubQ BPC-157 vs placebo for 14 days in MRI-confirmed grade II hamstring strain, estimated n=120, co-primary return-to-sport and Day-14 MRI volume; hasResults=false — acute sports injury, not aging, not gut (ClinicalTrials.gov, NCT07437547, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07437547; sponsor Hudson Biotech; site Peking University Shenzhen Hospital).

📚 NCT07803250 is not-yet-recruiting rotator-cuff recovery with BPC-157, estimated start 2027 (ClinicalTrials.gov, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07803250).

🐉 A ClinicalTrials.gov hunt on 2026-09-04 returned no completed subcutaneous or oral BPC-157 aging, leaky-gut, or barrier-restore RCT (ClinicalTrials.gov API v2 query BPC-157 OR Bepecin OR PL-14736).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 FDA’s withdrawn Category 2 nomination text states compounded BPC-157 may pose immunogenicity risk for certain routes, peptide-related impurities and API characterization are complex, and FDA lacks sufficient information to know whether the drug would cause harm in humans (FDA, Certain Bulk Drug Substances… Significant Safety Risks, https://www.fda.gov/drugs/human-drug-compounding/certain-bulk-drug-substances-use-compounding-may-present-significant-safety-risks).

📚 FDA staff’s 2026 PCAC evaluation concluded the 503A criteria weigh against listing BPC-157 free base or acetate: no USP/NF monograph, neither is a component of an FDA-approved drug, both are not well-characterized, historical-use data are thin, and there is a lack of clinical safety and effectiveness information for nominated UC use (FDA, BPC-157 BDS briefing, 2026-05-11, https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download).

📚 WADA’s 2026 Prohibited List names BPC-157 as an S0 Non-Approved Substance example, prohibited at all times in- and out-of-competition (WADA, 2026 List, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf). Sport-code status is not a harm series.

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful systemic harm from BPC-157 itself was found this round. Category 2 immunogenicity language and Sikiric-group “2 g/kg no toxicity” claims are not ☠︎︎. That gap is not proof of safety (FDA briefing; Józwiak 2025 reply).

## Fights

🥼 Józwiak 2025 vs Sikiric 2025 is a paper fight: the independent review flags single-lab dominance, single-dose animal designs, short plasma t½, and a hypothetical angiogenesis/NO tumor-promotion hazard; Sikiric et al. reply that the peptide “modulates” those paths and that toxicity at 2 g/kg was clean (Józwiak et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18020185; Sikiric et al., Pharmaceuticals, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/ph18101450; Józwiak et al. reply, https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8247/18/10/1571). Neither side is a human RCT.

🤼 Oral versus injectable versus intra-articular BPC-157, and whether to stack TB-500, is an amateur/clinic split: wiki and AutoimmuneFinder traffic both oral gut and SubQ injury protocols; Lee/Padgett is IA knee; Mindful sells vial, capsule, and BPC/TB blends (Peptide Protocol Wiki; AutoimmuneFinder; Lee and Padgett 2021; Mindful SKUs).

## Legal / access status

🤔 Mindful Research LLC states products are for in vitro laboratory research only (https://happypeptides.com/policies/terms-of-service).

📚 BPC-157 was among 12 peptides removed from 503A Category 2 in April 2026 after nominator withdrawals; removal is not Category 1 and not a bulks-list listing (McDermott Will & Emery, 2026, https://www.mcdermottlaw.com/insights/bulk-list-bound-pcac-backs-majority-of-peptides-in-two-day-public-meeting/).

📚 FDA PCAC met 23 July 2026 and voted 8 yes – 6 no – 1 abstain to recommend BPC-157 free base and acetate for the 503A Bulks List (use FDA reviewed: ulcerative colitis); FDA staff had recommended against; the vote is advisory, not a compounding authorization (McDermott, 2026; FDA meeting page, https://www.fda.gov/advisory-committees/advisory-committee-calendar/july-23-24-2026-meeting-pharmacy-compounding-advisory-committee-07232026).

⛔ A 8–6–1 advisory recommendation or a withdrawn Category 2 immunogenicity sentence is compounding policy, not a demonstration that oral or injected BPC-157 restores gut, tendon, or aging (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193343/download; McDermott, 2026).

📚 WADA 2026 S0 names BPC-157 (WADA 2026 List PDF).

## What is actually on the table

📚 BPC-157 is a real 15-mer with a large rodent cytoprotection file and rat/dog IV/IM PK (He 2022; Józwiak 2025).

📚 The compiled human file is a 16-person uncontrolled IA knee phone survey, a 2-person IV pilot, an unpublished/unknown Phase I, and a recruiting hamstring Phase 2 — not an aging or leaky-gut RCT (Lee and Padgett 2021; Lee and Burgess 2025; NCT02637284; NCT07437547).

⛔ That biology is not a completed human barrier-restore or healthspan result, and CID 9941376 is not the peptide (PubChem CID 9941957 vs 9941376; FDA PCAC briefing).

🐉 No BPC-157 aging RCT was on the registry this round (ClinicalTrials.gov API, 2026-09-04).

🤔 People can buy 10 mg RUO vials, 500–1000 mcg capsules, GLOW/KLOW blends, and 200–500 µg oral/SubQ protocols today. That is practice, not healthspan (Mindful Research; AutoimmuneFinder; Peptide Protocol Wiki).
