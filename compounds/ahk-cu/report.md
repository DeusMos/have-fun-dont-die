## Identity

Home hallmark: [13 extracellular matrix changes](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md). Adjacent copper peptide: [ghk-cu](../ghk-cu/report.md). Aliases: copper tripeptide-3, Ala-His-Lys-Cu. This page is the alanine-start copper tripeptide, not GHK-Cu and not free GHK.

💯 AHK is Ala-His-Lys; AHK-Cu is that ligand plus copper(II). One residue swap vs GHK (Gly-His-Lys).

📚 PubChem name “AHK-Cu” maps CID 168431292, formula C15H24ClCuN6O4−, MW 451.39 — the hydrochloride copper complex. CAS 682809-81-0 maps that same CID (PubChem PUG name/CAS fetch 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/168431292).

📚 PubChem name Ala-His-Lys maps CID 7408502, free tripeptide C15H26N6O4, MW 354.41 — no copper (https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/7408502).

## Mechanism

📚 Pyo et al. report L-alanyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine-Cu2+ (AHK-Cu) at 10⁻¹²–10⁻⁹ M stimulated human hair-follicle elongation ex vivo and dermal-papilla-cell proliferation in vitro; Bcl-2/Bax rose and cleaved caspase-3 / PARP fell at 10⁻⁹ M (apoptosis reduction on Annexin V/PI was not statistically significant) (Pyo, Yoo, Won, Lee, Kang, Eun, Cho, and Kim, *Arch. Pharm. Res.*, 2007;30:834–839, https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02978833, PMID 17703734).
