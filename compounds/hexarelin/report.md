## Identity

Parked home: [06 deregulated nutrient sensing](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md) for the GHRP / GHSR axis. Adjacent GH-axis SKUs: [ipamorelin](../ipamorelin/report.md), [cjc-1295-no-dac](../cjc-1295-no-dac/report.md), [sermorelin](../sermorelin/report.md). Aliases: examorelin (INN), EP-23905, MF-6003.

💯 Hexarelin / examorelin is the synthetic hexapeptide His-D-2-methyl-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH₂, a GHSR agonist derived from GHRP-6.

📚 NCATS lists EXAMORELIN, UNII 09QF37C617, and cites Ghigo 1994 as the human GH-releasing paper (NCATS, https://drugs.ncats.io/drug/09QF37C617).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO and clinic copy sells hexarelin as a strong GH pulse, sometimes as a cleaner GHRP (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/hexarelin-acetate). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating Ghigo 1994 acute GH AUCs, or Massoud 1996 cortisol/PRL rises, as a human aging or body-recomp restore is a definition swap (Ghigo et al., *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 1994, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.78.3.8126144; Massoud, Hindmarsh, and Brook, *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 1996, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.81.12.8954038).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Hexarelin releases GH via a GHRP-class pathway and, unlike later “selective” GHS copy, also moves PRL, ACTH, and cortisol in humans at GH-effective IV doses (Massoud, Hindmarsh, and Brook, *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 1996;81:4338–4344, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.81.12.8954038).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Ghigo et al.: 12 healthy young volunteers; IV 1 µg/kg GH AUC 3175±506 µg·min/L vs GHRH 1 µg/kg 1544±161 (P<0.001); IV 2 µg/kg AUC 4422±626, reproducible on repeat (4016±563 vs 3959±803); SC 1.5 and 3 µg/kg AUC 3180±392 and 4459±566; IN 20 µg/kg AUC 2642±452; oral 20 and 40 mg AUC 2278±442 and 4079±514; estimated bioavailability SC 77.0±10.5%, IN 4.8±0.9%, oral 0.3±0.1% (Ghigo, Arvat, Gianotti, Imbimbo, Lenaerts, Deghenghi, and Camanni, *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 1994;78:693–698, PMID 8126144, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.78.3.8126144).

📚 Massoud et al.: IV hexarelin 0–1.0 µg/kg in healthy adult males; GH dose-response plateau ~140 mU/L at 1.0 µg/kg, ED50 0.48±0.02 µg/kg; PRL max percent rise plateau ~180%, ED50 0.39±0.02 µg/kg; cortisol ~40% step at 0.5 µg/kg (Massoud et al., 1996, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.81.12.8954038).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Arvat et al.: IV 2.0 µg/kg hexarelin; GH AUC rose from prepubertal 769.5±122.2 to pubertal 1960.2±283.5 µg·min/L, stayed high in young adults (1829.7±243.1), fell in elderly 66–81 y (951.1±232.9); PRL and cortisol AUCs showed no significant age split; ACTH did (Arvat et al., *Eur. J. Endocrinol.*, 1997;137:635–642, https://doi.org/10.1530/eje.0.1370635). Age-related GH response, not an aging-restore RCT.

📚 Two or three SC hexarelin injections (1.5 µg/kg) in one day raised 24-h GH burst mass in six young men without raising 24-h PRL, ACTH, or cortisol or IGF-I that day (Ghigo group, *Eur. J. Endocrinol.*, 2002;146:310, https://doi.org/10.1530/eje.0.1460310). One-day schedule, n=6.

🐉 No published human aging / body-composition RCT of hexarelin is compiled on this page. no source; inference from the empty aging file.
