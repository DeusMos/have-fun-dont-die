## Identity

Parked home: [13 extracellular matrix changes](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md) for actin / wound / migration. That hallmark report does not name this SKU. Aliases on the storefront: thymosin beta-4, Tβ4, timbetasin, RGN-259. The name TB-500 is also used for a different molecule.

💯 Thymosin β4 is a 43-residue G-actin-sequestering peptide; its INN for the synthetic copy is timbetasin (NCATS / FDA UNII 2D5MRE3SSY; PubChem CID 16132341 title Timbetasin).

📚 PubChem CID 16132341 is titled Timbetasin, formula C212H350N56O78S, MW 4963; the CID RN dump lists 1198780-38-9 and 77591-33-4 (PubChem PUG CID/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16132341).

📚 PubChem name “thymosin beta-4” resolves to CID 45382195, same formula C212H350N56O78S / MW 4963, CAS 77591-33-4 — a second record for the same 43-mer, not a wrong-molecule collision (PubChem PUG name/CID fetched 2026-09-04).

📚 PubChem name “TB-500” resolves to CID 62707662, titled Unii-qhk6Z47gtg, formula C38H68N10O14, MW 889.0, CAS 885340-08-9 — the N-acetylated heptapeptide Ac-LKKTETQ (Tβ4 residues 17–23), not the 43-mer (PubChem PUG name/CID fetched 2026-09-04).

📚 FDA’s July 2026 PCAC briefing treats nominated “TB-500 (free base)” as that heptapeptide: Ac-Leu-Lys-Lys-Thr-Glu-Thr-Gln-OH, C38H68N10O14, MW 889.01, CAS 885340-08-9, PubChem CID 62707662; staff say the common name is not a USAN and that multiple moieties are sold under it (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

📚 NCATS lists TIMBETASIN, UNII 2D5MRE3SSY, primary CAS 77591-33-4 (NCATS, https://drugs.ncats.io/substance/2D5MRE3SSY).

🐉 Extra RN 1198780-38-9 sits on CID 16132341; this flight did not independently title that number (PubChem RN dump, 2026-09-04).

🤔 Mindful Research lists TB-500 (Thymosin Beta-4), CAS 77591-33-4, PubChem CID 16132341, formula C212H350N56O78S, MW 4963.4408 g/mol — the timbetasin / full Tβ4 pairing, sold under the TB-500 name (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/tb-500.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). ## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Injury-clinic and RUO copy sells TB-500 as systemic tissue repair, actin-driven migration, and a Tβ4 swap; Mindful’s page copies the 43-aa actin-sequestration story onto a 10 mg lyophilized vial (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/tb-500). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating a 10 mg RUO vial labeled TB-500 as RGN-259 (0.1% timbetasin acetate ophthalmic) or as the FDA-reviewed Ac-LKKTETQ fragment collapses three different sold objects (Sosne, Kleinman, Springs, Gross, Sung, and Kang, *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24010554; FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download; Mindful JSON).

⛔ A research-chemical CAS/HPLC listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/ (Mindful Research LLC footer, catalog pull September 3, 2026)).

## Mechanism

📚 Full-length Tβ4 sequesters G-actin and is cited for epithelial migration, laminin-332, stem-cell recruitment, and reduced oxidative stress / inflammation / apoptosis in ocular-surface models (Sosne et al., *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24010554).

📚 FDA’s briefing: the sold “TB-500” heptapeptide is the N-acetylated actin-binding motif (residues 17–23) of that 43-mer; staff did not treat the fragment and the parent as interchangeable APIs (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 FDA identified nonclinical PK in which SC/IP TB-500 (free base, the heptapeptide) reaches the circulation and is hydrolyzed to smaller peptides; staff did not identify IM PK for the nominated route, and did not identify human PK for the fragment (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

🐉 Human PK for a SubQ RUO “TB-500” vial — fragment or 43-mer — is not compiled on this page. no source; inference from the missing-systemic-PK file.

## Animal data

📚 Sosne et al. summarize alkali- and heptanol-injured mouse/rat cornea work in which synthetic Tβ4 sped surface healing and cut inflammatory infiltrate (Sosne et al., *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023, citing that animal file; https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24010554).

📚 FDA cites an in-vitro scratch assay in which TB-500 free base at 50 µg/mL did not induce wound healing in confluent fibroblast cultures, and notes the nomination’s supporting papers were Tβ4 studies, not fragment-in-animal wound studies (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 SEER-1 (NCT02600429): 0.1% RGN-259 (timbetasin acetate) ophthalmic solution five times daily vs vehicle in stages 2–3 neurotrophic keratopathy; 18 completers (planned 46, stopped for slow enrollment); complete epithelial healing at day 29 in 6/10 RGN-259 vs 1/8 placebo (Fisher p=0.0656, primary miss); day-43 healing without recurrence favored RGN-259 (p=0.0359); authors’ ad-hoc chi-square on the primary was p=0.0400; most AEs ocular and mild (Sosne et al., *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24010554). Ophthalmic NK trial, not systemic aging.

🤔 HLB Therapeutics / ReGenTree reported that European phase 3 SEER-3 of 0.1% RGN-259 for neurotrophic keratitis missed its primary endpoint (Ophthalmology Times, 2025-07-01, https://www.ophthalmologytimes.com/view/hlb-therapeutics-misses-primary-endpoint-in-phase-3-seer-3-trial-of-rgn-259). Company/news account, not the CSR.

📚 FDA did not identify clinical studies of TB-500 (the nominated heptapeptide) administered in humans (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

🐉 No human aging / healthspan RCT of either the fragment or a SubQ 43-mer vial is compiled on this page. no source; inference from the empty systemic-aging file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 SEER-1 reported 16 AEs in subjects who received at least one dose; most ocular and mild; one non-ocular SAE judged unrelated; no deaths or withdrawals from AEs (Sosne et al., *Int. J. Mol. Sci.*, 2023, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms24010554). That is topical ophthalmic timbetasin, not a gray-market vial.

📚 FDA staff: no identified clinical safety series for nominated TB-500 free base or acetate in humans; immunogenicity for IM/SC peptides is an open staff concern (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download).

🐉 A compiled RUO-vial harm series is not on this page. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-tox file.

## Fights

🤼 What “TB-500” names is split: PubChem and FDA’s 503A briefing use the Ac-LKKTETQ heptapeptide (CAS 885340-08-9); many storefronts, including Mindful’s table, print full Tβ4 / timbetasin numbers (CAS 77591-33-4, CID 16132341, ~4963 Da) on a TB-500 SKU (PubChem PUG; FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download; Mindful JSON).

🥼 SEER-1’s pre-specified primary (complete healing at day 29) missed at p=0.0656; the same paper reports a significant day-43 persistence contrast and an ad-hoc chi-square p=0.0400 on the primary (Sosne et al., 2023). Do not tidy that into a win.

## Legal / access status

📚 WADA 2026 Prohibited List S2.3 names “Thymosin-ß4 and its derivatives e.g. TB-500”; prohibited at all times (WADA, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).

📚 FDA staff concluded the 503A evaluation criteria weigh against placing TB-500 free base or TB-500 acetate on the bulks list (FDA, https://www.fda.gov/media/193349/download). Staff memo, not a final listing decision.
