## Identity

Adjacent GH-axis SKUs: [tesamorelin](../tesamorelin/report.md), [ipamorelin](../ipamorelin/report.md), [cjc-1295-no-dac](../cjc-1295-no-dac/report.md).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity-clinic and gray-market copy sells a 5 mg RUO vial, compounded nightly SubQ, or AgelessRx nasal sermorelin as GH/IGF-1 / body-comp / aging restore (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/sermorelin; AgelessRx XPRIZE page). Those pages sell or name the peptide.

⛔ Treating Geref’s withdrawn pediatric-GHD / diagnostic approvals as proof that ordinary-adult aging can be restored is a dead label doing a job it was not written for (Federal Register, 4 Mar 2013, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2013-03-04/html/2013-04827.htm).

⛔ Treating Corpas 1992 as a healthspan or body-composition result ignores the 14-day design and the paper’s own untested “could improve” leap (Corpas et al., J Clin Endocrinol Metab, 1992, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.75.2.1379256, PMID 1379256).

## Mechanism

📚 Sermorelin is GHRH(1-29): it binds pituitary GHRH receptors and stimulates pulsatile GH release; Corpas used that to raise 24-h GH and IGF-1 in old men (Corpas et al., 1992, PMID 1379256).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Corpas: old men received 0.5 mg and 1 mg GHRH(1-29) SC twice daily for 14 days each; only the 1 mg BID arm significantly raised mean 24-h GH, peak area, peak amplitude, and IGF-1 vs old baseline, to levels no longer different from the young controls (Corpas et al., 1992, PMID 1379256).

🐉 A current labeled-product PK table (bioavailability, t1/2, Vd) is not compiled because Geref is withdrawn and no DailyMed record was fetched this round (Federal Register 2013; inference).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Corpas 1992: healthy nonobese young men n=9 (26.2 ± 4.1 y) and old men n=10 (68.0 ± 6.2 y); old men took both 0.5 mg and 1 mg GHRH(1-29) SC BID × 14 days with a 14-day washout, randomized order; high dose restored mean 24-h GH (P<0.001) and IGF-1 (P<0.005) vs old baseline; fasting glucose, urinary C-peptide, BP, chemistry and hematology unchanged (Corpas et al., J Clin Endocrinol Metab, 1992, https://doi.org/10.1210/jcem.75.2.1379256, PMID 1379256).

📚 Vittone 1997: single nightly GHRH(1-29) for 6 weeks in healthy elderly men did not change weight, BMI, waist-hip, or DEXA muscle and fat; the authors wrote that nightly dosing is less effective than multiple daily doses for GH/IGF-1 effects (Vittone et al., Metabolism, 1997, https://doi.org/10.1016/s0026-0495(97)90174-8, PMID 9005976).

🐉 No compiled sermorelin RCT with a healthspan, mortality, or epigenetic-age primary endpoint was found this round (Corpas 1992; Vittone 1997; AgelessRx XPRIZE copy).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 FDA determined in 2013 that GEREF (sermorelin acetate) injection 0.5 mg and 1.0 mg base/vial and 0.05 mg base/amp were not withdrawn from sale for reasons of safety or effectiveness (Federal Register, 4 Mar 2013, 78 FR 14095, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2013-03-04/html/2013-04827.htm).

📚 Corpas reported no effect on fasting glucose, urinary C-peptide, blood pressure, or routine chemistry/hematology over 14 days at 0.5–1 mg BID (Corpas et al., 1992, PMID 1379256).

📚 Vittone reported no significant adverse effects in the 6-week nightly elderly-men study (Vittone et al., 1997, PMID 9005976).

🐉 No current DailyMed boxed-warning / contraindication table exists for a marketed Geref product. Labeled pediatric/diagnostic language was not re-fetched as XML this round (Federal Register 2013; inference).

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market sermorelin in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Stay-away is not issued.

## Legal / access status

📚 Geref (sermorelin acetate) was withdrawn from sale; FDA’s 2013 determination was that the withdrawal was not for safety or effectiveness, which opens an ANDA path if other requirements are met (Federal Register, 4 Mar 2013). That is not a current aging NDA and not a live branded SKU.

📚 WADA 2026 Prohibited List S2.2.4 names sermorelin with tesamorelin, CJC-1293, and CJC-1295; prohibited at all times (WADA, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).

## What is actually on the table

Two weeks of 1 mg BID raised old-men GH and IGF-1; six weeks of nightly shots did not move DEXA fat or lean (Federal Register 2013; Corpas 1992; Vittone 1997).
