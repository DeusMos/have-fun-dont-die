## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells an elongated Argireline analogue patterned on SNAP-25’s N-terminus as a SNARE-complex competitor for neuromuscular / expression-line lab work (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/snap-8). Lipotec marketed SNAP-8 as a topical wrinkle peptide (Lipotec / *COSSMA* product note, 2010, https://www.cossma.com/fileadmin/all/cossma/Archiv/ProductInfo/COS1005_14_ProdSnap8.pdf). Mindful sells the SKU. Lipotec sold the INCI.

⛔ Treating a manufacturer 10% cream profilometry series as a systemic aging or healthspan restore is a definition swap. A wrinkle-depth percent is a cosmetic endpoint (Lipotec / *COSSMA*, 2010).

⛔ A 9 mg lyophilized research-chemical vial is not a finished 10% SNAP-8 Solution serum (https://happypeptides.com/; Lipotec / *COSSMA*, 2010).
