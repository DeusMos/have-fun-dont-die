## Identity

Home hallmark: [08 cellular senescence](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md). Aliases in the extracted reports: Sprycel. Used as the D half of intermittent D+Q pulses, not as a daily CML exposure in the longevity scene. Pair: [quercetin](../quercetin/report.md).

**CAS:** 302962-49-8; additional CAS 863127-77-9 (📚 PubChem CID 3062316. Synonym CAS 302962-49-8 (anhydrous / Sprycel listing); DTP/NCI also lists 863127-77-9. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/3062316).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Mayo-copied D+Q pulses (100 mg dasatinib + 1000–1250 mg quercetin × 2–3 days) and gray-market dasatinib on Rapamycin News are sold as a zombie-cell flush (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2019; Rapamycin News D+Q thread). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

## Mechanism

📚 A senolytic preferentially kills some senescent-cell states via senescent-cell anti-apoptotic pathways (SCAPs); dasatinib and quercetin hit different lineages in the 2015 screen and are used as an intermittent pulse, not a daily CML-style exposure (Zhu et al., Aging Cell, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12344, PMID 25754370). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#mechanism).

## Animal data

📚 Dasatinib (≥50 nM) cut senescent human preadipocyte viability 30–40% by day 3 while proliferating cells expanded 2–5×; D+Q together cut senescent preadipocytes ~70%; a single D+Q dose in 24-month-old mice improved LV ejection fraction (n=8 males) and vascular nitroprusside relaxation (Zhu et al., Aging Cell, 2015, PMID 25754370; D 5 mg/kg + Q 50 mg/kg). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

📚 Intermittent oral D+Q starting at 24–27 months raised median post-treatment lifespan 36% and cut mortality hazard to 64.9% (p=0.01) versus vehicle, without increasing disease/tumor burden at death (Xu et al., Nature Medicine, 2018; N=71 D+Q vs 76 vehicle; D 5 mg/kg + Q 50 mg/kg, 3 days every 2 weeks). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 A single 3-day oral course of D 100 mg/day + Q 1000 mg/day in diabetic kidney disease reduced adipose p16INK4A+ cells 35% (p=0.001), p21CIP1+ cells 17% (p=0.009), and SA-β-gal+ cells 62% (p=0.005) at day 14; there was no placebo arm and no eGFR or hard kidney outcome (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2019, PMID 31542391; N=9, mean age 68.7, eGFR 27.0; NCT02848131). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 A 2020 corrigendum states that re-analysis of the Hickson raw data changed some conclusions in that paper (Hickson et al., EBioMedicine, 2020; same n=9 dataset). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 Intermittent D 100 mg + Q 1250 mg, 3 consecutive days/week for 3 weeks, in open-label IPF improved 6-minute walk +21.5 m, plus 4-m gait speed, chair-stands, and SPPB (all p<0.05); FEV1/FVC and circulating SASP were unchanged (Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019, PMID 30616998; N=14, age 70.8±7.9, NCT02874989). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

🥼 A later single-blind IPF RCT (n=12, 1:1) using the same D 100 / Q 1250, 3 days × 3 weeks, found intermittent D+Q feasible but 6-minute-walk and other function measures did not differ meaningfully from placebo (Nambiar et al., EBioMedicine, 2023, PMID 36857968, NCT02874989; 6MWD change +16.6 ± 20.7 m D+Q vs −6.4 ± 15.9 m placebo). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 Intermittent oral D+Q for 12 weeks in mild symptomatic AD was feasible: plasma D 12.7–73.5 ng/ml in all 5; CSF D 0.281–0.536 ng/ml in 4/5; cognition and neuroimaging did not change; CSF IL-6 (p=0.008) and GFAP (p=0.028) rose (Gonzales et al., Nature Medicine, 2023, PMID 37679434; N=5, mean age 76±5; 6 cycles of 2 days on / 14 off; NCT04063124). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 Exploratory highest tertile of T-cell p16 mRNA in a 20-week open-label RCT of intermittent D+Q in postmenopausal women showed +2.7% radius BMD vs control at 20 weeks (p=0.004); the prespecified primary (CTx at 20 weeks) did not differ (D+Q −4.1% vs control −7.7%, p=0.611) (Farr et al., Nature Medicine, 2024, PMID 38942993; N=60 women >60 y; D 100 mg × 2 days + Q 1000 mg × 3 days every 28 days × 5 cycles). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#human-data).

📚 In a Phase I open-label clock study at the Institute for Hormonal Balance (Orlando), 19 people took 50 mg dasatinib + 500 mg quercetin orally Mon–Wed each month for 6 months; first-generation epigenetic and mitotic clocks accelerated at 3 and 6 months and estimated telomere length fell, while second- and third-generation clocks did not move significantly (Lee et al., Aging, 2024, PMID 38421953). TruDiagnostic assayed the DNAm and sells the clocks used as endpoints. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

📚 Nambiar 2023 randomized 12 IPF patients 1:1 to intermittent D+Q versus placebo; the trial was powered for feasibility, not efficacy, and frailty, pulmonary, and physical-function measures did not differ meaningfully between arms (Nambiar et al., eBioMedicine, 2023, NCT02874989). Extracted from [13](../../hallmarks/13-extracellular-matrix-changes/report.md).

## Toxicity and hazards

☠︎︎ Continuous oncology-dose dasatinib (Sprycel) has an established human harm file: myelosuppression; Grade 3/4 fluid retention 5% including 3% Grade 3/4 pleural effusion after 5 years in newly diagnosed CP-CML (n=258); at 100 mg once daily, pleural effusion 28% all-grades / 5% Grade 3/4 in one labeled arm (n=165); plus a labeled PAH warning that can appear after >1 year (Bristol Myers Squibb, SPRYCEL US PI). That file is continuous oncology exposure, not a published pulsed 2–3-day senolytic safety file. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

📚 Justice still recorded one Grade 2 pleural effusion in the n=14 IPF open-label D+Q series (Justice et al., EBioMedicine, 2019). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Mayo / Kirkland hit-and-run doses actually used in the published human pilots are D 100 mg + Q 1000 mg × 3 days (Hickson DKD), D 100 mg + Q 1250 mg × 3 days/week × 3 weeks (Justice IPF), and D 100 mg + Q 1000 mg × 2 days every 2 weeks × 6 (Gonzales AD). Kirkland/Tchkonia do not sell a consumer SKU. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 AgelessRx longevity catalog fetched this round listed metformin, rapamycin, NAD+, LDN, GLP-1s and did not list dasatinib, D+Q, or fisetin as a live SKU. Substitute cash-pay clinic with posted D+Q: Lee / Institute for Hormonal Balance Orlando used 50 mg D + 500 mg Q × 3 days/month × 6 months (Lee et al., Aging, 2024). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

🤔 Betterhumans’ live projects page still lists an IRB-framed D+Q question in elderly metabolic-syndrome / OA muscle and fat; no posted results, dose, or price (Betterhumans, https://betterhumans.org/projects/). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Fights

🤼 Rapamycin News thread “Has anyone used dasatinib+quercetin (D&Q) as a senolytic?” (2024) records DIY users at 100 mg D + 1.5 g Q, 3 days × 3 weeks every 4–5 months with “no noticeable effect,” 100 mg D + 1200 mg Q × 2 days every other month with an n=1 OA/CRP story, “took it once, made me sick,” and others arguing senolytics are irrelevant before mid-50s. Split: DIY pulse vs trial-only / age-gated / fisetin-instead. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Legal / access status

☠︎︎ Continuous CML Sprycel (dasatinib) has an established pleural-effusion / PAH harm file; that labeled oncology exposure is not equated to intermittent D+Q pulses unless a source does that (filed under [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md)).

🤔 DIY gray-market D+Q pulses are forum practice, not a labeled senolytic indication ([08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md)).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Human senolytic evidence is open-label n=5–14 marker/feasibility (Hickson, Justice, Gonzales), a bone RCT that missed its primary CTx (Farr 2024), and an IPF RCT that did not reproduce the walk signal (Nambiar 2023). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).

☠︎︎ Continuous-dose Sprycel pleural effusion / myelosuppression / PAH are established oncology harms; they are not a pulsed-D+Q body count and they are not labeling theater (BMS SPRYCEL PI). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).

🤔 People are running Mayo-copied D+Q pulses and half-dose cash-pay D+Q under that slogan anyway (Hickson 2019 protocol; Lee 2024; Rapamycin News). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).
