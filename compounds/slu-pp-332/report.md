## Identity

Home hallmark: [07 mitochondrial dysfunction](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md) for the ERR / oxidative-fiber claim. Adjacent nutrient-sensing page: [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md). Alias: 4-hydroxy-N'-(naphthalen-2-ylmethylene)benzohydrazide.

💯 SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic estrogen-related receptor (ERR) pan-agonist. It is not a peptide and it is not an estrogen-receptor ligand.

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells SLU-PP-332 as an in-vitro research chemical with piperine “to enhance the exposure of the active compound” (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/slu-pp-332). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Mindful’s product description calls SLU-PP-332 a selective estrogen-receptor beta (ERβ) modulator. Billon 2023/2024 characterize it as an ERR (estrogen-related receptor) pan-agonist. ER and ERR are different nuclear-receptor families (Mindful JSON, 2026-09-04; Billon et al., *ACS Chem. Biol.*, 2023).

⛔ Treating Billon mouse treadmill or DIO fat-mass data as a completed human exercise-mimetic or aging restore is a definition swap (Billon et al., 2023; Billon et al., *J. Pharmacol. Exp. Ther.*, 2024).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 SLU-PP-332 is a synthetic ERR pan-agonist with highest potency at ERRα (EC50 98 nM), then ERRβ (230 nM) and ERRγ (430 nM) in a full-length cell-based reporter assay. It induced an ERRα-dependent acute aerobic-exercise gene program, including *Ddit4* as a direct ERRα target (Billon, Sitaula, Banerjee, Welch, Elgendy, Hegazy, Oh, Kazantzis, Chatterjee, Chrivia, et al., *ACS Chem. Biol.*, 2023;18:756–771, https://doi.org/10.1021/acschembio.2c00720, PMC11584170).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 After 30 mg/kg IP in mice, SLU-PP-332 was higher in skeletal muscle (~0.6 μM) than plasma (~0.2 μM) at 2 hours (Billon et al., 2023, Figure 2A).

## Animal data

📚 Sedentary mice treated 50 mg/kg IP b.i.d. for 7 days ran ~70% longer and ~45% further to exhaustion than vehicle. Muscle-specific ERRα knockout abolished the endurance gain. No overt toxicity was reported at 50 mg/kg b.i.d. IP for 10 days; CBC/electrolytes and serum CK were not flagged as abnormal in that package (Billon et al., 2023).

📚 Billon 2024: 50 mg/kg IP b.i.d. In chow C57BL/6J males at thermoneutrality for 28 days, body weight did not differ vs vehicle, fat-mass gain fell, and calculated fatty-acid oxidation rose ~25% with an acute RER drop within 2 hours of the first dose; locomotor activity and food intake were unaltered. In DIO males, 28-day treated mice weighed 12% less; vehicle gained ~5 g fat mass vs <0.5 g on drug. In *ob/ob* males (12 days, shortened for IP tolerance), adiposity and liver weight fell with food intake unaltered (Billon, Schoepke, Avdagic, Chatterjee, Butler, Elgendy, Walker, and Burris, *J. Pharmacol. Exp. Ther.*, 2024;388:232–240, https://doi.org/10.1124/jpet.123.001733, PMC10801787).

📚 T.P. Burris, J.K. Walker, and B. Elgendy are listed as stockholders in Myonid Therapeutics and Pelagos Pharmaceuticals on the 2024 paper (Billon et al., 2024).
