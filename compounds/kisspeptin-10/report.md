## Identity

Parked home: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md) for the GnRH-upstream slogan. Adjacent HPG SKUs: [enclomiphene](../enclomiphene/report.md), [hcg](../hcg/report.md). Aliases: KP-10, metastin (45-54), kisspeptin-10 (human).

💯 Kisspeptin-10 is the C-terminal decapeptide of KISS1 / metastin (residues 45–54), the minimal sequence with full KISS1R (GPR54) activity. It is not kisspeptin-54 and not GnRH.

📚 PubChem name “kisspeptin-10” maps CID 25240297, titled Kisspeptin-10, formula C63H83N17O14, MW 1302.4; synonyms include KP-10, Metastin 45-54, CAS 374675-21-5, and UNII FS1N52VS3S (PubChem PUG name/CID/synonyms/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/25240297).

## Mechanism

📚 Loss-of-function KISS1R / GPR54 mutations cause hypogonadotropic hypogonadism and absent puberty (de Roux et al., *Proc. Natl. Acad. Sci. USA*, 2003;100:10972–10976; Seminara et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2003;349:1614–1627).

📚 George et al. treat KP-10 as the minimal fully bioactive kisspeptin and show IV boluses evoke LH in healthy men, consistent with GnRH-neuron KISS1R activation upstream of pituitary LH (George, Veldhuis, Roseweir, Newton, Faccenda, Millar, and Anderson, 2011, PMID 21632807).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 George 2011: IV bolus 0.01–3.0 µg/kg in healthy men; maximal LH at 1 µg/kg (4.1±0.4 to 12.4±1.7 IU/L at 30 min, n=6, P<0.001). 3 µg/kg was smaller than 1 µg/kg. Infusion 4 µg/kg·h for 22.5 h: LH 5.4±0.7 to 20.8±4.9 IU/L (n=4); testosterone 16.6±2.4 to 24.0±2.5 nmol/L. Infusion 1.5 µg/kg·h: LH pulse frequency 0.7±0.1 to 1.0±0.2 pulses/h (George et al., 2011).

📚 Dhillo 2005 is **kisspeptin-54**, not KP-10: 90-min IV 4 pmol/kg·min in n=6 men; mean 90-min LH 10.8±1.5 vs saline 4.2±0.5 U/L; calculated KP-54 t½ 27.6±1.1 min (Dhillo et al., 2005). Do not paste that t½ onto KP-10.

🐉 A compiled human t½ for KP-10 itself is not extracted on this page. no source; inference from the George methods not stating a KP-10 t½.

## Animal data

📚 Kisspeptin-10 stimulates gonadotropin release in male and female rodents; Jayasena 2011 cites that background before the human dimorphism result (Jayasena et al., *J. Clin. Endocrinol. Metab.*, 2011;96:E1963–E1972, PMID 21976724, https://doi.org/10.1210/jc.2011-1408).

📚 Zhang et al. restored mouse aging histology with **GnRH** 2 ng/mouse SC, not with kisspeptin. That paper is adjacent hypothalamic-aging work, not a KP-10 vial trial (Zhang et al., *Nature*, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature12143; restored from [10](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md)).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Jayasena et al.: IV bolus KP-10 raised LH/FSH in healthy men at ≥0.3 / 1.0 nmol/kg. Follicular-phase women: no gonadotropin move after IV bolus 10 nmol/kg, SC bolus 32 nmol/kg, or IV infusion 720 pmol/kg/min (n=4–5/group). Preovulatory women: IV 10 nmol/kg raised LH and FSH (Jayasena, Nijher, Comninos, Abbara, et al., 2011, PMID 21976724).

📚 George 2011 is an acute IV physiology study in healthy men, not an aging or hypogonadism RCT (George et al., 2011).

🐉 No chronic RUO-vial or aging RCT of KP-10 is compiled on this page. An IV LH pulse is not a healthspan result. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.
