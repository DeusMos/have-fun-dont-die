## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 GLORY-1 (NCT05607680): Phase 3, China, n=610, mean weight 87.2 kg, mean BMI 31.1; adults 18–75 with BMI ≥28, or BMI 24–<28 plus one weight-related comorbidity; 4 mg vs 6 mg vs placebo, 48 weeks. Treatment-policy estimand, week 32: mean percent weight change −10.09% (95% CI −11.15 to −9.04) at 4 mg, −12.55% (−13.64 to −11.45) at 6 mg, +0.45% (−0.61 to 1.52) on placebo; ≥5% weight-loss rates 73.9%, 82.0%, 10.5% (P<0.001 vs placebo). Week 48: −11.00%, −14.01%, +0.30%; ≥15% rates 35.7%, 49.5%, 2.0%. Funded by Innovent (Ji, Jiang, Bi, et al., *N. Engl. J. Med.*, 2025;392:2215–2225, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2411528). Obesity trial, not an aging RCT.

📚 DREAMS-2 (*Nature*, 2025): 731 Chinese adults with T2D on oral agents; 4 mg vs 6 mg vs dulaglutide 1.5 mg for 28 weeks. Both mazdutide doses were non-inferior and superior to dulaglutide 1.5 mg on HbA1c (LSM difference −0.24%, P=0.0032 at 4 mg; −0.30%, P=0.0003 at 6 mg) and on percent weight (LSM difference −3.78% and −5.76%, both P<0.0001). Innovent’s efficacy-estimand numbers: week-28 HbA1c −1.69% / −1.73% / −1.38%; weight −7.31% / −9.24% / −2.86% (DREAMS-2, *Nature*, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-025-10031-z; Innovent, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/innovent-announces-mazdutide-received-approval-from-chinas-nmpa-for-glycemic-control-in-adults-with-type-2-diabetes-302561434.html).

📚 DREAMS-1 (NCT05628311), Innovent’s efficacy estimand at week 24: HbA1c −1.57% / −2.15% / −0.14% and weight −5.61% / −7.81% / −1.26% for 4 mg / 6 mg / placebo; HbA1c <7% in 68.6%, 87.4%, 10.7% (Innovent, 2025, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/innovent-announces-mazdutide-received-approval-from-chinas-nmpa-for-glycemic-control-in-adults-with-type-2-diabetes-302561434.html). Company release; the journal DREAMS-1 paper was not extracted this flight.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 GLORY-1: most frequent adverse events were gastrointestinal, mostly mild to moderate; regimen discontinuation 1.5% at 4 mg, 0.5% at 6 mg, 1.0% on placebo (Ji et al., 2025).

📚 DREAMS-2: most common TEAEs were diarrhea, nausea, and vomiting; GI incidence higher than dulaglutide 1.5 mg (DREAMS-2, *Nature*, 2025).

🐉 No US Prescribing Information exists for mazdutide as of this cutoff. Class GLP-1 boxed-warning language from Wegovy/Zepbound is not this label. no source; inference from the no-US-label file.
