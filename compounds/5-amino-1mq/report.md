## Mechanism

📚 5-Amino-1-methylquinolinium (5A1MQ) is a membrane-permeable small-molecule NNMT inhibitor. Neelakantan et al. reported that selective NNMT inhibitors reversed high-fat-diet-induced obesity in mice (Neelakantan, Vance, Wetzel, et al., *Biochem. Pharmacol.*, 2018;147:141–152, PMID 29155147, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bcp.2017.11.007).

📚 Babula et al. treat 5A1MQ as an NNMT inhibitor that limited weight/fat gain, improved OGTT and insulin sensitivity, and cut hepatic steatosis in DIO mice (Babula, Bui, Stevenson, Watowich, and Neelakantan, *Diabetes Obes. Metab.*, 2024;26:5272–5282, https://doi.org/10.1111/dom.15879).

## Animal data

📚 Neelakantan 2018: selective, membrane-permeable NNMT inhibitors reversed HFD-induced obesity in mice (Neelakantan et al., *Biochem. Pharmacol.*, 2018, PMID 29155147).

📚 Babula 2024: DIO mice, once-daily SC 10 or 32 mg/kg for ~28–30 days, n=8/group vs saline. Dose-dependent limit on body-weight and fat-mass gain; improved OGTT and insulin sensitivity; high dose cut fed-state insulin (control +112% from baseline, high-dose −9%); less hepatic steatosis, macrophage infiltration, liver weight and triglyceride (Babula et al., 2024).
