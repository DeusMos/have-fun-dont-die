## Identity

Home hallmark: [06 deregulated nutrient sensing](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md). Aliases: retatrutide, LY3437943, RT-GLP3. Adjacent incretin SKUs: [semaglutide](../semaglutide/report.md), [tirzepatide](../tirzepatide/report.md), [mazdutide](../mazdutide/report.md).

💯 Retatrutide (LY3437943) is a single peptide with a fatty-diacid conjugate that agonizes GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors (Jastreboff et al., N Engl J Med, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Gray-market copy sells 10–30 mg RT-GLP3 vials as “the Lilly triple agonist” / obesity-aging restore (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/glp3). Mindful Research sells the SKU.

## Mechanism

📚 Jastreboff: as compared with endogenous ligands, retatrutide is less potent at human GCG and GLP-1 receptors (0.3× and 0.4×) and more potent at the human GIP receptor (8.9×) (Jastreboff et al., 2023, citing Coskun et al., Cell Metab, 2022).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Jastreboff: pharmacokinetics are considered dose-proportional; half-life approximately 6 days, which enables weekly administration (Jastreboff et al., 2023, citing Urva et al., Lancet, 2022 Phase 1b). That is trial-product PK, not a gray-market vial table.

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Jastreboff 2023 Phase 2: 338 adults, BMI ≥30 or 27–<30 plus a weight-related condition, no type 2 diabetes; weekly SC retatrutide 1–12 mg vs placebo × 48 weeks plus lifestyle; 51.8% men; Lilly-funded. Primary 24-week weight change: −7.2% (1 mg), −12.9% (combined 4 mg), −17.3% (combined 8 mg), −17.5% (12 mg) vs −1.6% placebo. At 48 weeks: −8.7%, −17.1%, −22.8%, −24.2% vs −2.1%. At 12 mg, 100% / 93% / 83% reached ≥5% / ≥10% / ≥15% weight loss vs 27% / 9% / 2% placebo (Jastreboff et al., N Engl J Med, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2301972, NCT04881760).

📚 Exploratory cardiometabolic moves in that trial included waist, BP, HbA1c, fasting glucose, insulin, and lipids except HDL; 72% of retatrutide participants with baseline prediabetes were <5.7% HbA1c at week 48 vs 22% placebo (Jastreboff et al., 2023). Those are obesity-trial secondaries, not healthspan.

🐉 No compiled retatrutide RCT with a healthspan, mortality, or epigenetic-age primary endpoint was found this round (Jastreboff 2023; no DailyMed).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 There is no US labeled product. Trial AEs are the compiled file.

📚 Jastreboff: treatment-period AEs 73–94% retatrutide vs 70% placebo; discontinuation for AE 6–16% vs 0%; GI events (nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation) dose-related, mostly during escalation, partly mitigated by a 2 mg vs 4 mg start. Nausea at 12 mg 45% vs placebo 11%. SAE 4% both arms. One drowning death assessed unrelated. One acute pancreatitis SAE. Biliary disorders in 3 retatrutide participants (cholecystitis 1, cholelithiasis 2). Dose-dependent heart-rate rise to week 24 then decline. Cutaneous hyperesthesia 7% vs 1%. No level-2/3 hypoglycemia, MTC, or C-cell hyperplasia reported (Jastreboff et al., 2023, Table 3).

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market RT-GLP3 in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Trial GI / HR / pancreatitis / biliary events are not ☠︎︎ for the vial. Stay-away is not issued.

## What is actually on the table

📚 Retatrutide is a real GIP/GLP-1/glucagon agonist. CAS 2381089-83-2 sits on PubChem SID 523601838. A Lilly-funded obesity Phase 2 cut weight 24.2% at 12 mg weekly × 48 weeks, with GLP-1-class GI toxicity, a heart-rate bump, and rare biliary/pancreatitis events. There is no US label (Jastreboff 2023; PubChem SID).

⛔ That file is not organismal aging restore, and a Mindful vial is not the trial product (Mindful JSON).
