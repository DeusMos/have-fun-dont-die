## Identity

Home hallmark: [03 epigenetic alterations](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md). Aliases: tesamorelin, tesamorelin acetate, TH9507, Egrifta / Egrifta SV / Egrifta WR. Adjacent GHRH / TRIIM-stack SKUs: [sermorelin](../sermorelin/report.md), [thymosin-alpha-1](../thymosin-alpha-1/report.md), [ipamorelin](../ipamorelin/report.md), [cjc-1295-no-dac](../cjc-1295-no-dac/report.md).

💯 Tesamorelin is a synthetic 44-residue human GHRH analogue with an N-terminal trans-3-hexenoyl group; Egrifta products are that peptide prepared as an acetate salt (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, setid 3d783378-b02d-4f19-99dd-0fc91a042224; PubChem CID 16137828 title Tesamorelin).

📚 PubChem CID 16137828 lists Tesamorelin, formula C221H366N72O67S, MW 5136 g/mol, CAS 218949-48-5 (free peptide). The same CID’s RN dump also includes 804475-66-9 and 901758-09-6; UNII MQG94M5EEO (PubChem PUG name/CID/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/16137828).

📚 PubChem CID 44147413 is titled Tesamorelin acetate [USAN] by ChemIDplus, CAS 901758-09-6, formula C223H370N72O69S, MW 5196 — a mono-acetate drawing, not the labeled multi-acetate drug substance (PubChem PUG CID 44147413 + pug_view CAS, fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/44147413).

📚 DailyMed names the drug substance tesamorelin acetate, UNII LGW5H38VE3 (salt) / MQG94M5EEO (tesamorelin): C221H366N72O67S • x C2H4O2 (x ≈ 7); molecular weight as free-base equivalent 5135.9 Da; EGRIFTA SV 2 mg tesamorelin ≈ 2.2 mg tesamorelin acetate per vial (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 11).

🤔 Mindful Research lists CAS 901758-09-6, CID 44147413, formula C223H370N72O69S, MW 5195.908 g/mol, and aliases Egrifta (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/tesamorelin.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). ## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Peptide-clinic and gray-market copy sells a 10 mg RUO tesamorelin vial, or “FDA-approved Egrifta,” as visceral-fat / thymus / epigenetic-age restore in ordinary adults, including TRIIM-shaped stacks (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/tesamorelin; Rapamycin News thread 25573). Those pages sell or discuss the peptide.

⛔ Treating Egrifta’s HIV-lipodystrophy VAT reduction as proof that ordinary-adult aging or abdominal fat can be restored is a labeled indication doing a job it was not written for (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, sections 1 and 14).

⛔ Treating tesamorelin as TRIIM ignores that TRIIM used rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men, not a GHRH analogue (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028, PMID 31496122).

## Mechanism

📚 The Egrifta label: tesamorelin is a human growth-hormone-releasing factor analogue; GHRH acts on pituitary somatotrophs to stimulate synthesis and pulsatile release of endogenous GH; some GH effects are IGF-1-mediated (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 12.1).

📚 Tesamorelin raised GH, IGF-1, and IGFBP-3 in the labeled trials; TSH, LH, ACTH, and prolactin did not show clinically significant changes (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 12.2).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Absolute bioavailability after a 2 mg subcutaneous dose of original Egrifta (1 mg/vial) was <4% in healthy adults (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 12.3).

📚 After 1.4 mg EGRIFTA SV SC in healthy subjects: mean AUC0-inf 889.1 pg·h/mL (CV 57%), Cmax 2956.1 pg/mL (CV 47%), median Tmax 0.15 h, mean t1/2 8 minutes, Vd 4.8 ± 1.9 L/kg; HIV-infected patients without lipodystrophy had 34% higher AUC than healthy subjects after the original 2 mg dose, with similar Cmax (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 12.3).

📚 EGRIFTA WR 1.28 mg SC is labeled bioequivalent in exposure to original Egrifta 2 mg; mean t1/2 11 minutes (DailyMed EGRIFTA WR, setid 839334d3-8c1d-4c26-9036-2ab524a6ea75, section 12.3). That is labeled-product PK, not a gray-market vial table.

## Animal data

📚 Tesamorelin acetate given to rats during organogenesis produced hydrocephaly in offspring at ~2× and ~4× the clinical AUC (actual dose 1.2 mg/kg); delayed skull ossification at ~0.1–1× (0.1–0.6 mg/kg); rabbits up to ~500× showed no adverse developmental effects (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 8.1). Label developmental tox, not an aging lifespan study.

🐉 No compiled tesamorelin rodent lifespan or healthy-aging dataset was fetched this round (inference from the labeled-product file).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 DailyMed Study 1 (NCT00123253): 412 HIV-infected adults with excess abdominal fat, 2:1 to original Egrifta 2 mg SC daily vs placebo × 26 weeks; mean age 48, 86% male; ITT LOCF VAT −18% vs +2% (treatment difference −20%, 95% CI −24 to −15); IGF-1 +107 vs −15 ng/mL; weight change −0.4 vs 0.0 kg (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 14, Tables 2–3).

📚 DailyMed Study 2 (NCT00435136): 404 HIV-infected adults, same design; VAT −14% vs −2% (difference −12%, 95% CI −16 to −7); IGF-1 +108 vs +3 ng/mL; weight +0.5 vs +0.3 kg (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 14).

📚 Falutz / Grinspoon NEJM 2007 is Study 1: VAT −15.2% tesamorelin vs +5.0% placebo; triglycerides −50 vs +9 mg/dL; IGF-I +81% vs −5%; no significant glycemic difference in that paper; Theratechnologies funded (Falutz et al., N Engl J Med, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa072375, NCT00123253).

📚 Extension phases re-randomized 26-week completers; VAT reduction was maintained on continued tesamorelin and lost after switch to placebo (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 14; Falutz et al., J Acquir Immune Defic Syndr, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1097/qai.0b013e3181cbdaff).

📚 Stanley 2014: HIV-infected adults with abdominal fat accumulation, tesamorelin 2 mg SC daily (n=28 treated) vs placebo (n=22) × 6 months; VAT treatment effect −42 cm² (95% CI −71 to −14); liver fat lipid-to-water net −2.9%; fasting glucose +7 mg/dL at 2 weeks, not different at 6 months (Stanley et al., JAMA Intern Med, 2014, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4363137/).

📚 TRIIM reported a mean ~2.5-year drop in epigenetic-minus-chronological age after 12 months of rhGH+DHEA+metformin in nine men aged 51–65 with no placebo arm (Fahy et al., Aging Cell, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.13028, PMID 31496122). Restore from hallmark 03. That protocol is not tesamorelin.

📚 TRIIM-X (NCT04375657) is an ongoing Intervene Immune Phase 2 (estimated n=85) without posted results as of the hallmark 03 check (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT04375657).

🐉 No compiled tesamorelin RCT in HIV-uninfected ordinary adults with a healthspan, mortality, or epigenetic-age primary endpoint was found this round (DailyMed; Fahy 2019; Rapamycin News).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 EGRIFTA SV is contraindicated in disruption of the hypothalamic-pituitary axis (hypophysectomy, hypopituitarism, pituitary tumor/surgery, head irradiation or trauma), active malignancy, known hypersensitivity to tesamorelin or excipients, and pregnancy (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV patient labeling + Drugs.com/pro reprint of section 4, https://www.drugs.com/pro/egrifta-sv.html; DailyMed setid 3d783378-b02d-4f19-99dd-0fc91a042224).

📚 Among patients on Egrifta 26 weeks, 47% had IGF-1 SDS >2 and 36% SDS >3; at 52 weeks 34% and 23% respectively; the label says consider stopping if IGF-1 stays >3 SDS especially without a robust VAT response (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 5.2).

📚 Fluid retention (edema, arthralgia, carpal tunnel) is labeled as GH-related and usually transient or resolved on stop (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 5.3).

📚 Glucose intolerance: HbA1c ≥6.5% from baseline to week 26 in 5% Egrifta vs 1% placebo; ITT hazard odds ratio for new diabetes 3.3 (CI 1.4, 9.6); monitor glucose; watch retinopathy in diabetics because IGF-1 rises (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 5.4).

📚 Hypersensitivity 4% (pruritus, erythema, flushing, urticaria, rash); injection-site reactions 25% vs 14% placebo in the first 26 weeks (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, sections 5.5–5.6).

📚 Common adverse reactions >5%: arthralgia, injection-site erythema, injection-site pruritus, pain in extremity, peripheral edema, myalgia (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, highlights).

📚 Anti-tesamorelin IgG in 50% at 26 weeks and 47% at 52 weeks; neutralizing antibodies in vitro at week 52 in 10% (tesamorelin) and 5% (hGHRH); VAT and IGF-1 responses were similar with or without those antibodies in the labeled analysis (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 12.6).

📚 Most common adverse reactions in the clinical program also included hypersensitivity, edema-related events, hyperglycemia, and injection-site reactions (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 6).

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market tesamorelin in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Labeled HIV-lipodystrophy risks are not ☠︎︎ for the RUO vial. Stay-away is not issued.

## Interactions

📚 Co-administration with simvastatin (CYP3A substrate) had no significant PK impact in healthy subjects (simvastatin AUCinf −8%, Cmax +5%) (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, sections 7 and 12.3).

📚 The label cites published data that GH may alter CYP450-mediated clearance (corticosteroids, sex steroids, anticonvulsants, cyclosporine) and says monitor those combinations (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, section 7).

## Fights

🤼 Prescription Egrifta (1.28–2 mg/day labeled HIV-VAT product) versus a 10 mg RUO vial sold with the Egrifta alias is an amateur/clinic split, not a paper fight (DailyMed; Mindful JSON).

🤼 Rapamycin News splits daily-HGH TRIIM DIY vs clinic TRIIM-X vs tesamorelin+thymosin α1+rapamycin+imeglimin forks; TRIIM-X subject reports say Fahy’s protocol was not daily GH (Rapamycin News threads 24180 and 25573; extracted from hallmark 03).

📚 HIV-VAT RCTs and TRIIM (rhGH) are different drugs, populations, and endpoints; they are not a same-question literature fight (DailyMed section 14; Fahy 2019).

## Legal / access status

📚 Tesamorelin acetate (Egrifta family) is FDA-approved to reduce excess abdominal fat in HIV-infected adults with lipodystrophy; initial U.S. approval 2010; long-term cardiovascular safety is labeled unknown; not indicated for weight-loss management (DailyMed EGRIFTA SV, highlights and section 1).

📚 WADA 2026 Prohibited List S2.2.4 names tesamorelin with other GHRH analogues (sermorelin, CJC-1293, CJC-1295); prohibited at all times (WADA, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).

🤔 Mindful Research LLC states it is a chemical supplier and that products are sold only as research chemicals for in vitro use (https://happypeptides.com/policies/terms-of-service).

⛔ Calling a 10 mg RUO vial “Egrifta” or “FDA-approved tesamorelin for aging” collapses an HIV-lipodystrophy acetate injection into a research-chem SKU (DailyMed; Mindful JSON).

## What is actually on the table

📚 Tesamorelin is a real GHRH analogue. Tesamorelin acetate is the Egrifta drug substance (CAS 901758-09-6 / CID 44147413 / UNII LGW5H38VE3). The free peptide is CAS 218949-48-5 / CID 16137828. Labeled HIV-VAT trials cut visceral fat ~12–20% vs placebo at 26 weeks; the fat comes back when the shots stop; IGF-1 jumps; glucose-intolerance risk is on the label (DailyMed; Falutz 2007; Stanley 2014).

⛔ That file is not organismal aging restore, not TRIIM, and a Mindful vial is not Egrifta (Fahy 2019; DailyMed; Mindful JSON).

🤔 People can buy 10 mg RUO vials and labeled HIV-lipodystrophy injections today. That is practice plus a lipodystrophy label, not a healthspan result.
