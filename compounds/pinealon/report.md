## Mechanism

📚 Khavinson et al.: peptides Glu-Asp-Arg and Lys-Glu-Asp stimulated serotonin expression in aging cultures of brain-cortex cells. Molecular docking was used to argue complementarity to a CCTGCC stretch in the 5-tryptophan-hydroxylase gene. The authors called that epigenetic regulation of serotonin synthesis (Khavinson et al., 2014, PMID 24909721).

🐉 Nuclear DNA-binding by a three-residue peptide as a geroprotector mechanism is the Khavinson leap. Docking plus cell staining is not a chromatin occupancy result. no source; inference from the docking-only file.
