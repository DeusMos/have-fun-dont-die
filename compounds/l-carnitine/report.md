## Identity

Home hallmark: [07 mitochondrial dysfunction](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md). Adjacent TMAO / microbiota slogans also touch [12 dysbiosis](../../hallmarks/12-dysbiosis/report.md). Aliases: levocarnitine, (−)-carnitine, vitamin BT. This page is the L-enantiomer inner salt, not acetyl-L-carnitine and not D-carnitine.

💯 L-carnitine is (3R)-3-hydroxy-4-(trimethylazaniumyl)butanoate; it shuttles long-chain fatty acids across the inner mitochondrial membrane.

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells a carnitine-shuttle reagent for in-vitro fatty-acid oxidation (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/l-carnitine). Longevity and MIC-shot copy sells oral or injectable L-carnitine as a fat-burn / fatigue restore. Mindful sells the SKU. Leadiant sells Carnitor.

## Mechanism

💯 CPT1/CPT2 move acyl-carnitines across the inner membrane; that is textbook mitochondrial β-oxidation.

📚 The Carnitor label restates that levocarnitine facilitates long-chain fatty-acid entry into mitochondria and that fatty acids are the main energy substrate in skeletal and cardiac muscle, not brain (DailyMed Carnitor injection, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=cf801cc4-775e-433d-9d32-e5d9a98981d3).

📚 Gut microbes convert dietary L-carnitine to TMA, which host FMO3 oxidizes to TMAO (Koeth, Wang, Levison, et al., *Nat. Med.*, 2013;19:576–585, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.3145, PMC3650111).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Oral Carnitor absolute bioavailability after endogenous correction: 15.1 ± 5.3% (tablets) and 15.9 ± 4.9% (oral solution). After 4 days of 1,980–2,000 mg q12h in 15 healthy men, Cmax ~80 µmol/L at Tmax 3.3 h. IV 20 mg/kg: ~76% recovered in urine 0–24 h; distribution t½ 0.585 h; apparent terminal t½ 17.4 h. Not protein-bound. Oral [³H-methyl]-L-carnitine metabolites include TMAO in urine (8–49% of dose) and γ-butyrobetaine in feces (DailyMed Carnitor injection).

🐉 A compiled PK table for the Mindful 600 mg/mL concentrate is not on this page. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-PK file.

## Animal data

📚 Chronic dietary L-carnitine in mice with intact microbiota raised TMA/TMAO and increased atherosclerosis; that did not happen when microbiota was suppressed. TMAO, carnitine, or choline reduced reverse cholesterol transport in mice with intact flora (Koeth et al., 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/nm.3145).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Carnitor labeled uses: secondary carnitine deficiency from inborn errors; prevention/treatment of carnitine deficiency in ESRD on dialysis. Oral tablets/solution also cover primary systemic carnitine deficiency. The injection label states that raising carnitine levels in ESRD is demonstrated; effects on signs/symptoms and clinical outcomes in that population have **not** been determined (DailyMed Carnitor injection; DailyMed Carnitor oral solution, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/fda/fdaDrugXsl.cfm?setid=b2cbc620-59fd-47b6-9e4b-7e3e31533a1c).

📚 Malaguarnera et al.: randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled; N=66 centenarians with fatigue after slight activity; levocarnitine 2 g once daily vs placebo × 6 months (Sigma Tau). Vs placebo: fat mass −1.80 vs +0.6 kg (P<0.01); muscle mass +3.80 vs +0.8 kg (P<0.01); physical fatigue −4.10 vs −1.10 (P<0.01); mental fatigue −2.70 vs +0.30 (P<0.001); fatigue severity −23.60 vs +1.90 (P<0.001); MMSE +4.1 vs +0.6 (P<0.001). Authors noted mild cognitive deficit, sensory limits, and constant assistance as constraints (Malaguarnera, Cammalleri, Gargante, Vacante, Colonna, and Motta, *Am. J. Clin. Nutr.*, 2007;86:1738–1744, https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/86.6.1738, PMID 18065594).

📚 Koeth et al.: omnivores produced more TMAO than vegans/vegetarians after L-carnitine; plasma L-carnitine in N=2,595 undergoing cardiac evaluation predicted prevalent CVD and incident MACE only when TMAO was also high (Koeth et al., 2013).

⛔ A 6-month fatigue/MMSE delta in centenarians, or a TMAO-conditional CVD association, is not a human lifespan RCT.

🐉 No compiled healthy-aging mortality trial of L-carnitine is on this page. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Carnitor injection: serious hypersensitivity including anaphylaxis, laryngeal edema, and bronchospasm, mostly in ESRD on dialysis; some within minutes of IV. Chronic high-dose **oral** levocarnitine in severe renal insufficiency / ESRD may accumulate TMA and TMAO. Transient nausea, vomiting, body odor, gastritis (DailyMed Carnitor injection).

📚 Koeth 2013 is the TMAO/atherosclerosis file, not a ☠︎︎ “carnitine is a corrosive poison” finding for labeled replacement.

## Interactions

📚 Carnitor: INR increases reported with warfarin; monitor INR after start or dose change (DailyMed Carnitor injection).
