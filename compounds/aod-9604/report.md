## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO and clinic copy sells AOD-9604 as a fat-loss hGH fragment that skips IGF-1 and growth (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/aod-9604). Mindful sells the SKU.

⛔ Treating Heffernan 2001 mouse lipolysis, or Stier 2013 “no IGF-1 rise,” as a completed human obesity or aging restore is a definition swap. Metabolic’s 24-week OPTIONS Phase IIb did not show statistically significant weight loss at any tested oral dose (Heffernan et al., *Endocrinology*, 2001; Stier et al., 2013; Bioshares Edition 206, 2007, https://www.bioshares.com.au/PDF/db4ea946-c6e9-48ec-877b-6c2e25d6d61e/Edition206AcruxSignsWithOrganonMoreDealsExpected).

⛔ A research-chemical listing is not a human aging trial and not a completed restore result (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 AOD9604 is the C-terminal hGH fragment plus N-terminal Tyr. Stier et al. state it lacks hGH receptor site 2, so it cannot dimerize the GH receptor; competition binding in 125I-hGH-receptor cells was used to support that, and the human trials showed no IGF-1 rise (Stier et al., 2013, https://doi.org/10.4021/jem157w).

📚 Chronic ip AOD9604 and hGH raised adipose β3-AR RNA in obese mice and failed to change body weight or lipolysis in β3-AR knock-out mice; acute AOD9604 still raised energy expenditure and fat oxidation in the knock-outs. The authors conclude lipolysis is not mediated *directly* through β3-AR, though both compounds increase β3-AR expression (Heffernan, Summers, Thorburn, Ogru, Gianello, Jiang, and Ng, *Endocrinology*, 2001;142:5182–5189, PMID 11713213, https://doi.org/10.1210/endo.142.12.8522).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Metabolic ran IV (METAOD001–002) and oral capsule/tablet (METAOD003–006) arms. Stier et al. report no IGF-1 rise vs placebo after single IV doses 25–400 µg/kg, single oral 9–54 mg, 7-day oral 9–54 mg, 12-week oral 1–30 mg, or 24-week oral 0.25–1 mg (Stier et al., 2013).

## Animal data

📚 Heffernan 2001: 14-day chronic ip AOD9604 or hGH reduced body weight and body fat in obese mice and raised β3-AR RNA toward lean-mouse levels; the chronic weight/lipolysis signal was gone in β3-AR knock-outs (Heffernan et al., *Endocrinology*, 2001, https://doi.org/10.1210/endo.142.12.8522).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Stier, Vos, and Kenley summarize six randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled Metabolic trials, 2001–2006, 893 adults (mostly obese): METAOD001 IV 25–400 µg/kg n=15 healthy men; METAOD002 IV 25–100 µg/kg n=23 BMI ≥35; METAOD003 oral 9/27/54 mg n=17; METAOD004 7-day oral 9/27/54 mg n=36; METAOD005 12-week oral 1/5/10/20/30 mg n=300 BMI ≥35; METAOD006 24-week oral 0.25/0.5/1 mg, 534 enrolled / 502 randomized, BMI 30–45. The paper is a safety compilation. It does not report weight-change efficacy (Stier et al., 2013, https://doi.org/10.4021/jem157w).

🤔 BioSpace, still blinded, recorded OPTIONS (METAOD006) as 536 subjects, once-daily oral 0.25 / 0.5 / 1 mg or placebo, primary endpoint weight loss at 12 weeks, results expected March 2007 (BioSpace / Metabolic, 2006, https://www.biospace.com/metabolic-pharmaceuticals-s-obesity-trial-update-first-100-subjects-complete-the-phase-2b-trial-of-aod9604).

🤔 Bioshares Edition 206 reported that Metabolic terminated AOD9604 for obesity after unblinding: the peptide did not deliver a statistically significant weight loss at any of three doses in the 502-patient Phase IIb; the stock fell 71% the day of the announcement (Bioshares Edition 206, 2007, https://www.bioshares.com.au/PDF/db4ea946-c6e9-48ec-877b-6c2e25d6d61e/Edition206AcruxSignsWithOrganonMoreDealsExpected).

🐉 No published human aging / body-composition RCT of AOD-9604 is compiled on this page. OPTIONS was oral obesity, and the efficacy CSR was not published as a journal paper. no source; inference from the empty aging file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Stier et al.: no IGF-1 rise; OGTT unchanged vs placebo; no anti-AOD9604 antibodies through 24 weeks. METAOD005 listed five cancer SAEs on AOD arms (basal cell and squamous cell carcinoma and a lipoma at 20 mg; melanoma at 10 mg; breast cancer at 5 mg); none at 30 mg; the PI judged none related. Headache and GI events were common and not clearly dose-separated from placebo except more headache/diarrhea/flatulence at 54 mg for 7 days (Stier et al., 2013).
