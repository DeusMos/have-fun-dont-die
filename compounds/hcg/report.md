## Identity

Home hallmark: [10 altered intercellular communication](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md). Adjacent HPG SKUs: [enclomiphene](../enclomiphene/report.md), [kisspeptin-10](../kisspeptin-10/report.md). Aliases: human chorionic gonadotropin, chorionic gonadotrophin (CG), urinary hCG. Branded urinary products include Pregnyl and Novarel; recombinant choriogonadotropin alfa (Ovidrel) is a different manufacturing class.

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII 20ED16GHEB is CHORIONIC GONADOTROPIN, with synonyms HCG, HUMAN CHORIONIC GONADOTROPIN, PREGNYL, and CAS mappings including 9002-61-3 (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/20ed16gheb). UNII availability does not imply a review of the RUO vial.

📚 PubChem name “Human Chorionic Gonadotropin” 404 this flight. No small-molecule CID was locked. hCG is a glycoprotein heterodimer (α shared with LH/FSH/TSH; unique β), not a 232 Da monomer (PUG name fetch 2026-09-04).

🤔 Mindful Research lists HCG, CAS 9002-61-3, heterodimer (α and β), MW **232.404 g/mol**, no CID on the table; href is a name search `…/compound/Human%20Chorionic%20Gonadotropin` (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/hcg.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). ⛔ MW 232.404 g/mol next to “heterodimer” is a wrong-mass paste. Intact urinary hCG is a ~37 kDa glycoprotein, not a 232 Da small molecule (Pregnyl label; Mindful JSON).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells an LH-analogue for in-vitro steroidogenesis (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/hcg). TRT-clinic and forum copy sells hCG for testicular volume / fertility on testosterone. Simeons-diet copy sells hCG plus a ~500 kcal diet for fat-loss. Mindful sells the SKU. Organon/Ferring sell labeled urinary hCG.

⛔ The Pregnyl label states hCG has **not** been demonstrated effective for obesity and has no known effect on fat mobilization, appetite, or fat distribution (DailyMed Pregnyl, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=1e94155f-19be-4944-b197-be4edbb4faf9).

⛔ A 5,000 IU research-chemical listing is not Pregnyl and is not a completed aging RCT (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Pregnyl: action is virtually identical to pituitary LH, with a small degree of FSH activity; stimulates Leydig-cell androgens and corpus-luteum progesterone (DailyMed Pregnyl, https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=1e94155f-19be-4944-b197-be4edbb4faf9).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Labeled indications on the urinary product: prepubertal cryptorchidism not due to anatomical obstruction; selected hypogonadotropic hypogonadism in males; ovulation induction in selected anovulatory infertile women after menotropins (2011 Organon/FDA label, https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2011/017067s057lbl.pdf; DailyMed Pregnyl).

📚 The same labels print that hCG is not effective adjunctive therapy for obesity (DailyMed Pregnyl; 2011 FDA label).

📚 FDA/FTC, 6 December 2011: no FDA-approved HCG product for weight loss; OTC “homeopathic” HCG drops/pellets/sprays were warned; VLCD used with those products raises gallstone, electrolyte, and arrhythmia risk (FDA/FTC press packet, https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/attachments/press-releases/ftc-charges-hcg-marketer-deceptive-advertising/fda_hcg_products_press_release_12-6-11.pdf).

🐉 No compiled healthy-aging or lifespan RCT of hCG is on this page. An LH-like testosterone or ovulation pulse is not a healthspan result. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Urinary hCG labels: anaphylaxis / hypersensitivity reported; use with menotropins only by physicians who can monitor; OHSS can be life-threatening; contraindications include precocious puberty, prostatic carcinoma or other androgen-dependent neoplasm, and prior allergic reaction (2011 FDA label; DailyMed Pregnyl).

📚 Diluent for Pregnyl contains 0.9% benzyl alcohol, not for use in newborns (DailyMed Pregnyl).

⛔ The Simeons / OTC HCG-diet claim is a false efficacy claim on the label’s own terms. The FDA/FTC file is about unapproved OTC products plus VLCD risk, not a ☠︎︎ “hCG is a corrosive poison” finding for labeled IM use.

## Interactions

📚 hCG cross-reacts in gonadotropin immunoassays, especially LH; tell the lab (DailyMed Pregnyl).

📚 Combined gonadotropin / hCG protocols need estradiol and ultrasound monitoring; that is a labeled monitoring requirement, not a CYP table (DailyMed Pregnyl).

## Legal / access status

📚 WADA 2026 S2.2.1 names **chorionic gonadotrophin (CG)** among testosterone-stimulating peptides in males, prohibited at all times (WADA 2026 list, https://www.wada-ama.org/sites/default/files/2025-09/2026list_en_final_clean_september_2025.pdf).

📚 Urinary hCG is an FDA-approved prescription injectable for the labeled indications. It is not approved for weight loss (DailyMed Pregnyl; FDA/FTC 2011).
