## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 CASTA: double-blind RCT, acute hemispheric ischemic stroke within 12 h; 30 mL IV daily × 10 days plus aspirin 100 mg; N=1070 (529 Cerebrolysin / 541 placebo); NCT00868283. Primary combined global test of mRS, Barthel, and NIHSS: **no significant difference**. Post-hoc NIHSS >12 stratum trended; 90-day mortality in that severe slice 10.5% vs 20.2% (Heiss et al., 2012, https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.111.628537).

📚 CARS: RCT, 30 mL/day × 21 days starting 24–72 h, plus a standardized 21-day rehab program; primary ARAT day 90. Mann-Whitney estimator 0.71 (95% CI 0.63–0.79; p < 0.0001). Authors called the study exploratory and asked for a large confirmatory RCT. N on the order of 208 in the protocol/report stack (Muresanu, Heiss, Hoemberg, et al., *Stroke*, 2016;47:151–159, https://doi.org/10.1161/STROKEAHA.115.009416).

📚 Cochrane (Ziganshina et al.): six RCTs, 1,501 participants, cerebrolysin within 48 h of acute ischemic stroke vs placebo/no treatment. No beneficial effect on death. Total AEs similar; concern for more non-fatal serious AEs. Several included trials manufacturer-linked (Ziganshina, Abakumova, and Vernay, *Cochrane Database Syst. Rev.*, CD007026, https://doi.org/10.1002/14651858.CD007026.pub5).

📚 Bornstein / Guekht industry-adjacent metas pooled CARS-class and nine-trial NIHSS early-recovery sets and reported MW ~0.60 superiority (Bornstein et al., *Neurol. Sci.*, 2018; Guekht et al., *Neurol. Sci.*, 2017). Those sit opposite Cochrane on what “works” means.

⛔ An acute-stroke NIHSS or ARAT move is not a human aging RCT.

🐉 No compiled healthy-aging or lifespan trial of cerebrolysin is on this page. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 CASTA and CARS reported safety comparable to placebo in those publications (Heiss 2012; Muresanu 2016).

📚 Cochrane flagged a possible increase in non-fatal serious AEs vs placebo (Ziganshina et al., CD007026).

📚 The SPC contraindicates hypersensitivity, epilepsy, and severe renal impairment (EVER SPC).

🐉 A compiled RUO-powder harm series is not on this page. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-tox file.

## Interactions

📚 The SPC: incompatible with solutions that shift pH outside 5.0–8.0 and with lipid-containing solutions; vitamins and cardiac drugs may be given, not in the same syringe (EVER SPC).
