## Mechanism

📚 Liu et al. synthesized UK-5099 (JXL001) analogues as mitochondrial pyruvate carrier (MPC) inhibitors for hair loss; JXL069 is the 7-azaindole analogue with significant MPC-inhibition activity and a 3,5-bis(trifluoromethyl)benzyl N1 substituent (Liu, Flores, Situ, Gu, Wei, Mo, et al., *J. Med. Chem.*, 2021;64:2046–2063, https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.jmedchem.0c01570, PMC8939290).
