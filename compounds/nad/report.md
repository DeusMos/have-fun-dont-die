## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity clinics sell NAD IV and oral NR/NMN as “DNA repair” (Peach IV NAD protocol page). Peach IV sells the infusions. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

🤔 Clinic English uses “DNA repair” for NAD (Peach IV; DoNotAge). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

📚 NAD is a substrate for sirtuins, PARPs, and CD38; a blood NAD metabolome is not compartmental sirtuin activity and not OXPHOS capacity (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

⛔ Collapsing blood NAD into one “mitochondrial age” is a vocabulary collision (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018; TruDiagnostic TruHealth). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Oral NR 500 mg BID × 6 weeks raised blood NAD+ ~60% in 24 lean adults 55–79 (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). Substrate move ≠ genome or mito restoration. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md) and [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 NAD precursors raise blood NAD and are generally tolerated; functional benefits are unclear; Dollerup’s clamp was null; Elhassan did not raise muscle NAD+ or change OXPHOS (Martens 2018; Freeberg 2023; Dollerup 2018; Elhassan 2019). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Longevity IV clinics advertise NAD+ as cellular repair: Peach IV’s 2026 dosage guide lists IV NAD+ 100–1000 mg/session over 2–4 h (beginners 100–200 mg), loading ~weekly × 6 weeks then 500–1000 mg weekly/biweekly/monthly, and SubQ NAD ~50–100 mg/injection (Peach IV, https://www.peachiv.com/blog-post/nad-dosage-frequency-guide; Peach IV sells the infusions). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 AgelessRx sells compounded NAD+ nasal spray, 300 mg/mL in 14 mL, $125/month, 1–2× daily; copy is brain NAD / energy / DNA repair (AgelessRx, https://agelessrx.com/nad-nasal-spray/). AgelessRx sells the prescription. Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🤔 NCT07475546 90-day pilot titrates NAD nasal 30 mg/day with LDR, metformin, LDN, and a GSH patch (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07475546 protocol ALRx011v3). AgelessRx sells those agents. Extracted from [06](../../hallmarks/06-deregulated-nutrient-sensing/report.md).

## Fights

🤼 Clinic and protocol blogs split NAD IV vs daily oral NR/NMN vs nasal; oral has the RCTs (Martens/Dollerup/Elhassan); IV/nasal are cash-pay PK slogans without an aging-outcomes trial on those pages (Peach IV; AgelessRx; DoNotAge; Martens 2018; Dollerup 2018; Elhassan 2019). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🤼 MitoQ vs CoQ10 vs NAD as “the” mito pill is a brand fight, not a paper fight (Timeline; MitoQ; Elysium; Peach IV). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).
