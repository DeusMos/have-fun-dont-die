## Identity

Home hallmark: [01 genomic instability](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md). Aliases: NAD+, nadide, NAD IV, NAD nasal. Adjacent precursor pages: [nicotinamide-riboside](../nicotinamide-riboside/report.md), [nicotinamide-mononucleotide](../nicotinamide-mononucleotide/report.md). This page is the dinucleotide IV / nasal / RUO SKU, not a second NR or NMN dossier.

💯 NAD+ is the oxidized nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide coenzyme; it is a substrate for sirtuins, PARPs, and CD38 (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018).

📚 PubChem name `nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide` / `NAD` resolves to CID 5892, titled Nadide, formula C21H27N7O14P2, MW 663.4; the CID RN dump includes 53-84-9 plus 200-184-4, 4090-29-3, 477981-33-2, and 64417-72-7 (PubChem PUG name/CID/RN fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/5892).

📚 CID 925 is titled Endopride, same formula C21H27N7O14P2 / MW 663.4; PubChem synonyms on that record include beta-NAD and nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (PubChem PUG CID 925, fetched 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/925). That is a duplicate drawing, not a different molecule.

🐉 Extra RNs 4090-29-3, 477981-33-2, and 64417-72-7 sit on CID 5892; this flight did not independently title those numbers. A UNII was not returned by PUG xrefs this round (PubChem RN dump, 2026-09-04).

🤔 Mindful Research lists CAS 53-84-9, PubChem CID 925, formula C21H27N7O14P2, MW 663.43 g/mol, sold as NAD+ (Shopify product JSON, https://happypeptides.com/products/nad.json; catalog pull September 3–4, 2026). The CID is the Endopride-titled duplicate of the same dinucleotide, not a wrong-molecule collision.

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity clinics sell NAD IV and oral NR/NMN as “DNA repair” (Peach IV NAD protocol page). Peach IV sells the infusions. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

🤔 Clinic English uses “DNA repair” for NAD (Peach IV; DoNotAge). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md).

📚 Nearby true facts include NAD as a PARP/sirtuin substrate; the slogan launders those into a drip or nasal (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Peach IV; AgelessRx).

⛔ Collapsing blood NAD into one “mitochondrial age” is a vocabulary collision (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018; TruDiagnostic TruHealth). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

⛔ Treating a 500 mg RUO vial as a clinic NAD drip or an FDA aging drug collapses a research-chem SKU into a compounded infusion (Mindful JSON; Peach IV).

## Mechanism

📚 NAD is a substrate for sirtuins, PARPs, and CD38; a blood NAD metabolome is not compartmental sirtuin activity and not OXPHOS capacity (López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023; Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Grant’s IV metabolite profile is consistent with NAD+ glycohydrolase (CD38-class) cleavage to nicotinamide + ADPR and with extracellular pyrophosphatase activity raising NMN (Grant et al., Front. Aging Neurosci., 2019, https://doi.org/10.3389/fnagi.2019.00257, PMID 31572171). That is acute plasma chemistry, not tissue restoration.

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Grant 2019: 750 mg NAD+ IV over 6 h (~2 mg/min ≡ 3 µmol/min) in 8 healthy men vs 3 saline; plasma NAD+ and metabolites were unchanged for the first 2 h, then plasma NAD+ +398% at 6 h vs baseline (also NAM +409%, ADPR +393%); NAD+ still elevated at 8 h (2 h post-infusion); urinary NAD+ and methylnicotinamide rose at 6 h (Grant et al., 2019). Compounded Archway Apothecary product. Not a gray-market vial table.

🐉 Intranasal and oral NAD+ bioavailability are not compiled on this page; oral RCT file on this page is precursor NR, not the dinucleotide (Martens 2018; AgelessRx).

## Animal data

📚 Raising NAD in *C. elegans* via PARP loss/inhibitors or NR/NAM extended mean lifespan 15–29% in a sir-2.1- and UPRmt-dependent manner (Mouchiroud et al., Cell, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cell.2013.06.016, PMID 23870130). That is worm NAD→mito-stress signaling, not human blood-NAD restoration. Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

🐉 No compiled mammalian NAD+ IV lifespan study was fetched this round (inference from the human-IV / precursor file).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Oral NR 500 mg BID × 6 weeks raised blood NAD+ ~60% in 24 lean adults 55–79 (Martens et al., Nature Communications, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03421-7, PMID 29599478; NIAGEN supplied by ChromaDex). Substrate move ≠ genome or mito restoration. Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md) and [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Oral NR 2,000 mg/day × 12 weeks did not improve clamp insulin sensitivity, endogenous glucose production, glucose disposal, REE, lipolysis, lipid oxidation, or body composition in 40 obese insulin-resistant men, and the trial did not measure mutation burden (Dollerup et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1093/ajcn/nqy132, PMID 29992272, NCT02303483). Extracted from [01](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md) and [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 NR 1 g/day × 21 days in 12 men (median 75 y) raised muscle NAAD and nicotinamide clearance products but did not raise muscle NAD+ and did not change complex I/II OXPHOS, max respiratory capacity, citrate synthase, mtDNA copy number, or grip (Elhassan et al., Cell Reports, 2019, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.celrep.2019.07.043, PMID 31412242; ChromaDex supplied Niagen). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Freeberg 2023: oral NAD precursors are generally tolerated and raise NAD-related metabolites; “the clinical evidence that raising NAD+ concentrations can improve physiological function is unclear” (Freeberg et al., Journals of Gerontology Series A, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1093/gerona/glad106, PMC10692436). Extracted from [07](../../hallmarks/07-mitochondrial-dysfunction/report.md).

📚 Grant 2019 is a metabolome / safety pilot, not an aging-outcomes trial (Grant et al., 2019).

🐉 No compiled NAD+ IV or nasal RCT with a healthspan, mutation-burden, or OXPHOS primary was found this round (Grant 2019; NCT07475546).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 There is no US labeled NAD+ IV product. Grant observed no adverse events during the 6 h 750 mg infusion; GGT / LD / AST fell and bilirubin rose +2.75 µmol/L at 8 h, none judged clinically significant (Grant et al., 2019). n=8 on drug.

🤔 Clinic copy treats flushing, chest tightness, nausea, and vein burn as rate-dependent drip effects that slow when the infusion is lengthened (Peach IV dosage guide; cash-pay IV pages). That is practice, not a labeled AE table.

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market NAD+ vials in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Stay-away is not issued.

## What is actually on the table

📚 NAD+ is a real coenzyme. CAS 53-84-9 sits on CID 5892 (Nadide). Oral NR raises blood NAD and misses muscle NAD / OXPHOS / clamp endpoints. Grant’s 750 mg / 6 h IV delayed the plasma rise 2 h, then +398% with cleavage metabolites; n=8, no AEs in that protocol. No US aging label (Martens 2018; Dollerup 2018; Elhassan 2019; Grant 2019).

⛔ A drip or nasal is not genome or mito restoration, and a Mindful vial is not the clinic infusion (Peach IV; AgelessRx; Mindful JSON).

🤔 People can buy 100–1000 mg IV sessions, 30 mg nasal, and a 500 mg RUO vial today. That is practice, not a healthspan result.
