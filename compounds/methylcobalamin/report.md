## Identity

Home hallmark: [03 epigenetic alterations](../../hallmarks/03-epigenetic-alterations/report.md). One-carbon / homocysteine slogans also touch [01 genomic instability](../../hallmarks/01-genomic-instability/report.md). Aliases: mecobalamin, methyl-B12, Methycobal. This page is Co-methylcobalamin, not cyanocobalamin and not adenosylcobalamin.

💯 Methylcobalamin is the Me–Co form of cobalamin; it is the cofactor for methionine synthase (homocysteine → methionine).

📚 PubChem CAS 13422-55-4 maps a cluster of CIDs with formula C63H91CoN13O14P, MW 1344.4; CID 10898559 carries synonyms methylcobalamin, mecobalamin, and CAS 13422-55-4 (PubChem PUG CAS/name fetch 2026-09-04, https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/10898559).

📚 FDA SRS preferred name for UNII BR1SN1JS2W is METHYLCOBALAMIN / mecobalamin and maps CAS 13422-55-4 (FDA SRS, https://precision.fda.gov/uniisearch/srs/unii/br1sn1js2w). UNII availability does not imply a US NDA for the RUO ampoule.

## Mechanism

💯 MeCbl donates the methyl in methionine synthase; AdoCbl is the mutase cofactor. They are not interchangeable at the enzyme.

📚 Sawangjit et al. treat mecobalamin as the Me–Co analogue used in neuropathy trials, distinct from cyanocobalamin (Sawangjit, Thongphui, Chaichompu, and Phumart, *J. Altern. Complement. Med.*, 2020;26:1117–1129, https://doi.org/10.1089/acm.2020.0068).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Sawangjit et al.: systematic review / meta-analysis of 15 RCTs, N=1,707 peripheral neuropathy (mostly diabetic). Mecobalamin monotherapy vs active control: clinical therapeutic efficacy RR 1.17 (95% CI 1.03–1.33). Combination regimens RR 1.32 (95% CI 1.21–1.45). Nerve-conduction-velocity improvements were statistically significant only in combination trials; pain scores mixed (Sawangjit et al., 2020, https://doi.org/10.1089/acm.2020.0068).

⛔ A neuropathy “clinical efficacy” RR is not a human aging RCT.

🐉 No compiled healthy-aging or lifespan trial of methylcobalamin is on this page. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.
