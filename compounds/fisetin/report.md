## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Longevity brands sell Qualia Senolytic 1400 mg fisetin × 2 days / 28 off, DoNotAge 800 mg fisetin daily, and Life Extension 56 mg weekly “Senolytic Activator” as a zombie-cell flush (Qualia Life, https://www.qualialife.com/shop/qualia-senolytic; DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin; Life Extension, https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item02301/senolytic-activator). Those companies sell the capsules. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

⛔ Treating a Mayo NCT milligram or a Qualia page as proof that ordinary human senescent-cell burden can be scored and flushed so people live longer is a product page doing a job it was not written for (Qualia Life; DoNotAge; López-Otín et al., Cell, 2023). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

🤔 Clinic English uses “senolytic” for a 100–500 mg daily fisetin capsule or a 1400 mg Qualia burst (Qualia Life; DoNotAge; Life Extension). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 Among 10 flavonoids, fisetin was the strongest senolytic on oxidatively senescent Ercc1−/− MEFs and etoposide-senescent IMR90; 500 ppm dietary fisetin from 85 weeks extended median and maximum lifespan in WT C57BL/6:FVB mice (n=8–9/group, log-rank); 20 μM fisetin for 48 h on human omental explants (n=3, ages 55–66) reduced SA-β-gal+ cells and secreted IL-6, IL-8, and MCP-1 (Yousefzadeh et al., EBioMedicine, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ebiom.2018.09.015, PMID 30279143; exact % median extension is figure-only). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

📚 In the NIA Interventions Testing Program, fisetin at 600 ppm from 20 months in UM-HET3 mice did not extend lifespan on either continuous or 3-days-on / 11-days-off schedules: males Fis_Cyc n=150 median +7% log-rank p=0.54, Fis_On n=142 median −5% p=0.85; females Fis_Cyc n=134 −1% p=0.75, Fis_On n=144 +1% p=0.37; p16 mRNA in kidney/brain/liver was not reduced after 2–4 months on either diet (Harrison et al., GeroScience, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1007/s11357-023-01011-0, PMID 38041783; ~286–292 controls per sex, 3 sites). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

🤔 AFFIRM-LITE (NCT03675724), a Mayo Phase 2 randomized placebo-controlled fisetin frailty trial (20 mg/kg/day × 2 days; estimated n=40, age ≥70), started 2018-11-15 and as of the 2026 ClinicalTrials.gov record is still ENROLLING_BY_INVITATION with primary completion 2026-11 and no posted results. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

📚 Unformulated oral fisetin has poor human bioavailability: 1000 mg UF gave Cmax 9.97 ng/mL and AUC0–12h 12.67 ng·h/mL, was unquantifiable below 1000 mg, and was only measurable in plasma for ~2 h; geraldol Cmax/AUC exceeded parent (Krishnakumar et al., Journal of Nutritional Science, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1017/jns.2022.72, PMID 36265100; N=15 healthy crossover; Akay supplied the formulated product). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

⛔ Qualia Life Sciences sells Qualia Senolytic (1400 mg fisetin + stack, 2 days / month) while the product page treats Mayo-style fisetin pulses as the human senolytic result; their own open-label proteomics pilot (NCT06953518; Qualia-funded) found no significant change in pre-specified primary SASP proteins TNF, IL-1β, CXCL8, VEGFA (p>0.38 after correction; n=53 paired of 71 enrolled) (Blomquist et al., International Journal of Molecular Sciences, 2026, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms27104406; Qualia sells the capsule and funded the paper). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

⛔ The same Qualia-funded SASP-null pilot is reused in hallmark 11 (Blomquist et al., IJMS, 2026; NCT06953518). Extracted from [11](../../hallmarks/11-chronic-inflammation/report.md).

⛔ DoNotAge sells 400 mg fisetin capsules with a recommended daily 400–800 mg serving and copy that fisetin is “shown to support the selective removal of senescent cells”; that daily OTC milligram is not the Mayo 20 mg/kg × 2-day pulse and is not a completed human senolysis trial (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Qualia Senolytic’s product page lists 1400 mg fisetin + 750 mg Quercefit + a stack per 6-capsule serving, instructed as six capsules on two consecutive days then wait about four weeks; formulator pages cite Mayo NCT 20 mg/kg × 2 days as the 70 kg / 1400 mg alignment (Qualia Life). Qualia sells the capsule. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 DoNotAge Pure Fisetin is 400 mg fisetin per capsule, recommended daily serving 800 mg (2 capsules) with fat (DoNotAge, https://donotage.org/pure-fisetin). Daily 800 mg ≠ Mayo 20 mg/kg pulse. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 Life Extension Senolytic Activator is 56 mg Bio-Fisetin + 25 mg Bio-Quercetin + 275 mg theaflavins + 50 mg apigenin, three capsules once weekly (Life Extension, https://www.lifeextension.com/vitamins-supplements/item02301/senolytic-activator). Weekly 56 mg fisetin is two orders of magnitude below a 20 mg/kg pulse. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-clinics-and-self-experimenters-are-doing).

🤔 Mayo fisetin trial pulses on NCT03675724 are 20 mg/kg × 2 days; no posted results. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md).

## What is actually on the table

📚 ITP fisetin did not extend UM-HET3 lifespan; Mayo AFFIRM-LITE remains unpublished since 2018; unformulated oral fisetin barely appears in plasma (Harrison 2024; NCT03675724; Krishnakumar 2022). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).

⛔ A Qualia burst, a daily DoNotAge capsule, or a 56 mg weekly “activator” is not organismal senescent-cell restoration in healthy people (Qualia Life; DoNotAge; Life Extension; López-Otín 2023). Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).

🤔 People are running Qualia 1400 mg × 2 days, DoNotAge 800 mg/day, and Life Extension 56 mg weekly under that slogan anyway. Extracted from [08](../../hallmarks/08-cellular-senescence/report.md#what-is-actually-on-the-table).
