## The claim and the slogan

🤔 RUO copy sells an 11-residue helix-B EPO peptide as a selective innate-repair-receptor (IRR) agonist for inflammation and neuropathy lab work, without erythropoiesis (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/ara-290). Mindful sells the SKU. Araim Pharmaceuticals ran the Phase 2 file (Culver et al., *IOVS*, 2017; Brines et al., *Mol. Med.*, 2014).

⛔ Treating 28-day sarcoid or diabetic small-fiber trials — corneal-nerve area, SFNSL, PainDetect, HbA1c — as a completed aging or tissue-restore result is a definition swap. Those papers measured neuropathy biomarkers and symptoms in diseased adults, not healthspan (Dahan et al., 2013; Culver et al., 2017; Brines et al., 2014).

⛔ A 10 mg research-chemical listing is not Araim’s Bachem trial vial and is not a human aging RCT (https://happypeptides.com/; catalog pull September 3, 2026).

## Mechanism

📚 Brines et al. engineered non-erythropoietic peptides from the tertiary structure of EPO helix B; pyroglutamate helix B surface peptide (pHBSP / ARA-290) is the 11-mer that engages tissue-protective signaling without the EPOR homodimer erythropoietic program (Brines et al., *PNAS*, 2008;105:10925–10930, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.0805594105).

📚 The tissue-protective receptor is framed as an EPOR / β-common (CD131) heterocomplex — the innate repair receptor — induced by injury or inflammation rather than constitutively expressed on healthy tissue (Brines and Cerami, *Mol. Med.*, 2012;18:486–496, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2011.00414).

📚 Culver et al. add that cibinetide can antagonize TRPV1 on small fibers (Culver et al., *Invest. Ophthalmol. Vis. Sci.*, 2017;58:BIO52–BIO60, https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-21291, PMID 28475703).

🐉 Storefront “cellular repair mechanisms” copy is the IRR slogan, not a compiled healthy-aging pathway. no source; inference from the storefront vs Brines/Cerami file.

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 Dahan et al. crossover in 10 healthy volunteers: median plasma AUC above the ~1.3 ng/mL IRR threshold was 65 (2 mg IV), 23 (2 mg SC), 59 (4 mg SC), and 249 ng/mL·min (6 mg SC); 4 mg SC was chosen to match the prior 2 mg IV exposure (Dahan et al., *Mol. Med.*, 2013;19:334–345, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2013.00122, PMC3883966).

📚 Brines 2014: after 4 mg SC, peak plasma ~3 ng/mL (~2.4 nM); terminal t½ ~20 min. Preclinical PD cited benefit above ~1 nM (Brines et al., *Mol. Med.*, 2014;20:658–666, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2014.00215, PMC4365069).

📚 Brines and Cerami: pHBSP plasma t½ is ~2 min in the review framing, yet a single parenteral dose still moved injury models — a short-ligand / long-program claim, not a compiled RUO-vial PK table (Brines and Cerami, 2012, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2011.00414).

🐉 A compiled human PK table for the Mindful 10 mg powder is not on this page. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-PK file.

## Animal data

📚 Brines 2014 lists the preclinical pile the authors treat as background: diet-induced insulin resistance, diabetic retinopathy, diabetic autonomic neuropathy, MI, chronic heart failure, burns, TBI, and shock-induced multi-organ failure — prevention of injury, less inflammation, healing, without rhEPO hematocrit (Brines et al., 2014, citing that stack; PMC4365069).

📚 Culver et al. cite a mouse diabetic small-fiber model in which daily cibinetide reversed neuronal dystrophy (Culver et al., 2017, https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-21291).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Heij et al.: double-blind pilot, sarcoidosis + SFN symptoms; 2 mg IV Monday/Wednesday/Friday for 4 weeks; 12 ARA-290 vs 10 placebo completed. SFNSL 41.0 ± 4.6 → 29.8 ± 3.5 vs 30.6 ± 4.2 → 26.2 ± 4.0 (p < 0.05). BPI pain fell in both arms (NS between groups). SF-36 pain and physical-functioning improved from baseline in the ARA-290 arm (p < 0.01). Hemoglobin mmol/L 8.9 ± 0.21 → 8.7 ± 0.19 vs 8.6 ± 0.26 → 8.7 ± 0.34. No skin-biopsy confirmation of SFN (Heij et al., *Mol. Med.*, 2012;18:1430–1436, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2012.00332, PMC3563705; NTR3081 / EudraCT 2010-021518-45).

📚 Dahan et al.: investigator-initiated, single-site, double-blind; 38 sarcoidosis patients (18 F / 20 M, mean age 49.5); 4 mg SC daily × 28 days vs placebo; NTR3575 / EudraCT 2012-001492-37. Median corneal nerve-fiber area +14.5% / +185 µm² (p = 0.022) vs placebo −5.3% / −64 µm² (p = 0.462). Distal-leg IENFD +0.38 ± 0.48 vs −0.06 ± 0.42 fibers/mm (both ns). SFNSL at week 5: −12.2 ± 1.9 vs −3.8 ± 2.1 (p = 0.005). BPI pain intensity fell ~9% in both arms (no between-group difference). BPI pain interference 32.1 → 20.6 vs 36.5 → 30.8. 6MWT +18.7 m vs −15.1 m (p = 0.049). No anti-ARA-290 antibodies. No SAE in the dosing window (Dahan et al., 2013, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2013.00122, PMC3883966).

📚 Culver et al. Phase 2b: 64 sarcoid SNFL + neuropathic pain; 1 / 4 / 8 mg SC daily × 28 days vs placebo; NCT02039687 / EudraCT 2013-003016-45; Leiden + Cleveland Clinic. Primary CNFA day 28, placebo-corrected LS-mean difference: 109 (1 mg; 95% CI −429 to 647), **697 (4 mg; 159 to 1236; p = 0.012)**, 431 (8 mg; −130 to 992). GAP-43+ intraepidermal length up in the 4 mg arm (p = 0.035); CNFA correlated with GAP-43+ (ρ = 0.575, p = 0.025) and 6MWT (ρ = 0.645, p = 0.009). IENFD did not separate from placebo. Pain instruments moved in all arms including placebo; moderate-to-severe BPI ≥5 placebo-corrected 4 mg difference −1.01 (p = 0.157). CNFA trended back toward baseline after stop (Culver et al., 2017, https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-21291, PMID 28475703).

📚 Brines et al. T2D + painful neuropathy: 4 mg SC daily × 28 days, then 28 days off; 49 enrolled, 48 analyzed (24/24); NTR3858. HbA1c (n = 42 complete): ARA-290 −0.16% at d28 and −0.21% at d56 vs placebo −0.01% and +0.21% (p = 0.002 RM-ANOVA). Cholesterol/HDL and triglycerides improved (p = 0.039 and 0.043). PainDetect −3.3 vs −1.1 points at d28, −4.2 vs −0.74 at d56 (p = 0.037). NPSI fell ~20% in **both** arms at d28; SFNSL did not move. Whole-cohort CNFD +1.1 ± 1.1 vs +0.1 ± 1.2 (ns); post-hoc CNFD >1 SD below normal: +2.6 ± 1.0 (n = 18, p = 0.02) vs +0.7 ± 1.3 (n = 19, ns). QST unchanged. Bachem 4 mg vials in phosphate / 1% sucrose / 4% D-mannitol (Brines et al., 2014, https://doi.org/10.2119/molmed.2014.00215, PMC4365069). Araim authors on the paper.

⛔ A 28-day corneal-nerve or HbA1c move in sarcoid or T2D is not a human aging RCT and not organismal restore.

🐉 No compiled healthy-aging or lifespan trial of cibinetide is on this page. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Heij 2012: no infusion AEs; chemistry/hematology including hemoglobin unchanged (Heij et al., 2012, PMC3563705).

📚 Dahan 2013: no SAE in dosing or 12-week follow-up; no injection-site pain; no anti-drug antibodies. One ARA-290 moderate AE of 14 kg weight loss that had started before enrollment (Dahan et al., 2013, PMC3883966).

📚 Brines 2014: AE tallies similar across arms. Four SAEs in the ARA-290 arm; two judged possibly related — one creatinine rise 119 → 159 µmol/L after a furosemide increase (dosing stopped; creatinine did not recover while furosemide continued), and a fatal MI 2 weeks after last dose in a 70-year-old with lower-extremity cellulitis, judged **unrelated** by the safety committee (Brines et al., 2014, PMC4365069).

📚 Culver 2017: most frequent AEs injection-site pain, diarrhea, fatigue, headache, nausea; lowest incidence in the 4 mg arm. SAEs: syncope/headache/enteritis in 1 mg (unrelated); suicidal ideation in 8 mg (possibly related) (Culver et al., 2017, https://doi.org/10.1167/iovs.16-21291).

🐉 A compiled RUO-vial harm series is not on this page. Trial AEs are not a ☠︎︎ “sure, meaningful harm” file for the powder. no source; inference from the missing-RUO-tox file.

## Fights
