## Identity

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Peptide-clinic and gray-market copy sells a 10–50 mg RUO SS-31 vial, or “the FDA-approved mito drug,” as mitochondrial aging / ATP / fatigue restore in ordinary adults (Mindful Research, https://happypeptides.com/products/ss-31; Telehealth Ally / Defy-style lists already named in hallmark 07). Those pages sell or refer the peptide.

⛔ Treating Forzinity’s Barth accelerated approval as proof that ordinary mitochondrial aging can be restored is a rare-disease label doing a job it was not written for (FDA, 19 Sep 2025, https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome).

⛔ Treating Roshanravan 2021 as a healthspan result ignores the one-dose design, the low-ATP-max selection, the day-7 washout, and the non-significant fatigue-resistance endpoint (Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253849).

⛔ Filing MMPOWER-3 as a yes-restore Phase 3 ignores the paper’s own coprimary misses (Karaa et al., Neurology, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000207402, PMID 37268435).

## Mechanism

📚 The Forzinity label states elamipretide is a mitochondrial cardiolipin binder (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 11).

📚 FDA’s approval announcement says Forzinity works by binding to the inner part of the mitochondria, improving mitochondrial structure and function — Barth-syndrome labeling language, not an aging mechanism paper (FDA, 19 Sep 2025).

## Pharmacokinetics / pharmacodynamics

📚 DailyMed: elamipretide exposure increases proportionally over 2–80 mg daily SC with minimal accumulation; parent plus metabolites M1 and M2 are recovered in urine by 48 hours in normal renal function; no clinically significant QTc prolongation at 3× peak of the 40 mg dose (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 12). That is labeled-product PK, not a gray-market vial table.

📚 Dose is reduced by half in adults with eGFR <30 mL/min not on dialysis; not studied on dialysis (DailyMed FORZINITY, sections 2.2 and 8.6).

## Animal data

📚 DailyMed reproduction section: no embryo-fetal developmental toxicity in rat IV up to 10 mg/kg/day (~4× MRHD AUC) or rabbit IV up to 50 mg/kg/day (~10×); rat SC fertility up to 20 mg/kg/day (~5×) unaffected (DailyMed FORZINITY, sections 8.1 and 13.1). Label tox, not an aging lifespan study.

🐉 The broader Szeto/Stealth ischemia / kidney / heart animal file was not re-fetched as a new pile this round; it is mechanism/injury, not human aging (inference from hallmark 07 framing).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Roshanravan 2021: 39 older adults (60–85 y) selected for low FDI ATP max (<0.7 mM/s) and P/O <1.9; a single 2-hour elamipretide infusion raised ATP max (~27% vs ~12% placebo; %Δ P=0.045) immediately; the difference was gone at day 7; infusion-day fatigue resistance was not significant (Roshanravan et al., PLoS One, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0253849, PMC8282018). Restore from hallmark 07.

📚 MMPOWER-3: elamipretide 40 mg SC daily × 24 weeks in genetically confirmed primary mitochondrial myopathy did not meet coprimary 6MWT (LS mean Δ −3.2 m, 95% CI −18.7 to 12.3, P=0.69) or PMMSA total fatigue (P=0.37); Class I evidence of no 6MWT/fatigue benefit vs placebo (Karaa et al., Neurology, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1212/WNL.0000000000207402, PMID 37268435, NCT03323749; Stealth-funded). Restore from hallmark 07.

📚 FDA granted accelerated approval to Forzinity (elamipretide) injection on 19 September 2025 for Barth syndrome in patients weighing at least 30 kg, based on improved knee-extensor strength, with a required confirmatory RCT; daily SubQ (FDA, 2025, https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-accelerated-approval-first-treatment-barth-syndrome). Restore from hallmark 07.

📚 The Barth randomized crossover (n=12, 40 mg SC daily × 12 weeks) was not superior to placebo on the trial’s primary 6MWT or Barth Symptom Assessment Total Fatigue; knee-extensor strength increases were not seen in the randomized period and were described in the open-label extension (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 14). That is the labeled Barth file, not aging.

🐉 No compiled healthy-person aging or healthspan RCT for SS-31 / elamipretide was found this round beyond the one-dose Roshanravan infusion (hallmark 07; DailyMed).

## Toxicity and hazards

📚 Most common labeled adverse reactions are injection-site reactions: in the Barth placebo-controlled crossover, any local reaction 12/12 elamipretide vs 8/12 placebo; erythema 100% vs 25%; pain 75% vs 42%; induration and pruritus 67% vs 17% (DailyMed FORZINITY, Table 2).

📚 Contraindication: serious hypersensitivity to elamipretide or any excipient; monitor and do not rechallenge after a serious reaction (DailyMed FORZINITY, sections 4 and 5.2). FDA’s press note also says serious reactions have been reported (FDA, 19 Sep 2025).

📚 FORZINITY contains benzyl alcohol 20 mg/mL and is not approved for neonates or for IV use; the label reprints the gasping-syndrome warning for BA-containing IV drugs in low-birth-weight and preterm neonates (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 5.1). That is a labeled neonate/IV-BA warning, not a completed adult-RUO harm series.

📚 Eosinophil counts rose with ≥30 days of exposure, peaked around day 90, and returned toward baseline by ~12 months or after discontinuation, without reported clinical manifestations in that section (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 6.1).

🐉 No published case series establishing sure, meaningful harm from gray-market SS-31 in ordinary adults was compiled this round. Labeled Barth ISR and hypersensitivity are not ☠︎︎ for the RUO vial. Stay-away is not issued.

## Interactions

📚 DailyMed: elamipretide does not inhibit CYP1A, 2D6, 2E1, 2B6, 2C8, 2C9, 2C19, or 3A and does not induce 1A2, 2B6, or 3A4; it does not inhibit OCT2, BCRP, OAT1, OAT3, OATP1B1, OATP1B3, P-gp, or MATE2-K; it is a MATE1 inhibitor (IC50 3.53 µM) (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 12.3).

📚 Aspirin 650 mg and clopidogrel 300 mg single-dose interaction studies with IV elamipretide did not show a PK or antiplatelet/antithrombotic activity hit in the labeled experiments (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 12.3).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

📚 Labeled Barth dose is 40 mg SC once daily for patients ≥30 kg; 20 mg SC daily if adult eGFR <30 mL/min not on dialysis (DailyMed FORZINITY, section 2). That is the approved indication, not an aging protocol.

🤔 Off-label SS-31 chatter exists on the same peptide-telehealth lists that sell MOTS-c (Telehealth Ally Defy review listing SS-31; extracted from hallmark 07).

## Fights

🤼 Prescription Forzinity 40 mg/day for Barth versus gray-market 10–50 mg vials sold as mito-aging restore is an amateur/clinic split, not a paper fight (FDA 2025; DailyMed; Mindful JSON; Telehealth Ally).

📚 MMPOWER-3 (PMM 6MWT/fatigue miss) and Forzinity (Barth accelerated approval on a strength surrogate after a randomized 6MWT/fatigue miss) are different indications and are not a same-endpoint literature fight (Karaa 2023; DailyMed section 14).

## What is actually on the table

⛔ That file is not organismal mitochondrial restore in healthy people, and a Mindful vial is not Forzinity (DailyMed; Mindful JSON).

🤔 People can buy 10/50 mg RUO vials and 40 mg/day labeled Barth injections today. That is practice plus a rare-disease label, not a healthspan result.
