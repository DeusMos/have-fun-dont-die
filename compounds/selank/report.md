## Identity

## Mechanism

📚 Volkova et al.: male rat frontal cortex, 84 neurotransmission genes by qPCR, 1 h and 3 h after 300 µg/kg Selank or GABA. 45 genes moved at 1 h; 22 at 3 h. Selank and GABA expression changes correlated at 1 h. Authors read that as a possible allosteric GABAergic modulation, not as a proven receptor occupancy study (Volkova, Shadrina, Kolomin, Andreeva, Limborska, Myasoedov, and Slominsky, *Front. Pharmacol.*, 2016;7:31, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphar.2016.00031).

📚 Zozulia 2008: patients with GAD and neurasthenia had a low serum τ½ of leu-enkephalin that tracked disease duration and anxiety / asthenia severity; that parameter rose on Selank mainly in the GAD subset (Zozulia et al., 2008, PMID 18454096).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Zozulia et al.: N=62 GAD and neurasthenia; 30 Selank vs 32 medazepam; Hamilton, Zung, CGI. Anxiolytic effects were similar; Selank also had antiasthenic and psychostimulant effects on the authors’ reading. Exact HAM-A deltas are not in the English abstract this flight used (Zozulia et al., 2008;108(4):38–48, PMID 18454096). PubMed tags the paper a randomized comparative study.

🐉 No Western-standard human aging RCT of Selank is compiled on this page. A Russian GAD / neurasthenia comparator is not a healthspan result. no source; inference from the empty aging-RCT file.
