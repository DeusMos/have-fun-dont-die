## Identity

Home hallmark: [14 psychosocial isolation](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md). Aliases in the extracted reports: IN OT, Oxipops, Pitocin.

💯 Oxytocin is a nine-amino-acid hypothalamic nonapeptide released from the posterior pituitary. The FDA-approved product is injectable obstetric oxytocin (Pitocin class), not a nasal spray or troche (DailyMed Pitocin label; Superpower 2026; Bayview 2026). Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

**CAS:** 50-56-6 (📚 PubChem Oxytocin CID page; CAS 50-56-6. https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/compound/Oxytocin).

## The claim and the slogan

🤔 Compounded oxytocin (“Oxipops”), IN spray, and troches are sold as isolation / social-bonding restore (Superpower; Mochi Health; Bayview). Those companies sell the consult or the compound. Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md#the-claim-and-the-slogan).

Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

## Animal data

📚 Four weeks of sibling isolation in female prairie voles raised resting heart rate, cut HR variability and vagal tone, reduced sucrose intake, and increased FST immobility; 14 days of systemic oxytocin (20 µg SC) prevented the autonomic and behavioral changes (Grippo, Trahanas, Zimmerman, Porges, and Carter, Psychoneuroendocrinology, 2009; N=26 autonomic + 31 behavioral). Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

📚 Plasma oxytocin and muscle-stem-cell oxytocin receptor fall with mouse age; oxytocin signaling through MAPK/ERK is required for satellite-cell activation in that system (Elabd et al., Nature Communications, 2014, PMID 24915299). Extracted from [10](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md).

📚 Systemic oxytocin restored aged-mouse muscle regeneration; Ot-null mice were not developmentally muscle-defective but showed premature sarcopenia at 12 months (Elabd et al., Nature Communications, 2014, PMID 24915299). Extracted from [10](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md).

## Human data — aging, off-label, and geroscience

📚 Five weekly group-therapy sessions reduced trait UCLA loneliness through 3-month follow-up (b=−1.90, d=−0.33; 3-month d=−0.41 vs T1); 24 IU IN oxytocin before sessions did not augment trait loneliness (PLC −3.63±6.57 vs OT −0.43±7.49), quality of life, or stress, but did cut in-session state loneliness (d=−0.34) and raise positive group bonding (Berger et al., Psychotherapy and Psychosomatics, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1159/000538752; N=78 high-lonely adults, UCLA ≥55; NCT04137432). Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

📚 SOARS-B 48 IU/day IN oxytocin for 24 weeks did not improve social functioning versus placebo in ASD (ABC-mSW LS-mean difference −0.2, −1.5 to 1.0; p=0.61) (Sikich et al., NEJM, 2021, https://doi.org/10.1056/NEJMoa2103583, PMID 34614324; N=277 modified ITT, ages 3–17). ASD social-functioning trial, not an isolation-aging trial; it still kills “OT restores social biology” as a general claim. Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

📚 A small intranasal-oxytocin sarcopenic-obesity pilot reported lean-mass gain, not a communication-restore or healthspan trial (Espinoza et al., 2021, PMC8567747). Extracted from [10](../../hallmarks/10-altered-intercellular-communication/report.md).

## Observed practice

Practice is not efficacy. Doses are observed, not instructions.

🤔 Superpower’s oxytocin guide offers compounded IN spray and sublingual “Oxipops,” states these are not FDA-approved for psychiatric/neurological uses, lists social-cognition / anxiety / PTSD / pain / sexual-function papers next to a biomarker panel, cites Sikich 2021 as a negative ASD trial, and still routes prescriptions through Superpower’s licensed-provider network (Superpower, https://superpower.com/guides/peptides/oxytocin). Superpower sells the consult. Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

🤔 Mochi Health pitches IN oxytocin for “social bonding, stress reduction, or emotional wellness,” states research doses 18–48 IU with 24 IU most common, and says compounded bottles are typically 10–30 mL at 50–500 IU total (Mochi Health, https://joinmochi.com/blog/oxytocin-nasal-spray-benefits-side-effects-how-it-works). Mochi sells the telehealth prescription and ships the vial. Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).

🤔 Bayview Pharmacy compounds metered IN oxytocin at 25, 40, 100, and 120 units/mL and a sublingual/oral troche at ~200 units; starting price listed $118 / 10 mL; refrigeration required for the spray; the page explicitly declines bonding/intimacy efficacy claims (Bayview Pharmacy, https://www.bayviewrx.com/formulations/oxytocin; updated 28 Aug 2026). Bayview sells the compound. Extracted from [14](../../hallmarks/14-psychosocial-isolation/report.md).
