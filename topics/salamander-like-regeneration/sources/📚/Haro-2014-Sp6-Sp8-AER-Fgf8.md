# Haro et al., 2014. Sp6 and Sp8 Transcription Factors Control AER Formation and Dorsal-Ventral Patterning in Limb Development. PLoS Genetics.
https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pgen.1004468
PMID 25166858
PMC4148220
Used for: Developmental support that Sp6/Sp8 function upstream of Fgf8 in limb ectoderm — FGF8 as an SP target — not adult digit regeneration or AAV therapy.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration:
- Newborn skeletal series from Sp6+/−;Sp8+/− intercrosses, 102 progeny.
- Sp6−/−;Sp8−/−: 3/102, 100% tetra-amelic, no elements distal to scapula; Fgf8 not activated.
- Sp6+/−;Sp8−/−: 10/102, always tetra-amelic.
- Sp6−/−;Sp8+/−: 11/102, SHFM/claw-like paws, bidorsal digit tips, occasional absent radius.
- qPCR E10.5 limb ectoderm: Sp8 ~3-fold (FL) and ~5-fold (HL) higher than Sp6.
- Pop=mouse embryos/neonates. Endpoint=skeletal prep, Fgf8/En1 in situ, ectoderm qPCR. Duration=embryogenesis to newborn.
Conflict if any: Authors declared none.
