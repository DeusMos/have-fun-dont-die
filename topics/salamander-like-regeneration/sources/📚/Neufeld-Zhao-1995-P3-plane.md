# Neufeld DA, Zhao W, 1995. Bone regrowth after digit tip amputation in mice is equivalent in adults and neonates. Wound Repair and Regeneration.
https://doi.org/10.1046/j.1524-475x.1995.30410.x
PMID 17147657
Used for: Amputation through the proximal 20% of mouse terminal phalanx (P3) precluded nail-plate regrowth and bone grew minimally or regressed; cuts in the distal 40% usually attained or surpassed original length by 5 weeks in adults and neonates.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Adult and neonatal mice; alizarin-red whole-mount bone length at intervals >5 weeks. Distal 40% of P3: grossly normal digits, bone usually ≥ original length. Intermediate 40%: bone correlated with nail, did not reach original. Proximal 20%: no nail plate, bone minimal or regress. Adults ≈ neonates.
Conflict if any: none stated.
