# Robey and Murry, 2008. Absence of regeneration in the MRL/MpJ mouse heart following infarction or cryoinjury. Cardiovasc Pathol.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.carpath.2007.01.005
PMID 18160055 PMC2766607
Used for: 🥼 Counter-pole to MRL organ-regeneration claims: no heart regeneration after infarct or cryoinjury.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 38 male MRL/MpJ and 35 male C57BL/6, 6–10 weeks. LAD infarct: 32 MRL / 28 C57 (survivors by timepoint in Table 1: day 1 n=5/5, day 4 n=7/5, day 7 n=4/5, day 14 n=7/5, day 28 n=4/4, day 90 n=5/4; sham n=4/4 at 90 d). Cryoinjury: 8 MRL / 9 C57; scored at 7 d (n=4/5) and 28 d (n=4/3). Endpoint=scar vs muscle, infarct % LV circumference, BrdU pulse, CD31/vessel density. Infarct size 38% vs 36% LV (p=NS); BrdU peak day 4 6.5±1.2% vs 8.5±2.2% (p=NS); cryoinjury scar volume 8.4±1.0 vs 7.7±1.3 mm³ at 1 week and 11±1.9 vs 12±1.2 mm³ at 4 weeks (p=0.66 / 0.57). Duration=infarct 1–90 d, cryoinjury to 28 d.
