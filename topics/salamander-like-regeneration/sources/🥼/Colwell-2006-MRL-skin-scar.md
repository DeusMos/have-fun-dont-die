# Colwell et al., 2006. Skin wounds in the MRL/MPJ mouse heal with scar. Wound Repair Regen.
https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1524-475X.2005.00092.x
PMID 16476076
Used for: 🥼 Counter-pole to MRL-as-epimorphic-regeneration: MRL/MpJ dorsal skin wounds heal with scar.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: n not reported in the PubMed/Wiley abstract (methods PDF not retrieved). 7-week MRL/MpJ vs C57BL/6 vs Balb/c; same animals also got ear punches. Full-thickness dorsal 5 mm incisional and 2 mm excisional wounds. Endpoint=reepithelialization, gross closure, collagen/fibrosis histology. Incisions reepithelialized by 48 h in all strains; excisions still open at 24/48/72 h; all dorsal wounds grossly healed by day 14; day-14 and day-28 dorsal sections showed dense collagen and similar fibrosis across strains. MRL/MpJ ears closed by day 28; C57BL/6 and Balb/c ears did not.
