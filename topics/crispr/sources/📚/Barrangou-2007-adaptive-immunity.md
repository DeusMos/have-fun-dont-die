# Barrangou et al., 2007. CRISPR provides acquired resistance against viruses in prokaryotes. Science.
https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1138140 PMID 17379808
Used for: 📚 Barrangou et al. showed that after phage challenge, S. thermophilus integrated new phage-derived spacers, and that adding or removing particular spacers changed the phage-resistance phenotype; CRISPR plus cas genes provided acquired resistance whose specificity tracked spacer–phage sequence match.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Phage-challenged S. thermophilus dairy isolates. Effect=spacer acquisition ↔ resistance; removal/addition of spacers flips phenotype. Endpoint=phage resistance.
Conflict if any: Several authors at Danisco/DuPont Nutrition (industrial starter-culture phage resistance).
