Used for: 📚 FDA approved Casgevy on 8 Dec 2023 as the first CRISPR/Cas9 medicine (autologous HSPC BCL11A erythroid-enhancer edit); 29/31 evaluable SCD subjects (93.5%) were VF12 responders.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 44 SCD treated; 31 VF12-evaluable; 29/31 (93.5%; one-sided 98% CI 77.9–100%) free of severe VOCs ≥12 months; 30/30 HF12 (100%) free of sVOC hospitalizations ≥12 months; all 31 had HbF ≥20% ≥12 months; mean blood allelic editing ≥70% from month 2; mean HbF 36.9% (SD 9.0) at month 3, ≥40% from month 6. Indication: SCD ≥12 y with recurrent VOCs. Min dose 3.0×10⁶ CD34+/kg. Edit=Cas9/SPY101 RNP at GATA1 site of BCL11A erythroid enhancer. Interim lock 14 Jun 2023.
Conflict if any: Applicant Vertex Pharmaceuticals; CRISPR Therapeutics partner. Lyfgenia (lentiviral, not CRISPR) approved the same day — not this claim.
