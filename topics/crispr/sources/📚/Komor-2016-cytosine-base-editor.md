# Komor et al., 2016. Programmable editing of a target base in genomic DNA without double-stranded DNA cleavage. Nature.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nature17946 PMID 27096365 PMC4873371
Used for: 📚 Komor et al. fused rat APOBEC1 to Cas9 nickase plus UGI (BE3) and converted C•G to T•A in a ~5-nt window without a classical DSB or donor, correcting ~15–75% of alleles in four human/murine cell lines with typically ≤1% indels.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Four transformed human and murine cell lines, ~3 days post-transfection. Effect=15–75% C→T, typically ≤1% indels. Endpoint=HTS of targeted amplicons. Liu lab; later Beam Therapeutics commercializes base editors.
Conflict if any: Liu lab / HHMI; base-editor IP later licensed (Beam sells BE platform).
