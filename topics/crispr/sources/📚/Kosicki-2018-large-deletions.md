# Kosicki, Tomberg, Bradley, 2018. Repair of double-strand breaks induced by CRISPR–Cas9 leads to large deletions and complex rearrangements. Nature Biotechnology.
https://doi.org/10.1038/nbt.4192 PMID 30010673 PMC6390938
Used for: On-target Cas9 repair frequently produces kilobase-scale deletions and complex rearrangements missed by short-amplicon genotyping.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: Mouse ES (PigA, Cd9), mouse BM progenitors (GFP), human RPE1 (PIGA). 10/48 (21%) PigA alleles from an exonic gRNA had deletions >250 bp up to 6 kb; 23/133 recovered alleles had non-contiguous lesions; 35/96 GFP− progenitor clones had 100 bp–~3 kb deletion PCR products. Largest PigA deletion 9.5 kb.
