# Pai et al., 2012. Transmembrane voltage potential controls embryonic eye patterning in Xenopus laevis. Development.
https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759 PMC3243095 PMID 22159581
Used for: 📚 Injecting dominant-negative Kir6.1 into all four blastomeres yielded ~20% ectopic eye tissue and 7.5% complete ectopic eyes (RPE + retina + lens) far outside the anterior neural field.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: n=5 batches for DN-KATP quantification; 7.5% complete ectopic eyes; ~20% any ectopic eye tissue; ~25% disrupted endogenous eyes; 46–48% malformed eyes after dorsal EXP1 or GlyR+IVM; *X. laevis*; stage-42 anatomy + IHC; inject 4-cell, score ~3 days later at tadpole stages.
Conflict if any: Levin lab; NIH/NEI funded.
Methods (required for this flight): model *X. laevis* in 0.1× MMR. Inject 3 nl/blastomere in 3% Ficoll. Read: CC2-DMPE 0.2 µM, 1.5 h, 405/460 nm; sharp electrodes 80–100 MΩ, 2 M K-acetate, ~10 mV hyperpolarization in dye-bright eye-field cells (n≥6).
