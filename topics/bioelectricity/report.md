## The claim and the slogan

The wet claim under test is that resting-Vmem patterns and gap-junction coupling in non-neural cells are instructive: a brief ion-channel, pump, or GJ write can change organ identity or polarity in frogs and planaria, and in the two-head case persist across later cuts in water.

🤔 The slogan in the wild adds a stored anatomical “software” layer, two-headed worms that “breed true” without a DNA change, eyes anywhere, tumor suppression despite oncogenes, adult-frog limb regeneration as a voltage-map rewrite, and (soon) aging reset / anatomical compiler / freedom of embodiment (Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025; Levin, BioEssays, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1002/bies.202400196).

💯 Resting membrane potential and ion channels exist in non-excitable cells; gap junctions couple neighbors electrically and by small-molecule flux (Adams and Levin, Cold Spring Harb Protoc, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1101/pdb.prot067702, PMID 22474652).

⛔ Existence of Vmem is not proof that voltage is the body’s software or the aging setpoint (Adams and Levin, 2012 vs Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025).

📚 Murugan 2022 BioDome is a 24 h silk bioreactor plus a five-drug gel on adult *Xenopus*, already filed under salamander-like regeneration; it is not a DiBAC/CC2 or Kir rewrite (Murugan et al., Sci Adv, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj2164, PMID 35080969; [topics/salamander-like-regeneration](../salamander-like-regeneration/report.md)).

🤔 Morphoceuticals (Levin + David Kaplan; Juvenescence / Prime Movers Lab $8M seed, 15 Feb 2023; CEO Jim Jenson from 18 Apr 2024) sells a bioelectrome / electroceutical company story from that frog (Morphoceuticals, 2023, https://www.morphoceuticals.com/21523; Morphoceuticals, 2024, https://www.morphoceuticals.com/4-18-24).

🤔 Astonishing Labs is the interview’s aging / anthrobot company; Fauna Systems is the xenobot / environmental-biobot company (Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025; Astonishing Labs, https://astonishinglabs.com/science/; Fauna Systems, https://www.faunasystems.com/).

🤼 PEMF / microcurrent / “bioelectric therapy” vendors cite Levin then sell a coil, pad, or VCF protocol — a different stimulus than the papers below (eMedica, https://emedica.in/; Digital Dharma, https://digitaldharma.io/hw/bioelectric-medicine-clinical-applications/).

📚 Levin is corresponding or last author on the core frog/planaria/aging papers on this page; Morphoceuticals sells electroceutical regeneration from the frog; Astonishing Labs funded the keratinocyte senescence paper via a sponsored research agreement (author lines on Pai 2012, Durant 2017, Chernet 2013, Murugan 2022, Pio-Lopez 2024, Sediqi 2025; Sediqi and Levin, iScience, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.113275, PMID 41054538).

## What the words mean

💯 Vmem is the steady voltage across a cell’s plasma membrane, set by ion gradients and channel/pump conductances (Adams and Levin, Cold Spring Harb Protoc, 2012, PMID 22474652).

📚 In this lab’s operational sense, “developmental bioelectricity” means spatiotemporal Vmem patterns in non-neural cells, read with voltage-sensitive dyes / reporters / electrodes and written by ion-channel mRNA, optogenetic actuators, GJ blockers, or channel/pump drugs (Levin, Annu Rev Biomed Eng, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-bioeng-071114-040647; Adams and Levin, 2012).

⛔ Cardiac, neuronal, or muscle action-potential electrophysiology is not that operationalization (Adams and Levin, 2012).

⛔ Wound / stump-current work with a vibrating probe and applied DC (Borgens, Vanable, Jaffe, PNAS, 1977, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.74.10.4528, PMID 270701) is adjacent history, not Pai channel-mRNA or Durant octanol.

⛔ PEMF, TENS, CES, and clinic “frequency medicine” are applied fields or currents; Levin’s 2025 interview denies those as the lab method (Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025 vs eMedica, https://emedica.in/).

📚 “Software / memory” in the papers means a brief ion-channel or GJ intervention can change anatomy and, in the two-head planaria case, persist across later cuts in water after the drug is gone by GC-MS; it is a metaphor, not a sequenced human engram (Durant et al., Biophys J, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2017.04.011, PMID 28538159; Oviedo et al., Dev Biol, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ydbio.2009.12.012, PMID 20026026).

📚 A “hyperpolarized eye spot” is a dye-ratio region in the *Xenopus* anterior neural field that is relatively more negative inside the cell before eye-field transcription factors; it is not an electrode millivolt map of an adult eye (Pai et al., Development, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759, PMID 22159581).

⛔ Planarian “immortality” / non-senescence in asexual strains is species natural history, not a Levin aging-intervention result (Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025 vs Durant 2017 / Beane 2011 polarity rewrites).

⛔ BioDome is a wearable silk + five-drug protocol, not a voltage-dye rewrite (Murugan et al., 2022).

⛔ Xenobots and anthrobots are explant self-assembly constructs, not morphostasis restoration or limb regeneration (Kriegman et al., PNAS, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1910837117; Gumuskaya et al., Adv Sci, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202303575).

## Mechanism

💯 Non-excitable cells hold a resting Vmem; changing channel or pump conductance moves that voltage (Adams and Levin, 2012).

📚 The lab’s transduction list for non-neural Vmem includes voltage-gated Ca2+ channels, voltage-sensitive phosphatases, and voltage-dependent transporters (Levin, Mol Biol Cell, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1091/mbc.e13-12-0708, PMID 25425556).

📚 In Pai 2012, verapamil (0.1 mM) blocked ectopic-eye induction; 10 µM fluoxetine or lindane did not — the paper treats Ca2+ as the transducer and GJ/serotonin blockade as not required for that phenotype (Pai et al., Development, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759).

🥼 Whether the instructive GJ signal in planaria is voltage itself or a GJ-permeant small molecule (serotonin, Ca2+, and the like) is left open in the head-shape paper; the authors cannot conclusively rule out octanol off-targets besides GJs (Emmons-Bell et al., Int J Mol Sci, 2015, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms161126065, PMID 26610482).

📚 n-Alkanols used as “GJ blockers” also block Na+ current: 0.29 mM octanol was the squid-axon INa half-block point; 1–3 mM heptanol shifted cardiac INa availability (Nelson and Makielski, Circ Res, 1991, https://doi.org/10.1161/01.res.68.4.977, PMID 1849060; N=5–6 Purkinje cells).

📚 Heptanol at 150 µM — next door to Durant’s 127 µM octanol — activated K+ current, inhibited Ca2+ current, hyperpolarized, and dropped tension in rat mesenteric arteries while barely raising input resistance; GJ uncoupling needed >200 µM (Matchkov et al., Br J Pharmacol, 2004, https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bjp.0705870, PMID 15210581).

🥼 Whether octanol / SCH-28080 two-heads are an instructive Vmem write or a dirty perturbation that lands in the same Wnt/β-catenin attractor is a live paper fight: Levin’s group treats GJ block and H+,K+-ATPase as voltage memory, while β-catenin RNAi already makes a head at a posterior wound without a software metaphor (Durant et al., 2017; Beane et al., Chem Biol, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2010.11.012, PMID 21276941; Gurley, Rink, and Sánchez Alvarado, Science, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1150029, PMID 18063757).

📚 Beane’s 18 µM SCH-28080 acidifies fragments as expected for blocked H+ efflux; bulk pH 4 (n=50) and pH 9 (n=60) baths did not flip polarity (Beane et al., Chem Biol, 2011).

📚 SCH-28080 reduced β-catenin-RNAi double-heads from 66.7% to 25%, so Wnt and Vmem are not independent stories in that paper (Beane et al., 2011).

🥼 Independent developmental biologists treat causality from bioelectric currents to morphogenetic programs as often unproven because of uncalibrated voltage dyes, nonphysiological applied DC, and broad channel pharmacology that yield phenotypes without a clear mechanism; the Levin papers treat those same dyes and baths as instructive writes (Nunes and Barriga, Annu Rev Cell Dev Biol, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-cellbio-101323-032747, PMID 40829786; Pai 2012; Durant 2017; Adams and Levin 2012).

📚 Membrane depolarization as a senescence step was already published outside the Levin aging frame: SCN9A / NF-κB-driven depolarization promotes oncogene-induced senescence via Ca2+/Rb/E2F (Warnier et al., Aging Cell, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1111/acel.12736, PMID 29446526).

📚 Levin 2014 cited the later-retracted Obokata STAP paper as support that acid / SLO depolarization induces stemness without reprogramming factors (Levin, J Physiol, 2014, https://doi.org/10.1113/jphysiol.2014.271940, PMID 24882814; Obokata et al., Nature, 2014, retracted, https://doi.org/10.1038/nature13598).

## Animal data

Nulls, low penetrance, and off-target pharmacology stay in the same subsection as the positive write.

### Ectopic eye (channel mRNA, not Kir2.1)

📚 In *Xenopus laevis*, a bilateral hyperpolarized cluster appears in the anterior neural field at stage 17/18, ~10 mV more negative than flank neighbors by sharp-electrode recording; those cells contribute to lens and retina (Pai et al., Development, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.073759, PMID 22159581, PMC3243095; N≥6 embryos for electrodes; photoconversion EosFP + CC2-DMPE; inject 4-cell, dye stage 17–19, score stage 42).

📚 Depolarizing the dorsal two blastomeres at the 4-cell stage with EXP1 or GlyR + 10 µM ivermectin (stage 17–23) produced malformed endogenous eyes in 46–48% of stage-42 tadpoles; ventral injections did not; raising bath Cl− toward 60 mM reduced the GlyR phenotype from 48% to 21% (Pai et al., 2012; n=3 batches for Cl− titration).

📚 Injecting dominant-negative Kir6.1 (DNKir6.1p or DNKir6.1-ER) into all four blastomeres (3 nl/blastomere in 3% Ficoll) yielded ~45% / ~35% eye-related phenotypes at stage 42: ~25% disrupted endogenous eyes, ~20% ectopic eye tissue, and 7.5% complete ectopic eyes (RPE + retina + lens) including gut, lateral mesoderm, and tail; GFP/lacZ controls did not (Pai et al., 2012; n=5 batches).

📚 Co-injecting hyperpolarizing Kv1.5 with DNKir6.1p dropped ectopic eyes to 2% and disrupted eyes to 11% versus 20% / 25%; Nav1.5 + 15 mM (not 10, 20, or 40 mM) sodium gluconate was the only Na+ window that induced ectopic eyes (Pai et al., 2012).

📚 Most of those ectopic tissues still formed in the head (Pai et al., 2012, Fig. S3B).

⛔ The ectopic-eye write is DNKir6.1 / GlyR / Nav1.5 voltage windows, not Kir2.1 overexpression and not a PEMF coil (Pai 2012 vs Adams et al., J Physiol, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1113/JP271930).

📚 Pax6 overexpression already induces ectopic vertebrate eyes without a Vmem protocol (Chow, Altmann, Lang, Hemmati-Brivanlou, Development, 1999, https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.126.19.4213, PMID 10477290).

📚 The “functional ectopic eyes confer vision” paper is eye-primordia grafts at stage 24, not Kir/KATP voltage misexpression (Blackiston and Levin, J Exp Biol, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1242/jeb.074963, PMID 23447666).

🤔 No independent-lab, peer-reviewed replication of Pai-style channel-mRNA ectopic eyes was found this round; absence of a published failure is not a found failure (PubMed / web search, 4 Sep 2026).

📚 Ivermectin at Pai’s 10 µM GlyR-opener dose also induces static (non-remodeling) craniofacial defects in *Xenopus* tadpoles (Phipps, McLaughlin et al., J Dev Biol, 2025, https://doi.org/10.3390/jdb13030026).

### Electric face

📚 Course-II “electric face” Vmem/pH domains in *Xenopus* neurulae, read with CC2-DMPE (5 mM stock 1:1000, 45–60 min) then DiBAC4(3) (1.9 mM stock 1:8000, ≥45 min), match folding face and head-gene territories (Vandenberg, Morrie, Adams, Dev Dyn, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1002/dvdy.22685, PMID 21761475).

📚 *xduct-noTM4* at the 1-cell stage raised craniofacial phenotypes from 3% (n=239) to 28% (n=493); wild-type *xduct* 1-of-2-cell from 2% (n=288) to 44% (n=280); 9/13 injected embryos had disrupted Course-II patterns and those sides made abnormal faces, scored stage 45–48 (Vandenberg et al., 2011).

📚 Concanamycin produced craniofacial phenotypes only when applied stage 13–16 (neural plate/folds), not before 13 or after 16 (Vandenberg et al., 2011).

📚 Human Andersen–Tawil KCNJ2 variants (including D71V) injected as mRNA into *Xenopus* 1–4 cell at least doubled craniofacial-anomaly rate versus ~14% background (a priori meaningful threshold 24%) at stage 45; ChR2-D156A light on ectoderm raised CFA vs dark-raised injected controls; electroneutral NHE3 did not (Adams et al., J Physiol, 2016, https://doi.org/10.1113/JP271930, PMC4908029).

### Left–right (early H+/K+-ATPase)

📚 H+/K+-ATPase-dependent Vmem asymmetries are an early left–right step in *Xenopus* and chick, upstream of asymmetric gene expression; SCH-28080-class reagents randomize situs; higher-dose ouabain / NPPB / veratridine also wreck AP/DV (Levin, Thorlin, Robinson, Nogi, Mercola, Cell, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(02)00939-x).

### Planaria AP polarity and two-head “memory”

📚 18 µM SCH-28080 for the first 72 h after amputation hyperpolarized *Dugesia japonica* pharynx fragments (DiBAC4(3) 0.475 µM, 24 hpa, n=13 vs control n=19), blocked anterior gene programs, and produced lasting headless regenerates; 24 h SCH did not (Beane, Morokuma, Adams, Levin, Chem Biol, 2011, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.chembiol.2010.11.012, PMID 21276941, PMC3278711; scored at 14 d).

📚 1 µM ivermectin (3-day presoak + 72 h post-cut) depolarized both blastemas and made a second posterior head in 91.7% of fragments; intact worms’ Vmem moved but anatomy did not (Beane et al., 2011).

📚 Transient GJ block that phenocopies triple innexin RNAi rewrites subsequent *D. japonica* target morphology after octanol is gone by GC-MS; pretail fragments were previously reported to give 100% double-heads (Oviedo et al., Dev Biol, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ydbio.2009.12.012, PMID 20026026).

📚 *D. japonica* pharyngeal fragments soaked in 127 µM 1-octanol (10 µL into 500 mL Poland Spring; replaced daily 3 days, then water; 20°C 7 d then 10°C) regenerated 25% double-head and 72% single-head “cryptic” at day 21 (N=573–593); recutting double-heads in water gave 100% double-head (N=100); recutting cryptics in water gave 23% double-head (N=155) (Durant et al., Biophys J, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.bpj.2017.04.011, PMID 28538159; pop=5–20 mm starved clonal *D. japonica*; endpoint=≥1 eye each end).

📚 Cryptic worms were indistinguishable from wild-type on PC2 / ndk / FzT / H3P neoblast maps (n≥5–13) but showed an ectopic posterior DiBAC4(3) depolarization; SCH-28080 reset 34% of recut double-heads to stable one-head across ≥4 generations (N=102) versus 100% persistent double-head in DMSO (N=102) (Durant et al., 2017; DiBAC soak ≥30 min, ~50 µm depth).

📚 Durant et al. did not sequence those genomes; “no DNA change” is inferred from octanol washout by GC-MS in Oviedo 2010 and from unchanged polarity-gene stains, not from WGS (Durant et al., 2017; Oviedo et al., 2010).

📚 *Smed-β-catenin-1* RNAi makes a head at the posterior wound and can turn an uncut tail into a head without a Vmem protocol (Gurley, Rink, and Sánchez Alvarado, Science, 2008, https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1150029, PMID 18063757).

📚 *Girardia dorotocephala* pre-tail fragments + octanol stochastically make other-species head shapes; hexanol had no effect; intact worms soaked >3 d did not change shape (Emmons-Bell et al., Int J Mol Sci, 2015, https://doi.org/10.3390/ijms161126065, PMID 26610482).

📜 An outside OSF run of octanol two-heads got 3/13 treated survivors two-headed (~23%) after many fragments died; the author blamed missing salts and did not recut for memory (Seebold, OSF, 2025, https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/e27mq_v1).

📜 In *Dugesia dorotocephala*, 5 µM ivermectin lysed worms within 48 h (100% dead); 1 µM reached 91.67% mortality; 0.5 µM still killed 45% (bioRxiv, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1101/186650; 8-day exposure).

### Tadpole tail (H+ pump, then Na+)

📚 *Xenopus* tails amputated at stage 40–41 fail to regenerate in 150 nM concanamycin (regeneration index 216→49, n=226) or with dominant-negative V-ATPase E subunit YCHE78 in the tail (n=66); yeast PMA1.2 H+ pump (~5 ng mRNA at 1–2 cell) rescued concanamycin (3-fold RI rise, n=127) (Adams, Masi, Levin, Development, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1242/dev.02812, PMID 17329365; 50–100 larvae/dish, 7 d score, amp where tail tapers).

📚 DiBAC 10 ng/ml ≥30 min showed bud depolarization at 6 hpa and V-ATPase-dependent repolarization by 24 hpa; refractory stage 46–47 buds stayed depolarized despite V-ATPase protein (Adams et al., 2007).

📚 MS-222 150/250 µM dropped tail RI from 265 (n=68) to 114 (n=65) / 44 (n=63); NaV1.2 appears in bud mesenchyme by 18 hpa; NaV1.2 shRNA (40% mRNA knockdown) RI=198 (n=100) vs dsRed RI=261 (n=72) (Tseng, Beane, Lemire, Masi, Levin, J Neurosci, 2010, https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.3315-10.2010, PMID 20881138; amp midpoint anus-to-tip).

📚 1 h of 20 µM monensin + 90 mM Na+ at 18–19 hpa in refractory stage 46–47 raised RI from 16.8 (n=179) to 48.5 (n=101); monensin alone (RI 10.8, n=117) or high Na+ alone (RI 20.8, n=125) did not (Tseng et al., 2010; CoroNa Green 90 µM, 45 min).

### Oncogene-induced tumor-like structures (not mammalian cancer)

📚 Human oncogene / Xrel3 / p53Trp248 mRNA (0.5–2 ng into one 16-cell blastomere) made induced tumor-like structures (ITLSs) in up to 50% of *Xenopus* embryos; ITLSs are DiBAC-depolarized; Xrel3 cells were ~11 mV depolarized vs neighbors (n=5); stage-15 depolarized foci predicted later ITLSs in ~51–56% of dye-positive embryos (Chernet and Levin, Dis Model Mech, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1242/dmm.010835, PMID 23471912, PMC3634644; immobilize with BTS, not channel anesthetics; score stage ~28–40).

📚 Co-injecting hyperpolarizing Kir4.1 or GlyR-F99A + 70 mM Cl− with Xrel3 suppressed morphologically apparent ITLSs despite remaining oncogene-tdTomato; GlyR-F99A + 70 mM Cl− hyperpolarized cells 19.4 mV (Chernet and Levin, 2013).

📚 Downstream of that hyperpolarization, ITLS rate fell 1.28-fold with 75 µM butyrate and 1.42-fold with trichostatin (Chernet and Levin, 2013, Table 2).

📚 The authors say the amphibian system does not recapitulate full mammalian tumor complexity (Chernet and Levin, 2013).

📚 Remote Kv1.5-GFP in a different 16-cell blastomere cut Xrel3 ITLS 34.9% (N=225) and Gli1 29.4% (N=292); 70 mM Cl− cut KRASG12D ITLS 37.5% (N=349); IAA blocked the Cl− rescue (Chernet and Levin, Oncotarget, 2014, https://doi.org/10.18632/oncotarget.1935, PMID 24830454; scored by stage 34; ITLS=overproliferation, +10% nuclear area, pH 6.6 vs 7.41, 12-fold hypoxia stain).

🥼 Distant hyperpolarization suppressing ITLS and GJ disruption (H7) also suppressing ITLS, while Cx26-enhanced GJC increases ITLS, are opposite directions for “reconnect the network” as a single slogan (Chernet and Levin, Oncotarget, 2014; Chernet, Adams, Lobikin, Levin, Front Physiol, 2014, https://doi.org/10.3389/fphys.2014.00519, PMID 25646081).

📚 ChR2-D156A or Arch light-gated hyperpolarization lowers KRAS ITLS incidence and can increase regression after tumors form (Chernet, Adams, Lobikin, Levin, Oncotarget, 2016, https://doi.org/10.18632/oncotarget.8036).

### Birth-defect rescue (frog teratogen, not a human RCT)

📚 Notch-ICD in the two dorsal 4-cell blastomeres produced ~46% malformed brains at stage 45; co-injecting hyperpolarizing Kv1.5 or Bir10 dropped that to ~26% and ~19%; depolarizing DNKir6.1p made it worse; CC2-DMPE:DiBAC showed Notch-ICD flattened the neural-plate hyperpolarization (Pai et al., J Neurosci, 2015, https://doi.org/10.1523/JNEUROSCI.1877-14.2015, PMID 25762681; 10 µM IVM + [Cl−]ex to flip GlyR sign).

📚 Nicotine 0.1 mg/mL from *Xenopus* stage 10–35 produced ~55% major brain morphology defects vs ~6% controls; HCN2 mRNA, predicted by BETSE and confirmed on CC2:DiBAC + whole-cell (~−50 mV neural tube vs ~−5 mV lateral ectoderm in the calibration story), restored neural-tube hyperpolarization and rescued brain shape and learning (Pai et al., Nat Commun, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03334-5, PMID 29519998; morphometrics n>26/group at stage 45).

📚 HCN2 misexpression or delayed small-molecule HCN2 activators also partially rescued nicotine / ethanol / Notch-perturbed brain, eye, heart, and gut patterning in *Xenopus* — still frog embryogenesis, not a human Vmem-rewrite trial (Pai and Levin, Wound Repair Regen, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1111/wrr.13032, PMID 35662339).

### BioDome (silk + five drugs; not a Vmem rewrite)

📚 Adult female *Xenopus* (5–6.25 cm) wearing a 24 h silk BioDome + five-drug gel (n=115) grew 18-month patterned hindlimbs versus spike controls; cocktail was 1,4-DPCA 0.014 µg/ml, BDNF 0.5 µg/ml, GH 0.5 µg/ml, RD5 0.036 µg/ml, RA 0.015 µg/ml (Murugan et al., Sci Adv, 2022, https://doi.org/10.1126/sciadv.abj2164, PMID 35080969; nerve histology MDT n=10, BD n=8, ND n=6; Levin/Kaplan Morphoceuticals).

⛔ That protocol is a wearable bioreactor plus a drug gel, not a DiBAC/CC2 rewrite and not Kir2.1 mRNA (Murugan 2022 vs Pai 2012).

📚 Methods, concentrations, and the Morphoceuticals conflict are already filed next door; this page does not re-score BioDome as a voltage-map write and does not collapse unpublished mouse BioDome into Brown 2026 AAV ([topics/salamander-like-regeneration](../salamander-like-regeneration/report.md); [Murugan-2022-BioDome-Xenopus](../salamander-like-regeneration/sources/📚/Murugan-2022-BioDome-Xenopus.md)).

🤔 Mouse BioDome / electroceutical limb data remain unpublished as of the 2025 interview and the 2023 seed PR (Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025; Morphoceuticals, 2023, https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/morphoceuticals-raises-8-million-to-advance-electroceutical-approach-to-regenerative-medicine-301747549.html).

### Xenobots and anthrobots (explant constructs)

📚 Kriegman, Blackiston, Levin, Bongard 2020: an evolutionary algorithm designs shapes in silico; *Xenopus* blastula stem cells are dissociated, aggregated, then manually carved with forceps / 13-µm cautery; cardiac progenitors are embedded for actuation; no genome edit; locomotion transfers from sim to vivo (Kriegman et al., PNAS, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1910837117).

📚 Those xenobot constructs remain explant sculptures with no genome edit, not an aging or limb trial (Kriegman et al., 2020).

📚 Gumuskaya et al.: adult human NHBE / tracheal epithelial cells → Matrigel 14 d (cilia-in spheroids) → dissolve matrix → low-adhesion + retinoic acid → cilia-out motile “anthrobots,” 30–500 µm, 5–50 µm/s, live 45–60 d, ~20 d single-cell-to-cilia-out; ~200 motile spheroids behavior-typed; can traverse and close scratches on human neural monolayers in vitro (Gumuskaya et al., Adv Sci, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1002/advs.202303575).

⛔ Xenobot / anthrobot press as “new life forms that reverse aging or grow limbs” is a different protocol from Vmem-rewrite or BioDome (Kriegman 2020; Gumuskaya 2023; Mazin / Lifespan.io, 2025).

## Human data

📚 KCNJ2 / Kir2.1 mutations cause Andersen–Tawil syndrome: periodic paralysis, ventricular arrhythmia, and dysmorphic features; Xenopus-oocyte voltage clamp showed loss of function / dominant negative (Plaster, Tawil, Tristani-Firouzi et al., Cell, 2001, https://doi.org/10.1016/s0092-8674(01)00342-7, PMID 11371347).

⛔ A Mendelian ion-channel face is not a completed human Vmem-rewrite, regeneration, or aging trial (Plaster et al., 2001 vs Adams et al., 2016 frog CFA model).

📚 Replicative-senescent neonatal human keratinocytes depolarized on BeRST (2.5 µM / 30 min) by culture day 50 (n=6, p<0.001), with p16 ~2-fold (n=4) and SA-β-gal 9.8-fold (n=6); Pinacidil / NS1643 responsiveness fell (n=3); funded via a sponsored research agreement with Astonishing Labs (Sediqi and Levin, iScience, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.isci.2025.113275, PMID 41054538; OSF methods overlap https://doi.org/10.31219/osf.io/py86e).

## Measurement

What the assay scores, and what clinics sell as a score, are different objects.

📚 DiBAC4(3) is an anionic oxonol: it enters as the interior becomes more positive; brighter DiBAC = relatively depolarized (Adams and Levin, Cold Spring Harb Protoc, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1101/pdb.prot067702, PMID 22474652).

📚 CC2-DMPE stays in the outer leaflet as a relatively voltage-insensitive reference; after darkfield/flatfield correction, brighter CC2/DiBAC ratio = relatively more polarized (Adams and Levin, 2012; Vandenberg et al., Dev Dyn, 2011).

📚 Typical soak in the protocol paper: CC2 ~5 µM / 30–90 min, then DiBAC 0.95 µM in whole embryos or 47.5 µM in culture, ≥30 min, imaged in dye; filter sets EX 405/20→EM 460/50 (CC2) and EX 470/20→EM 517/23 (DiBAC) (Adams and Levin, 2012).

📚 Pai 2012 used CC2-DMPE at 0.2 µM for 1.5 h (1 mg/ml DMSO stock), 405/460 nm (Pai et al., 2012).

📚 Vandenberg 2011 used CC2 5 mM stock 1:1000 for 45–60 min, then DiBAC 1.9 mM stock 1:8000 ≥45 min (Vandenberg et al., 2011).

📚 Adams 2007 tail used DiBAC 10 ng/ml, ≥30 min, 488/515 nm confocal (Adams et al., 2007).

📚 Planaria DiBAC is 0.475 µM (Beane) or the Adams/Oviedo Invitrogen stock, ≥30 min, 2% LMP agarose + immobilization chips; imaging depth ≤~50 µm (Beane et al., 2011; Durant et al., 2017).

📚 Adams and Levin 2012 warn that some oxonols accumulate in cortical intracellular vesicles, so the scored object can be the wrong membrane if resolution is low (Adams and Levin, 2012).

📚 Sharp electrodes remain the calibration gold standard: 80–100 MΩ, 2 M K-acetate, OC-725C in Pai; eye field vs flank ~10 mV; Chernet Xrel3 ~11 mV depolarized; GlyR-F99A + 70 mM Cl− +19.4 mV hyperpolarization (Pai et al., 2012; Chernet and Levin, 2013).

📚 Pai 2018 reports neural-tube ~−50 mV vs lateral ectoderm ~−5 mV in the BETSE/calibration story (Pai et al., Nat Commun, 2018, PMID 29519998).

📚 Later senescence work used BeRST (Berkeley Red Sensor of Transmembrane Potential) at 2.5 µM / 30 min, not the DiBAC/CC2 pair (Sediqi and Levin, iScience, 2025).

📚 Write tools in the methods papers: capped mRNA (3 nl/blastomere typical in Pai 2012) for DNKir6.1, Kv1.5, GlyR, EXP1, HCN2, Kir2.1 ATS alleles, Kir4.1, PMA1.2, YCHE78; optogenetic ChR2-D156A and Arch (Adams 2016; Chernet 2016); GJ blockers octanol 127 µM, hexanol as inactive control, lindane, H7 dominant-negative connexin; H+/Na/K reagents concanamycin 150 nM, SCH-28080 18 µM, ivermectin 1 µM (planaria) or 10 µM (frog GlyR), monensin 20 µM + 90 mM Na+, high/low Cl− or Na+ baths (Pai 2012; Beane 2011; Durant 2017; Adams 2007; Tseng 2010).

📚 Palytoxin appears on review laundry lists; Levin 2002 used SCH-28080 and noted ouabain at higher dose caused AP/DV defects — that is not one protocol (Levin et al., Cell, 2002).

📚 Adjacent wound-current measurement: vibrating probe on regenerating newt stumps, 10–100 µA/cm² leaving the stump for 5–10 days, 1–3 µA/cm² entering intact skin, Na+-dependent (Borgens, Vanable, Jaffe, PNAS, 1977, https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.74.10.4528, PMID 270701).

🤔 Clinics selling VCF / PEMF as a “−70 mV restore” score are not running DiBAC/CC2, sharp electrodes, or BeRST (eMedica, https://emedica.in/).

## What is actually on the table

💯 Vmem and gap junctions exist.

📚 The dyes have a published recipe (Adams and Levin, Cold Spring Harb Protoc, 2012, https://doi.org/10.1101/pdb.prot067702, PMID 22474652).

📚 The frog and planaria baths and injections were run with stated concentrations, stages, N, and endpoints (Pai 2012; Beane 2011; Durant 2017; Adams 2007; Chernet 2013).

📚 Channel-mRNA and pump/GJ pharmacology change *Xenopus* eye, face, tail, ITLS, and teratogen-brain scores, and change planarian head number, with penetrances that are often stochastic (7.5% complete ectopic eyes; 25% first-cut double-heads on pharynx fragments; ITLS cuts of ~30–38%).

🥼 Instructive Vmem-software vs Wnt attractor, vs GJ-permeant molecule, vs dye/pharma epiphenomenon is a live literature fight. Both sides are cited above.
