# SCCS, 2025. Scientific Opinion on Hydroxyapatite (nano) — Submission IV. SCCS/1677/25.
https://health.ec.europa.eu/publications/sccs-scientific-opinion-hydroxyapatite-nano-submission-iv_en
Used for: 📚 Specified rod-shaped nano-hydroxyapatite in toothpaste/mouthwash is not an established swallow-toxicity harm; the 2025 SCCS opinion found gastric dissolution of ingested particles and no nano-specific safety concern for the assessed specs.
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: EU SCCS final opinion adopted 26 Jun 2025, published 1 Jul 2025. Safe at ≤29.5% toothpaste and ≤10% mouthwash for uncoated rod-shaped HAP nano (aspect ratio ≤3 for ≥87% of particles; remainder ≤9; max length 122±43 nm). Unintentionally ingested particles “undergo rapid dissolution in the gastric fluid.” Opinion is not applicable to needle-shaped HAP nano.
Conflict if any: Regulatory opinion requested by the European Commission on industry submission IV.
