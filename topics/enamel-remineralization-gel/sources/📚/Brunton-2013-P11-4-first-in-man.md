# Brunton et al., 2013. Treatment of early caries lesions using biomimetic self-assembling peptides – a clinical safety trial. British Dental Journal.
https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bdj.2013.741 / PMC3813405
Used for: A first-in-man uncontrolled safety series of a single P11-4 application on Class V white-spot lesions reported lesion-size decrease at 30 days (P=0.02) and a shift from arrested/progressing toward “remineralising” (P<0.001).
Mark: 📚
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 15 healthy adults, Class V white-spot lesions, single P11-4 application. Lesion size decreased at 30 days (P=0.02). Progression ratings shifted from arrested/progressing to remineralising (P<0.001). Global impression of change improved at day 30 vs baseline (P<0.001). Safety follow-up 180 days. 11 adverse events; 2 possibly protocol-related. No parallel control — efficacy is a signal, not a comparative effect size.
Conflict if any: Early Leeds / P11-4 development paper; later commercialized as Curodont by Credentis / vVARDIS.
