# Sedlakova Kondelova et al., 2020. Efficacy of P11-4 for the treatment of initial buccal caries: a randomized clinical trial. Scientific Reports.
https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-77057-3
Used for: In a quadruple-blind split-mouth sequential RCT, a single SAP P11-4 application reduced buccal white-spot relative size by −0.19 ± 0.25 at 90 days versus −0.07 ± 0.24 with placebo (HLM difference −0.14, 95% CI −0.04 to −0.24, P=0.008) and beat later fluoride varnish on the control tooth (P=0.001).

N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 44 subjects (26 female, mean age 27.1 y), 44 tooth-pairs from University of Geneva clinic/students. Control: placebo at D0; test: SAP P11-4 at D0; both received FV at D90. Relative WSL size D0–D90: test −0.19 ± 0.25 (17/41 decreased ≥0.2; 0/41 increased ≥0.2) vs control −0.07 ± 0.24 (11/43 / 5/43). HLM H1 P11-4 vs placebo −0.14 (95% CI −0.04 to −0.24), P=0.008; H2 P11-4 vs FV −0.18, P<0.001. Endpoint=blinded morphometry on standardized photographs. Duration=90-day P11-4-vs-placebo window; follow-up to D270. NCT02020681. Post-ortho WSLs absent by design.
