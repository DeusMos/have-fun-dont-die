# Keeper et al., 2023. Systematic review and meta-analysis on the effect of self-assembling peptide P11-4 on arrest, cavitation, and progression of initial caries lesions. JADA.
https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2023.03.014 PMID: 37245138
Used for: 🥼 The 2023 JADA P11-4 meta-analysis rated every included trial moderate-to-high risk of bias; the cavitation pooled RR was 0.32 (95% CI 0.10–1.06) and crossed 1; no trial was long enough for the pre-specified 24-month progression endpoint; the manufacturer coauthored or sponsored multiple included trials.
Mark: 🥼
N / effect / population / endpoint / duration: 6 RCTs; 227 patients; 319 active lesions at baseline; 132 CR-treated lesions assessed (87% retention); follow-up 6–12 months (mean 8). Arrest RR 1.82 (1.32–2.50) across 192 lesions, but the two largest-effect trials were unmasked and combined CR with fluoride varnish; the remaining subgroup RR 1.30 (0.84–2.02) crossed 1. Cavitation RR 0.32 (0.10–1.06) across 143 lesions (3 trials with any events). Merged ICDAS RR 3.68 (0.42–32.3) across 109 lesions, CI crosses 1. RoB drivers: no masking of clinicians/statisticians, no placebo/patient masking, missing or non-followed registration, missing data, baseline imbalance. Manufacturer coauthored and sponsored 2 trials and sponsored a third.
Conflict if any: Open-access ADA article. Several included trials funded/coauthored by Credentis/vVARDIS.
