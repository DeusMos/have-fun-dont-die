# Topic: Enamel remineralization gels

**Evidence cutoff:** September 3, 2026  
**Last updated:** September 3, 2026

This repository is not medical advice. It is a sourced information dump for anyone who wants the details.

The slogan in the wild is a 2025–2026 “scientists made a gel that regrows enamel” fad: a fluoride-free protein gel from the University of Nottingham / Mintech-Bio (Epinamel; Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 4–5 Nov 2025) paints on like a varnish, replaces fillings, heals cavities, and restores worn teeth. Feeds recirculated it in July 2026, often dated as if new. Parallel slogans already sell chairside Curodont Repair (self-assembling peptide P11-4, vVARDIS) as “guided enamel regeneration” of early white spots, and sell nano-hydroxyapatite pastes and CPP-ACP crèmes as the same story. Nearby true facts — a ~10 μm epitaxial film on extracted teeth, acellular mature enamel that can take mineral into porous early lesions, and P11-4 RCTs on non-cavitated white spots — get laundered into a filling-replacing consumer cure. “The gel” is not one SKU.

## The claim and the slogan

The scientific claim under test is that a painted-on gel rematerializes teeth: lost enamel comes back at a usable thickness, cavities fill in, and restorations become unnecessary.

🤔 University of Nottingham’s 4 Nov 2025 press release describes a fluoride-free protein gel that “can be rapidly applied to teeth in the same way dentists currently apply standard fluoride treatments,” names spin-out Mintech-Bio, and quotes Álvaro Mata hoping “to have a first product out next year” (University of Nottingham News, 2025, https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/news/new-gel-restores-dental-enamel-and-could-revolutionise-tooth-repair; Mata and Hasan hold Mintech-Bio equity).

🤔 ScienceDaily the same week headlined a gel that “repairs and regenerates tooth enamel”; a 23 Jul 2026 ScienceDaily recirc retitled it “Goodbye, cavities? New gel could regrow tooth enamel” and still quoted Mata’s “first product out by next year” on the same November 2025 paper (ScienceDaily, 6 Nov 2025, https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2025/11/251106003151.htm; ScienceDaily, 23 Jul 2026, https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/07/260717033046.htm).

🤔 MedicalDaily (29 Jul 2026) mapped that July feed spike back to the November 2025 extracted-tooth paper, stated no commercial product was on sale, and noted some outlets attached a 2026 publication date (Mercer, MedicalDaily, 2026, https://www.medicaldaily.com/enamel-regrowth-gel-nottingham-evidence-check-476545).

⛔ Collapsing that paper into a living-mouth crown restoration, a filling replacement, or “goodbye cavities” is a press omission of extracted-tooth methods, a ~10 μm layer, and the authors’ own in-vivo-required limit (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; University of Nottingham, 2025; ScienceDaily, 6 Nov 2025 and 23 Jul 2026).

🤔 Mintech-Bio’s site sells the slogan “Enamel, Remineralized™” and a contact form; it does not list an orderable SKU, price, or IFU (Mintech-Bio, https://www.mintech.bio/; copyright line still said 2024 at fetch).

🤔 vVARDIS sells Curodont Repair as dentist-only “guided enamel regeneration” and leads with “above 90% clinical success” for arrest/regression of initial lesions (vVARDIS professional, https://professional.vvardis.com/product/curodont-repair/; vVARDIS sells the applicator).

🤼 Clinic and consumer copy splits on whether “the gel” is Nottingham/Epinamel, Curodont, or a drugstore nano-HA / CPP-ACP tube, and on whether white-spot fade means a tooth grew back (Gold Coast Dental, 2 Dec 2025, https://goldcoastdental.com/blog/enamel-regeneration-gels-hype-or-real-breakthrough/; Community Shores Dental, https://www.communityshoresdental.com/dental-services/preventative-dentistry/curodont-enamel-regeneration/; NCT07730359 uses Curodont as the Epinamel comparator, which keeps the devices distinct).

## What the words mean

💯 After enamel maturation, many ameloblasts die by apoptosis or regress, and remaining enamel-organ cells are shed at eruption or folded into the junctional epithelium, leaving mature erupted enamel acellular with no resident cell that can grow a new crown (Lacruz et al., Physiological Reviews, 2017, https://doi.org/10.1152/physrev.00030.2016, PMID 28468833; Farci and Soni, StatPearls, 2023, https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK572055/).

💯 Human occlusal/working enamel is on the order of millimeters thick — commonly cited up to about 2.5 mm — and tapers to a feather edge at the cervix, not tens of micrometers (Nanci, Ten Cate's Oral Histology, ch. 7, https://pocketdentistry.com/7-enamel-composition-formation-and-structure/).

📚 Remineralization in this literature is mineral-ion deposition into a porous early lesion or onto a surface, not de novo crown formation (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; Ismail et al., Community Dentistry and Oral Epidemiology, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0528.2007.00347.x).

📚 Epitaxial mineralization, as Hasan et al. use it, is new apatite growing in crystallographic continuity with existing enamel crystals; layer thickness tracked the elastin-like recombinamer (ELR) coating and was reported up to ~10 μm (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y).

🤔 Guided enamel regeneration is vVARDIS marketing English for P11-4 monomers assembling a matrix inside a non-cavitated lesion and templating hydroxyapatite from saliva (vVARDIS professional, https://professional.vvardis.com/product/curodont-repair/).

🤔 Epinamel is Mintech-Bio’s 2-part professional ELR device in NCT07730359 (Part A dried, then Part B), not a consumer tube and not Curodont (ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07730359, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07730359; Mintech Bio Ltd is the sponsor).

⛔ Treating white-spot arrest, a ~10 μm epitaxial film, or a fluorescence-score drop as “the tooth grew back” fails the millimeter-scale meaning of enamel the slogan uses (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025; Nanci, Ten Cate's Oral Histology, ch. 7; Ismail et al., 2007).

## Mechanism

📚 An ELR coating on acid-etched extracted human molars triggered epitaxial fluorapatite / hydroxyapatite-like nanocrystals (~50 nm diameter) that recreated prismatic, interprismatic, and aprismatic architecture in a mineral layer up to ~10 μm thick, matching the coating (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y, PMID 41188228; N=extracted molars, representative SEM n=3–12 independent experiments by region; endpoint=SEM/TEM/SAED/EDX; duration=10 days in fluorapatite-supersaturated solution or artificial saliva, 14 days in whole saliva from 3 donors; Hasan and Mata are co-founders and equity holders in Mintech-Bio Ltd).

📚 On acid-etched extracted prismatic enamel, that ELR-guided layer restored Young’s modulus from 36.9 ± 14.3 GPa (etched) to 76.3 ± 18.7 GPa and hardness from 1.1 ± 0.6 GPa to 3.1 ± 0.8 GPa, statistically indistinguishable from native enamel (80.7 ± 18.3 GPa / 3.4 ± 0.9 GPa) (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; N=indentations from 3 independent experiments per group; endpoint=nanoindentation E/H; duration=10-day mineralisation; same Mintech-Bio equity).

📚 ELR-coated extracted enamel incubated 14 days in unstimulated whole saliva from 3 donors (age 20–40, replenished every 3 h daytime) recreated prismatic architecture and recovered E = 74.6 ± 15.3 GPa and H = 2.7 ± 1.1 GPa (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; still dishes, not a mouth).

📚 Hasan et al. wrote that the tests “do not fully recreate the complexity of the in vivo oral environment” and that in vivo validation is required (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y).

📚 Self-assembling peptide P11-4 is a different chemistry: a monomeric peptide sold as Curodont Repair / Curodont Repair Fluoride Plus, said to assemble a scaffold inside a non-cavitated lesion (Alkilzy, Tarabaih, Santamaria, Splieth, Journal of Dental Research, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1177/0022034517730531, PMID 28892645; Keeper, Kibbe, Thakkar-Samtani et al., JADA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2023.03.014).

📚 Nano-hydroxyapatite dentifrice and casein-phosphopeptide–amorphous calcium phosphate (CPP-ACP) crèmes are again different chemistries: particle or peptide-stabilized mineral reservoirs in consumer pastes, not the Nottingham ELR coating (Cavalcante et al., Caries Research, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1159/000533547; SCCS/1677/25, 26 Jun 2025, https://health.ec.europa.eu/publications/sccs-scientific-opinion-hydroxyapatite-nano-submission-iv_en).

📚 Duraphat-class fluoride varnish is 5% NaF (~22,600 ppm F / 2.26% F), professionally painted; it is the usual positive comparator in white-spot and P11-4 trials and a coating benchmark in Hasan, not the viral gel (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025; Cardoso et al., Brazilian Dental Science, 2021, https://doi.org/10.14295/bds.2021.v24i3.2478).

## Animal data

📚 The paper behind the 2025 viral gel mineralized acid-etched extracted human molars in mineralization baths or donor-saliva dishes for 10–14 days — ex vivo human tissue, not a living-animal mouth (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; N=extracted molars, n=3–8 per SEM arm, 3 saliva donors; layer up to ~10 μm).

## Human data

🤔 As of the 2026-09-03 ClinicalTrials.gov snapshot, NCT07730359 is a recruiting, unmasked, estimated-N=20 split-mouth pilot of Epinamel (2-part ELR device) versus Curodont Repair on post-orthodontic white-spot lesions, actual start 2026-07-06, estimated primary completion 2027-01-31, hasResults=false (Mintech Bio Ltd, NCT07730359, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07730359; ages 12–60, braces off 24–120 h; primary=morphometric WSL size change >20% at 6 weeks; secondary=DIAGNOdent; site Guy’s and St Thomas’ / King’s College London; Avijit Banerjee is a contact and a Hasan 2025 coauthor; Innovate UK grant 10072636).

⛔ NCT07730359 excludes cavitated lesions, lasts 6 weeks on white-spot photographs, and treats Epinamel and Curodont as different devices — not a heal-cavities / replace-fillings / restore-worn-teeth operationalization (Mintech Bio Ltd, NCT07730359, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT07730359).

🐉 In-mouth efficacy of Epinamel — white-spot regression, worn-enamel restoration, or filling replacement — has no sourced human outcome as of 3 Sep 2026 (inference from Hasan 2025 being extracted-tooth only + NCT07730359 having no results).

🥼 A 2023 JADA systematic review of P11-4 (Curodont Repair; no included trial used Curodont Repair Fluoride Plus) reported a large increase in arrest of initial non-cavitated lesions versus parallel controls (RR 1.82, 95% CI 1.32–2.50; attributable risk 45%; NNT 2.8) and a mean 32% (SD 28%) reduction in photographed lesion size (Keeper, Kibbe, Thakkar-Samtani et al., JADA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2023.03.014, PMID 37245138; N=6 RCTs, 227 patients, 319 active lesions, 132 CR-treated lesions assessed; duration=6–12 months, mean 8; pop=initial lesions in permanent teeth).

🥼 The same Keeper 2023 review rated every included trial moderate-to-high risk of bias (unmasked assessors, missing data, registration gaps; manufacturer coauthored or sponsored multiple trials); the two largest-effect arrest trials were unmasked and combined P11-4 with fluoride varnish, and the remaining subgroup arrest RR was 1.30 (95% CI 0.84–2.02); cavitation pooled RR was 0.32 (95% CI 0.10–1.06) and crossed 1; no trial met the pre-specified 24-month progression endpoint (Keeper et al., JADA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2023.03.014; arrest remaining-subgroup 192-lesion context as reported; cavitation 143 lesions / 3 trials with events).

🥼 In a single-blind RCT, one chairside Curodont Repair (P11-4) plus fluoride varnish on erupting-molar occlusal ICDAS 1–3 lesions inactivated 80% of test lesions by Nyvad at 6 months versus 34% with fluoride varnish alone, with laser-fluorescence odds ratio 3.5 (P=0.015) favoring P11-4 (Alkilzy, Tarabaih, Santamaria, Splieth, Journal of Dental Research, 2018, https://doi.org/10.1177/0022034517730531, PMID 28892645, PMC6429572, NCT02724592; N=70 randomized, analysed n=31 test / 34 control, one lesion per child aged 7–13; LF 45.2→27.2 vs 33.0→32.6; supported in part by Credentis AG, which then sold Curodont Repair).
🤔 vVARDIS quotes that 80% vs 34% Nyvad figure on the Curodont Repair product page (vVARDIS professional, https://professional.vvardis.com/product/curodont-repair/; Alkilzy et al., 2018).

🥼 In a three-arm RCT of non-cavitated occlusal caries, monomeric P11-4 plus fluoride varnish, or P11-4 plus twice-weekly home self-assembling-peptide-matrix gel, dropped laser fluorescence by −8.5 ± 5.9 and −7.7 ± 7.8 at day 360 while fluoride varnish alone rose +5.5 ± 7.8 (P<0.0005 vs control), and Nyvad inactive/active moved to 27/0 in both P11-4 arms versus 13/10 in control (Doberdoli, Bommer, Begzati et al., Scientific Reports, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-60815-8; N=90, 30 per arm; ICDAS regression 6.7% and 20.0% vs 0% control and 23.3% control progression; C. Bommer is a Credentis employee).

🥼 In a quadruple-blind split-mouth sequential RCT, a single P11-4 application reduced buccal white-spot relative size by −0.19 ± 0.25 at 90 days versus −0.07 ± 0.24 with placebo (HLM difference −0.14, 95% CI −0.04 to −0.24, P=0.008) and beat later fluoride varnish on the control tooth (P=0.001) (Sedlakova Kondelova, Mannaa, Bommer et al., Scientific Reports, 2020, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-020-77057-3; N=44 adults, 44 tooth-pairs; C. Bommer is a Credentis employee).

🥼 Curodont Repair Fluoride Plus (P11-4 plus fluoride) lowered DIAGNOdent more than Bifluorid 10 NaF varnish at 3 and 6 months and moved 65.5% of lesions from DIAGNOdent score 3 (11–20) to score 1 (0–4) versus 13.8% with varnish, with 60% less risk of caries progression at 6 months (Shaalan, Fawzy El-Sayed, Abouauf, Clinical Oral Investigations, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1007/s00784-024-05822-z, PMID 39037455, NCT05094492; N=28 participants, 58 incipient lesions; this is CRFP, not Epinamel).

🥼 In a 2025 triple-blind pediatric white-spot RCT, P11-4 produced the smallest color improvement and the largest residual lesion dimension versus two fluoride-containing varnishes at 9 months (Abd Elaziz et al., BDJ Open, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41405-025-00353-5; N=39 WSLs, 13/arm, ages 8–12, ICDAS 1–2; 9-month ΔE 12.21±3.03 for P11-4 vs 9.37±3.79 arginine-NaF and 9.15±2.74 TCP-F; residual WSL 27.93±8.98% vs 14.98±7.55% vs 22.32±8.61%; Ain Shams, NCT05127889; no vVARDIS/Credentis sponsorship stated in the fetched text).

📚 A first-in-man uncontrolled P11-4 safety series on Class V white spots reported lesion-size decrease at 30 days (P=0.02) and a shift toward “remineralising” ratings (P<0.001), with 11 adverse events of which 2 were rated possibly protocol-related (Brunton, Davies, Burke et al., British Dental Journal, 2013, https://doi.org/10.1038/sj.bdj.2013.741, PMC3813405; N=15 healthy adults; 180-day safety follow-up).

📚 The vVARDIS “>90% success” citation is an uncontrolled retrospective of noncavitated proximal lesions, paid and coauthored by vVARDIS, with a median 6.9-month follow-up and no concurrent control: 37% radiographic stage regression, 56% no change, 7% progression, 371/405 unrestored (Godenzi et al., JADA, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.adaj.2023.07.009, PMID 37642611; N=219 children ages 10–19, 405 lesions; Swiss school-clinic proximal RA1–RA3).

📚 Consumer CPP-ACP “remineralizing” pastes did not beat fluoride alone on fluorescence endpoints in a 2024 meta-analysis (Cavalcante et al., Caries Research, 2024, https://doi.org/10.1159/000533547; N=15 RCTs quantitative; LF SMD −0.30 / −0.47 / −0.49 at 1 / 3 / 6 months, all CIs cross 0; GRADE very low).
📚 CPP-ACP is a different chemistry than the Nottingham ELR gel (Cavalcante et al., 2024; Hasan, Mata et al., 2025).

📚 Specified rod-shaped nano-hydroxyapatite in toothpaste/mouthwash is not an established swallow-toxicity harm; SCCS/1677/25 found gastric dissolution of ingested particles and no nano-specific safety concern for the assessed specs (SCCS, 26 Jun 2025, https://health.ec.europa.eu/publications/sccs-scientific-opinion-hydroxyapatite-nano-submission-iv_en; safe ≤29.5% toothpaste / ≤10% mouthwash for uncoated rod-shaped HAP nano; opinion not applicable to needle-shaped HAP nano).

## Measurement

📚 ICDAS / ICDAS II is a visual ordinal caries code, not a mineral-mass assay: code 1 = first enamel change after air-drying; code 2 = distinct change visible when wet; codes 3–6 track localized breakdown through extensive cavitation (Ismail et al., Community Dentistry and Oral Epidemiology, 2007, https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0528.2007.00347.x).

📚 Quantitative light-induced fluorescence (QLF) reports ΔF (percent fluorescence loss vs sound enamel, used as a mineral-loss proxy) and ΔQ (ΔF × lesion area) — a longitudinal optical assay, not a crown-thickness measurement (Pretty, Edgar, Higham, Journal of Biomedical Optics, 2002, https://doi.org/10.1117/1.1427044).

🥼 DIAGNOdent (KaVo) is a 655-nm laser-fluorescence score (0–99) driven largely by porphyrin fluorescence in carious tissue, not a direct mineral-content meter; several papers report poor correlation with histologic depth, while P11-4 trials and NCT07730359 still use it as a lesion endpoint (Amaechi, Open Dentistry Journal, 2018, PMC5750685, https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5750685/; ClinicalTrials.gov NCT07730359 maps 0–12 sound, 13–24 initial enamel, ≥30 likely dentin).

📚 Surface/cross-sectional nanoindentation and SEM on extracted teeth measure GPa and crystal morphology of a thin film, not clinical cavity fill (Hasan, Mata et al., Nature Communications, 2025, https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-025-64982-y; E/H numbers as in Mechanism).

📚 Hasan’s reported ELR/mineral layer (~2–10 μm) is about two hundred times thinner than ~2 mm occlusal enamel (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025; Nanci, Ten Cate's Oral Histology, ch. 7).

🤔 Dentists apply Curodont Repair chairside on non-cavitated “watch” lesions (vVARDIS FAQ: ICDAS 1–3 / E1–D1 if not cavitated): ~2% NaOCl pellicle removal, 35% phosphoric-acid etch 20 s, sponge application, 5 min dwell, no rinse, NPO 30 min; one applicator is marketed for 1–2 lesions; box of 10 listed ~€330 ex VAT on the Swiss professional shop when in stock (vVARDIS professional, accessed 2026-09-03, https://professional.vvardis.com/product/curodont-repair/; vVARDIS sells Curodont Repair; FAQ: does not fill cavities; white appearance may fade but not vanish; claimed mineral change 4–6 months).

## Speculative

🐉 That the Nottingham ELR coating will rematerialize enamel inside a living mouth — white-spot regression, worn-enamel restoration, or filling replacement — is a translational leap from a ~10 μm epitaxial film on extracted teeth; the authors said in vivo validation is required (inference from Hasan, Mata et al., 2025 + NCT07730359 having no results).

🐉 Commercial availability of Epinamel as a dentist or consumer product “next year” from the Nov 2025 quotes remains a hope sitting on a press sentence and a recruiting 20-person pilot, not a listing (inference from University of Nottingham 2025 + Mintech-Bio site + NCT07730359).

🐉 Restoring millimeter-scale lost occlusal enamel or replacing a cavitated filling with any of these gels would require a thickness and a clinical operationalization that none of the sourced human endpoints measure (inference from Hasan ~10 μm + ICDAS 1–2 / WSL morphometry endpoints + NCT exclusion of cavities).

## What is actually on the table

💯 Mature erupted enamel is acellular and does not grow a new crown; occlusal enamel is millimeter-scale (Lacruz 2017; Farci 2023; Nanci / Ten Cate).

📚 The 2025 viral gel is an ELR coating that grew a ~10 μm epitaxial apatite film with recovered nanoindentation numbers on extracted teeth in dishes (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025).
📚 Hasan and Mata hold Mintech-Bio equity (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025 competing interests).
🐉 There is no sourced in-mouth Epinamel outcome (Hasan, Mata et al., 2025; NCT07730359 recruiting, hasResults=false).

⛔ Press “repairs and regenerates / goodbye cavities / product next year” is a quote overlay on that extracted-tooth paper, recirculated in July 2026 as if new (University of Nottingham 2025; ScienceDaily Nov 2025 and Jul 2026).

🥼 P11-4 / Curodont is a different, already-sold chairside peptide for non-cavitated white spots. Arrest and size-change RCTs exist and so do a high-RoB meta-analysis, a cavitation CI that crosses 1, and a 2025 pediatric RCT in which P11-4 lost to fluoride-containing varnishes on color and residual lesion size (Alkilzy 2018; Doberdoli 2020; Sedlakova Kondelova 2020; Shaalan 2024 vs Keeper 2023 and Abd Elaziz 2025).
